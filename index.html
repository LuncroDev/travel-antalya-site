About Antalya

Antalya is where the Taurus Mountains drop into a turquoise sea. In one trip you can walk Roman streets in Perge, swim off Konyaaltı Beach and eat dinner inside the old walls of Kaleiçi.

Stay in a Belek golf resort, a boutique house in the old town or a quiet harbour hotel in Kaş. Catch a concert, a festival or an evening at Aspendos.

Every hotel, restaurant and event in this guide links to its official source, so you can check the details that matter before you book. Whatever you’re looking for, start here.
