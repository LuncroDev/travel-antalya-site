# Hotel source register

Editorial review: 9 November 2026. Sources reviewed through indexed official hotel/group pages and direct web reads for key corrections. This is not a live room inventory or proof of availability. Hotel copy is original, short editorial description; no scraped review scores, inferred prices or reused property photography. Confirm dated facilities, policies and packages with the provider.

## Corrections

- Akra Antalya: official contact confirms Şirinyalı, Muratpaşa; removed Konyaaltı/museum/tram claims. Kept `/hotels/akra-hotel/`.
- Hotel SU: official contact confirms Meltem, Muratpaşa at the Konyaaltı beach end; removed Lara coordinates, unverified all-inclusive and private-waterpark claims. Kept `/hotels/hotel-su-aqualand/`.
- Mardan Palace: current official brand/site used, not historical Titanic affiliation. Opening/renovation dates require direct confirmation.
- Generic reused photos and unsourced price tiers removed from all hotel records.
- Area filters are travel areas, not municipal districts: Side, Belek, Kalkan and Konyaaltı coast are intentionally traveller-facing.
