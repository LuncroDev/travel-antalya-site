Lures Hotel is an adults-only boutique hotel in Kalkan, on Kalamar Bay, in the Kaş district. Guests must be 16 or over, and stays are bed and breakfast. The site's footer carries a 2026 date, and the rooms, dining and spa pages were all live when we checked on 14 November 2026.

## Where it is

The address is Karanfil Sokak, Blok A No. 30, Kalkan, Kaş. The hotel's own Kalkan guide gives distances from the hotel: about 8 km (a 10–15 minute drive) to [Kaputaş Beach](/blog/kaputas-beach-travel-antalya/), 15–17 km (20–25 minutes) to [Patara](/blog/patara-beach-and-ancient-city/), about 19 km (25–30 minutes) to [Xanthos](/things-to-do/xanthos-ancient-city/) and about 39 km (40–45 minutes) to Saklıkent. Our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/) covers the wider coast.

## The setting

The hotel has a private beach on the Mediterranean with shaded seafront cabanas, which are sold as separate packages from a price list. There are open-air pools and an infinity pool kept for in-house guests, plus an indoor pool in the spa. No pool sizes, heating details or opening hours are published.

The spa has a hammam with a scrub-and-foam treatment, a sauna, massage rooms and a gym called Lures Fit. Treatment prices and spa hours are not on the site. The honeymoon package adds a welcome cocktail and 20% off spa treatments. There are GelDol charging points for electric cars.

## Rooms & dining

There are six room types, each for two guests. The site gives no total number of rooms.

- **Superior Room with Sea View**: 60 m².
- **Deluxe Room with Sea View & Jacuzzi**: 90 m².
- **Junior Suite with Private Pool and Sea View**: 90–100 m², with a 45 m² pool terrace.
- **Senior Suite with Private Pool and Garden View**: 130–200 m², with a 60–190 m² pool terrace.
- **Penthouse Suite with Jacuzzi and Sea View**: 140 m² on the upper level, with a 50 m² terrace and its own jacuzzi.
- **Villa with Private Pool and Sea View**: 110–130 m² inside, over two levels, with a 70 m² terrace and a private garden.

Breakfast is an open buffet from 08:00 to 11:00, with a view over Kalamar Bay. The Snack Restaurant & Bar runs from 11:00 to 18:00. Asedo, the à la carte restaurant, serves Turkish and international dishes from 19:00 to 23:00. Both menus are online. Rates are bed and breakfast, so lunch and dinner are paid separately.

## Who it suits

Couples, honeymooners among them, who want a child-free base with a private pool or jacuzzi. It won't work for families with children under 16. The hotel also says its facilities are not accessible for guests with disabilities because of the building's structure. Compare other [Kalkan hotels](/hotels/?area=Kalkan) such as [Villa Mahal](/hotels/villa-mahal/) and [Fidanka Hotel](/hotels/fidanka-hotel/). For a long breakfast in the hills above town, [Avlu Kalkan](/restaurants/avlu-kalkan/) is in Bezirgan.

## Getting there

The hotel names Dalaman and Antalya airports as the gateways to Kalkan but publishes no distance to either. Its reservations team handles transfer requests. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) compares Antalya and Dalaman for the Kaş coast, and our [Antalya to Kaş day trip](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) describes the coast road.

## What to check for this stay

The site publishes no check-in or check-out times and no season dates, so confirm the hotel is open on your dates. Bookings made on the hotel's website can be cancelled free of charge up to 30 days before check-in. Pets are not accepted. If you want a cabana, ask for the current price list when you book.
