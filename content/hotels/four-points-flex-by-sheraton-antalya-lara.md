Four Points Flex by Sheraton Antalya Lara is a small, no-frills Marriott hotel on the airport side of the city. It has 52 rooms, a 24-hour gym and one restaurant, and it suits a night before or after a flight better than a beach week. We built this profile from Marriott's own hotel pages, read on 26 November 2026; the footer reads © 1996–2026.

One naming quirk: the page title says **Four Points Flex**, but parts of the text and the Turkish logo link still say **Four Points Express by Sheraton Lara**. It is the same hotel, so search for either name.

## Where it is

The address is Çağlayan Mahallesi, Bülent Ecevit Bulvarı No:144, Muratpaşa, 07230 Antalya. That puts it on the main road between the airport and the Lara hotel strip, in the [Muratpaşa district](/destinations/muratpasa/). Marriott's pages mention sandy Lara Beach, the old town, shopping malls and the [Düden Waterfalls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/) nearby, but give no distances to any of them. For the beach itself, see our [Lara Beach guide](/things-to-do/lara-beach/).

## The setting

This is a city block, not a resort. No pool, spa menu or garden appears in the hotel's amenities list. What you get is a 24-hour fitness centre, a business centre, a meeting room the text calls the "Express Meeting Hall", a 24-hour front desk, daily housekeeping and staff who speak English, Turkish, German and Russian. The hotel says it holds Türkiye's Sustainable Tourism Industry Criteria certificate and publishes a water footprint of 339.06 litres per room night.

## Rooms & dining

Marriott counts **52 guest rooms**. The photo gallery names five types: Standard Double, Superior Double, Superior King, Superior Triple and Executive Triple, with double, king, or double-plus-single beds. No room sizes or views are published.

Rooms have free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, a work desk, a laptop safe, a mini fridge, an HD TV and a coffee and tea machine. The booking engine allows up to eight guests per room, which looks like a default setting rather than a real room size.

There is one restaurant, **Aspendos**. Its hours are not on the pages we read. Breakfast is unclear: the rooms page invites you to enjoy breakfast, while the FAQ says the hotel does not offer free breakfast. Treat it as an extra unless your rate says otherwise.

## Who it suits

Adults on a short stop: an early flight, a late arrival, or a work trip to the airport side of town. The minimum check-in age is 18, pets are not allowed, and there is no pool, so it is not a family holiday base. Families should compare [Lara's beach hotels](/hotels/?area=Lara%20%26%20Kundu).

## Getting there

The airport listing gives **7.0 km** to Antalya Airport, while the hotel description says "about 5 km". The hotel runs **no shuttle**. It lists Çağlayan Taxi at **€20 one way**, on request (+90 544 502 05 06). There is **no on-site parking**, so a hire car is a poor fit.

Our guide to [hotels near Antalya Airport](/blog/hotels-near-antalya-airport/) compares this hotel with its neighbours, including the [Hampton by Hilton Antalya Airport](/hotels/hampton-by-hilton-antalya-airport/).

## What to check for this stay

- **Check-in and out:** check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00. Ask about an early or late room if your flight doesn't fit.
- **Breakfast:** the pages contradict each other, so confirm whether your rate includes it.
- **Accessibility:** the overview lists a roll-in shower, grab rails and hearing kits, but the rooms page says "No Accessible Rooms Available at this Hotel". Call +90 242 329 10 10 before booking.
- **Wi-Fi:** free for Marriott Bonvoy members, according to the amenities list.
- **Transport:** no parking and no shuttle, so book the taxi in advance for an early flight.
