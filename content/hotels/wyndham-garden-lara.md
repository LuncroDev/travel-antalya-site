Wyndham Garden Lara is an all-inclusive resort in Kemerağzı, at the eastern, Kundu end of Antalya's Lara hotel strip. Wyndham's page promises a beachfront water park, an outdoor pool, two à la carte restaurants and a spa, but it is thin on hard numbers. We read it on 26 November 2026; the footer reads ©2026.

## Where it is

The address is Kemerağzı Mahallesi, Yaşar Sobutay, 07112 Antalya, and the phone number is +90 242 352 32 32. Kemerağzı belongs to the [Aksu district](/destinations/aksu/), east of the city. Wyndham names TerraCity mall and the old town as places to visit but gives no distances. For the sand nearby, see our [Lara Beach guide](/things-to-do/lara-beach/) and the free [Lara public beaches](/blog/lara-public-beaches/).

## The setting

The overview describes a beachfront water park and an outdoor pool. Its gallery adds a family pool and water slides. Wyndham does not say whether any pool is heated or indoors, which matters if you come between November and April.

Lara Beach is described as nearby, and the beach amenities listed are sunbeds, volleyball and food and drink service. The hotel's gallery shows a sandy beach with a long wooden pier, but the text never says whether that beach is the hotel's own or how far it is from the rooms. There are also tennis courts, a gym and a children's play area.

## Rooms & dining

Wyndham gives no room count, room types or sizes. Rooms are non-smoking and have a balcony, a flat-screen TV, a minibar, a coffee and tea maker, a desk, a safe, an iron, slippers and a hair dryer.

The board is **all-inclusive**, with one catch: the dining section says premium alcoholic drinks cost extra. Published hours:

- **Main Restaurant (buffet):** breakfast 07:00–10:00, late breakfast 10:00–11:00, lunch 12:30–14:30, dinner 19:00–21:30.
- **Mexican Restaurant:** dinner 19:00–21:30, reservation required.
- **Asian Restaurant** (Chinese dishes): dinner 19:00–21:30, reservation required.
- **Room service:** 24 hours, for a fee.

The spa has a sauna and a steam room.

## Who it suits

Families and couples who want an all-inclusive base close to the city and the airport, with a water park on site. Golfers heading to Belek can reach the courses quickly; see our [airport-to-Belek golf guide](/blog/antalya-airport-to-belek-golf-courses/). Adults wanting a quieter, adults-only stay should compare our [adults-only hotels guide](/blog/adults-only-hotels-antalya/).

## Getting there

Wyndham puts the hotel **12 km from Antalya Airport**. No shuttle is mentioned, so plan on a taxi or a transfer; parking is **free**, and there is a car-rental desk on site. Our [Antalya airport hotels guide](/blog/hotels-near-antalya-airport/) covers the other options on this side of town.

## What to check for this stay

- **Season:** no opening dates are published. Ask which pools, slides and restaurants are open in winter.
- **Beach:** ask whether the hotel has its own beach and how you get there.
- **Drinks:** confirm which drinks the all-inclusive package covers.
- **Check-in and check-out times** and the room types are not on the page, so ask when you book.
