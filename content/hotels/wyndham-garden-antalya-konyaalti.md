Wyndham Garden Antalya Konyaaltı is a modern mid-size hotel in Sarısu, where the long Konyaaltı seafront ends and the mountains begin. It suits travellers who want a self-contained suite, a pool and a car park rather than a big all-inclusive resort. We read the hotel's official Wyndham page on 27 November 2026; its footer reads ©2026. The page is brief, so here is what it does and doesn't say.

## Where it is

The address is Sarısu Mahallesi, 149. Sokak No. 4, 07130 Antalya, and the phone number is +90 242 228 81 81. Sarısu sits at the western end of the [Konyaaltı district](/destinations/konyaalti/). Wyndham places the hotel off the D400 coast road, **17 km from Antalya Airport**, with "easy access" to the city centre and the marina, but prints no other distances.

The page calls it a hotel "near Konyaaltı Beach" and lists the Sarısu beach and picnic area among nearby sights, again without distances. Our [Konyaaltı Beach guide](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/) covers the shingle shore to the east.

## The setting

There is an **outdoor pool** and a **kids' pool**; Wyndham does not say whether either is heated. Its two exterior photos show a six-storey building with curved white balconies right under a steep rock face. The pool and restaurant images in the gallery look like architectural renderings rather than photographs, so we show only the exterior shots.

Facilities named are a spa with a **Turkish bath**, a gym, a business centre and **107 m² of event space** in two rooms, for up to 85 people at a conference or 65 at a banquet. Free parking, free Wi-Fi, a 24-hour front desk, car and bike rental and local and airport shuttles are also listed.

## Rooms & dining

Wyndham names standard guest rooms, a **residence-style suite** and a **family room**. Suites and family rooms add a terrace, a living room, a **kitchenette and a washing machine**, which is useful for longer stays. All rooms have a smart TV, a minibar, a coffee and tea maker, an iron and a hair dryer. The page gives **no room count or sizes**.

- **Lykia Restaurant:** buffet breakfast daily 07:00–10:30; all-day snacks 12:00–22:00; "local cuisine and international favorites".
- **Olympos Bar:** daily 09:00–23:00.
- **Room service:** 24/7.

The page does not state a board basis, so check whether your rate includes breakfast.

## Who it suits

Families and long-stay visitors who want a kitchenette, and drivers heading west to Kemer or up to [Termessos](/things-to-do/termessos/). It is also a quiet base for the Konyaaltı seafront and the beach park. If you'd rather walk to the sea in minutes, compare our [Konyaaltı beach hotels guide](/blog/konyaalti-beach-hotels-compared-winter/).

## Getting there

From the airport, Wyndham gives 17 km along the D400. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/) covers taxis and buses. Kaleiçi lies at the other end of the Konyaaltı seafront; ask the front desk about the hotel shuttle.

## What to check for this stay

- **Board:** breakfast is described, but no meal plan is stated.
- **The beach:** ask the distance and whether the shuttle runs to the sea.
- **Pools in winter:** ask whether the outdoor pool is heated or open.
- **Room type:** suites and family rooms have the kitchenette; standard rooms are not said to.
