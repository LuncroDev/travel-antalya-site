Kaya Belek is the group's separate Belek hotel in the Üçkum Tepesi area. It is listed alongside, rather than as another name for, Kaya Palazzo Golf Resort.

## What to check for this stay

Do not assume access to Kaya Palazzo facilities or golf is included in a Kaya Belek booking.
