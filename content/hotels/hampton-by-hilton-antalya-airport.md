Hampton by Hilton Antalya Airport is a plain, practical stop for the night before an early flight or after a late one. It belongs to Hilton's Hampton brand, has 150 rooms, and keeps things simple: a free hot breakfast, free Wi-Fi and free parking, but no pool, spa or bar. Hilton's pages for the hotel carry a "©2026 Hilton" footer.

## Where it is

The address is Altınova Sinan Mahallesi, Serik Caddesi No. 25/B, in the Kepez district, on the airport side of the city. Hilton's figures for the airport don't agree: one block on the location page says 5 km, the "What's nearby" list says 0 km, and Hilton's own location search puts the hotel about 1.6 miles (2.6 km) from its airport point. The city centre is given as 5 km. Our [Kepez district guide](/destinations/kepez/) covers this side of Antalya, and our [guide to getting to Antalya](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/) explains the two terminals and the transport into town.

## The setting

This is a city-edge business and transit hotel, not a resort. Hilton lists a fitness centre (no hours given) and three meeting rooms, the largest set up for 178 m². No pool, spa or beach is mentioned. If you want a pool for a winter night in the city, look at [Crowne Plaza Antalya](/hotels/crowne-plaza-antalya/) or the [DoubleTree by Hilton Antalya City Centre](/hotels/doubletree-by-hilton-antalya-city-centre/) instead.

## Rooms & dining

There are 150 rooms, all non-smoking. Hilton's rooms page doesn't list types or sizes; the photo captions name Super King rooms, King Studios and twin rooms. Rooms have a work desk, an HD TV and tea and coffee makers, and Hilton offers connecting rooms, cots and digital keys.

The one place to eat is The Hub, which Hilton describes as a breakfast buffet and limited-menu restaurant; its hours aren't published. The hot breakfast is free, and the main page says children under 18 eat breakfast free. No bar is listed.

## Who it suits

Travellers with an early departure or a late arrival, drivers collecting or returning a hire car, and anyone visiting the Altınova area on business. It also suits people travelling with a dog: pets are allowed for a non-refundable fee of TRY 2,000, up to 30 kg. For a beach stay, browse [Antalya city hotels](/hotels/?area=Antalya%20city) or go straight on to Lara, Belek or Kemer.

## Getting there

Hilton says plainly that there is no airport shuttle, and it publishes no directions or public transport details. Plan on a taxi, or a hire car: self-parking is free and on site, indoors and outdoors. If you're heading into the city by tram or bus the next day, our [AntalyaKart guide](/blog/antalyakart-guide/) explains fares and top-ups.

## What to check for this stay

- **Airport time:** with no shuttle and conflicting distances, ask the hotel how long a taxi to your terminal takes at your hour.
- **Times:** check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00. Breakfast hours aren't published, so ask whether The Hub opens early enough for your flight.
- **Policies:** Hilton's cancellation and payment sections were empty when we checked, and no minimum check-in age is given. Read the terms on your booking.
- **Room type:** room sizes aren't published, so confirm bed type and size when you book.
