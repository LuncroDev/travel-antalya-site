TUI MAGIC LIFE Jacaranda is an all-inclusive club resort on the coast east of Side, close to the village of Çolaklı. Unlike most clubs on this stretch, it stays open through the winter: TUI lists a winter season from **8 November 2026 to 1 May 2027**, then a summer season from 1 May to 7 November 2027. It suits families and couples who want a sandy beach, lots of included sport and a winter break without a closed hotel around them. The page we used carries a 2026 TUI MAGIC LIFE footer.

## Where it is

TUI puts the club about 12 km from Side and about 3 km from Çolaklı, the local centre. Antalya Airport is about 50 km away, a transfer of roughly 60 minutes. The grounds cover about 130,000 m², with a main building and three rows of bungalows. TUI says the club opened in April 2014. For the wider area, see our [Manavgat district guide](/destinations/manavgat/) and the ruins of [Side Ancient City](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/).

## The setting

The club sits on a shallow, gently sloping sandy beach. TUI gives the beach as about 300 m long, with sunbeds and parasols free. Depending on where your room is, the walk to the sea can be up to 600 m.

TUI lists **7 pools and 4 water slides**:

- **Activity Pool, Family Relax Pool, Kids Pool and Baby Pool:** outdoors, unheated.
- **Aqua Park:** 4 slides, unheated, open April to October, for ages 10 and up (younger children with an adult).
- **Indoor pool and indoor baby pool:** heated from November to April.
- **Three lagoon pools:** shared, in the bungalow area, unheated.

Loungers, parasols and beach towels are included.

## Rooms & dining

TUI lists several room types in the main building and the bungalows, including family rooms. The one size we could read in full is the **Double Room Economy**: about 34 m², land view, one double bed and a balcony, last fully renovated in 2014. TUI marks it "very limited supply". Ask for the size of the category you book.

There are **8 restaurants** (the Magico buffet and seven speciality restaurants) and **9 bars**. The Wunderbar is open 24 hours. All-inclusive covers buffet breakfast, lunch and dinner, snacks, non-alcoholic drinks and local alcoholic drinks around the clock. The Dublin Bar is for over-18s and costs extra.

## Who it suits

Families with children, given the kids' pools, the aqua park in season and MyMo SwimClub lessons for children from age 4. Active couples, too: TUI lists tennis, beach volleyball, football, cycling and water sports. Under-18s can't stay on their own, and pets aren't allowed. ## Getting there

Antalya Airport is about 50 km away, roughly an hour by transfer. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) covers taxis and shuttles to Side, and our [club holidays guide](/blog/antalya-club-resorts-open-in-winter/) compares the clubs that stay open this winter.

## What to check for this stay

- **Times:** rooms are ready from 15:00 and must be vacated by 12:00. Late check-out costs extra; TUI publishes no price.
- **Winter pools:** only the indoor pools are heated; the aqua park closes from November to March.
- **Wi-Fi** is free across the club and in rooms. TUI calls the club generally suitable for guests with reduced mobility, but suggests contacting it first.
