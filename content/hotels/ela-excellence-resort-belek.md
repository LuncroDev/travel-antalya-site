Ela Excellence Resort Belek is a big all-inclusive resort on the Belek coast. The hotel's winter factsheet says it opened in May 2007, was fully renovated in 2022, stands on 110,000 m² of grounds and opens all year. The website is current, with a Christmas package for 21–28 December 2026 and 2027 early-booking offers.

## Where it is

The address is İskele Mevkii, Belek, in Serik district. The factsheet gives Antalya Airport as 35 km away, Antalya city centre 45 km and Belek centre 2 km. Our [Serik and Belek district guide](/destinations/serik/) covers the area, and the [Aspendos theatre](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/) is the classic half-day trip from here.

## The setting

The hotel describes a 270-metre beach of soft golden sand with a water-sports area and a pier with sun loungers and its own bar. Pools include Lake House pools among olive trees, swim-up pools and an aquapark. Four are heated: an 865 m² semi-Olympic outdoor pool and a 216 m² indoor pool, both 140 cm deep, plus outdoor and indoor children's pools. The hotel says heated pools stay open through the winter, but opening days depend on weather and occupancy.

The **Elazen Spa** covers 4,500 m², with 14 massage rooms and Turkish baths, and opens from 09:00 to 20:00. Saunas, the steam room, heated pools and the gym are included; massages and treatments cost extra. **Everland Kids World** covers 5,000 m², with a Lego room, VR and games consoles.

## Rooms & dining

The factsheet counts 583 rooms. Sizes on the website start at 38 m² for a Lake House Pool View room and 45 m² for a Superior Garden or Sea View room, which sleeps two adults and two children or three adults. Above those come sea-view rooms with jacuzzis, rooftop duplex suites and villas with private pools; the largest villa is listed at 794 m².

Board is all-inclusive, which the factsheet brands "FeelWell All Inclusive". The **Hayat** main restaurant serves a buffet breakfast from 07:00 to 11:00. The à la carte list includes La Sofa (grill), Galata Tavern (Aegean), La Sante (Mediterranean), Akari Teppanyaki, La Salsa (Mexican), Ela Anatolian and La Perla (Italian).

## Who it suits

Families who want a kids' club, an aquapark and a sandy beach, and winter visitors who want heated pools and a large spa. The resort has no course of its own, but Belek's [golf clubs](/golf/) are close by; read our [Belek golf season guide](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/) before booking a golf week. Compare other [Belek hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek) such as [Kempinski The Dome](/hotels/kempinski-hotel-the-dome-belek/).

## Getting there

The hotel offers paid VIP transfers, free for suite and villa guests staying five nights or more. Our [Antalya Airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) compares taxis, transfers and public transport to Belek.

## What to check for this stay

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00. The factsheet says the person checking in on an individual booking must be 18 or over with ID, pets are not allowed, and five Superior garden-view rooms are adapted for guests with special needs. Outside visitors are not accepted after 18:00, and a dress code may apply in some restaurants.

The official sources disagree on some details. The Premier Roof Top Suite is 105 m² on the website and 150 m² in the factsheet. The kids' club age bands differ: the website lists Baby 0–3, Mini 4–7, Midi 8–10 and Teens 10–15, while the factsheet gives 0–48 months, 4–7, 8–11 and 12–15. In winter, the factsheet lists La Sante and La Perla as free and Akari as paid, so ask which restaurants are included on your dates.
