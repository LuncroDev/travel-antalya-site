Villa Hotel Tamara is a small hotel on the Çukurbağ Peninsula in Kaş, looking across the water to the Greek island of Kastellorizo (Meis). It belongs to Bozca Group, and the site says the owner's architecture firm, Bozca Architecture, designed the building in local stone for natural temperature control. The site carries a © 2026 footer, though it publishes no prices or season dates.

## Where it is

The address is Aydın Köker Sokak 5, Kaş. The hotel puts itself 4 km from the centre of Kaş, so you will want a car or taxis for evenings in town. It gives no distances to the harbour, the beaches or either airport. The peninsula is also home to [Radisson Blu Hotel, Kaş](/hotels/radisson-blu-hotel-kas/). Our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/) covers the town.

## The setting

The hotel's swimming spot is a private platform built on the rocks, meant, it says, for sunbathing, swimming and lazing about. Its own photos also show a pool terrace above the sea, though no page describes a pool, its size or its season. There is no sand here: for a beach day, drive to [Kaputaş](/things-to-do/kaputas-beach/) or Patara.

The site describes a sunset bar and a farm that supplies its kitchen with organic produce. It also hosts weddings, proposals and company events.

## Rooms & dining

There are 39 rooms in three types:

- **18 Infinity Rooms**
- **18 Terra Rooms**
- **3 Aqua Suites**

The site says many rooms have sea views but gives no sizes, bed types or views by room type. Its booking page mentions suites set a little apart from the main building, with panoramic windows over Kaş and a jacuzzi in the bedroom, without saying which suite type that is.

The signature restaurant, **Aliv**, has a sea-view dining room and a cocktail bar. The hotel calls the food Mediterranean, Turkish and international on one page, and Mediterranean and international on another. Aliv's own website is still a placeholder that says "We'll open soon", and no breakfast times or menus are published anywhere. In town, try [Bahçe](/restaurants/bahce-restaurant-kas/) or [Elia](/restaurants/elia-restaurant-kas/).

## Who it suits

Couples who want a quiet, design-led stay outside the town, with the sea on the doorstep. The site calls the hotel both romantic and family-friendly but states no age policy. It suits people who will spend their days on the water: see our guides to [diving in Kaş](/blog/diving-in-kas/) and [Kekova boat trips](/blog/kekova-boat-trips/).
