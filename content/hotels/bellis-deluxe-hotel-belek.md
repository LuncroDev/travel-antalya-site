Bellis Deluxe Hotel is a large family resort on the Belek coast. Its history page says it took its first guests in 1992 and was fully renovated in 2014. The grounds cover 135,000 m² of pine and eucalyptus. The site is current: it has an accommodation page for COP31, the climate conference held in Antalya from 9 to 20 November 2026.

## Where it is

The address is Kadriye Mahallesi, Kadriye 5. Sokak No. 5, Serik, on the Kadriye side of Belek. The hotel publishes no distance to the airport or to Belek centre. Its transport page gives rough cycling distances to nearby sights: [Perge](/things-to-do/perge-ancient-city/) about 25 km, [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/) about 46 km and [Köprülü Canyon](/things-to-do/koprulu-canyon/) about 61 km. Our [Serik and Belek district guide](/destinations/serik/) covers the wider area.

## The setting

The hotel describes a 290-metre private beach of fine sand that shelves gently, which suits small children, with VIP pavilions and a pier with its own bar. There are seven pools. Two of them are heated aquaparks with slides, one for adults and one for children, and the hotel says they work in all four seasons.

The 1,000 m² spa has a hammam, sauna, steam room and indoor pool. The spa page calls it part of the "Ultra All Inclusive" concept but doesn't say whether massages cost extra. There is also a private zoo, horse riding with instructors, football, tennis and basketball, a gym, the Pony Kids Club and a nightclub.

## Rooms & dining

Rooms sit in the main building and in garden blocks. Published sizes:

- **Camelia Garden Room**, 22 m², for two adults and a child, about 100 m from the beach.
- **Standard Room**, 25 m², with sea, side-sea or garden views.
- **Junior Suite** and **Mimosa Garden Room**, both 35 m².
- **Family Room**, 45 m², with two bedrooms for up to four adults and a child.
- **Camelia Superior Family Room**, 50 m².

The **Jasmine Lake Houses** add 40 m² rooms, some with a terrace onto a shared pool, and 80 m² family versions. Their guests get the Jasmine Bar and the Begonvil à la carte restaurant. Two villas have private pools and jacuzzis. The site gives no total room count.

The **Bellissimo** main restaurant serves a buffet with a children's section, and **White Bistro** does burgers and pizza all day. À la carte options are Kalamata (seafood), Steak House, Ikebana (Asian), Gusto (Italian) and Begonvil, which is free for Jasmine guests and charged for others. The site doesn't say whether the rest cost extra.

## Who it suits

Families with young children who want a gentle beach, a kids' club and heated slides. It also suits riders and conference groups: the largest of six meeting halls seats 660 theatre-style. ## Getting there

No transfer service or airport distance is published. The hotel has an electric-vehicle charging point and encourages public transport. Our [Antalya Airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) compares taxis, transfers and buses to Belek.

## What to check for this stay

The site gives no season dates and doesn't say whether the hotel opens all year. Its COP31 offer targets November 2026 guests and it calls the heated aquaparks four-season, but confirm what is open on your dates. The villa page gives two sizes for each villa: 64 m² or 200 m² for Villa Bellis, 104 m² or 200 m² for Villa Diamond. Kids' club ages, check-in times, massage prices and step-free rooms are not published either.
