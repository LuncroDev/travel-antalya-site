Robinson Çamyuva is an adults-only club run by ROBINSON, the German club-holiday brand, on a pine-backed bay south of Kemer. Guests must be 18 or over, and the programme leans hard on sport: tennis, padel, catamarans and a big open-air fitness area. The club page carries a "© 2026 Robinson Club GmbH" footer.

## Where it is

The address is Turizm Caddesi No. 2, Çamyuva, in the Kemer district. ROBINSON gives Kemer centre as 10 km away, the ruins of [Phaselis](/things-to-do/phaselis-ancient-city/) 3 km, Olympos 20 km and Antalya centre 50 km. A small supermarket is opposite and a pharmacy about 300 m away. Our [Kemer district guide](/destinations/kemer/) covers this stretch of coast; for a longer outing south, see [Olympos and the Chimaera flames](/things-to-do/olympos-and-the-chimaera-flames/).

## The setting

The grounds cover 100,000 m² around a wide bay, bounded by a wooded spur of land. The beach is pebble. The location panel says the club is "directly at" it and adds "approx. 240 m", without saying whether that is the beach's length or a walk. Sunloungers and parasols are free; of about 40 beach daybeds, some cost extra. The club plan marks a bathing jetty.

There is one pool: freshwater, 19 × 14 m. No indoor or heated pool is listed.

Sport is the point of the place:

- **Tennis:** 12 courts, five floodlit, with a tennis bar and an external tennis school. The page calls the surface quartz sand, but its own FAQ says clay.
- **Padel and pickleball:** two courts each, booked in the ROBINSON app. Padel costs extra; pickleball is free.
- **Water sports:** eight catamarans and ten stand-up paddleboards, with VDWS-licensed instructors. Courses cost extra.
- **WellFit:** a fitness studio, outdoor training areas and a spa of about 210 m² with a sauna, steam bath and two beach massage pavilions. The hamam is run by an outside company and costs extra.

## Rooms & dining

There are 332 rooms on two floors, all non-smoking, each with a balcony or terrace. The Economy, Classic and Superior doubles are about 29 m², and the Economy rooms are flagged as being in a "non-optimal location". Single rooms are about 23 m². Two suite types, of about 43 m² and 54 m², add a separate living room; ROBINSON says neither has had a comprehensive renovation.

The concept is all inclusive. There's a main buffet restaurant, a beach restaurant and a local speciality restaurant on selected evenings, plus four bars, among them a late-night club open 01:00–03:30. Selected wines, sparkling wines and spirits cost extra. Vegetarian, vegan and lactose-free options are listed.

## Who it suits

Active couples and solo travellers who want sport by day and a lively evening, with a weekly gala, an all-white beach party and a meeting point for single travellers. It is not for families. Pets aren't allowed. Compare other [Kemer hotels](/hotels/?area=Kemer), such as [Rixos Premium Tekirova](/hotels/rixos-premium-tekirova/).

## Getting there

ROBINSON gives Antalya Airport as 65 km and about 1 hour 15 minutes in one section, and 67 km and about 1 hour 10 minutes in another. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) compares the options. Parking is free.

## What to check for this stay

- **Season:** the club is closed now. It reopens on 16 April 2027 and closes on 7 November 2027; the 2026 season ran 17 April–8 November.
- **Times:** check-in is from 15:00 and check-out by 11:00. For late check-out, one section quotes €55 until 15:00 or €90 after, while the contact section says £40.
- **Room:** what "non-optimal location" means before booking an Economy double.
- **Pool:** whether one outdoor pool, with no heating mentioned, is enough on April or late-October dates.
