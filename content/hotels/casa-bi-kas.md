Casa Bi is a small hotel on the Çukurbağ Peninsula in Kaş. It has 16 rooms, all looking out to sea, and some come with their own pool. The owners, Işın and İsmail Erdoğmuş, say they first came to the peninsula looking for somewhere to retire. They built on the rocky slope and later opened the house to guests.

## Where it is

The published address is Andifli Mahallesi, Metin Toker Sokak No. 14, on the peninsula (Yarımada) in Kaş. Our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/) covers the town, the Lycian tombs and the coast.

## The setting

The hotel is built on rock above the sea, and the owners write that the terrain made building hard. Pools matter more than lawns here. Some rooms share a pool and garden terrace with the room opposite. The top category has its own pool. The official pages say nothing about a beach or a sea platform, so don't assume you can swim straight from the hotel.

## Rooms & dining

The rooms page lists four types. All are 37 m² and sleep two:

- **Deluxe**: top floor, panoramic sea view, balcony.
- **Executive, partial sea view**: shares a pool and garden terrace with the room opposite.
- **Executive, sea view**: shares a 25 m² pool, with a balcony and panoramic view.
- **Premium**: private 30 m² pool, balcony and panoramic sea view.

The rooms have air conditioning, a minibar, tea and coffee facilities, a rain shower and Wi-Fi. The official site does not describe breakfast or a restaurant. Kaş town has plenty of choice, including [Naturel Restaurant](/restaurants/naturel-restaurant-kas/) and [Bi Lokma](/restaurants/bi-lokma-restaurant/).

## Who it suits

Couples and adult friends who want a quiet base with a view. The hotel does not accept guests aged 12 and under, or pets.

## Getting there

The Highways Directorate (KGM) distance table puts Kaş 187 km from Antalya by road, along the coast via Kemer, Kumluca, Finike and Demre. Our [Antalya to Kaş day trip guide](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) describes the drive and the stops worth making. The hotel publishes no airport distance, so ask it about transfers.

## What to check for this stay

Ask whether breakfast is included, how you reach the sea, and whether the hotel is open in your travel month. The site gives none of these. If a pool matters to you, book the room type that names it: only the Premium room has a private pool. For other places to stay on this stretch of coast, see [Hideaway Hotel](/hotels/hideaway-hotel/) in Kaş and [Villa Mahal](/hotels/villa-mahal/) in Kalkan.
