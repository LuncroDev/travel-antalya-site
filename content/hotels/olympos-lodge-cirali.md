Olympos Lodge is a small hotel in Çıralı, the quiet beach village south of Kemer where the Olympos valley meets the sea. Its own pages describe a garden that starts at the foot of Mount Olympos and follows a river down to the beach, with the rooms set among the greenery. The lodge says everything in it was designed by artists, and that its staff numbers are almost equal to its guest numbers.

## Where it is

The address is Ulupınar Mahallesi, Çıralı Sokak No. 9, in Kemer district. Çıralı is not Kemer town: it lies on its own bay further south, reached down a valley road. The lodge's Çıralı page says the ruins of Olympos are across the stream, and that the Chimaera flames at Yanartaş are best visited in the evening. Our page on [Olympos and the Chimaera flames](/things-to-do/olympos-and-the-chimaera-flames/) covers both, and the [Kemer district guide](/destinations/kemer/) covers the wider coast.

## The setting

The hotel says it sits right on the sea, on Çıralı beach, where a stream joins the bay. The garden is for guests only. Its size is given differently on different pages (twenty acres on the English homepage, 20,000 m² on the About page), so read it simply as large. There is a sauna in the middle of the garden. The site does not mention a swimming pool; the sea is the point here.

## Rooms & dining

The English homepage speaks of 14 rooms, while the About page says 17. Every room faces part of the garden and is heated and air-conditioned. The room pages list:

- **Super Deluxe Double** (105 m²): a bathtub and a jacuzzi bath, an outdoor shower, a terrace, a fireplace and a sitting area.
- **Deluxe Double** (70 m²): a veranda and terrace, a bathtub, a fireplace and a sitting area.
- **Lake House** (50 m²): set in the garden, with a jacuzzi, a bathtub, a terrace and a fireplace.
- **Double Room** (35 m² on the page, 32 m² in its summary): a bathtub and a veranda.

The lodge has a restaurant, shown in its photos, but the site gives no name, menu or hours. The Turkish homepage describes long breakfasts under the palm trees. Wi-Fi is mentioned for the restaurant and front garden.

## Who it suits

Couples and walkers who want a garden, a quiet beach and ruins within walking distance. The lodge's Çıralı page notes that the Lycian Way passes by, and lists walking and climbing tours, boat fishing and day trips to [Phaselis](/things-to-do/phaselis-ancient-city/), [Termessos](/things-to-do/termessos/), Myra and Kekova. The fireplaces make it a plausible base outside high summer, but check that it is open in your month.

## Getting there

The lodge says most guests fly into Antalya Airport, and it can send a car to meet you. By public transport, it suggests the Havaş bus to Antalya's bus terminal, a Kumluca bus to the Çıralı junction, then a dolmuş into the village and a 150 m walk. By car, it gives about 80 km along the southern coast road, ending with a 7 km run down the valley.

## What to check for this stay

Ask how many rooms the lodge now has, whether breakfast is included in your rate, and what its policy is on children and pets. Book the room by name if a jacuzzi or fireplace matters.
