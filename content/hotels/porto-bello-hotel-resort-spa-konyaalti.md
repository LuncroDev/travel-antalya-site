Porto Bello Hotel Resort & Spa is a large beach hotel on the Konyaaltı seafront. Its about page says it was built in 2002 and last restored in 2019. The site is current: its campaigns page offers early-booking discounts on stays from November 2026 to the end of October 2027.

## Where it is

The address is Liman Mahallesi No. 4A/4B, Konyaaltı. The hotel says it sits on Konyaaltı Beach, close to the city centre, but publishes no distances, not even to the airport. Our [Konyaaltı Beach guide](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/) and [Konyaaltı district page](/destinations/konyaalti/) cover the promenade. In the same Liman neighbourhood, [Közz Liman](/restaurants/kozz-liman-ocakbasi-konyaalti/) grills over charcoal.

## The setting

The hotel has a 150 m private beach with a Blue Flag, a lifeguard, sunbeds, umbrellas and towels. The main outdoor pool covers 1,000 m². A heated indoor pool of 100 m² is pitched as a year-round option, and the facilities list adds a pool with a water slide and a children's pool.

The spa has a hammam, a sauna, massage rooms and a fitness centre. The hotel's blog says massages and skin care are for adult in-house guests. No spa prices or hours are published. For children there's a mini club, a playground and an animation team, but no age bands are given.

## Rooms & dining

The about page counts 346 rooms: 282 standard rooms, 48 family rooms, eight junior suites and eight senior suites.

- **Standard rooms** (double, single or triple) are 22 m², with a balcony, a minibar, a kettle and a safe, and partly sea, partly land views.
- **Family rooms** have two bedrooms, with a double bed and twin beds, for up to five guests. The rooms list says 48 m²; the room's own page says 40 m².
- **Junior suites** have a bedroom, a lounge and a sea view. Different pages give 48 m² and 45 m².
- **Senior suites** are 54 m², for two adults.

The room pages list central heating but don't mention air conditioning.

The Main Restaurant is a 450-seat open buffet for breakfast, lunch and dinner. A 60-seat à la carte restaurant serves Mediterranean food, and there are snack, lobby, pool and beach bars. The site never names the board basis: it only says a booking covers "the selected concept".

## Who it suits

Families and couples who want a beach hotel within reach of the city, and anyone who wants to swim indoors in the cooler months. For a design hotel on the same coast, compare [Hotel SU](/hotels/hotel-su-aqualand/), or browse all [Konyaaltı hotels](/hotels/?area=Konyaalt%C4%B1).

## Getting there

The hotel's blog says airport transfers are paid and should be booked ahead. There's a car park. Our [guide to getting to Antalya](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/) covers taxis and buses from the airport.

## What to check for this stay

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00, and pets are not allowed. The campaigns page is selling winter nights: 38% off stays from 14 November 2026 to 31 March 2027 if booked by 5 January 2027. Ask which pools, bars and restaurants run in winter and what your rate includes. Our [Antalya in winter guide](/blog/antalya-in-winter/) covers the weather.
