Xanadu Resort is a big all-inclusive hotel in Belek, which it sells as "High Class All Inclusive". It sits on 184,000 m² of land at Acısu Mevkii in the Belek tourism zone, beside the mouth of the Acısu stream. The hotel says it is celebrating 26 years of hospitality, and its news page already lists a festive programme from 24 December 2026 to 1 January 2027, so it is open over the holidays.

## Where it is

The address is Acısu Mevkii, Belek Turizm Merkezi, Serik. The hotel gives drive times rather than distances: 38 minutes to Antalya Airport, 11 minutes to Belek centre and 25 minutes to [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/). Our [Serik and Belek guide](/destinations/serik/) covers the area.

## The setting

The private beach is 415 m long, sand and pebbles, with a pier, and the hotel says it holds the Blue Flag. The website lists three outdoor pools, one indoor pool, two children's pools and three slides on an antique-style tower; the kids' club adds four more pools and two slide towers. The 2025 catalogue gives different figures (two heated indoor pools, nine slides), so ask if pools matter to you. The Shang-Du Spa has heated indoor and outdoor pools, a sauna, hammam, steam room and gym; younger guests may use it only at set hours. This winter the spa offers 35% off its regular menu prices.

Other facilities include two floodlit tennis courts with a coach, bowling and billiards. The website calls the golf feature an "18-hole golf training area" while the catalogue calls it an 18-hole course; for real golf, see the [Belek clubs](/golf/).

## Rooms & dining

The resort has 421 rooms. The website says half are in the X-shaped main building and the rest in villa-style bungalows; the catalogue says all 421 are in the four-storey main building, plus 12 duplex villas. Published sizes:

- **Standard room:** 26 m², king or twin beds, sea, pool or garden view.
- **Junior suite:** 45 m², with a separate living area.
- **Family suite:** 63 m², two bedrooms and two bathrooms.
- **Golf suite:** 100 m² on the top floor; **presidential suites** 202–227 m². These and the three-bedroom presidential villas (private pool and sauna) are being renovated, with no date given.

Food is built around the Silk Road main restaurant, plus Mexican, French (La Cascade), Italian (La Dolce Vita), Chinese (Dragon), a fish restaurant by the river (Uskuna) and a Kebap House that serves a village breakfast. Bars include a lobby bar, an Irish pub and a beach bar. No opening hours or à la carte charges are published.

## Who it suits

Families first. The Fancyland kids' club covers 5,500 m², with groups for ages 1–3 (in its own building), 4–7 and 8–15. It runs seasonally; when it is closed, a 65 m² Mini Club in the main building takes ages 4–12. The 2,500 m² congress area also makes it a conference hotel. For a quieter Belek stay, compare [other Belek hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek) such as [Kempinski The Dome](/hotels/kempinski-hotel-the-dome-belek/).

## Getting there

Allow about 40 minutes from the airport by car. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) compares the options.

## What to check for this stay

- Which pools, slides and kids' club are open on your dates; the hotel says services "may vary seasonally".
- Check-in and check-out times, which the site does not publish.
- Whether your room type is affected by the suite and villa renovation.
