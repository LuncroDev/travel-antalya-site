Fame Residence Kemer & Spa is a mid-sized beach hotel in the middle of Kemer, run by the small Fame Residence group, which also has hotels in Lara and Göynük. It publishes a winter programme: the "Fame Style All Inclusive" concept for 2026–2027 runs from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027, and the website footer carries a 2026 date.

## Where it is

The address is Merkez Mahallesi, Deniz Caddesi No. 15, Kemer. The hotel's factsheet puts the beach at 0 km, Kemer bus station 1 km away, Antalya city centre 40 km and Antalya Airport 50 km. Our [Kemer district guide](/destinations/kemer/) covers the town. Good day trips from here include [Phaselis](/things-to-do/phaselis-ancient-city/) and the [Tahtalı cable car](/things-to-do/tahtali-olympos-cable-car/).

## The setting

The hotel has a private beach, and sunbeds, umbrellas, cushions and towels are included. The factsheet lists a 270 m² outdoor pool, a 195 m² indoor pool and a 20 m² outdoor children's pool. It also says outdoor pools and outdoor activities close in winter, so on a November stay the indoor pool is the one to plan around.

The spa has a hammam with scrub rooms, a sauna, a steam room, massage rooms, a beauty salon and a gym. Under the winter concept, the hammam, sauna, indoor pool and fitness centre are free. Massages, kese (the Turkish scrub) and other spa treatments cost extra. The Mini Club takes children aged 4 to 12. Billiards, bowling and the arcade games are paid extras.

## Rooms & dining

The factsheet counts 246 rooms with 508 beds: 220 standard rooms, 24 family rooms and 2 rooms adapted for guests with disabilities. The website lists Standard, Standard French Balcony, Family, Family Superior and De Luxe Suite rooms. The De Luxe Suite faces the sea. Other rooms look over the sea, the mountains, the pool or the garden. Rooms have air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, satellite TV, a minibar, a safe and a tea and coffee set. No room sizes are published.

Winter meals are buffets: breakfast 07:00–10:00, lunch 12:30–14:00 and dinner 18:30–20:30, with special-diet and children's sections. Café Fame serves coffee and cake from 14:30 to 17:00, and there is soup from 23:30 to midnight. The Lobby Bar opens 24 hours, but drinks between midnight and 10:00 cost extra. The Pub Bar opens 10:00–00:00, the Arcade Bar 14:00–00:00 and the Disco Bar 21:00–00:00. The minibar has soft drinks.

## Who it suits

Travellers who want an all-inclusive base in Kemer town in the cooler months, with an indoor pool and hammam rather than a big outdoor resort. ## Getting there

The hotel publishes no transfer service. Kemer is 50 km from the airport by the hotel's own figure. Our [Antalya Airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) compares taxis, transfers and buses to Kemer.

## What to check for this stay

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00, and cots are available on request. The winter concept lists the Turkish, Italian and fish à la carte restaurant (18:30–20:30, by reservation) as included, then adds a €20-per-person supplement. The restaurant page names a Chinese restaurant, Dragon, that the winter list doesn't mention, so ask what runs on your dates. The concept doesn't say whether local alcoholic drinks are included. Room service, fresh juice, imported drinks, laundry and babysitting are paid. The hotel can change the hours of outdoor outlets in bad weather.
