Kempinski Hotel The Dome sits on the Belek coast east of Antalya, and golf shapes almost everything about it. The hotel's own pages describe villas placed between two 18-hole championship courses, and its golf packages are built around the neighbouring [Antalya Golf Club](/golf/antalya-golf-club/).

## Where it is

The resort is in Belek, the golf and resort strip in Serik district. Kempinski's location page lists only "Belek, Türkiye" rather than a street address, so use the hotel name and the map link from the official site when you book a transfer. See our [Serik district guide](/destinations/serik/) for what else is nearby, including the Roman theatre at [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/).

## The setting

Kempinski says the hotel sits directly on the Mediterranean beach. Its rooms page names the two neighbouring courses as PGA Sultan and The Pasha, both part of Antalya Golf Club. The architecture is described as Seljuk-inspired. For guests who want to swim more than they golf, the Lagoon Suites open straight onto a swimming lagoon.

## Rooms & dining

The rooms page lists Superior rooms with garden, golf or side-sea views and Deluxe Sea View rooms, all 38 m². There are also 48 m² Family Rooms with two separate rooms joined by an inner door. Above these come the Lagoon Suites, Royal Golf Villas, Sultan Villas and a Presidential Villa. The villas have shared or private pools and a 24-hour butler. Every room and suite has a balcony or terrace.

For dining there are two buffet restaurants, Lale and Seljuk. The à la carte choices are Palm Lounge, Cilantro (Italian) and Trader's Spice (Pan-Asian), plus Sofra By The Lake, which opens only in summer. The bars include a swim-up bar, Pier Bar, Piano Bar and Putters, the golfers' bar. The Lux Spa is listed at 3,600 m² with hammams, saunas, a salt room and a heated indoor pool.

## Who it suits

The resort suits golfers who want two courses next door, and families who want space, since interconnecting rooms are offered for family stays. Kempinski sells rates with a luxury all-inclusive option or as golf packages, so you can choose how much is bundled.

## Getting there

The hotel does not publish a transfer time. SunExpress's golf page puts Belek about 30 km from Antalya Airport. For other options on the same coast, browse our [Belek hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek), and read our guide to [Belek's golf season](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/) before you pick dates.

## What to check for this stay

Ask exactly what your rate covers: room only, luxury all-inclusive or a golf package. Confirm green fees, buggy hire and tee times in writing. The club's own pages publish different handicap limits for the Sultan and Pasha courses, so check the limit for the course you want before you travel. If you want to eat at Sofra By The Lake, check that it is open for your dates, because it opens only in summer.
