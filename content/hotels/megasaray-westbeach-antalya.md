Megasaray Westbeach Antalya is a 334-room ultra-all-inclusive hotel on Konyaaltı Beach, on the city's western seafront. It joined the Megasaray Hotels group in 2022, alongside [Megasaray Club Belek](/hotels/megasaray-club-belek/), and bills itself as a city resort. Its pages carry a © 2026 footer and list winter opening hours.

## Where it is

The address is Akdeniz Bulvarı 192, Gürsu, Konyaaltı. The group gives the plot as 12,000 m². Konyaaltı Beach runs for 7 km, and the rooms look out over the sea or towards the Bey Mountains. Our [Konyaaltı district guide](/destinations/konyaalti/) and [Konyaaltı Beach page](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/) cover the area.

## The setting

The hotel's own stretch of sand is short: 68 m, run as Allatra Beach & Bistro. The beach page puts it directly in front of the hotel but doesn't say if you cross Akdeniz Bulvarı or the promenade to get there. Ask if that matters.

The pools come in pairs:

- **Two outdoor pools** and **two outdoor children's pools:** summer only.
- **Two indoor pools:** heated in winter.
- **Two indoor children's pools:** heated, and open in the winter season.

The spa lists a hamam, sauna, steam room, indoor pool and gym. Massages, facials and Dr Fish treatments cost extra; no prices or hours are published. Activities include water gym, aqua bike and Kangoo jumping, with live music at night.

## Rooms & dining

Comfort Standard and Deluxe rooms are all 34 m², with city or sea views. Comfort Standard rooms come with three singles or a double plus a single. The 40 m² Corner Superior and the 44 m² Family Room (a double, two singles and a sofa bed) give more space. All six standard types list a shower, air conditioning, a minibar, a safe and free Wi-Fi. Suites start with the 60 m² Junior Suite and the 75 m² Terrace Suite, which has a private terrace. The 225 m² King Suite has three bedrooms and a living room with a dining area.

The main restaurant serves buffets, plus a late-night snack. Two à la carte restaurants need booking and cost extra: La Prima (Italian) and Lagos Seafood. The Lobby Bar runs 24 hours all year, room service is 24 hours at a charge, and Pinkys Coffeehouse serves Starbucks coffee and pastries.

## Who it suits

Couples and families after an all-inclusive beach hotel near the city, and winter visitors who want to swim indoors. The Misi Kids Club runs from 10:00 to 18:00, with a mini disco at 20:30. ## Getting there

The hotel doesn't publish its distance from Antalya Airport or the old town, and its free-transfer offer covered summer 2026 only. Our [guide to getting to Antalya](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/) covers taxis and buses from the airport.

## What to check for this stay

- **Winter:** dinner runs 18:30–21:00 and La Prima is the only à la carte option. Lagos Seafood, the Pool Bar, the Food Court, Beach Snack and Allatra Beach & Bistro close. Compare other [heated pools in Antalya](/blog/antalya-hotels-with-heated-pools-winter/).
- **New Year:** a gala runs on 31 December 2026. Gala-only seats are priced at 10,000, 13,000 or 15,000 TL a person, at tables of ten. Stay packages are priced on request.
- **Kids' club ages:** the main and activities pages say 4–11. The Misi Club page says 4–7 in its text and 4–12 in its summary, closing at 18:00 or 17:00.
- **Views:** some room labels and descriptions disagree. City View rooms, for example, are described with mountain views. Ask which view you'll get.
- **Times:** check-in and check-out times aren't published.
