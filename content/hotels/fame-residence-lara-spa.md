Fame Residence Lara & Spa is a 559-room all-inclusive resort on the beach east of Antalya, one of three Fame Residence hotels. It says it has been running since 2006. Its "Fame Style All Inclusive" concept for summer 2026 ran from 1 April to 31 October 2026, and the website footer carries a 2026 date.

## Where it is

The address is Kemerağzı Mevkii, Lara, Antalya. The factsheet puts Antalya Airport 14 km away, the city centre 16 km away and the beach at 0 km. Our [Aksu district guide](/destinations/aksu/) covers this stretch of coast and the ruins of [Perge](/things-to-do/perge-ancient-city/).

## The setting

The private beach is about 125 m long and holds a Blue Flag. Sunbeds, mattresses, umbrellas and towels are included. The factsheet lists six pools: a 3,900 m² outdoor pool, a 205 m² indoor pool, a 40 m² relax pool, a 175 m² pool with five slides, and children's pools indoors (18 m²) and out (90 m²). The website says "eight pools" covering about 3,800 m², less than the factsheet's main pool alone. Children aged 8 and under can't use the adult pool, and some slides need a height of 120 cm. Outdoor pools close in winter.

The spa has a hammam with scrub rooms, a sauna, a steam room, a relaxation room and the indoor pool. In the summer concept the hammam, sauna, steam room, indoor pool and gym were free, while massages and treatments cost extra. The gym is for ages 16 and up. The Mini Club takes children aged 4 to 12; babysitting is paid. There are tennis courts, a cinema and two football pitches (55 × 100 m and 45 × 90 m).

## Rooms & dining

The factsheet counts 559 rooms and 1,203 beds. Most (407) are De Luxe Standard rooms with twin or French beds. The rest are family rooms of several kinds, two connecting rooms, four suites and eight accessible rooms. The website adds duplex family rooms in two-storey garden buildings, with one bedroom, a living room and two bathrooms, and a King Suite with a kitchen, a sauna and a jacuzzi. No room sizes are published.

The Ana Restaurant serves buffets with themed nights. In summer 2026, dinner ran 18:30–21:00 and a night buffet 23:00–06:00. Five à la carte restaurants (Turkish, Chinese, Italian, Mexican and fish) opened 18:30–21:15 by reservation for €20 per person, or €10 for a children's menu. The names don't match: the concept calls the Italian and Mexican ones Grande Gusto and Elora, the restaurant page Lagos and Can Cun.

## Who it suits

Families who want slides and a kids' club close to the airport, and conference groups: the three Perge halls combine into 1,100 m² and seat 850 theatre-style. Compare other [Lara hotels](/hotels/?area=Lara%20%26%20Kundu) such as [Sherwood Exclusive Lara](/hotels/sherwood-exclusive-lara/), or the group's [Fame Residence Kemer & Spa](/hotels/fame-residence-kemer-spa/).

## Getting there

The airport is 14 km away by the hotel's figure, and no transfer is published. The summer concept listed a paid shuttle to the city centre, run by an outside company. Our [guide to getting to Antalya](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/) covers taxis and buses from the airport.

## What to check for this stay

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00. The hotel published a winter concept last year, from 1 November 2025 to 31 March 2026, when à la carte dinner was limited to once a week. We found no 2026–27 version, so confirm the hotel is open and what runs on your dates. Our [Antalya in winter guide](/blog/antalya-in-winter/) covers the weather. In summer, imported drinks, fresh juice, room-service drinks and alcohol between 03:00 and 10:00 cost extra.
