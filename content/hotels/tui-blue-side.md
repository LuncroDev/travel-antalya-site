TUI BLUE Side is an adults-only hotel in the Kumköy area west of Side, run by TUI's own hotel brand. TUI says it "is for guests aged 16+ only", and every room type on its page shows a minimum age of 16. It is all-inclusive, aimed at couples, and leans on food, fitness and a large spa rather than shows and slides. The TUI BLUE page we used carries a "© 2026" footer.

## Where it is

The contact address is Evrenseki Mahallesi, 07330 Manavgat, and the FAQ places the hotel in Kumköy, on a long sandy beach. TUI gives Side Old Town as 7 km away (the FAQ says 7–8 km), which puts [Side's ancient city](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/) and the [Temple of Apollo](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/) within an easy taxi ride. See our [Manavgat district guide](/destinations/manavgat/) for the area.

## The setting

TUI describes the beach as the hotel's own, fine and sandy, mostly soft sand with a few small pebbles at the waterline and a gentle slope into the sea. The same page also says a street separates the accommodation from the beach.

As published:

- **Outdoor pool:** fresh water, 56 × 13 m, not heated, open 08:00–19:00 all year, with a pool lift.
- **Indoor pool:** fresh water, 18 × 8 m, heated, open 08:00–19:00, also with a pool lift.
- **Spa:** a wellness area of more than 2,000 m², with sauna, steam bath, hammam and treatment rooms.
- **Fitness:** TUI's BLUEf!t programme, with classes, yoga, a gym, nordic walking, beach volleyball and boccia.

Evenings are low-key: the FAQ mentions live music and small shows.

## Rooms & dining

There are 234 rooms in four buildings; the main building has four floors and lifts. The page lists eight room types:

- **Standard Double Room:** 35 m², with sea view, partial sea view or (Economy) garden view.
- **Deluxe Room:** 40 m² with sea view, or 35 m² with land view.
- **Junior Suite:** 50 m², sea view or partial sea view.
- **Superior Double Room with Sea View:** 50 m², two rooms joined by a sliding door, for up to four adults.
- **Suite with Sea View:** 90 m², two rooms joined by a sliding door.

All restaurants are included in the all-inclusive. The buffet, The Restaurant, has a terrace. Three à la carte restaurants serve set dinners on given nights: **Culinarium** (international, five courses on Monday, Thursday and Saturday), **Defne** (Mediterranean, five courses on Tuesday, with vegetarian, vegan and low-carb options) and **Blue Mare** (seafood, on the beach, six courses on Monday, Thursday and Sunday, from mid-May to the end of October). A beach snack restaurant opens from April to October. Six bars include an indoor cocktail bar, a rooftop Romance Bar where smoking is allowed, and a coffee shop, THEA'S.

## Who it suits

Couples and friends who want a quiet, adults-only all-inclusive near Side, with a heated pool and spa for cooler months. Our guide to [adults-only hotels around Antalya](/blog/adults-only-hotels-antalya/) compares it with the others, and you can browse all [Side hotels](/hotels/?area=Side).

## Getting there

TUI gives Antalya Airport as about 60 km away. Parking is free, outdoors and subject to availability, and there's an EV charging point. See our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) for the drive to Side.

## What to check for this stay

- **Season:** the page gives no closing period, but Blue Mare and the beach restaurant close from the end of October, so winter stays have fewer dining options.
- **Times:** check-in from 14:00, check-out by 12:00. Reception is open 24 hours, in German, English and Turkish.
