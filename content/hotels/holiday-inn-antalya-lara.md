Holiday Inn Antalya - Lara is an IHG hotel in Güzeloba, on the Lara side of Antalya. It is a city hotel rather than a resort: breakfast in an open lobby restaurant, a Starbucks counter, an outdoor pool on the third floor and a terrace bar with sea views. The IHG pages we used carry a "© 2026 IHG" footer.

## Where it is

The address is Güzeloba Mahallesi, 2290 Sokak No 5, Lara, 07230 Antalya. IHG lists the Sand Museum at 1 km and Düden Park Waterfall at 2.6 km, which it also calls a 10-minute drive; that is the [Düden Waterfalls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/) area. [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/) is 15 km, about 30 minutes by car, and the Anfas convention centre 12 km. IHG says the TerraCity shopping centre is about 15 minutes' drive. Our [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/) covers the area.

## The setting

IHG describes sandy [Lara Beach](/things-to-do/lara-beach/) as a 15-minute walk in the hotel description, but as 5 minutes in the page summary. No private beach, sunbeds or beach service are listed, so see our [Lara public beaches guide](/blog/lara-public-beaches/).

As published on IHG's amenities page:

- **Outdoor pool:** on the third floor, with a view, open 08:00–20:00, with loungers, towels and poolside service. It is closed for the season from 1 November 2026 to 30 April 2027. Heating isn't mentioned, and no indoor pool is listed.
- **Wellness:** steam bath, sauna and massage.
- **Fitness centre:** 09:00–19:00, with treadmills, elliptical machines, a bike and free weights.

## Rooms & dining

There are 139 rooms over 13 floors, one of them accessible. The rooms page lists two types:

- **Standard room:** 20 m², two twin beds or one queen.
- **One-bedroom suite:** 38 m², one queen bed.

Photo captions also mention sea-view rooms and a King Suite with a terrace, but IHG gives no sizes for them, and a note says the room type is assigned at check-in, subject to availability. Rooms have air conditioning, a minibar, tea and coffee, a safe, a flat-screen TV and free Wi-Fi, and sofa beds for families.

Breakfast is a continental buffet in the lobby restaurant, which also serves local and international dishes in the evening. There is a Starbucks to-go counter, a sea-view terrace bar for evening drinks and 24-hour room service. IHG publishes no hours for any of them. It mentions full-board packages for summer, without dates.

## Who it suits

City-break travellers, visitors to events at Anfas, and couples who want Lara's beaches and the Düden falls nearby without an all-inclusive resort. Kids stay and eat free, but IHG says children's activities are off site only. The minimum check-in age is 18, and pets aren't accepted. Compare other [Lara & Kundu hotels](/hotels/?area=Lara%20%26%20Kundu).

## Getting there

IHG gives Antalya Airport as 10.6 km (6.59 miles). There is no airport or local shuttle; our guide to [getting to Antalya](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/) covers taxis and buses. Parking is free, but there are only 15 outdoor spaces, plus two EV charging spaces (charging costs extra).

## What to check for this stay

- **Season:** the pool is closed until 30 April 2027, so winter stays have no pool.
- **Times:** check-in from 14:00, check-out by 12:00; late check-out is available.
- **Room:** whether you'll get a sea view, since views aren't tied to a room type.
- **Parking:** space, if you're driving in summer.
