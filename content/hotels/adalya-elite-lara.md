Adalya Elite Lara is a large ultra all-inclusive resort at the Kundu end of Antalya's Lara coast, opened in 2016. It is close to the airport, has its own sand-and-pebble beach with a pier, and keeps several pools heated through the winter. We read the hotel's official pages and its English fact sheet on 28 November 2026; the website footer reads © 2026.

## Where it is

The fact sheet gives the address as Kemerağzı Mahallesi, Yaşar Sobutay Bulvarı No: 78/1, in the Kundu tourism centre of the [Aksu district](/destinations/aksu/); the website itself just says "Lara, Antalya". Antalya Airport is **about 10 km** away (around 15 minutes by car, the hotel says) and the city centre **15 km**. For the public sand nearby, see our [Lara Beach guide](/things-to-do/lara-beach/) and [Lara's free public beaches](/blog/lara-public-beaches/).

## The setting

The beach is a **100-metre private strip of sand and pebble** with Blue Flag status and a pier with a bar and cabanas. The pool list is long: a 1,200 m² outdoor pool, a 312 m² semi-Olympic pool, a 714 m² wave pool (summer only), a 165 m² relax pool reserved for adults, and a 200 m² indoor pool. The fact sheet marks the semi-Olympic, relax and indoor pools, plus a small indoor children's pool, as **heated in winter**. In summer there is an aquapark with five slides (120 cm minimum height) and a children's park with three more.

The 1,300 m² spa has a Turkish bath, sauna, steam room and salt room; the hamam, sauna, steam room and gym are free under the board, while massages cost extra.

## Rooms & dining

The fact sheet counts **606 rooms** on seven floors. Standard rooms (land, sea or side-sea view) are **32 m²** for up to three guests; family rooms are **50 m²**, two connecting rooms with one bathroom, for up to four; the Superior Suite is also 50 m². An accessible room type (28–30 m²) is listed.

The board is **Ultra All Inclusive**, with drinks served by the glass. The Art Main Restaurant serves breakfast 07:00–11:00, lunch 12:30–14:30 and dinner from 18:30, and a night buffet runs **22:30–07:00**. Four à la carte restaurants — Bosphorus (Turkish), Basilico (Italian), Cai (Far Eastern) and Especias (Mexican) — open 19:00–21:30, and stays of seven nights or more include one free visit. **Halikarnas**, the fish restaurant, is paid and summer-only. The Mahall Bar is open 24 hours.

## Who it suits

Families: the **Mare Kids Club** takes ages 4–12 all year, 10:00–17:00, moving to an 80 m² indoor room in winter. It also suits travellers who want an easy transfer and warm water in the off-season; compare it in our guide to [hotels with heated pools in winter](/blog/antalya-hotels-with-heated-pools-winter/).

## Getting there

From the airport it is a short taxi or transfer ride; our [hotels near Antalya Airport](/blog/hotels-near-antalya-airport/) guide lists other options close by. Kaleiçi and the [Düden Waterfalls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/) are easy half-day trips west.

## What to check for this stay

- **Season:** no opening or closing dates are published, only summer and winter timetables.
- **Pool heating:** confirm which pools are heated, and from when.
- **Extras:** Halikarnas, room service, bottled drinks, spa treatments and babysitting cost extra.
- **New Year:** the site advertises a gala dinner for 31 December 2026; ask what it costs.
