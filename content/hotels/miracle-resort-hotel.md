Miracle Resort Hotel is a beachfront resort on Antalya’s Lara coast. Its published ultra all-inclusive concept combines accommodation, dining and family activities on one site. Compare the precise room category and seasonal inclusions rather than assuming every advertised feature comes with every booking.

## Where it is

The official address is **Güzeloba Mah., Yaşar Sobutay Bulvarı No:34, Muratpaşa, Antalya**. It sits in the Lara resort area rather than within the streets of Kaleiçi. Use the [Lara and Kundu hotel collection](/hotels/?area=Lara%20%26%20Kundu) to compare other stays, and the [Kaleiçi guide](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/) when planning a separate old-town visit.

## The setting

The hotel describes a sandy beachfront with a pier, swimming pools and water slides. Its family facilities include a children’s aquapark, splash park and kids club; age and height restrictions apply to some slides. There is also an indoor pool connected to the spa and an outdoor heated pool. Check operating dates and heating arrangements for your stay, especially outside summer.

**About these photographs:** all three gallery images were taken in **March or May 2023**. They show the identified property, but predate the hotel’s advertised **2026 new building**. They are archival views, not a record of today’s rooms, landscaping or facility condition. Dates and Creative Commons credits accompany each image.

## Rooms and dining

The official website lists standard rooms, suites and newer swim-up categories. Ask which building your booked category occupies and whether pool access is direct from that particular room. A balcony or pool view is not the same as swim-up access.

The hotel names Italian, Mexican and seafood à la carte restaurants, alongside its main dining provision. Reservations are required for à la carte meals. Confirm any category-specific privileges, included visits and additional charges directly before booking.

## Who it suits

Its published facilities make it worth comparing for a family resort stay with beach and pool time. Travellers mainly interested in walking out to old-town streets should also compare city accommodation. Accessible rooms and a designated accessible pool are advertised; ask the hotel about the precise route and facilities needed for your party.

## Getting there

The hotel estimates about 20 minutes from Antalya Airport. This is its published estimate, not a guaranteed transfer time; traffic and the transfer route can change the journey. Confirm your arrival arrangements with the hotel or your transfer provider.

## What to check for this stay

Confirm the exact room and building, children’s age rules, seasonal pool and beach access, restaurant reservations, total price and cancellation terms.
