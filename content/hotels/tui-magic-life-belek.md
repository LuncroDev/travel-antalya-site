TUI MAGIC LIFE Belek is an all-inclusive club on the beach between Kadriye and Belek, built around a large pool and slide area. It runs as a summer club: TUI lists seasons from 11 April to 8 November 2026 and from **17 April to 14 November 2027**, and no winter season, so it is closed now. It suits families who want slides and kids' clubs, and couples who like a 16+ pool of their own. The page we used carries a 2026 TUI MAGIC LIFE footer.

## Where it is

The club is beachfront, about 6 km from Kadriye's centre and about 10 km from Belek. TUI's own page gives two airport distances: about 25 km in its distance list and 33 km in its introduction, with a transfer of about 30 minutes. The grounds cover 125,000 m² and the buildings have three floors. TUI says the hotel opened in 1994 and was last fully renovated in 2018. For the wider area, see our [Serik district guide](/destinations/serik/).

## The setting

The beach is sand and pebbles, about 250 m long, with loungers and umbrellas included. TUI lists **7 pools and 10 water slides**, all unheated:

- **Activity Pool and Oasis Pool:** the two main pools.
- **Relax Pool:** for guests aged 16 and over, with its own bar.
- **Children's pool:** with one slide, plus a covered baby pool.
- **Water park:** 4 slides.
- **Kids Splash Area:** 5 slides.

Sun loungers and bath towels are free. Daybeds cost extra and are booked through the club's app.

## Rooms & dining

TUI lists several double rooms and suites with garden views. The doubles were last renovated in 2022:

- **Double Room Economy:** about 23 m², which TUI calls a "sub-optimal location".
- **Double Room Classic:** about 23 m², for two adults.
- **Double Room Superior:** about 28 m², with a sofa bed for one child aged 2–15.
- **Double Room Superior Plus:** about 28 m², for two adults and two children.

There are **6 restaurants** (the Magico buffet and five speciality restaurants) and **6 bars**, including a 24-hour Wunderbar. All-inclusive covers buffet meals, snacks, non-alcoholic drinks and local alcoholic drinks around the clock. Alcohol is served from 18.

## Who it suits

Families, first: the slide areas and MyMo SwimClub lessons for children from age 4 are the draw. Couples get the 16+ Relax Pool. TUI lists tennis, beach volleyball, water sports and fitness among the activities. Under-18s can't stay alone and pets aren't allowed. Golfers are close to [Carya](/golf/carya-golf-club/) and [National](/golf/national-golf-club/).

## Getting there

Antalya Airport is 25–33 km away by TUI's two figures, about 30 minutes by transfer. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) covers the options, and our [winter club holidays guide](/blog/antalya-club-resorts-open-in-winter/) lists the clubs that stay open while this one is closed.

## What to check for this stay

- **Season:** the club reopens on 17 April 2027; no pool is heated, so check sea and pool conditions for April.
- **Times:** rooms are ready from 15:00 and must be vacated by 12:00. Late check-out costs €55 until 15:00 or €90 until 18:00, subject to availability.
- **Wi-Fi** is free in the club and in rooms; parking is subject to availability.
