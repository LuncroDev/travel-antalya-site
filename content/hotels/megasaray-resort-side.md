Megasaray Resort Side is a five-star, all-inclusive family resort in the Titreyengöl area of Side, in Manavgat district. The group says the hotel was completely renovated in 2024, including its rooms, restaurants and beach area, and the adult aquapark was redesigned in 2025. It has 347 rooms. One page's summary says 346, but the main text, the group's About page and its home page all say 347. It is the Side sister of [Megasaray Westbeach Antalya](/hotels/megasaray-westbeach-antalya/) and [Megasaray Club Belek](/hotels/megasaray-club-belek/). The site carries a © 2026 footer.

## Where it is

The address is Titreyengöl Caddesi, 07600 Manavgat. The hotel says it sits in a forest beside the inland lake of Titreyengöl. It publishes no distance to Antalya Airport or to Side's old town. The ruins of [Side Ancient City](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/) and the [Manavgat Waterfall](/things-to-do/manavgat-waterfall/) make the obvious days out. Our [Manavgat district guide](/destinations/manavgat/) covers the area.

## The setting

The beach, which the hotel calls Megabeach, is soft golden sand "only 250 meters from the hotel". The site gives no beach length and mentions no pier or Blue Flag. Pools:

- **Relax Pool** and **Activity Pool**, outdoors.
- **Indoor Pool**, which the site says you can use "any time of the year". No page says any pool is heated.
- **Adult Aquapark**, redesigned in 2025, and a **Kids' Aquapark**, plus a children's pool with double slides.

The spa has a Turkish bath, saunas, steam rooms and massage rooms, and the gym is open all day. Daytime sport runs from archery and beach volleyball to water polo and mini golf. Evening shows finish at 23:00. There are no tennis courts here; for those, see [Megasaray Club Belek](/hotels/megasaray-club-belek/).

## Rooms & dining

All rooms have air conditioning, a shower, a safe, a minibar and Wi-Fi. The types, as listed:

- **Standard Garden View** and **Standard Sea View** (with balcony): 22 m².
- **Comfort Standard**, garden view: 28 m².
- **Junior Family**: 37 m², a double bedroom plus a second room with two single beds.
- **Terrace Family**: 45 m², two bedrooms and a private terrace.
- **King Suite**: 80 m², sea view, a living room and a jacuzzi, for up to two adults and one child.

The main restaurant runs the ultra all-inclusive buffet: breakfast 07:00–10:45, lunch 12:30–14:30, dinner 18:45–21:15, plus a night snack from midnight. Snack points include a pool food court and a gözleme house on the beach. Room service runs round the clock at an extra charge. Three à la carte restaurants open 19:00–21:30, and all need booking: **The Butcher** (steakhouse, paid menu), **Lagos Seafood** and **La Prima** (Italian), each with a cover charge. The bars page names a fourth, Köz by Megasaray, a grill and meze place.

## Who it suits

Families first. The Misi Club takes children in four groups: babies 0–3, Mini 4–7, Midi 8–11 and a teen club for 12–16. The hotel's main page says "ages 4–17" instead. Couples who want a quiet adults' pool will find the Relax Pool, but this is a busy, slide-heavy resort. Compare other [Side hotels](/hotels/?area=Side).

## Getting there

No airport distance or transfer service is published. A free-transfer offer on the group's campaign page covered summer 2026 stays only and has ended. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) covers the road to Side.

## What to check for this stay

- **Season:** every restaurant and bar on the dining page is listed as "Winter: Closed", and no 2027 dates are published. Ask when it reopens.
- **Extra charges:** à la carte cover charges and room service are not priced online.
- **Times:** check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00 (group-wide terms).
- **Cancellation:** the group's sales contract lets you cancel free up to three days before arrival; after that, and for no-shows, you pay in full.
- **Children:** the group's terms say a first child aged 0–12 and an infant stay free, but that page carries Club Belek's details. Confirm it applies to Side.
