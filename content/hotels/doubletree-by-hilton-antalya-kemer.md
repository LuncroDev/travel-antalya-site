DoubleTree by Hilton Antalya-Kemer is an all-inclusive beach resort on the northern side of Kemer, run under Hilton's DoubleTree brand. It suits a week of beach, pool and food without leaving the grounds, with Kemer's marina and old-town streets a short taxi ride away. The Hilton pages we used carry a "© 2026 Hilton" footer.

## Where it is

The address is Yeni Mahalle, Atatürk Bulvarı No. 28, Kemer. Hilton puts the hotel 56.2 km from Antalya Airport. For Kemer Marina it gives two figures on the same page: 3.0 km in the introduction and 2.8 km in its list of nearby places. That list also includes the Olympos Beydağları Coast National Park (6.4 km), the ancient city of Idyros (8.7 km), [Phaselis](/things-to-do/phaselis-ancient-city/) (14.9 km), the [Tahtalı cable car](/things-to-do/tahtali-olympos-cable-car/) station (19.9 km) and Tahtalı Mountain itself (31.0 km). Our [Kemer district guide](/destinations/kemer/) covers the town.

## The setting

The resort has its own beach and beach club, where Hilton says guests can swim and snorkel, and the loungers and parasols there are free. The pools, as published:

- **Heated indoor pool**, the one to ask about in the cooler months.
- **Outdoor pool**, which winds past a palm-tree island and under bridges.
- **Children's pool**, part of the kids' club area. One of Hilton's photos shows water slides, but the text doesn't describe them.

There is a full-service spa with a sauna, steam room, Turkish bath and a treatment menu, plus tennis courts, live music and evening entertainment. The kids' club runs daily crafts, games and themed activities.

## Rooms & dining

Hilton lists four room types but no sizes or total room count:

- **King room:** sleeps three, with a balcony facing the mountains or the sea.
- **King Deluxe room with terrace:** sleeps three, with a private terrace.
- **Duplex family suite, sea view:** sleeps four, on two floors with a living room and two bathrooms.
- **Presidential suite, sea view:** sleeps six, with a living room and two bathrooms.

The all-inclusive package covers five restaurants, seven bars and ten food trucks, with drinks and cocktails included. The restaurants named are Turks Court (Turkish), Sauce, Brandson and a BBQ restaurant by the water. Hilton doesn't say which ones need booking or carry a charge.

## Who it suits

Families who want a kids' club, a children's pool and duplex suites, and couples who prefer a big-brand resort close to Kemer town rather than out on its own. Golfers and nightlife seekers are better served elsewhere. ## Getting there

Hilton lists no airport shuttle. Parking is free, both self-parking and valet. For taxi times and shared transfers from the airport, see our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/). Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00.

## What to check for this stay

- That the resort is open on your dates: Hilton's pages give no season or winter information.
- Which restaurants are included and which take reservations.
- Room size and view, since only the room type and bed count are published.
- Whether the outdoor pools and slides are running if you travel between November and April.
