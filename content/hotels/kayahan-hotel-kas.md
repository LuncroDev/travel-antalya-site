Kayahan Hotel Kaş is a small, family-run bed-and-breakfast on a quiet side street in the middle of town. It has taken guests since 1988 and stays open all year. Breakfast comes with every rate, and the rooftop terrace, with the sea and the Greek island of Meis in front of it, is the centre of the stay. The hotel's site carries a © 2026 footer.

## Where it is

The address is Andifli, Koza Sokak No. 9, Kaş. The hotel puts the old bazaar, the harbour, the town square and the seafront 250–300 m away, a five- or six-minute walk. Küçük Çakıl, the nearest beach, is a minute or two on foot, and Büyük Çakıl is about ten minutes' walk. The ancient theatre of Antiphellos is a short stroll. Our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/) covers the town.

## The setting

This is a town-centre hotel, not a beach resort. The rooftop terrace serves breakfast and stays open through the day until sunset; it closes after dark. Down in the courtyard there is a swimming pool, open from May to October, 10:30 to 18:30.

The local beaches are pebble. For sand, the hotel points you to Patara, about 45–50 minutes by car; Kaputaş is 20–25 minutes away. See our pages on [Kaputaş Beach](/things-to-do/kaputas-beach/) and [Patara](/blog/patara-beach-and-ancient-city/). There is a lift, a 24-hour front desk and free Wi-Fi.

## Rooms & dining

Rooms are compact: the hotel says all of them are about 13–14 m². It does not publish a room count. The sea-view types are:

- **Double Room, Sea View:** a double bed and a private balcony.
- **Triple Room, Sea View:** space for three, aimed at small families.
- **Twin Room, Sea View:** two beds, which can be pushed together on request.

Cheaper standard rooms without a sea view are also offered. Every room has a balcony, air conditioning that heats and cools, a mini-fridge, a TV and a hair dryer; reception lends an iron and a kettle.

Breakfast is a buffet on the roof terrace, 08:00 to 10:30. There is no hotel restaurant, so dinner means a walk into the old town. Try [Bahçe](/restaurants/bahce-restaurant-kas/) or [Etna Fish House](/restaurants/etna-fish-house-kas/).

## Who it suits

Couples, solo travellers and small groups who want to walk everywhere and spend their days out on the water or the trails: see our guides to [diving in Kaş](/blog/diving-in-kas/), the [Lycian Way](/blog/lycian-way-near-antalya/) and [Kekova boat trips](/blog/kekova-boat-trips/). Children are welcome. Pets are too, if you tell the hotel in advance. It is not for anyone who wants a big room or a beach at the door.

## Getting there

The hotel does not publish airport distances. It can book a transfer from Antalya or Dalaman Airport through a partner company; send your flight details, and pay the fare in cash at reception. Free open-air parking takes about six or seven cars, first come, first served, and can't be reserved. Our [Antalya to Kaş guide](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) and [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) cover the drive.

## What to check for this stay

- **Room type:** agency bookings get a fixed room type. The hotel says booking direct by WhatsApp gives more flexibility.
- **Winter:** the hotel is open all year, but the pool runs only from May to October.
- **Times:** check-in is from 14:00, check-out by 11:00.
- **Paperwork:** the hotel asks every guest to send ID or passport photos by WhatsApp before arrival.
- **Meis:** the hotel notes that visa rules may apply to the ferry trip.
