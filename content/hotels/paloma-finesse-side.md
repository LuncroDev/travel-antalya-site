A contemporary low-rise resort in the Sorgun–Titreyengöl area with an 80-metre pool and a 350-metre sandy beach. Its minimalist design, swim-up rooms and Chef's Kitchen programme distinguish it from Paloma's other Side properties.

## What to check for this stay

Ask about Chef's Kitchen reservations, swim-up room rules and seasonal Palomaqua access.
