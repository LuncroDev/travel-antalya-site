DoubleTree by Hilton Antalya City Centre is a Hilton-brand hotel in Muratpaşa, the central district that also holds the old town. It is a base for business trips and short city breaks rather than a beach resort, with two pools, a spa and free parking. Hilton's pages for it carry a "©2026 Hilton" footer.

## Where it is

The address is Adnan Menderes Bulvarı No. 61, Etiler Mahallesi, Muratpaşa. Hilton puts the MarkAntalya shopping centre 1 km away and [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/) under 2 km, close enough to start our [Kaleiçi walking route](/blog/kaleici-walking-route/) on foot. For the beach, Hilton gives two versions: [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/) is about 10 minutes by bus on one page and "within 20 minutes" on another. Our [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/) covers the area.

## The setting

This is an urban building, so the pools do the job a beach would at a resort:

- **Outdoor rooftop pool:** named in the photo gallery's captions.
- **Indoor pool:** listed among the amenities. Hilton doesn't say whether either pool is heated or when they open.
- **Spa:** the gallery shows treatment rooms, a sauna, a steam room and a room captioned "Haramm", most likely the hammam. There's also a fitness centre.

For events there's a ballroom, meeting rooms and a pre-function area.

## Rooms & dining

Hilton doesn't publish a room count, room sizes or views. Its gallery shows King Guest and King Deluxe rooms, twin rooms with a lounge area, accessible king rooms, pairs of twin rooms joined by a connecting door, and a one-bedroom suite. The rooms page says suites have a separate living area and two 55-inch TVs. All rooms are non-smoking, and smoking in one costs up to USD 250.

Three outlets are named:

- **Sage Restaurant:** Turkish, French and international dishes at lunch and dinner. Breakfast is a buffet and costs extra.
- **Ocean Bar:** in the lobby, open 24 hours.
- **Gao Club:** cocktails and pizza by the pool, then Asian and international food in the evening, with a DJ or live music from 21:00 to 01:00.

There's also room service, a 24-hour market and a children's menu.

## Who it suits

Business travellers and conference guests, and city breakers who want Kaleiçi and MarkAntalya within easy reach. Out of season, the indoor pool and spa make it a sensible choice when the beach isn't the point. Families get cribs and connecting rooms, but Hilton lists no childcare. Pets aren't allowed. Compare other [Antalya city hotels](/hotels/?area=Antalya%20city), such as [Rixos Downtown Antalya](/hotels/rixos-downtown-antalya/), or the brand's resort on the coast, [DoubleTree by Hilton Antalya-Kemer](/hotels/doubletree-by-hilton-antalya-kemer/).

## Getting there

Hilton gives the airport as 15 km away and offers no airport shuttle. Self-parking and valet parking are free, covered and secured, but there's no EV charging. For city buses and trams, see our [AntalyaKart guide](/blog/antalyakart-guide/); for taxis and transfers, see [how to get to Antalya](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/).

## What to check for this stay

- **Times:** check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00.
- **Breakfast:** whether your rate includes the buffet.
- **Room:** the type and size you're booking, since Hilton publishes neither sizes nor views.
- **Pools:** opening hours and heating for the rooftop pool if you travel outside summer.
