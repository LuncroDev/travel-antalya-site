ROBINSON Nobilis is an all-inclusive ROBINSON club at Acısu, east of Belek, built around its own golf course. ROBINSON says it has run the club since 2002 and that it opens all year. It suits golfers and tennis players first, families second. The ROBINSON pages we used carry a "© 2026 Robinson Club GmbH" footer.

## Where it is

ROBINSON gives the address as Acısu Mevkii, Belek. The club is 40 km from Antalya Airport, a transfer of about 45 minutes, and 8 km from Belek's centre. For the wider area, see our [Serik district guide](/destinations/serik/); [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/) is the classic half-day out from Belek.

## The setting

The grounds cover 850,000 m². A small river runs between the club and the sea, so you cross a bridge to reach the beach. ROBINSON describes it as a 20 km sand-and-shingle beach, with sunbeds, parasols and towels included.

The pools, as published:

- **Main pool:** 950 m² and 1.40 m deep, not heated.
- **Sports pool:** 25 m long and 312 m², not heated.
- **Relaxation pool:** for adults from 16, heated from about November to April.
- **Indoor pool:** 360 m², heated, with a water slide that runs from November to March.
- **Children's pool:** with a slide, open from April to early November; there are also outdoor and indoor baby pools.

Sport is the club's other draw. The page lists six quartz-sand tennis courts, three of them floodlit, two grass courts at extra cost, an indoor tennis hall from November to March and two padel courts (fee). The WellFit Spa is for adults and has saunas, a steam bath and hammams.

### Golf

The **[ROBINSON Nobilis Golf Club](/golf/robinson-nobilis-golf-club/)** is an 18-hole parkland course in the grounds; some bungalows face it. ROBINSON sells green-fee packages and a golf academy, and lists a paid shuttle to Belek's other courses. Compare the courses in our [Belek golf courses guide](/blog/belek-golf-courses-compared/).

## Rooms & dining

There are 404 rooms in 13 categories. Sizes as published:

- **Double Room Classic, Village:** about 27–33 m², last renovated in 2012.
- **Double Room Classic, Main Building:** about 27–33 m², renovated in 2025; a sea-side version is about 27 m².
- **Bungalow Classic:** about 45 m², in an annex on the golf-course side.
- **Bungalow Superior:** about 90 m², with a golf-course view.

Family rooms of about 51–55 m² and suites are also listed.

ROBINSON lists five restaurants and seven bars. Buffets in the main restaurant are included, along with drinks at the bars all day, except selected wines, sparkling wines, spirits and specialities. The gourmet restaurant and the River Lounge cost extra.

## Who it suits

Golfers who want a course at the door, and couples who play tennis or padel. Families have kids' clubs from babies up: ROBY CLUB for ages 3–6, R.O.B.Y. for 7–12 and ROBS for teens of 13–17, run in German and English. Under-18s can't book on their own. Pets aren't allowed. ## Getting there

Antalya Airport is 40 km away, about 45 minutes by transfer. Parking in front of the club is free. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) covers taxis and shuttles to Belek.

## What to check for this stay

- **Times:** rooms are ready from 15:00 and must be vacated by 11:00. Late check-out costs €55 until 15:00 or €90 after.
- **Pools:** in winter, only the relaxation pool (adults) and the indoor pool are heated.
- **Golf dates:** the course closes for overseeding each autumn, so check the dates before a September or October golf week.
