Doğan Hotel is a small hotel inside Kaleiçi, Antalya's walled old town. Its own pages describe a garden with lush planting and a cascading fountain, where breakfast is served, and a pool with a separate children's pool. The site is current: its footer carries a 2026 date, and its news page has a post from 21 October 2026 about planning a stay during COP31.

## Where it is

The address is Kılıçarslan Mahallesi, Mermerli Sokak No. 2, Muratpaşa, and the hotel's about page places it in "Old Antalya, known as Kaleiçi". The contacts page says Mermerli Beach is 150 metres away; another page calls it a three-minute walk and notes that the beach is privately run and charges for entry. For a free public beach, the hotel points to Konyaaltı, 3 km away. Our [Kaleiçi old town guide](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/) and [Muratpaşa district page](/destinations/muratpasa/) cover the streets around it.

## The setting

The garden is the centre of the hotel. The outdoor pool is seasonal: the Turkish services page gives 7 April to 15 October, from 10:30 to 20:00, while the English page shows a garbled date range. On a November stay, expect the pool to be closed. Wi-Fi is free throughout. The site mentions no spa or gym.

## Rooms & dining

The rooms page lists four types:

- **Eco Room** and **Standard Double**, which share a description of bright Turkish bedspreads, upholstery and rugs.
- **Deluxe Double**, with wooden floors and ceilings, stone walls and turquoise furniture.
- **Junior Suite**, with the double bed in a separate room and a sitting area.

Rooms are non-smoking. Listed amenities include air conditioning, TV, a wardrobe, a hairdryer, a bathtub or shower, toiletries and housekeeping. The site gives no room sizes and no total number of rooms.

The hotel sells three rates: room only, bed and breakfast, and half board with a buffet breakfast and dinner. **Begonia Restaurant & Bar** serves à la carte Turkish food at extra cost, including meze, cheese plates, grills, seafood, güveç (clay-pot stews), pasta and desserts. The menu is read by QR code, and no opening hours are published.

## Who it suits

Couples and families who want to stay inside the old town and walk everywhere. The about page calls the pools suitable for families. If you want a larger heritage property nearby, compare [Alp Paşa](/hotels/alp-pasa-hotel/) and [Tuvana Hotel](/hotels/tuvana-hotel/), or browse all [Kaleiçi hotels](/hotels/?area=Kalei%C3%A7i).

## Getting there

The hotel publishes no distance to the airport or city centre. Kaleiçi's lanes are narrow, so ask the hotel how close a taxi can drop you. Our [Antalya Airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) explains the taxi, tram and bus options.

## What to check for this stay

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00. The hotel takes a cash deposit at check-in, refunded on departure, and offers free cancellation up to seven days before arrival. The restaurant page says the breakfast buffet runs "during the busy periods", so confirm how breakfast is served if you book B&B out of season. Ask about parking, step-free access and children's beds, as none is published.

The hotel's excursions page suggests a Kaleiçi walking tour, a harbour walk, a trip to the [Düden waterfalls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/) and the [Tahtalı cable car](/things-to-do/tahtali-olympos-cable-car/). It also lists the archaeological museum, but the [Antalya Museum](/things-to-do/antalya-museum/) is temporarily closed. For a self-guided route, follow our [Kaleiçi walking route](/blog/kaleici-walking-route/) from Hadrian's Gate to the harbour.
