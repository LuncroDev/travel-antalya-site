Radisson Blu Hotel, Kaş is a small, adults-only hotel on the Çukurbağ Peninsula. It has 50 rooms and suites in pastel Mediterranean colours, and several suites come with their own pool. Radisson announced the opening on 7 June 2023, calling it the group's first hotel in the Antalya region. The hotel pages we used carry a "© 2026 Radisson Hotel Group" footer.

## Where it is

The address is Andifli Mahallesi, Çukurbağ Yarımadası, Demokrasi Caddesi No. 28, Kaş. Radisson puts Kaş Marina 5 km away. Its list of nearby sights gives Antiphellos at 3.5 km, the King's Tomb at 4.2 km, [Kaputaş Beach](/things-to-do/kaputas-beach/) at 14 km and [Patara](/things-to-do/patara-ancient-city/) at 26.5 km. See our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/) and [Lycian rock tombs](/blog/lycian-rock-tombs/) piece.

## The setting

This is not a beach hotel. The current pages list an outdoor pool and a "nearby beach", with a free shuttle that runs only between the hotel and that beach. The 2023 opening release put the beach about 800 m away, with free towels, sunbeds and umbrellas. The spa, Longevity Society, lists no treatments, facilities or hours, and no gym is listed.

## Rooms & dining

Radisson lists these room types:

- **Standard Room, garden or sea view:** 21 m², two adults, king or twin beds.
- **Superior Room, partial sea view:** 23 m², king bed.
- **Corner Premium Room, sea view:** 30 m², king bed.
- **Suite with Terrace:** 45 m², up to three adults.
- **Suites with Private Pool:** 45 m² (Suite), 57 m² (Premium and Mezzanine Suites) and 50 m² (Two-Bedroom Suite, four adults).
- **Presidential Suite with private pool:** named in photo captions only, with no details.

Floors are handmade stone tiles, and showers have a Turkish-bath basin. Air conditioning and Wi-Fi are free, and the hotel is smoke-free.

The restaurant is **Zeugma**, serving Turkish dishes at breakfast and dinner (18:00–00:00). **Ares Bar** is open daily from 07:00 to midnight. Breakfast hours differ on the same page: the buffet block says 07:00–10:30 on weekdays and 07:00–11:00 at weekends, while Zeugma's entry says 07:00–12:30. The 2023 release also named Terasu'shi, an Asian restaurant; the current page shows it only in a photo caption. No meal plan is stated.

## Who it suits

Couples and friends who want a small base with a terrace or private pool rather than a big resort. The age rule is unclear: one line says 12 and over, another says over 13. On pets, Radisson says only to ask for details. Radisson lists a step-free entrance and a wheelchair to borrow. ## Getting there

Dalaman Airport is 148 km away, about two hours by car; Antalya Airport is 205 km, about three hours. Without a car, Radisson suggests bus 600 from Antalya Airport to the city bus station, or the Havaş bus from Dalaman to Fethiye, then a Batı Antalya coach to Kaş and a taxi to the hotel. An airport shuttle is listed, but with no times or prices. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) compares the official distances.

## What to check for this stay

- **Season:** Radisson publishes no opening dates, so check the hotel is open in your month.
- **Times:** check-in from 14:00, check-out by 12:00.
- **Beach:** ask for shuttle times and whether sunbeds are still free.
- **Parking:** parking and valet parking are listed, with no fees given. For days out, see our [Kekova boat trips guide](/blog/kekova-boat-trips/).
