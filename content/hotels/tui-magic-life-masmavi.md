TUI MAGIC LIFE Masmavi is an all-inclusive club on the beach at Kadriye, in the Belek resort area. It is built for active families: 13 pools, a five-slide water park, kids' clubs from age three and plenty of sport. The club page carries a "© 2026 TUI MAGIC LIFE" footer and already lists the 2027 seasons.

## Where it is

The address is Kadriye Mahallesi, Kadriye 5 Sokak No 9/1, 07500 Serik. TUI MAGIC LIFE puts Kadriye about 3 km away and Belek about 5 km, on grounds of roughly 120,000 m². It lists 11 golf courses nearby, the closest being [Carya](/golf/carya-golf-club/) at about 1 km and [Sueno](/golf/sueno-golf-club/) at about 1.5 km. Our [Serik district guide](/destinations/serik/) covers the area, and [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/) and [Köprülü Canyon](/things-to-do/koprulu-canyon/) make good days out.

## The setting

The beach is sand with a gently sloping shoreline. Loungers, parasols and towels are included; daybeds cost extra. The FAQ warns that beach access may be harder for wheelchair users.

The pools:

- **April–October only:** the 50 m Activity Pool, the Yakamoz and two Lagoon pools, the children's pool and the water park (slides from age 10, or younger with an adult).
- **All year:** the Island Pool, plus an indoor pool and a 16+ Relax Pool, both heated in winter depending on the weather.

Tennis is on quartz-sand courts: six, two floodlit, in one section, seven in another. There are also two padel courts, floodlit beach volleyball and a 16+ fitness centre (07:00–22:00). Sauna and steam bath are included; the hamam and massages cost extra.

## Rooms & dining

There are ten room categories, renovated in 2015 or 2016 depending on type. No total room count is published.

- **Doubles:** 22–24 m² in the village, or about 26 m² in the main building, where there are sea-view and single-parent versions.
- **Family suites:** about 32 m² in the village, 38 m² in the main building and 48–55 m² in the Lagoon area, where ground-floor suites open onto the pool.

The main restaurant, Magico, serves buffets at breakfast (07:00–11:00), lunch (12:30–14:30) and dinner (19:00–21:30). Five more restaurants cover local, international, Asian, teppanyaki and steak; the last two cost extra. The others are included once per room per week with a reservation, then €10 per adult and €5 per child. Eight bars include the 24-hour Wunderbar. International spirits and the Wine House cost extra.

## Who it suits

Families with children aged 3 to 15, with clubs split into MyMo Minis (3–5), Maxis (6–8), YoungStars (9–11) and teens, plus a swim club from age four. Couples get 16+ corners, and golfers have Belek's courses close by. The club lists German, English and Turkish as its languages. Compare other [Belek hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek).

## Getting there

The club gives the airport as about 35 km and 40 minutes by car. Its FAQ names Antalya Airport, but the transfer section names Dalaman for the same drive, so read that line with care. Transfers are included only in TUI packages that include them; our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) compares the options. Parking is subject to availability.

## What to check for this stay

- **Season:** winter runs 9–29 November 2026, then 27 February–30 April 2027; summer 2027 runs 1 May–7 November. No dates are listed for December to February.
- **Winter limits:** the Asian, teppanyaki and steak restaurants open April–October only.
- **Times:** rooms are ready from 15:00 and check-out is by 12:00.
- **Extras:** daybeds, room service, laundry and the Lagoon Plus package (€110 per room per night, from summer 2027). Pets aren't allowed.
