Mardan Palace is one of the best-known resorts on the Lara coast, east of central Antalya. The architecture leans into the grand and ornamental, with domes, columns and an enormous pool area winding between the buildings and the beach.

It suits travellers who want a big, self-contained resort with plenty of dining and entertainment, and who are comfortable with a base outside the old town: Kaleiçi is a taxi ride away, not a walk. Check the current board types (all-inclusive or otherwise) and room categories when you book, as these change between seasons.
