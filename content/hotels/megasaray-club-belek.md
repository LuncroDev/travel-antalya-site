Megasaray Club Belek was the Megasaray group's first hotel, open since 1992 on the coast at Kadriye. It is a large "ultra-all-inclusive" resort with 591 rooms, a sandy beach with a pier, and a serious tennis set-up. Its pages carry a © 2026 footer.

## Where it is

The address is Kadriye Mahallesi, Üçkum Tepesi Caddesi No: 8, Belek. The hotel pages give the grounds as 130,000 m²; the group's About page says 121 decares. The Megasaray Football Center is within walking distance. Our [Serik district guide](/destinations/serik/) covers the area, and [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/) and the [Köprülü Canyon](/things-to-do/koprulu-canyon/) make good days out.

## The setting

The beach is 300 m of sand. A free pier with its own bar opens in summer; beach and pier cabanas cost extra. In winter, beach services are limited and depend on the weather.

The pools:

- **Relax Pool (16+):** heated in winter and cooled in summer. Younger guests may use it from 6 November to 15 May.
- **Turkuaz Pool and Activity Pool:** summer only.
- **Indoor Pool and Indoor Kids Pool:** both heated in winter.
- **Mega Aquapark:** summer only. Adult slides need a height of 120 cm, and the Rocket Slide is for ages 14 and up. The children's Misi Aquapark is summer-only too.

Sport is the big draw. The hotel lists 26 courts of its own (10 artificial grass, 11 red clay, four padel, one pickleball), and the Megasaray Tennis Academy adds 38, for 64 in all. The About page gives the academy 60 courts, opened in 2019, and says it hosts Davis Cup and ATP Challenger events. There is also football, basketball, beach volleyball, archery and a gym. The spa has a hamam, sauna, salt room and massage rooms; prices aren't published.

## Rooms & dining

Club Standard rooms are 29 m² for up to three; the ground-floor Garden Up version (30 m²) has a terrace. Club Family Suites are 62–64 m² with two bedrooms and two bathrooms. Deluxe rooms run from 32 m² to the 80 m² Deluxe Family Room. Swim-up rooms open onto the pool from a private terrace, from 50 m² up to the 120 m² King Suite for six. The 80 m² Pasha Suite is a duplex for couples with an upstairs jacuzzi.

The main restaurant serves buffets with themed nights. Seven à la carte restaurants cost extra and need booking: La Prima (Italian), Lagos (seafood), Köz (grill and meze), El Porto (Mexican), Red Far East, Barbeque Court and a Brazilian restaurant. The Mega Bar is open 24 hours all year.

## Who it suits

Tennis players above all, and sports groups. Families too: the 2,000 m² Misi Kids Club was revamped in 2024. Its own page gives four age groups, from Happy Babies (0–3, with a parent) to a T-Club for 12–16s; the hotel's main page says 4–12. See our [winter guide for families](/blog/antalya-with-kids-in-winter-2026-27/) or compare other [Belek hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek).

## Getting there

The hotel does not publish its distance from Antalya Airport. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) covers taxis and shuttles to Belek.

## What to check for this stay

- **Winter:** only two à la carte restaurants, La Prima and El Porto, open (18:30–21:00). The snack outlets, the Turkuaz and Activity pools and both aquaparks close. Compare other [heated pools in Antalya](/blog/antalya-hotels-with-heated-pools-winter/).
- **Relax Pool:** the pool page says it is heated in winter, but the early-booking page gave its heated period as 1 April to 15 May 2026. Ask before you book.
- **Times:** check-in and check-out times aren't published.
- **Room size:** Deluxe Standard rooms vary from 32 to 42 m², so ask which you'll get.
