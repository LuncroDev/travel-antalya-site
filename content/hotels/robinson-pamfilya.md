Robinson Pamfilya is a ROBINSON club on the open sandy coast east of Side, run by the German club-holiday brand since 1987. It is built for families: kids' clubs start at one year old, the pool has a 55 m waterslide, and the programme mixes tennis, sailing and a nightly club. The club page carries a "© 2026 Robinson Club GmbH" footer and already sells the 2027 season.

## Where it is

ROBINSON gives a postal address only: P.O. Box 41, 07601 Manavgat, Side. The club says Side's centre is 5 km away, or about 30 minutes on foot along the beach, and that the nearest supermarket is in Manavgat, about 6 km and five minutes by dolmuş or taxi. That puts the ancient city of [Side](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/) within a beach walk, and the [Manavgat Waterfall](/things-to-do/manavgat-waterfall/) a short drive inland. Our [Manavgat district guide](/destinations/manavgat/) covers the area, and the [Side day trip guide](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/) plans a day in the old town.

## The setting

The club sits directly on a sloping sandy beach that ROBINSON says runs for kilometres, with free sunloungers and parasols. A jetty hosts a sundowner hour from 17:00 to 18:00 with a DJ or live music. The grounds cover 155,000 m², and the guest rooms are in six blocks named after ancient cities, from Termessos to Aspendos.

The pools:

- **Main pool:** about 1,000 m², with a whirlpool and a 55 m waterslide.
- **Children:** a baby and toddler pool with two small slides, and a separate children's pool.
- **Heating:** none is mentioned, and there is no indoor pool.

Sport is a big part of the stay. There are 11 tennis courts with a quartz-sand surface, three of them floodlit, plus a tennis school and a tennis bar; court use is free. Two padel courts cost extra. On the water there are catamarans, windsurfing, stand-up paddleboards and Optimist dinghies for children, with VDWS-licensed instructors. The WellFit spa of about 650 m² is for guests aged 16 and over and has a Finnish sauna, a relaxation pool and four treatment rooms; the hamam and hairdresser cost extra.

## Rooms & dining

There are 284 rooms in six categories, spread over three floors with no lifts. ROBINSON says every room type was last fully renovated in 2013.

- **Doubles:** Classic and Economy, both about 25–29 m². The Economy rooms are flagged as being in a "non-optimal location".
- **Family rooms:** about 40 m² with two bedrooms, either with a balcony or around an internal atrium that opens only upwards and has no outside view. A larger connecting-door option is about 51–60 m².
- **Single parent:** a Classic double for one adult with a child.

The concept is ROBINSON's all inclusive, with buffets at every meal and drinks all day, except selected wines, sparkling wines and spirits. There are three restaurants (the main restaurant with a terrace, a beach restaurant and a speciality restaurant that costs extra) and four bars, including a nightclub.

## Who it suits

Families with young children who want a full kids' programme: paid baby care for 1–23 months, the Super Minis at two, the Minis and Maxis from three to six all day, R.O.B.Y. for 7–12 and a teen club in the main school holidays. Couples without children are welcome too. One thing to know: ROBINSON lists German as the language spoken at the club. For an adults-only ROBINSON, see the sister club [Robinson Çamyuva](/hotels/robinson-camyuva/) near Kemer, or compare other [Side hotels](/hotels/?area=Side).

## Getting there

The club gives Antalya Airport as 70 km and about one hour in one section, and 67 km in its arrival notes. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) compares taxis and shuttles to Side. Parking at the club is free.

## What to check for this stay

- **Season:** the club is closed now. The 2027 season runs 23 April to 6 November; 2026 ran 27 March to 8 November.
- **Times:** rooms are ready from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Late check-out is quoted as €55 until 15:00 or €90 after in one section, and £40 in another.
- **Room choice:** whether an atrium family room, with no outside view, is right for you, and where the Economy doubles are.
- **Extras:** the speciality restaurant, premium drinks, padel, hamam and baby care all cost extra. Pets aren't allowed.
