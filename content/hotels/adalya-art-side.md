Adalya Art Side is a five-star all-inclusive resort in Evrenseki, on the long sandy coast east of Side. It opened in 2005, was last renovated in 2019 according to its fact sheet, and is aimed squarely at families, with slides, a mini club and a new outdoor play park. We read the official Adalya pages and the hotel's English fact sheet on 28 November 2026; the footer reads © 2026.

## Where it is

The fact sheet places the hotel in the **Evrenseki tourism centre** in [Manavgat district](/destinations/manavgat/) but gives no street number. It puts **Side** 10 km away, **Manavgat** 12 km and **Antalya Airport 60 km** (about 55 minutes). The ruins of [Side Ancient City](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/) and the [Manavgat Waterfall](/things-to-do/manavgat-waterfall/) are both easy taxi trips.

## The setting

The hotel is **not on the sand**: the fact sheet says the beach is 300 m away. It is a private sandy beach with Blue Flag status; no pier is mentioned. Back at the hotel, the 1,800 m² outdoor pool is decorated with dolphin mosaics. Add a 226 m² indoor pool (its "opening and heating depends on weather conditions"), a children's pool, two small aquapark pools, and three big and three small slides that run in two sessions a day, 10:00–12:00 and 14:00–16:00, weather permitting.

The spa offers a Turkish bath, sauna and paid massages; the fact sheet lists the hamam and sauna as both free and extra in different places, so ask.

## Rooms & dining

The fact sheet counts **417 rooms**, all **land view**. Standard rooms are about **29 m²** for three guests plus a child; larger standard rooms are 31–32 m² (the website and fact sheet differ); connecting family rooms are about **57 m²** for up to six. Accessible rooms are listed.

The website calls the hotel all-inclusive, and the fact sheet says drinks are served by the glass. The Sultan Main Restaurant serves breakfast 07:00–11:00, lunch 12:30–14:30 and dinner 18:30–21:00, with a midnight snack 22:00–07:00. Three à la carte restaurants open 18:30–21:00: **Garda** (Italian), **Zhen Guen** (Chinese) and **Divan** (Turkish), with one free visit on stays of seven nights or more. The fact sheet also lists a fish à la carte that the restaurant page leaves out. The Lobby and Pool bars open 10:00–00:00, and the Perge Bar takes over from midnight to 08:00.

## Who it suits

Families on a beach holiday who don't mind a short walk to the sand. The **Mini Club** (113 m²) takes ages 4–12, and the outdoor **Mare Kids Park** has slides, climbing frames, swings and a trampoline. Couples wanting quiet or a sea view should look elsewhere in our [Side hotel list](/hotels/?area=Side).

## What to check for this stay

- **Season:** no opening or closing dates are published.
- **The beach:** confirm how you get there (walk or shuttle) and whether loungers are included.
- **Views:** every room type is land view.
- **Extras:** spa treatments, the games hall, water sports and floodlit tennis cost extra.
