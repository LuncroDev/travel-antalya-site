Carruba Boutique Hotel is a small hotel in Kaş named after a carob tree. The hotel says the tree at its entrance is about 250 years old, and that the owners' family once gathered its pods in the garden to make carob molasses. *Carruba* is Italian for carob. The hotel's own site places the start of its story there.

## Where it is

The address is Andifli Mahallesi, Engin Sokak No. 3, in Kaş. The hotel says it is about an eight-minute walk to the centre of town and the ancient theatre. Our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/) covers the harbour, the Lycian tombs and the coast on either side.

## The setting

The hotel describes a garden with old olive trees. Breakfast is served there or in the breakfast area, with views of the garden and the pool. Several room types look over the pool and the sea. The site does not describe a beach or sea platform, so plan to swim at the town's coves or further along the coast.

## Rooms & dining

The rooms page lists many room types, from 25 m² to 55 m². All have air conditioning, Wi-Fi, a TV and a phone, and most list a kettle, minibar and hairdryer.

- **Hazeran rooms** (28–40 m²) face the garden. The Lemon Garden room has its own garden and an outdoor jacuzzi.
- **Terrace rooms** sit under a pitched roof with a terrace balcony. The double and triple are 25 m²; the Comfort is 55 m² with sea and pool views.
- **Mediterranean** (55 m²) and **Silver** (40 m²) rooms have sea and pool views and a balcony.
- **White rooms** (55 m²) have a sitting area; the White Garden adds an outdoor jacuzzi.
- **Bohemian rooms** include a 55 m² Deluxe with an indoor jacuzzi and sea and pool views.

Breakfast is an open buffet of home-made products. The site does not say whether it is included in every rate, or mention a restaurant for other meals. For dinner in town, try [Naturel Restaurant](/restaurants/naturel-restaurant-kas/) or [Bi Lokma](/restaurants/bi-lokma-restaurant/).

## Who it suits

Couples and friends who want to walk into town and come back to a garden. The larger rooms, with a double and a single bed, suit a family of three. The site gives no age policy, so families with young children should ask first.

## Getting there

The hotel puts Dalaman Airport about 148 km away and Antalya Airport about 199 km away. From Antalya, it suggests the 600 bus, Havaş or the tram to the bus station, then a Batı Antalya bus to Kaş, which takes about four hours. KGM puts Kaş 187 km from Antalya by road. Our [Antalya to Kaş day trip guide](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) describes the drive.

## What to check for this stay

Ask whether breakfast is included and whether the hotel is open in your month. Book by room name if a jacuzzi or sea view matters, because the room types differ a lot. The hotel says [Kaputaş Beach](/blog/kaputas-beach-travel-antalya/) is 20 km away and Patara 40 km.
