Suna Sun Hotel is a small hotel on the Çukurbağ Peninsula, the long finger of land that runs west from Kaş town. It describes itself as small and elegant, and its rules shape the stay: guests must be 14 or older, breakfast is included, and pets are not allowed.

## Where it is

The hotel gives its address as Andifli Mahallesi, Bülent Kalkavan Sokak No. 10, on the Çukurbağ Peninsula in Kaş. It puts the town centre about 4 km away, roughly ten minutes by taxi, and says local minibuses run in summer. Parking is free for guests. Our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/) covers the harbour, the Lycian tombs and the coves on either side of town.

## The setting

The hotel's swimming area is a private platform on the natural rocky shore, reached by a short walk from the rooms, with ladders down into the sea. It is for hotel guests only. Sunbeds, umbrellas and towels are free, and food and drinks are served to the loungers. There is no sandy beach, so if you want sand, plan a day at [Kaputaş Beach](/blog/kaputas-beach-travel-antalya/) or [Patara](/blog/patara-beach-and-ancient-city/). The hotel also has a pool, which the breakfast terrace overlooks.

## Rooms & dining

The rooms page lists six room types. Four have published details, all with air conditioning, TV, Wi-Fi and a safe:

- **Deluxe Room with Sea View**: 27 m², with a balcony and shower.
- **Deluxe Room with Side Sea View**: 27 m², with a jacuzzi bathtub.
- **Deluxe Room with Jacuzzi**: 30 m², with a jacuzzi on a garden terrace, a living area and wooden ceilings.
- **Deluxe Room with Jacuzzi & Garden View**: 30 m², also with a terrace jacuzzi.

Two junior suites are also listed, without details.

Breakfast runs from 08:00 to 10:30 at the open-air restaurant by the pool, with sea views. Expect a Turkish breakfast with eggs to order, tea and filter coffee, plus vegetarian and vegan options. Gluten-free food is available on request, but the hotel says it cannot guarantee a safe kitchen for people with severe coeliac disease.

The hotel restaurant, **Taşra Kaş**, serves Mediterranean food and is open to non-guests. It opens from 08:00 to midnight, with snacks from 13:00 to 17:30 and dinner from 19:00 to 22:00. For dinner out, [Elia Restaurant](/restaurants/elia-restaurant-kas/) is on the same peninsula, and [Naturel](/restaurants/naturel-restaurant-kas/) is in town.

## Who it suits

Couples and adult friends who want sea views, a jacuzzi room and a swim before breakfast. It is not for families with younger children, given the 14+ rule. The hotel states that it is not suitable for disabled access.

## Getting there

The hotel puts Dalaman Airport about 150 km away, around 2 hours 15 minutes by car, and says the drive from Antalya Airport takes about three hours. It can arrange private transfers from both airports if you send your flight details. Our [Antalya to Kaş day trip guide](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) describes the coast road.

## What to check for this stay

Ask for the season dates: the site announces that the season is open but gives no dates. Check-in is from 14:00; the check-out time is printed as "12:00 AM", so confirm it. The hotel says its staff help plan boat tours and local outings; its area guide lists Kekova and Simena, and notes that the ferry to Meis (Kastellorizo) needs a passport. For a cultural day out, see [Xanthos](/things-to-do/xanthos-ancient-city/).
