Corendon Hydros Club Kemer is a club-style resort on the beach in Kemer town. It opened in 1989 and reopened under the Corendon name on 1 May 2026 after a renovation, with an "Ultra All Inclusive" concept. Its April 2026 factsheet covers May to November 2026.

## Where it is

The address is Yeni Mahallesi, Şehit Polis Cemal Ilgaz Caddesi No. 1, Kemer. The factsheet puts Kemer town centre 900 m away and Antalya Airport 59 km away. The 64,000 m² grounds hold 21 three-storey club blocks and a four-storey hotel building. Our [Kemer district guide](/destinations/kemer/) covers the town, and [Phaselis](/things-to-do/phaselis-ancient-city/) and the [Tahtalı cable car](/things-to-do/tahtali-olympos-cable-car/) make good day trips.

## The setting

The private beach is 270 m of fine pebbles, with a renovated pier and cabanas you reserve and pay for. The opening announcement says about 300 m. The factsheet lists five pools: a 484 m² outdoor pool, a 300 m² relax pool, a 99 m² indoor pool, and children's pools outdoors (29 m²) and indoors (6 m²). There are two slides; heating isn't mentioned.

The hammam, sauna and steam room are free; massages and scrubs cost extra. The Cory & Friends Kids Club takes ages 4 to 12 from 10:00 to 22:00, and the VibeHub teen club opens 10:00–20:00. Free sports include yoga, SUP, canoeing and pickleball; motorised water sports are paid. Pets of approved breeds up to 10 kg are allowed with approval at booking, for €20 a night.

## Rooms & dining

The factsheet counts 379 rooms and 816 beds, with these sizes:

- **Club Standard**, 24 m², garden view
- **Club Family**, 40 m², two bedrooms
- **Hotel Standard**, 28 m², sea or garden view
- **Hotel Comfort**, 30 m²
- **Hotel Terrace**, 37 m², with a furnished terrace
- **Hotel Superior**, 52 m², with a terrace
- **Hotel Family**, 57 m², two bedrooms and two bathrooms
- **Accessible rooms**, 22 m²

The website gives smaller sizes for the same types, such as 31 m² for Club Family and 30 m² for Superior, and the group's opening article counted 371 rooms. Rooms have a capsule coffee machine and a minibar with water, soft drinks and beer.

Mosaic, the main restaurant, serves buffets, with dinner 18:30–21:00. The factsheet lists two free à la carte restaurants, Rikia (Asian) and Velora (international), open six days a week. The website also names Velora, but lists Grilliant (fish and meat) and Sea Breeze (world cuisine) instead of Rikia. The Day & Night Bistro runs around the clock. Local and standard imported drinks are free at the bars 24 hours a day; premium brands and room service cost extra.

Active families who want kids' and teen clubs and free sport near Kemer centre. For tennis players, the factsheet puts Corendon Tennis Club Kemer nine minutes away, and the website says it has 14 clay courts in Beldibi. ## Getting there

The factsheet's highlights put the 59 km airport run at 60 minutes, and no transfer is published. Our [Antalya Airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) compares taxis, transfers and buses to Kemer.

## What to check for this stay

The factsheet gives no closing day or winter programme, so confirm the hotel is open on late-autumn dates. It also says some promotional images of the renovation are CGI. Check-in and check-out times aren't published. All three lifts are in the hotel building, so ask about stairs in the club blocks. Confirm your room's size and which à la carte restaurants are open.
