SeaLife Family Resort Hotel is a five-star family hotel in Konyaaltı, Antalya's long western beach. It sits on GMK Bulvarı in the Gürsu neighbourhood. On 15 November 2026 its offers page listed early-booking discounts for stays from 14 November 2026 to 31 October 2027, so it takes winter bookings, although the site never says it is open all year. The site's header lists [Porto Bello](/hotels/porto-bello-hotel-resort-spa-konyaalti/) among its group's hotels.

## Where it is

The address is Gürsu Mahallesi, GMK Bulvarı No. 1, Konyaaltı. The hotel puts itself 4 km from the city centre and 20 km from the airport. It promotes an "internationally blue-flagged beach" but does not give the walk to the sand or say whether you cross the road. See our [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/) page for the beach itself and the [Konyaaltı district guide](/destinations/konyaalti/) for the area.

## The setting

The hotel is made up of three connected blocks on 7,179 m². It opened in 1995 and was last renovated in 2018, according to its facts table. There are four pools on the site's pool pages:

- **Pool with slides**: 70 m², fresh water, with a lifeguard.
- **Outdoor pool 2 ("Relax")**: 140 m².
- **Indoor pool**: heated, 79 m².
- **Children's pool**: listed, with no size given.

The outdoor pools open from 1 April to 31 October and the beach service from 1 May to 15 October, both weather permitting. Outside those months the heated indoor pool is the one to count on. Pool depths are printed in "cm" with figures like 1.25, so read them as metres. There is also a sauna, a hammam, massage rooms and a fitness room, but no hours or prices.

## Rooms & dining

The about page says 239 rooms and 640 beds; the facts table on the same page says 240 rooms. Seven room types are listed:

- **Standard rooms** (single, double or triple): 22 m², with a balcony.
- **Family Room**: 48 m², two bedrooms and a living room, for up to four.
- **Suite**: 54 m², two bedrooms and a living room, sea view.
- **Royal Suite**: 49 m², for two adults.
- **Kral Suit** ("King Suite"): 77 m², three bedrooms and two bathrooms, for up to seven.

All rooms are non-smoking. Meals are in the main buffet restaurant (Turkish and world dishes), with an à la carte restaurant (Mediterranean and Turkish) open on "certain days". A snack bar faces the pool, and there is a lobby bar, a spa bar and a vitamin bar. The hotel's blog mentions halal, vegetarian, vegan and diet menus.

## Who it suits

Families with younger children who want a beach base close to the city, in or out of summer. There is a mini club, a children's disco and a playground. Couples wanting quiet may prefer a smaller place; compare other [Konyaaltı hotels](/hotels/?area=Konyaalt%C4%B1). For days out with children, read [is Antalya good for families?](/blog/is-antalya-good-for-families-things-to-do-in-antalya-with-kids/).

## Getting there

The hotel gives 20 km to Antalya Airport and does not publish a transfer price. Our [airport transport guide](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/) and [AntalyaKart guide](/blog/antalyakart-guide/) cover the public options. There is a car park.

## What to check for this stay

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00. Pets are not allowed. The site does not say whether rates are all-inclusive, so confirm the board basis, and ask which pools and restaurants are open on your dates. Planning a cooler-month stay?
