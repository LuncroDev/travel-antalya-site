Ramada Resort by Wyndham Lara is an all-inclusive resort in Kemerağzı, the eastern, Kundu end of Antalya's Lara hotel strip. Wyndham sells it on its beachfront water park, a lobby bar that never closes and a buffet that keeps serving after midnight. We read the hotel's official Wyndham page on 27 November 2026; its footer reads ©2026. It is thin on numbers, so this profile sticks to what Wyndham actually says.

## Where it is

Wyndham gives the address simply as Kemerağzı Mahallesi, 07160 Antalya, and the phone number as +90 242 352 33 33. Kemerağzı sits in the [Aksu district](/destinations/aksu/), east of the city, among the big Lara and Kundu resorts. The page puts downtown Antalya **17 km** away and calls the airport "a short drive" without a figure. It says the beach and Akkapark Mall are within walking distance and Lara Beach Park is a short drive.

The same neighbourhood has its sister brand, [Wyndham Garden Lara](/hotels/wyndham-garden-lara/). For the sand itself, see our [Lara Beach guide](/things-to-do/lara-beach/) and the free [Lara public beaches](/blog/lara-public-beaches/).

## The setting

The headline feature is a **beachfront water park and pool**. Wyndham's gallery shows two large outdoor pools, one with a tower of slides, plus a children's splash pool with a tipping bucket. The page says nothing about indoor or heated pools, which matters between November and April.

For the beach, Wyndham lists towels, sunbeds, umbrellas, volleyball, food and drink service and a shuttle. Its aerial photo shows a sandy beach with a long pier beyond the pools, but the text never says how far it is or whether it is the hotel's own. Other facilities named are a Turkish bath, sauna and steam room, a library, a mini disco, a nightclub and a business centre.

## Rooms & dining

Wyndham gives **no room count, room types or sizes**. Rooms are described with views of the green hills and the sea, a minibar, a coffee and tea maker, a flat-screen TV, free Wi-Fi, central heating, a safe, a make-up mirror, a hair dryer and slippers.

The board is **all-inclusive**. Published hours at Mood's Main Restaurant (buffet, indoor or terrace seating):

- **Breakfast:** 07:00–11:00
- **Lunch:** 12:30–14:30
- **Dinner:** 19:00–21:30
- **Late-night buffet:** 23:00–00:00
- **Late-night snacks:** 01:00–06:00

The **Lobby Bar** is open 24/7 and, in Wyndham's words, part of the "unlimited all-inclusive package". No à la carte restaurants are named, and the page does not say what costs extra.

## Who it suits

Families who want slides and a splash pool close to the city and the airport, and night owls who like food and drinks around the clock. Golfers get a short hop east: Wyndham names Titanic Golf Club and [National Golf Club](/golf/national-golf-club/) as minutes away, and our [airport-to-Belek golf guide](/blog/antalya-airport-to-belek-golf-courses/) explains the drive. Couples wanting quiet should compare our [adults-only hotels guide](/blog/adults-only-hotels-antalya/).

## Getting there

Antalya Airport is the obvious gateway; Wyndham gives no distance, so check the transfer time when you book. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) covers taxis and shuttles. Kaleiçi and the [Düden Waterfalls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/) make easy half-day trips west along the coast.

## What to check for this stay

- **Room count and type:** none are published, so ask what you are booking.
- **The beach:** confirm the walking distance and whether the sunbeds are the hotel's.
- **Winter:** ask which pools and slides are open and heated on your dates.
- **Extras:** the page does not list paid items, so ask about premium drinks and spa treatments.
