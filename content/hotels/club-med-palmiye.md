Club Med Palmiye is a large all-inclusive resort on the coast of Kemer, south-west of Antalya. Club Med's Turkish page places it in Kemer, and the English page describes a long, pearl-grey beach with the Taurus Mountains rising behind it. It is built for active holidays: most sport is included, and there are kids' clubs for every age from babies to teenagers.

## Where it is

The published address is Yeni Mahalle, Dedeler Küme Evleri No. 59, in Kemer district. The Highways Directorate (KGM) distance table puts Kemer 44 km from Antalya by road. Our [Kemer district guide](/destinations/kemer/) covers the town, its marina and the coast to the south.

## The setting

The resort has two parts. The **Villagio** is a garden area with bungalows a short walk from the pools. The **Hotel** area has rooms in a building with views over the mountains, the gardens or the sea. Club Med's FAQ mentions banana trees and bougainvillea in the grounds, and the main page says old fruit trees have been kept in the gardens.

There are six pools, according to the Turkish page: a main pool and a Zen pool in each of the two areas, an indoor pool in the Hotel area, and a pool for the kids' club. The pages do not say whether the Zen pools are adults-only, so ask if a quiet pool matters to you.

## Rooms & dining

Club Med lists Superior rooms in both the Villagio and the Hotel area, plus a small number of suites. Rooms include Wi-Fi, a coffee and tea station and a minibar with soft drinks.

Meals are buffet or à la carte, with show cooking. The Turkish page names a renovated restaurant called **Phaselis** and two renovated bars, **Le Tulipe** and **Le Lalezar**. Drinks are included all day, among them cocktails, wine, spirits and beer.

## Who it suits

Families first. The kids' clubs cover babies from four months up to 17-year-olds, split into age groups, and childcare runs until 9 pm. Club Med lists 29 included activities: waterskiing, wakeboarding, sailing, kayaking, paddleboarding, tennis, padel, archery, and a flying trapeze and circus school. Yoga, Pilates and other fitness classes are included too. Horse riding, private water-sports lessons and treatments at the Sothys spa, including a Turkish bath, cost extra.

## Getting there

Club Med gives the drive from Antalya Airport as about one hour. Most guests arrive on a transfer booked with their package. For a day out, the ruins of [Phaselis](/things-to-do/phaselis-ancient-city/) and the [Tahtalı Olympos cable car](/things-to-do/tahtali-olympos-cable-car/) are on this stretch of coast.

## What to check for this stay

Club Med's pages give no opening or closing dates for Palmiye. The Turkish page says bookings for the Hotel area for summer and winter 2027 are taken on the same page, and the English page says all room types are listed together from November 2026. Confirm your dates, then check which area your room is in: the Villagio and the Hotel feel quite different. The "kids under 4 stay free" offer has conditions, so read them.
