Akra Hotel sits on the Konyaaltı side of Antalya, close to the long pebble beach and the Beach Park. It is a good fit if you want a city location rather than a remote resort: the Antalya Museum is nearby and the tram connects to the old town.

The mountains behind the bay make for strong sunset views. Check recent room refurbishments and beach-access arrangements when you book.
