Liberty Lara is the Lara-area resort of the Turkish group Liberty Hotels & Resorts. It sits on the long hotel strip east of the city, where Lara runs into Kundu, and sells an all-inclusive holiday built around pools, restaurants and a kids' club. The group's pages carry a "© 2026 Liberty Hotels" footer.

## Where it is

The address is Kemerağzı Mah., Yaşar Sobutay Bulvarı No. 64, in the Aksu district of Antalya. That is the Kundu end of the coast, past the public sections of [Lara Beach](/things-to-do/lara-beach/) and the cliffs of the [Lower Düden falls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/). Liberty doesn't publish distances to the airport or the city centre. Our [Aksu district guide](/destinations/aksu/) covers the area.

## The setting

Pools are the main event here. Liberty's beach-and-pools page lists:

- **Main pool:** 2,000 m², described as cold water, so it isn't heated.
- **Main pool with water slides:** several slides, with no count or heights given.
- **Apollon adult pool:** for guests aged 16 and over, 140 cm deep, with a jacuzzi. It is heated in November and March.
- **Indoor pool:** heated, for adults and children, open from 1 November to 1 April. Liberty says hours may change with the weather.
- **Kids' pool:** 40 cm deep, with fountains and small slides.

There is also a Balinese-style pavilion with sea views and food and drink service, and a spa and wellbeing area. The beach gets only a passing mention, in the weddings section: the pages give no length, pier, sunbed or Blue Flag details.

## Rooms & dining

Liberty lists five room types without sizes or a total count: Economic, Standard, Large (described with a sea view), Family (two interconnecting rooms plus a separate living room) and Duplex.

The main restaurant is Myra. The à la carte restaurants named are Pınara (Turkish), Balbura (Italian), Tatre, and a far-eastern restaurant called Nysa, though one photo label calls the same room the BBQ restaurant. Bars include the Apollon Bar, Lukka Chill Out Bar, Tlos Bar and the Gagai lobby bar terrace. Liberty doesn't say whether the à la cartes cost extra or need booking.

## Who it suits

Families first: the Libby & Berty Kids Club, a shallow kids' pool and family rooms with a separate living area are the draw. Couples get the 16+ Apollon pool and its jacuzzi. In winter, the indoor heated pool is the one to plan around. Compare other [Lara and Kundu hotels](/hotels/?area=Lara%20%26%20Kundu), such as [Fame Residence Lara](/hotels/fame-residence-lara-spa/).

## Getting there

No distances or shuttle details are published. For taxis and transfers from the airport, see [how to get to Antalya](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/). During the [COP31 conference](/events/cop31-antalya/), 9–20 November 2026, the group was also selling stays for delegates, so expect busy roads in Lara until it ends.

## What to check for this stay

- Room size and view for your room type, since none are published.
- Whether the outdoor main pool and slides are open if you travel in winter.
- Which à la carte restaurants are included and how to reserve them.
- Beach arrangements, such as sunbeds and the walk from your room.
