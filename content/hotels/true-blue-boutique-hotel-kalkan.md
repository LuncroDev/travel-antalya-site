True Blue Boutique Hotel is a small hotel in Kalkan, in the Kaş district. It describes its spot as the Teke Peninsula, above Kalamar Bay and along the Lycian Way. Rooms run from attic suites to stone houses and a villa. The site carries a "© 2022 – 2026" footer and lists its partner beach clubs for the 2026 season.

## Where it is

The address is Begonvil Sokak No. 25, Kalkan, Kaş. The hotel gives these distances: 250 m to the nearest beach, 300 m to a Carrefour supermarket, 1 km to a pharmacy and health centre, and 1.5 km to Kalkan town. By car, it says [Kaputaş Beach](/blog/kaputas-beach-travel-antalya/) is 8 km (13 minutes) and [Patara](/blog/patara-beach-and-ancient-city/) 17 km (26 minutes). Our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/) covers the wider coast.

## The setting

The outdoor pool is open from 08:00 to 20:00, April to November, with sunbeds and showers. A pool bar runs from 09:00 to 23:59, and a snack café from 11:00 to 16:30. The hotel has no beach of its own. For 2026 it named two partner beach clubs where guests get free entry:

- **Kalamar Beach Club**, about 250 m away, open 09:00–19:00, with sunbeds and showers.
- **Çakıl Beach Club**, about 2 km away, free entry 08:00–19:30. It accepts dogs.

There is a weekly programme: free yoga on Sunday mornings, a poolside meze and barbecue buffet on Mondays, a sunset picnic on Patara beach on Wednesdays, and a Friday trip to Kaş market and the old town.

## Rooms & dining

The home page counts ten types of accommodation. The suites page lists 16 suites in four types, from 35 m² (Standard and Romantic Roof) to 45 m² (Roof Corner), each with a living area and balcony. The Romantic Roof is an attic room on the third floor with a queen bed. The residences range from a 50 m² garden studio to 75 m² family stone houses and cottages, and a villa of about 200 m² with four bedrooms and a jacuzzi is let for stays of more than five days.

Breakfast is a daily buffet, included in the suites and studios. In the two-bedroom houses and the villa it costs €11 for adults and €8 for children aged 7–12; under-sevens eat free. The hotel says it uses no margarine, corn syrup or frozen products.

## Who it suits

Couples and families who want a quiet, self-contained base and don't need a full resort. There are no age limits, but at least one guest must be over 18. Children up to 3 stay free, 4–6s pay €5 per stay and 7–12s pay €10 a night. One dog up to 15 kg is welcome in three of the garden units. The hotel has no wheelchair-accessible rooms. For nearby alternatives, compare [Kalkan hotels](/hotels/?area=Kalkan) such as [Lures Hotel](/hotels/lures-hotel-kalkan/) (adults only) and [Villa Mahal](/hotels/villa-mahal/).

## Getting there

The hotel's travel page, updated September 2026, gives Dalaman Airport as 110 km (about 1 hour 30 minutes) and Antalya Airport as 200 km (about 2 hours 30 minutes). Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) compares the two for the Kaş coast.

## What to check for this stay

Check-in is after 15:00 and check-out by 11:00, with photo ID at arrival. No prepayment is needed, and cancellation is free up to five days before arrival; after that the full stay is charged. Smoking is banned indoors but allowed on balconies. The site publishes no opening or closing dates for the hotel, so confirm it's open if you travel in winter.
