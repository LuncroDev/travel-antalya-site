Crowne Plaza Antalya is an IHG hotel on the Konyaaltı seafront, west of the city centre. IHG calls it a business hotel, but its pools, spa and position facing the beach make it a practical year-round base for holidays too. The pages we used carry a "© 2026 IHG" footer.

## Where it is

The address is Gürsu Mahallesi, Akdeniz Bulvarı, 304. Sokak, Konyaaltı. IHG puts it 7.35 km from downtown Antalya. For the airport it gives two figures: 23 km in the hotel description and about 21 km in its FAQ. The Tünektepe cable car is next door, and IHG says the historic sites are about 15 minutes away by car, which covers [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/). See our [Konyaaltı district guide](/destinations/konyaalti/) for the area.

## The setting

[Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/) and its seafront running track are across the boulevard. IHG's own pages describe it as 7 km long in one place and 18 km in another. No private beach, sunbeds or beach service are listed.

Pools and spa, as published on IHG's amenities page:

- **Indoor pool:** heated, open 09:00 to midnight, with a children's pool and a lifeguard.
- **Outdoor pool:** 08:00–20:00, also with a children's pool and a lifeguard. Neither pool charges a fee.
- **Spa:** 10:00 to midnight, with a resting pool, Turkish bath and Finnish sauna. The amenities page calls it Velvet Aura, but a photo caption says Aphrodite Spa.
- **Fitness centre:** 08:00–22:00.

## Rooms & dining

There are 194 rooms over 10 floors, two of them accessible. IHG lists three room types:

- **Standard room:** 25–35 m², two singles or one king bed.
- **Junior suite:** 50–55 m², king bed.
- **Corner suite:** 70–75 m², king bed.

The FAQ counts two restaurants. The one named is Neapolis Fine Dining on the 10th floor, with a terrace. Weekend breakfast runs 07:00–11:00; weekday hours aren't published, and the dining page wasn't loading when we checked.

## Who it suits

Business travellers and conference guests, and couples who want a city hotel near a long beach in winter, when the heated indoor pool earns its keep. Families get two children's pools, but IHG says children's activities are off site. Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00; the minimum check-in age is 18. Pets aren't allowed. Compare other [Konyaaltı hotels](/hotels/?area=Konyaalt%C4%B1) such as [Porto Bello](/hotels/porto-bello-hotel-resort-spa-konyaalti/) and [SeaLife Family Resort](/hotels/sealife-family-resort-konyaalti/).

## Getting there

There's no hotel shuttle. Parking is free, with valet service and two EV charging points. IHG says city buses pass every five minutes; our [AntalyaKart guide](/blog/antalyakart-guide/) explains how to pay. For taxis and transfers from the airport, see [how to get to Antalya](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/).

## What to check for this stay

- Which restaurant serves dinner on your dates, since IHG names only one.
- Whether your room faces the sea; IHG doesn't list views by room type.
- Beach arrangements, if you want sunbeds.
