The Düden stream rises in the Taurus foothills and disappears underground before resurfacing in Antalya, where it forms two quite different waterfalls.

**Upper Düden** lies in a park north of the centre. The stream spreads into a series of cascades surrounded by shaded gardens, picnic lawns and cafes, which makes it a relaxed place to escape the summer heat. There are paths behind and beside the falls.

**Lower Düden** is on the coast in the Lara area: the river drops off a cliff roughly 40 metres into the sea. The best views come from a clifftop park above, and from the water on boat tours that leave from the Lara coast and nearby harbours; check with operators for current schedules.

**Practical notes**

- Upper Düden is easiest by taxi or bus from the centre; Lower Düden is close to the Lara hotels and the Karpuzkaldıran area.
- Late spring brings the strongest flow; in summer the volume can be lower.
- Sunset is the best time at Lower Düden, when the sea turns copper.
- Carry water and wear shoes with grip: paths near the falls can be wet.
