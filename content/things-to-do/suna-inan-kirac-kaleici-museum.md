Kaleiçi Museum is a good choice if your old-town walk leaves you wondering what happened behind the façades. Put it into the middle of a [Kaleiçi walking route](/blog/kaleici-walking-route/), rather than treating it as a reason to race through the neighbourhood.

## Two buildings, two perspectives

The museum occupies two protected historic buildings restored by Suna and İnan Kıraç in 1993–1995. The house presents late Ottoman domestic life through reconstructed scenes, including coffee service, a groom's shave and a henna night. These are staged historical displays, not photographs of living residents.

Across the courtyard, the former Aya Yorgi church offers a different setting. The provincial culture directorate records its restoration in 1863 and identifies Çanakkale ceramics among the museum displays. The house and church together make this more than a collection of objects: the buildings themselves deserve attention. [Official museum description](https://antalya.ktb.gov.tr/TR-310233/suna--inan-kirac-kaleici-muzesi.html).

## How to approach a visit

Our suggestion is to start with the domestic scenes, then move to the church. Look at the way rooms are arranged before concentrating on individual exhibits. That gives the visit a clear thread without needing an elaborate itinerary.

This is a different stop from [Antalya Museum](/things-to-do/antalya-museum/). Choose Kaleiçi Museum for the old-town setting and domestic culture; read our [Antalya museums guide](/blog/antalya-museums-rainy-day/) if you are deciding between several collections. Do not assume one museum's ticket or closure day applies to another.

## Address and practical checks

The official address is Barbaros Mahallesi, Kocatepe Sokak No. 25, Kaleiçi, Muratpaşa, Antalya. Use the museum's full name when searching a map to avoid confusing it with Antalya's other museums.

Current opening hours, entry charges and step-free arrangements were not confirmed for this guide. Check with the museum before making a dedicated journey, particularly if you need accessible entry or are visiting on a public holiday. Treat online prices from older travel articles as historical until confirmed.
