Cleopatra Beach (Kleopatra Plajı) is Alanya's town beach: a long run of sand on the west side of the castle peninsula, about 1 km from the centre. Swim in clear water below the castle hill, then ride the cable car up from the Damlataş end. Two sections of it hold the Blue Flag for 2026.

## What Cleopatra Beach is

Alanya Municipality places the beach on the shore west of the historic peninsula, in front of Damlataş Cave. The back of the sand rests against the foot of the castle hill, and rocks frame it, so the municipality compares it to a natural pool. TÜRÇEV, which runs the Blue Flag programme in Türkiye, calls it Alanya's most popular beach.

The name comes from a legend. The municipality says the beach is famous because Cleopatra, Queen of Egypt, and Mark Antony are said to have swum here. GoTürkiye's version is that Cleopatra loved the bay and came here to swim on her Mediterranean voyages, and it adds that the story may not be true. Enjoy it as a story, not as history.

**The names overlap at the cave end.** TÜRÇEV describes the west public beach as part of Damlataş Beach. The municipality's beach page puts Damlataş Public Beach at the east end of the Kleopatra shore, next to the cave.

**The length depends on the source.** TÜRÇEV's description says about 2 km, but its data table gives 250 m by 100 m for each flagged section. We found no official figure for the whole shore.

## Highlights

- **Sand in town.** TÜRÇEV lists the west section as sand and the east section, by the cave, as sand and pebble. Descriptions of the grain differ: GoTürkiye says fine and white on one page and golden on another, and the municipality's English page says thick.
- **Clear water.** A small, dark cove of large stones runs from the beach toward the peninsula and carries Cleopatra's name. The municipality says it is known for clear water, and with a mask you can watch fish over the seabed.
- **Two Blue Flag sections.** Kleopatra Batı (West) Public Beach held the flag from 1999 to 2002 and every year from 2004 to 2026. Kleopatra Doğu (East) Public Beach has held it every year from 2004 to 2026.

## How to get there

TÜRÇEV puts both flagged sections about 1 km, or roughly five minutes, from Alanya's centre. It says public transport serves the beach but names no route, so ask your hotel which bus or dolmuş to take. The municipality's beach page places the west public beach on Atatürk Bulvarı. TÜRÇEV gives Gazipaşa as the nearest airport, 40 km away. Our [Alanya district guide](/destinations/alanya/) covers the wider area.

## Facilities and rules

TÜRÇEV's listing for the west public beach says:

- Entry is free. Sunbeds and umbrellas cost extra.
- Refreshment kiosks stand about every 50 m.
- There is an accessible toilet on the beach, a ramp, and accessible sunbeds and umbrellas. There is no lift or handrail ramp into the sea.
- Parasailing, jet skis and pedal boats run beyond the swimming area.
- Sports fields, parks, hotels and cafés back parts of the beach.

The east section's listing shows an accessible toilet on the beach, but no ramp or accessible sunbeds. Neither listing says whether lifeguards are on duty or showers are provided, and we found no published beach rules. Follow the flags and signs on the day.

## Tips

- **Mind the drop.** TÜRÇEV says the water reaches a man's height after three or four steps, and the municipality also notes that it gets deep close to shore. Keep children within reach.
- **Stay inside the swimming zone.** Jet skis and parasail boats work beyond it.
- **Choose your section.** For the ramp and accessible sunbeds, head for the west public beach.

## What's nearby

- **Damlataş Cave** is at the east end of the beach. The municipality says it was found in 1948 while stone was quarried for the harbour, and it and GoTürkiye call it Türkiye's first cave opened to tourism. A 50 m passage, up to 15 m high, leads inside. The municipality says the air stays at about 22°C and 95% humidity all year. Neither the municipality nor GoTürkiye publishes opening hours or a fee, so check at the entrance.
- **The cable car (teleferik)** opened in October 2017. Its 900 m line runs from the Damlataş social facilities, over Damlataş and Cleopatra beaches, to the Ehmedek Gate of [Alanya Castle](/things-to-do/alanya-castle/). The municipality said its 17 cabins carry 1,130 people an hour. We found no current official timetable or fare.
- **Food and beds.** Across the peninsula on the harbour, try [Güverte Restaurant](/restaurants/guverte-restaurant-alanya/). To stay close, browse [Alanya hotels](/hotels/?area=Alanya).
