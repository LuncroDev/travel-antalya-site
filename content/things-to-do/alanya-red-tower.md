Kızılkule, Alanya's Red Tower, is the harbour landmark to choose when you want to understand the city's seaward defences. It makes a natural companion to [Alanya Castle](/things-to-do/alanya-castle/), but it is a separate building and deserves more than a distant photograph.

## What to look for

Alanya Municipality dates the tower's inscription to April 1226, during Sultan Alaaddin Keykubat's reign. Its purpose was to protect the harbour, shipyard and castle from the sea. The octagonal structure combines stone lower walls with red brick above, the feature behind its familiar name.

Inside, the municipality describes five levels, a central cistern and high stone steps. The small entrance and narrow passage contrast with the building's imposing exterior. Notice the construction materials and defensive openings as well as the view. [Municipal history of Kızılkule](https://www.alanya.bel.tr/S/417/KizilKule).

## Plan the harbour part of your day

Our suggested pairing is the tower and [Alanya Shipyard](/things-to-do/alanya-shipyard-tersane/). Read them together as two parts of a maritime landscape: one guarded the waterfront, while the other served working vessels. If you also want the castle, avoid squeezing all three into the final part of the afternoon.

Use our [Alanya in a day](/blog/alanya-in-a-day/) guide to organise the wider outing. Travellers more interested in portable objects than defensive architecture can add [Alanya Museum](/things-to-do/alanya-museum/) instead of trying to cover every monument.

## Stairs, tickets and timing

Historic stairs are part of the experience, not a minor detail. Wear shoes you can climb in and ask staff about the route before buying a ticket if steep steps are a concern. Do not assume that reaching the harbour means every interior level is accessible without stairs.

Current admission prices, last-entry times and any combined-ticket arrangements were not confirmed for this guide. Check with the ticket desk or the [municipality's monument service](https://isletmeler.alanya.bel.tr/alanya-oren-yerleri/) before planning a tight connection. A photograph taken outside does not establish whether the interior is open that day.

Our real photograph shows the Red Tower in the foreground with the castle hillside behind. It illustrates their relationship, not a direct, level walking route between them.
