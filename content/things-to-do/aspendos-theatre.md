Aspendos is known for its remarkably preserved Roman theatre. The archaeological site also includes other ancient remains, with aqueducts nearby. Use the official visitor information below when planning a daytime visit.

## Opening hours and ticket office

Checked on **10 November 2026**, the [Ministry's Aspendos archaeological-site listing](https://muze.gov.tr/muze-detay?DistId=ASP&SectionId=ASP01) states that the site is **open every day, 08:00–17:00**, with the **ticket office closing at 16:30**. Times are local to Antalya (Europe/Istanbul, UTC+3).

**16:30 is the published ticket-office closing time, not the site's 17:00 closing time.** The listing does not establish a separate last-admission rule. Leave sufficient time for your visit and reconfirm the hours and any access restrictions with the official source before travelling, particularly if arriving late in the day.

## Daytime admission and performances

The Ministry lists MüzeKart as valid for Turkish citizens. Check the current rules for your own pass or ticket; this is not confirmation that every visitor pass is accepted. Ticket prices and eligibility can change.

**Do not assume that a daytime archaeological-site ticket or museum pass includes an evening concert, opera or ballet performance.** Check the specific organizer's event date, venue, admission conditions and ticket requirements separately. The daytime visiting hours above are not performance times.
