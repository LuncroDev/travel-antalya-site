Syedra is a worthwhile change of pace from Alanya’s beaches: a landscape of ancient walls and hillside paths rather than an indoor museum. Choose it for a history-focused excursion, and leave time to explore without rushing back for a tightly scheduled activity.

The official address is Seki Mahallesi, Asar Mevkii, Alanya. Use the archaeological site name for navigation, not just the wider Seki neighbourhood.

## What to look for

The [Ministry of Culture and Tourism’s site profile](https://turkishmuseums.com/museum/detail/1973-antalya-syedra-archeological-site/1973/4) identifies a colonnaded street, temple, acropolis church and spring cave among the structures investigated here. The remains are spread across the site; follow the marked visitor route rather than treating every visible ruin as an accessible path.

The setting is part of the appeal. The photograph above shows the acropolis in December 2023, before the latest visitor-centre arrangements. It is an actual Syedra archive image, not a picture of present-day facilities.

## Tickets: do not rely on old “free entry” listings

The provincial culture directorate [announces a new visitor centre and revised entry arrangements](https://antalya.ktb.gov.tr/TR-458944/syedra-antik-kentine-girislerde-ziyaret-uygulamasi-guncellendi.html). Its notice states that Müzekart is accepted and admission for visitors without it is **€7**.

The museum portal still displays conflicting free-admission rows alongside a €7 foreign-visitor price. Use the newer provincial announcement as the planning reference, and check eligibility and the final ticket price before travelling. Müzekart and the visitor MuseumPass products are not interchangeable names. The portal says payments are accepted in Turkish lira only, even though the visitor rate is advertised in euros.

## Hours and facilities

Checked on 18 November 2026, the museum portal lists winter hours of **08:30–17:00**, for 1 November to 1 April. It lists parking, toilets, a café and a shop. Allow enough daylight for both the ruins and the return walk; do not aim to arrive just before closing.

Wear shoes with good grip, take water and keep to designated areas. Published facility icons do not establish that the entire archaeological site is step-free. If access is essential, ask the operator about the specific route before setting out.

## Make it part of an Alanya stay

Read the [Alanya district guide](/destinations/alanya/) to place Syedra in a broader itinerary. For an indoor archaeology stop, see [Alanya Museum](/things-to-do/alanya-museum/); for the fortified peninsula above town, choose [Alanya Castle](/things-to-do/alanya-castle/).

Treat these as separate visits rather than promising a fixed all-in-one driving time. Return transport and the amount of walking that suits your group matter more than collecting every stop.
