Simena Castle stands above Kaleköy on the coast near Demre. The castle is the uphill stop on a Kekova outing, not the submerged ruins seen from a boat. Its short rock-cut theatre is inside the walls, and the view from the site looks across the protected harbour and islands toward Kekova.

## What is inside the walls?

The [Ministry of Culture and Tourism’s site guide](https://muze.gov.tr/muze-detay?DistId=SIO&SectionId=SIO02) traces Simena to at least the fourth century BC on the evidence of inscriptions. The fortifications visible on the hill were used in the Middle Ages. Inside, the ministry records a small theatre cut directly into the rock. It has seven rows and is distinct from the much larger theatre at nearby [Myra](/things-to-do/myra-ancient-city/).

Look for the contrast between the masonry walls and the exposed rock as you climb. The site guide also identifies remains of a temple and portico, so these are places to look for foundations and blocks, not intact buildings. Lower down, the shoreline and hillside have ancient tombs and remnants of baths.

## How to include Kaleköy in a Kekova trip

Start with the practical distinction between **being shown Kaleköy from the boat** and **having time ashore to climb to the castle**. If a boat stop matters, ask the operator whether it lands in the village, how long you will have there and whether site admission is included. A weather-dependent itinerary can change on the day. Our [Kekova boat trip guide](/blog/kekova-boat-trips/) compares departures from Üçağız and Kaş.

The climb from the waterfront is the reason to leave time in the village. Choose shoes with grip, bring water, and allow time to return to the quay. Ask about the steps before booking if steady uphill walking is difficult. To add a second archaeological stop, use our [Demre guide](/destinations/demre/) for the wider area rather than assuming a single boat booking includes Myra.

## Opening and admission

The ministry gives the site address as Üçağız Mahallesi, Kale Köyü Mevkii. Check its current hours and the last ticket-office time before departure, especially in winter. Ask the operator which costs are separate from the boat fare. The ministry’s Müzekart listing is explicitly for Turkish citizens; it does not establish that every visitor can use that card.
