Perge was one of the most important cities of ancient Pamphylia and is one of the most rewarding sites near Antalya. Roughly 15 kilometres east of the city, it is easy to reach by bus, taxi or as part of a tour.

The Hellenistic gate and its twin towers mark the entrance to the old city. Beyond that, a long colonnaded street runs through the centre, with a water channel running down the middle. The stadium, with seating for many thousands of spectators, is among the best-preserved in Anatolia, and the theatre and agora are also worth a look. Many of the finest statues recovered here are displayed in the Antalya Museum.

**Practical notes**

- Allow around two hours.
- Visit early or late in summer; the site is open and sun-exposed.
- Combine Perge with Aspendos and Side for a full day of classical ruins along the coast.
- Check current opening times and ticket prices before you go.
