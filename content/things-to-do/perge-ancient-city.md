Perge was one of the most important cities of ancient Pamphylia and is one of the most rewarding sites near Antalya. Roughly 15 kilometres east of the city, it is easy to reach by bus, taxi or as part of a tour.

The Hellenistic gate and its twin towers mark the entrance to the old city. Beyond that, a long colonnaded street runs through the centre, with a water channel running down the middle. The stadium, with seating for many thousands of spectators, is among the best-preserved in Anatolia, and the agora is another feature of the city. The ancient theatre is temporarily closed for restoration; do not plan to enter it. Many of the finest statues recovered here are displayed in the Antalya Museum.

## Current access and opening hours

**Checked on 10 November 2026:** Perge archaeological site is listed as open daily, **08:00–17:00**, with the **ticket office closing at 16:30** (Antalya local time). This does not mean every monument is accessible: the **ancient theatre is temporarily closed until restoration work is completed**. The official notice gives no reopening date.

Check the [Ministry’s Perge listing](https://www.muze.gov.tr/muze-detay?DistId=PRG&SectionId=PRG01) and [the theatre closure notice](https://dosim.ktb.gov.tr/TR-374571/perge-orenyeri-antik-tiyatrosunun-gecici-sureyle-ziyarete-kapatilmasi-hakkinda.html) before travelling. Follow barriers and staff instructions, and confirm current tickets and any further restrictions.

**Practical notes**

- Allow around two hours.
- Visit early or late in summer; the site is open and sun-exposed.
- Combine Perge with Aspendos and Side for a full day of classical ruins along the coast.
- Check current opening times and ticket prices before you go.
