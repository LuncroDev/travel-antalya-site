Kaputaş Beach is the small turquoise cove on the coast road between Kaş and Kalkan, the one you have probably seen on a poster. You see it from above first: a strip of pale sand between cliffs, where a narrow gorge meets the sea. Then you walk down. It's a swim-and-photo stop rather than a full day out, and it pairs well with [Patara](/blog/patara-beach-and-ancient-city/), [Xanthos](/things-to-do/xanthos-ancient-city/) or a night in Kaş.

## What it is

Antalya Metropolitan Municipality's beach guide places Kaputaş on the coast road, 20 km from Kaş and 7 km from Kalkan, in a bay ringed by steep cliffs of the same name. It describes golden sand and turquoise water. The municipality's tourism portal also mentions near-white pebbles, calls it Canyon Mouth Beach, and says spring water from the mountains keeps the sea fairly cool.

GoTürkiye, the official tourism site, describes a narrow canyon that carries on underwater, and its beach site calls Kaputaş narrow, set in a V-shaped gorge. No official page gives the beach's length, but it is small and fills up fast.

## Highlights

- **The view from above.** The portal recommends the viewpoint over the beach for photos.
- **The water.** Clear and turquoise, says GoTürkiye, with varied depths and rock formations for snorkelling and diving.
- **Boats in the bay.** The municipality says Kaputaş is a favourite stop for blue-cruise boats, so you may share it with a gulet. Our [Kekova boat trips guide](/blog/kekova-boat-trips/) covers trips from Kaş.
- **Its reputation.** Kaputaş came 29th on the 2026 World's 50 Best Beaches list (Euronews, 29 April) and 9th on European Best Destinations' 2026 list of Europe's best beaches (Time Out, 28 May).

## How to get there

- **By car:** GoTürkiye puts it about 20 minutes' drive from Kaş and 15 minutes from Kalkan. The road is the D400 coast highway. For the long drive from the city, see our [Antalya to Kaş day trip](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/).
- **By bus or taxi:** GoTürkiye says you can take a local bus or a taxi from nearby towns, but we found no official timetable. The municipality says minibuses leave Kaş bus station for Patara every 20 minutes in summer. Patara lies further west on the same road, so ask the driver whether they will drop you at Kaputaş.
- **The steps:** You reach the sand by a staircase from the roadside. The municipality and its portal count 187 steps; Wikipedia says 170, and a 2017 Türkiye Gazetesi report said 193. Whatever the number, the climb back up in the heat is the hard part.
- **Parking:** No official page describes it. Wikipedia mentions limited roadside spaces, and the 2017 report described cars parked along about a kilometre of road. This is the main coast highway, so pull fully off the carriageway, never stop on the road for a photo, and keep to the edge when walking.

## Best time to go

GoTürkiye recommends the warm summer months. Expect crowds then: in an Anadolu Agency report from the Kurban Bayramı holiday (Habertürk, 30 May 2026), a visitor called the beach very crowded. In July and August, come early or late in the day. The spring water can make the sea feel cooler than elsewhere; our [beach weather guide](/blog/antalya-beach-weather-which-months-are-best-for-sea-sun/) has sea temperatures by month.

## Access, fees and facilities

- **Entry:** free, according to the municipality's tourism portal.
- **Sunbeds, food and toilets:** no official page lists current facilities, sunbed prices, opening hours or lifeguard cover. GoTürkiye mentions cafés, restaurants and bars near the beach, and Wikipedia lists toilets, showers, a café-bar and changing cabins without citing a source. Bring water and shade just in case.
- **Smoking:** DHA reported that Antalya's governor launched a smoke-free beach rule at Kaputaş on 4 June 2026. A province-wide circular, reported by DHA on 24 July, sets a 7,250 TL fine for individuals. We couldn't open the circular itself, so follow the signs.
- **Sea turtles:** Kaputaş isn't among the 21 nesting beaches closed at night in summer (list reported by Sözcü, 29 May 2026), but nearby Patara is. Our [sea turtle guide](/blog/sea-turtle-beaches-in-antalya/) explains the rules.

## Tips

- Wear shoes with grip for the steps, and carry plenty of water.
- Swim with care: the portal warns that the seabed drops off quickly and that there are currents further out.
- Wikipedia says you can walk into the gorge on the other side of the road from the beach, under the bridge, when no water is flowing. We found no official page on this, so stay out after rain.
- Want a quieter cove? The municipality lists Küçük Çakıl (also written Seyrek Çakıl) very close to Kaputaş, reached by a small road, with white pebbles and no businesses.
