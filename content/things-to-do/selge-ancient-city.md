Selge is a mountain-history excursion, not a quick stop between beach appointments. Its theatre makes a strong focal point for a visit, while the broader ruins reward travellers who want to spend time looking at an ancient city in its landscape.

## The main attraction

The [Ministry’s Culture Portal](https://www.kulturportali.gov.tr/turkiye/antalya/gezilecekyer/selge) places Selge at Altınkaya in Manavgat district, within Köprülü Canyon National Park. It describes the theatre as the best-preserved building, restored in the third century AD, and identifies remains of the city walls, acropolis, stadium, gymnasium, agora and temples.

That list is a guide to the archaeological remains, not a promise that every structure has a signed or accessible visitor route. Make the theatre your anchor, then assess the surroundings on arrival. Do not climb onto fragile masonry for photographs.

## Getting there

The official approach follows the Köprüçay north from the Taşağıl junction on the Antalya–Manavgat road, along a route the Culture Portal describes as 55 km of paved road. Its destination address is **Altınkaya Mahallesi, Manavgat**.

Plan this as a deliberate mountain journey. A route distance is not a reliable drive-time estimate, particularly if your day includes stops. Check current road and weather conditions, arrange the return journey in advance, and avoid leaving the descent until you are tired or short of daylight.

## What to check before leaving

The official page checked on **18 November 2026** does not provide a current ticket price or opening-hours table. We therefore do not describe Selge as free or open around the clock. Confirm access locally before making a special trip.

Take water, suitable walking shoes and a layer for changing conditions. Carry what you need rather than assuming a café, toilets, card-payment terminal or step-free trail will be available. Visitors with limited mobility should establish the exact walking route and surface beforehand.
