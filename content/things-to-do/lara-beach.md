Lara Beach is the long sandy shore east of Antalya, and the closest place to the city for sand rather than pebbles. The Ministry of Culture and Tourism's Antalya office puts it 12 km east of the centre and describes very fine sand backed by pine woods. Most of the coast here belongs to big resort hotels, but a stretch of public beach at the western end is free to enter and has held the Blue Flag for years. If you're staying in the centre or in [Konyaaltı](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/), this is the easy swap for a sand day.

## What it is

Coming from the city, you pass the cliffs where the [Lower Düden falls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/) drop into the sea. The metropolitan municipality's waterfalls booklet says the water falls about 40 m from the cliffs in the Lara area. GoTürkiye says the real sand beach starts east of there and runs for several kilometres, and that the first big stop is Lara Public Beach (Lara Halk Plajı). Beyond it, the hotel strip runs on towards Kundu, and those beach sections serve the hotels' own guests.

GoTürkiye calls the sand "darkish" with some small pebbles, so don't expect white sand. No official page gives the total length of the beach.

## Highlights

- **Free public beach.** TÜRÇEV, which runs the Blue Flag in Türkiye, lists *T.C. Kültür ve Turizm Bakanlığı Lara Halk Plajı 1* as a sand beach and says no entrance fee is charged for the day facilities. It has flown the Blue Flag in 2011–2017 and every year from 2019 to 2026.
- **Two more flagged sections.** TÜRÇEV also lists the metropolitan municipality's *Lazlo Beach* and *Red&White Beach*, each 200 m × 50 m, with the Blue Flag from 2019 to 2026. Lazlo offers sunbed and umbrella hire. A fourth entry, the municipality's *Lara Halk Plajı 2*, is marked cancelled.
- **Facilities.** TURAŞ, the ministry's company that runs the public beach, lists a restaurant, café, rest and play areas, toilets, showers, sports areas, prayer rooms and a children's playground, with room for 3,000 people. TÜRÇEV adds an accessible toilet, a ramp and accessible sunbeds.
- **Water quality.** TÜRÇEV's page publishes recent sea-water test results; the latest we saw was dated 20 October 2026.

## How to get there

TÜRÇEV gives Lara Halk Plajı 1 as 15 km (about 20 minutes) from the city centre and 10 km from the airport, so it's also a handy last swim before a flight. It says public transport serves the beach, but no official page we could open lists the bus lines; check routes in the AntalyaKart app and read our [AntalyaKart guide](/blog/antalyakart-guide/) before you go. TURAŞ's page says the beach has no car park of its own, though it also mentions camera-watched parking, so don't count on a space in summer.

## Best time to go

The Blue Flag bathing season runs **15 May to 30 September**, which is when services are fully staffed. June and September are the sweet spots: warm sea, fewer people than in July and August. Out of season the sand is still good for a walk, and our guide to [swimming in Antalya in winter](/blog/can-you-swim-in-antalya-in-winter-weather-sea-temperature-guide/) covers sea temperatures month by month.

## Tickets and opening

Entry to the public beach is free. No official sunbed prices or opening hours are published for Lara, and no page says when lifeguards are on duty, so ask at the beach. TURAŞ says no alcohol is sold on its beaches.

## Tips

- **Dogs:** under the Blue Flag rules, dogs and other pets aren't allowed on a flagged beach during the bathing season, except assistance dogs.
- **Smoking:** in summer 2026 news reports (Takvim, 4 June; DHA, 24 July) described a smoke-free beach rule under an Antalya governorship circular covering Lara, Kaputaş, Belek and Konyaaltı Beach Park, with separate smoking areas and fines. We couldn't open the circular itself, so follow the signs on the day.
- **Plans for change:** in November 2024 the metropolitan council voted to lease a large stretch of the Güzeloba coast for 30 years under a build-operate-transfer deal (reported by Cumhuriyet), so facilities may change.
- **Sand or pebbles:** bring water shoes if you're sensitive; GoTürkiye mentions small pebbles mixed in.

## What's nearby

- **Sandland:** the sand sculpture park sits at the Lara public beach end, at Güzeloba, Lara Caddesi No: 208/1. Its own site says it has run since 2006 on 7,000 m², uses 10,000 tonnes of river sand, and opens all year: 09:00–21:00 from 1 June to 31 October and 09:00–18:30 from 1 November to 31 May.
- **Cliff beaches:** Muratpaşa Municipality runs three small beaches at the foot of the Lara cliffs, Erenkuş, İnciraltı and Konserve Koyu, open 06:30–20:00.
- **Kundu:** further east in Aksu, the municipality opened its Huzur Plajı by the Kopak stream in May 2026, one of four public beaches it runs. See our [Aksu guide](/destinations/aksu/).
