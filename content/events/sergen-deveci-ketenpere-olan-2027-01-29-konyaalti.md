**Sergen Deveci** brings his stand-up show **Ketenpere / Olan** to Konyaaltı's **Nazım Hikmet** culture and congress centre on **Friday 29 January 2027 at 21:00**. It is the last date currently listed for the tour, and the show is in Turkish.

## Ketenpere / Olan: the show

Biletinial describes a set built around relationships. Deveci takes a familiar idea, how the duties we take on quietly earn us society's approval, and sets it against love, friendship and the attempt to live a sustainable life. The show runs **75 minutes** with no interval listed, and it is strictly **18+**. The organiser on every tour date is **Tight Five**.

The tour has 15 dates in 13 cities, from Istanbul and Ankara to Karabük, Bodrum and Mersin. Antalya gets a single night.

## Date and venue

This listing is for **2027-01-29**. The year is printed on Biletinial's session line and in its "Ocak 2027" heading, and the venue page for Nazım Hikmet repeats "29 Ocak 2027". The show plays the centre's **Tarık Akan Salonu**. The building is in Pınarbaşı, a short taxi ride inland from [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/) and the hotels of [Konyaaltı](/destinations/konyaalti/); Biletinial gives the address as 734. Sokak No. 36.

## Tickets

On 28 November 2026 seats started at **₺750**, rising to ₺950 and ₺1,150 for the better categories. Tickets are only valid for the printed date and time, unused tickets are not refunded or exchanged, and age ID is checked at the door.
