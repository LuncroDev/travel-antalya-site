The **U.S. Kids Golf Antalya Turkish Open 2027** is an international junior championship at **[Carya Golf Club](/golf/carya-golf-club/)** in Belek on **Saturday 30 and Sunday 31 January 2027**. It is for golfers aged 5 to 12, and the organiser calls it the fifth annual edition.

## The format

From 2027 the event is split by age. Players aged 5–12 play a two-day championship at Carya; teenagers aged 13–18 play a separate three-day International Teen Series event at National Golf Club, which has its own listing. Divisions start at Boys 6 & Under and Girls 8 & Under. The youngest groups (boys 6 & under, 7 and 8, girls 8 & under and 9–10) play nine holes; older children play 18. Ages are fixed on 15 January 2027.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2027-01-30 to 2027-01-31**. The location page's schedule starts with registration on Thursday 28 January, followed by practice rounds and a parent-and-child event before the competition days. No tee times are given on the pages we read. Carya is the Regnum Carya resort's 18-hole course in Kadriye, next to Belek, and Regnum says it is fully floodlit.

## Entering

Places go to players with Priority Status invitations or through an open application, first come, first served. On 19 November 2026 the fees were $165 for nine-hole divisions and $265 for 18-hole divisions. General registration closes on 26 January 2027.

One rule needs checking: the description page says families must buy an accommodation or external package to enter Regnum Carya, while the registration page says staying at the resort isn't required and only practice rounds need an external package.
