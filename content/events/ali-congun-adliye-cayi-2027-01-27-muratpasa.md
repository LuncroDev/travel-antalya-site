**Ali Congun: Adliye Çayı** is the new stand-up show from Turkish comedian **Ali Congun**. The Ali Congun Adliye Çayı tour stops at **Türkan Şoray Kültür Merkezi** in Muratpaşa for one night, **Wednesday 27 January 2027 at 20:30**. The show is in Turkish.

## Ali Congun's Adliye Çayı stand-up

The title means "Courthouse Tea". Biletinial sums the show up as topical, candid and unfiltered, and notes that Congun's earlier shows, *Şehir Eliti* and *Aşk Acısı*, pulled big crowds. It runs as a single act of **75 minutes** and is for **ages 16 and up**. Congun is the only name in the credits.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2027-01-27**. Biletinial's event page puts the Antalya session under "January 2027" as a Wednesday at 20:30, and its Antalya theatre list shows "27 Ocak 2027". The tour covers 13 dates in 11 cities between 15 December 2026 and 6 February 2027; Antalya falls between Istanbul on 23 January and Konya on 28 January. The hall is at Çınarlı Caddesi No. 7, Yeşilbahçe, in [Muratpaşa](/destinations/muratpasa/). The night before, the same stage hosts the comedy [Ölünce Ara](/events/olunce-ara-2027-01-26-muratpasa/).

## Tickets and rules

On 24 November 2026 Biletinial's event page started at 1,120 TL, with Category 2 at 1,344 TL, Category 1 at 1,568 TL and a protocol price of 1,848 TL. Its venue page and Antalya list said "from ₺1000" instead, so check the total at checkout. A 20% "Kaçmaz Cuma" discount was showing until 23:59 on 30 November 2026. The rules:

- Tickets not used on the day and time are void, with no refund or exchange.
- If you can't go, contact Biletinial at least 48 hours before the start.
- ID is checked for the 16+ limit and for discounted or student tickets.
- Biletinial won't guarantee entry if you arrive late.
