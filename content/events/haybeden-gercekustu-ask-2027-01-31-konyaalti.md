**Haybeden Gerçeküstü Aşk** is a two-person Turkish comedy adapted from Yılmaz Erdoğan's book *Haybeden Gerçeküstü Konuşmalar*. It plays the **Konyaaltı Belediyesi Nazım Hikmet Fuar ve Kongre Merkezi** on **Sunday 31 January 2027**. The play is in Turkish.

## The play

Biletinial describes a man and a woman whose relationship we follow from its first moment to its end, with humour and tragicomic turns that mirror modern love — not one couple's story, it says, but every relationship's. **Selen Adıgüzel** and **Yusuf Kenan Adıgüzel** play the pair. Biletinial credits Nail Ercah as director (with İlknur İnaç assisting) and Kemal Yasin Başağaç as producer; Biletix's page for the same KYB Medya production names Cengiz Sezgin as director, so the credits may differ on the night. It runs **90 minutes in two acts** and is for ages 13 and up. Don't confuse it with a Tiyatro Punta show of the same name that also sells on Biletinial.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2027-01-31**. Biletinial's venue page and its Antalya theatre list both give the date with the year, but no page shows a start time, so check it when you book. The hall is at Pınarbaşı, 734. Sokak No. 36, in [Konyaaltı](/destinations/konyaalti/). Earlier in January the same stage has [Hastalık Hastası](/events/hastalik-hastasi-2027-01-09-konyaalti/) on the 9th and the children's musical [Çizmeli Kedi](/events/cizmeli-kedi-muzikali-2027-01-24-konyaalti/) on the 24th; *Yaramazlar Sirki*, a children's show, is listed for 31 January too.

## Tickets and rules

On 20 November 2026 Biletinial listed tickets from ₺920. Its rules:

- Tickets not used on the day and time are void, with no refund or exchange.
- If you can't go for reasons other than force majeure or cancellation, contact Biletinial at least 48 hours before the start.
- Bring ID: it is checked for the 13+ limit and for discounted tickets.
- The organiser may change your seat, and Biletinial won't guarantee entry to latecomers.
