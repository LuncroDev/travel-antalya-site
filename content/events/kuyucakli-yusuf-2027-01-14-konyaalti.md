**Kuyucaklı Yusuf**, a stage version of Sabahattin Ali's novel, comes to the **Konyaaltı Belediyesi Nazım Hikmet Fuar ve Kongre Merkezi** on **Thursday 14 January 2027 at 20:00**. Sirius Entertainment produces the tour, which visits 27 cities and districts between late November 2026 and February 2027.

## Kuyucaklı Yusuf: the play

Biletinial describes it as a drama about injustice, love, loneliness and the search for justice in rural Anatolia. Sabahattin Ali's novel is widely read in Türkiye, so many in the audience will know the story.

**Atakan Yılmaz** adapted and directed it. Biletinial lists **Yusuf Arslan** as the only performer, with sets and costumes by Nur Koyuncu and lighting by Lev Lebovicky.

## Date, venue and running time

The hall is in Pınarbaşı, Konyaaltı, a short taxi ride from the beach hotels. The show runs **70 minutes** and is for ages **10 and up**. It's performed in Turkish, with no surtitles mentioned; our guide to [theatre and comedy for non-Turkish speakers](/blog/antalya-theatre-comedy-non-turkish-speakers/) helps you decide.
