Composer and pianist **Kirill Richter** brings his album *Towards the Beloved City* to the **Aspendos hall of the Atatürk Kültür Merkezi (AKM)** on **Sunday 14 March 2027 at 20:00**.

## The concert

Biletix describes Richter as one of the most prominent names in today's neoclassical scene, a style that draws on classical piano writing as well as film and ambient music. The Antalya date presents his new album, which the ticket page says was released at the end of 2025 on Warner Classics. The page does not list other musicians or a set list, so expect the album to anchor the evening rather than a fixed programme.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2027-03-14 at 20:00**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). The ticket page asks you to be at the entrance at least 30 minutes before the start. AKM's address is Sakıp Sabancı Bulvarı No. 7 in Meltem, west of the old town. The same hall hosts other concerts this season, including [Gökhan Özen](/events/gokhan-ozen-live-in-concert-2026-11-14-muratpasa/) in November. Our [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/) covers the area.

## Tickets and practical planning

Tickets were on sale when we checked; see the ticket page for current prices. The venue rules are strict: you must stay in your assigned seat, latecomers are not let in once the concert has begun, and food, drink and cameras are not allowed. Tickets are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged, and the organiser says the venue and time may change, so check the page again before the day.
