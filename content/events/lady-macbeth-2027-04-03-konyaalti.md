**Lady Macbeth** is a one-woman drama built on Shakespeare's *Macbeth*, and it comes to the **Konyaaltı Belediyesi Nazım Hikmet Fuar ve Kongre Merkezi** on **Saturday 3 April 2027 at 20:00**. **Özge Durmuşcan** adapted it, directs it and plays it alone. Prömiyer Sanat organises the Antalya date.

## Lady Macbeth: the play

Biletinial's summary turns the familiar story around. Instead of following Macbeth's climb to the throne, the show stays with his wife: the ambition, the push for power and the cost that comes with the crown. It mixes the world of Shakespeare's play with present-day power games. The tagline asks whether only kings carry the weight of the crown ("Tacın ağırlığını sadece krallar mı taşır?").

The listing is in Turkish and mentions no surtitles, so expect a Turkish-language evening. If you know the play well, a solo version is easier to follow than most. Our guide to [theatre and comedy for non-Turkish speakers](/blog/antalya-theatre-comedy-non-turkish-speakers/) helps you choose.

## Cast and credits

Durmuşcan is the only performer. Biletinial credits Esin Doğan as acting coach and Fulya Selvi as assistant director, with lighting and sound by Berker Selvi, make-up and hair by Azra Zehra Kalfa, and costumes by Hülya Sağlık.

## The date and venue

The hall is at 734. Sokak No. 36, Pınarbaşı, Konyaaltı. Neither seller gives a running time or an age limit, so plan on a 20:00 start with no fixed finish. Biletix asks you to arrive 30 minutes early and warns that latecomers may lose their seats.
