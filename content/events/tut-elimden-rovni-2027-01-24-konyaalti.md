**Tut Elimden Rovni** ("Hold My Hand, Rovni") is a stage version of a play by **Aziz Nesin**, one of Türkiye's best-known satirists. F Prodüksiyon brings it to the **Konyaaltı Belediyesi Nazım Hikmet Fuar ve Kongre Merkezi** on **Sunday 24 January 2027 at 20:00**.

## Tut Elimden Rovni: the play

Biletinial lists it as a tragedy. Two acrobats, Rovni and Melâ, have performed the same circus act together for years. Behind the routine lie buried fears, old resentments, jealousy and a reckoning with the past, told as a story of love, loneliness and passion.

**Fatih Çağırtekin** directs. **Erdinç Kılıç** and **Ece Yaşar** play the leads, and Yaşar also choreographs. Seven more performers fill out the circus: Asel Dide Dağ, Bihter Dülger, Ekinsu Karaata, Mert Yılmaz, Kays Aykut, Melis Zeynep Atılgan and Özkan Özipek.
