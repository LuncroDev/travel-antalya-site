**Bir Başyapıt: Dvořák** ("A Masterpiece: Dvořák") opens the second International Antalya Chamber Music Festival at **Re22 Kültür Sanat** in Kaleiçi on **Saturday 5 December 2026 at 20:00**. One work fills the evening: **Antonín Dvořák's Piano Quintet No. 2 in A major, Op. 81**.

## The players

Biletinial lists five musicians:

- **Veriko Tchumburidze** and **Can Özhan**, violins
- **Sevinç Duran Yalaz**, viola
- **Pelin Baysal Ateşok**, cello
- **Kandemir Basmacıoğlu**, piano

The ticket page introduces Tchumburidze as a first-prize winner of the International Henryk Wieniawski Violin Competition. Özhan is the festival's artistic director. The page sells the quintet on its singing melodies and the Czech dance rhythms beneath them, which makes it a friendly first chamber concert if you're new to the form.

The concert is for **ages 8 and up**. Biletinial gives no running time.

## The festival

Biletinial's festival pass page describes five concerts plus masterclasses in Kaleiçi from **5 December 2026 to 21 January 2027**, organised by Re22 Kültür Sanat. The pass covers all five concerts but not the masterclasses. Next comes [Romantizmin İki Ustası](/events/romantizmin-iki-ustasi-2026-12-22-muratpasa/), a Mendelssohn and Brahms night on 22 December. January brings [Renkler ve Yansımalar](/events/renkler-ve-yansimalar-2027-01-18-muratpasa/) and the closing [Floransa Anıları](/events/floransa-anilari-2027-01-21-muratpasa/).

## The venue and tickets

Re22 Kültür Sanat Konser Salonu is at Paşa Cami Sokak No. 22, in the Tuzcular quarter of Kaleiçi, Muratpaşa.
