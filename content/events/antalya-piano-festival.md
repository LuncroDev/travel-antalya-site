The Antalya Piano Festival is one of Turkey's best-known music events. Programmes mix classical, jazz and contemporary music, with established pianists alongside emerging young players.

According to the festival's listing, the 2026 edition runs from 26 November to 12 December in the Konyaaltı district and admission is free. Exact venues and the daily programme are published by the organisers, so check the official website before you plan. Late November is a quiet, mild time to visit Antalya, which makes the festival a good reason for an off-season trip.
