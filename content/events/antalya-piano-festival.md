The **26th International Antalya Piano Festival** runs from **Friday 27 November to Sunday 13 December 2026**, with eleven concerts at **20:30** in the Aspendos Hall of Antalya's Atatürk Cultural Centre (AKM).

## The programme

- **27 November:** Anna Malikova, opening concert — Chopin & Schumann
- **28 November:** Duo Fidelis — "Love and Death"
- **29 November:** Hugues Leclère — "Vive la France"
- **1 December:** Piano Next — three young pianists
- **3 December:** Bosphorus Trio — "Bosphorus D'light"
- **4 December:** Can Saraç — "The Art of Virtuosity"
- **5 December:** Çağdaş Özkan — "Anatolian Piano"
- **7 December:** İsmail Baha Sürelsan Conservatory — seven young pianists
- **8 December:** Salih Can Gevrek — "Concertos Without Orchestra"
- **10 December:** Burak Çebi — "The Sparkle of Joy on the Keys"
- **13 December:** Szymon Nehring with conductor Michał Nesterowicz and the Antalya State Symphony Orchestra, closing concert
