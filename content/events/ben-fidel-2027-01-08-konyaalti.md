**Ben Fidel** ("I, Fidel" or "I Am Fidel") is a one-act drama about Fidel Castro, and it comes to the **Konyaaltı Belediyesi Nazım Hikmet Fuar ve Kongre Merkezi** on **Friday 8 January 2027 at 20:00**. Tiyatro Kavuk organises the Antalya date, and **Tolga Pancaroğlu** is the only actor on the cast list.

## Ben Fidel: the play

The title puts Castro in the first person, and the show builds on that. Biletinial's summary sets his life against the Cuban revolution and the island's long struggle for independence. It paints him as the "Maximo" Leader who would not trade away Cuba's sovereignty, however hard imperial powers squeezed. Then it asks how Cuba managed it, and promises the answer from Fidel himself.

Onur Erbilen wrote and directs it, with dramaturgy by Ozan Çobanoğlu and lighting design by Abdullah Akbaş. Pancaroğlu also produces. The listing is in Turkish and mentions no surtitles, so expect a Turkish-language evening. Our guide to [theatre and comedy for non-Turkish speakers](/blog/antalya-theatre-comedy-non-turkish-speakers/) can help you decide.

## The date and venue

Biletinial gives a running time of **80 minutes** in a single act and an age limit of **12+**. From a 20:00 start, expect to be out at about 21:20. The hall is at 734. Sokak No. 36, Pınarbaşı, Konyaaltı. Antalya falls mid-tour: Istanbul in November and December, İzmir the next night, Adana in late February.

For a second night out, [Hastalık Hastası](/events/hastalik-hastasi-2027-01-09-konyaalti/) plays the same hall on 9 January.

## Tickets and rules

On 24 November 2026, Biletinial's event page priced Antalya tickets from ₺1,120, while its venue list said ₺1,000. The rules:
