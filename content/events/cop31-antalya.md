COP31 is scheduled in Antalya from 9 to 20 November 2026. This is a major international conference, not an open-admission visitor attraction. Consult UNFCCC participant information for accreditation and access rules.

Visitors travelling through Antalya during the conference should allow flexibility in their plans and check transport arrangements with their accommodation.

## Separate city programme

The municipality also publishes a [COP31 city-events programme](/events/cop31-city-events-2026/) for 9–20 November, including performances at Hıdırlık and Üç Kapılar. These city activities are distinct from conference admission; confirm the individual activity's entry conditions rather than assuming conference accreditation or unrestricted access applies.
