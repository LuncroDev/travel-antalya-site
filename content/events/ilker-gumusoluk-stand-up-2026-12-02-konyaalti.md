Stand-up comedian **İlker Gümüşoluk** plays the **Konyaaltı Belediyesi Nazım Hikmet Fuar ve Kongre Merkezi** on **Wednesday 2 December 2026 at 20:30**. The show is in Turkish.

## The show

This is a one-man set. Biletinial says it ranges over stories and small observations from daily life, with most of the material about life as a couple. Both ticket sellers say he has done more than 1,000 shows around Türkiye over 15 years. Many fans first found him through his YouTube series *Apartman Sohbetleri* and his Instagram sketches, and Biletinial's bio says he also makes special projects for YouTube as one of its "Change Ambassadors" (Değişim Elçisi). Antalya is one stop on a tour: Biletix lists İzmir on 18 November and Istanbul on 25 November before it, and Adana on 15 December after. It suits fluent Turkish speakers best.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2026-12-02 at 20:30**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). Biletinial gives a running time of **105 minutes**, so an on-time start means you are out at about 22:15. Biletinial lists the venue at 734. Sokak No. 36 in Pınarbaşı. The same hall stages Reverans Sanat's [Bir Evlenme Teklifi](/events/bir-evlenme-teklifi-2026-12-06-konyaalti/) on 6 December and [Hamlet](/events/hamlet-2026-12-24-konyaalti/) on 24 December. Our [Konyaaltı district guide](/destinations/konyaalti/) covers the area.

## Age limit, tickets and rules

The sellers disagree: Biletix says children under 18 are not admitted, Biletinial lists the show as 16+. If you plan to bring a teenager, ask the seller first or go by the stricter rule.

On 14 November 2026 Biletix listed tickets from 1,100 TL, and Biletinial also sells them; prices change, so check before you buy. Biletix's rules:

- Be at the venue at least 30 minutes before the start.
- Sit in your assigned seat. Tickets, including those on a phone, are checked at the door.
- Tickets admit one person, and lost tickets are not reissued.
- No professional audio or video recording equipment.
- No refunds or exchanges. Student tickets need a valid student ID.
