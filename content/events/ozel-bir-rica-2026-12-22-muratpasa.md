Sevinç Erbulak, Tayanç Ayaydın, Emel Çölgeçen and Kaya Akkaya star in **Özel Bir Rica** ("A Special Request") at the **Türkan Şoray Kültür Merkezi** on **Tuesday 22 December 2026**. The play is in Turkish, and the ticket sites disagree on the start time (see below).

## The play

This is Poetika Yapım's Turkish staging of **Die Niere**, a comedy by Stefan Vögel, and both Biletix and iTicket bill it as the play's Turkish premiere. Erce Kardaş translated it, Gizem Ertürk and Serkan Üstüner adapted it, and Üstüner directs. Two couples (Zeynep, Cem, Figen and Gökhan) sit down to a celebration dinner, and one guest's unexpected request knocks the evening sideways. From there the play puts marriages and friendships to the test and asks how far love and loyalty really stretch. It suits fluent Turkish speakers best.

This listing is for **2026-12-22**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). Biletix's event and venue pages both say **20:30**; iTicket's event page says **20:00**. On 12 November 2026 iTicket listed four categories from 1,413 TL to 2,414 TL, fees included, and Biletix started at 1,413.50 TL; prices change, so check before you buy. iTicket and Biletinial set an age limit of **12+** and may ask for ID.
