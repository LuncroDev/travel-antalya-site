**Rumba** (*Румба*) is a Russian-language drama staged by the **Otrazheniya** ("Reflections") theatre company, listed in Turkish as Yansımalar, at **Kemer Belediyesi Kültür Salonu** on **Saturday 12 December 2026 at 19:00**. It is played in Russian with Turkish subtitles.

## The play

The company describes Rumba as a story about a meeting you can't prepare for. A past comes back, a memory won't let go, and some feelings outlast time. It all plays out inside one family, where words often carry more weight than they seem to. The listing promises a mix of pain, irony and warmth, and says this is not a loud drama but an honest conversation with the audience. The writer, director and cast aren't named on the ticket page, and no English subtitles are listed, so it suits Russian speakers best.
