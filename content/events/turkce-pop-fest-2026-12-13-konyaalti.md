The ticket page lists four acts: the singers **Çelik**, **Reyhan Karaca** and **Mansur Ark**, and **DJ Hakan Küfündür**. A fifth name, **Ümit Sayın**, appears in the event title but not in the artist list, so treat his appearance as unconfirmed until the organiser says more. Biletix gives no description, running order or set times. Expect a night of Turkish-language pop rather than a single headline concert.
