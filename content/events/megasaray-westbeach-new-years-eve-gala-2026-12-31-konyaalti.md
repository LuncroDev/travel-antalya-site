**Megasaray Westbeach Antalya** holds a seated **New Year's Eve gala** on **Thursday 31 December 2026** at its beach hotel in Konyaaltı. The headline act is singer **Erkan Güleryüz with his orchestra**, and outside guests can book a gala-only table without staying at the hotel.

## The programme

The evening starts with a welcome cocktail in the foyer, with a small classical ensemble. Then comes the gala dinner in the hotel's hall, which has a stage and a 40 m² dance floor. The hotel's line-up reads "Erkan Güleryüz, Jale and Orchestra, Mezdeke show and DJ", plus an oriental show. A late-night buffet runs from midnight to 02:30 at the Grill restaurant.

The hotel publishes no start time for the gala, so ask when you book. Children have their own programme from 19:00 to 23:30 in the Mini Club, with bingo, board games, a balloon show, a magic show and a film.

## Tables and prices

Prices are per person for the gala only, as shown on 26 November 2026:

- **Front row** (9 tables closest to the stage): 15,000 TL
- **Stage-side** (10 tables behind the front row): 13,000 TL
- **Hall** (61 tables): 10,000 TL

Every table seats ten. The hotel says prices rise in stages as tables sell, and it does not say whether drinks are included. A stay package with the gala and the 1 January brunch is priced on request.

## The venue

Megasaray Westbeach stands on Konyaaltı's seafront road; our [hotel profile](/hotels/megasaray-westbeach-antalya/) has the details. If you stay over, the brunch on 1 January runs from 07:00 to 14:30.
