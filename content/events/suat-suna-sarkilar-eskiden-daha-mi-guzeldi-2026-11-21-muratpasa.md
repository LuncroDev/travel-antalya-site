Pop singer **Suat Suna** brings his one-man music show **Şarkılar Eskiden Daha mı Güzeldi?** ("Were Songs Better in the Old Days?") to the **Türkan Şoray Kültür Merkezi** on **Saturday 21 November 2026 at 20:30**. The songs and the stories between them are in Turkish.

## The show

Biletix's artist page describes Suna as a singer, songwriter and composer who made his name in the 1990s with hits such as "Ansızın Çektin Gittin", "Aramızda Uçurumlar" and "Hadi Git". Here he looks wider than his own records. Biletinial bills the evening as a solo musical show built on his 35 years on stage, working through Turkish pop from the 1970s to today. The names run from Sezen Aksu and Kayahan to Erol Evgin, Zülfü Livaneli, Tanju Okan, Levent Yüksel, Barış Manço and Cem Karaca. Between songs Suna shares his own memories and backstage stories, and he plays several instruments live. Neither ticket site names a band or a set list. If you grew up on Turkish radio, expect to sing along.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2026-11-21 at 20:30**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). Biletinial gives a running time of **80 minutes**, so an on-time start means you are out at about 21:50. Biletinial lists the venue at Çınarlı Caddesi No. 7 in Yeşilbahçe. The same stage hosts [Düş Payı](/events/dus-payi-2026-11-25-muratpasa/) on 25 November and [İclal Aydın: Ama Değil, Rağmen](/events/iclal-aydin-ama-degil-ragmen-2026-11-27-muratpasa/) on 27 November. Our [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/) covers the area.

## Tickets and practical planning

On 13 November 2026 Biletix listed tickets from 1,100 TL, and Biletinial also sells them; prices change, so check before you buy. Neither seller publishes an age limit, but Biletinial checks ID at the door for age-limited and discounted tickets. Biletinial's rules are short:

- Tickets not used on the day and time are void and cannot be refunded or changed.
- The organiser may move you to a different seat.
- Biletinial takes no responsibility for getting latecomers in, so arrive early.
