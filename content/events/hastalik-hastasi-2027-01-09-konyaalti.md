Prömiyer Sanat brings **Hastalık Hastası**, a Turkish adaptation of Molière's *The Imaginary Invalid*, to the **Konyaaltı Belediyesi Nazım Hikmet Fuar ve Kongre Merkezi** on **Saturday 9 January 2027 at 20:00**. The play is in Turkish.

## The play

Molière's last comedy is about a man so convinced he is ill that his fear runs his household. Biletinial describes this version as a tragicomedy that steps outside the classic telling: a man clinging to life in the shadow of paranoia and deceit, and a health obsession that turns his relationships upside down. It promises to make the audience laugh and think. Fulya Selvi adapted and directs it. Biletinial's credits list Kenan Pekdiker, Fulya Selvi, Özge Durmuşcan and Barış Ozan Doğru in the cast, but its description names Defne Üçkardeş instead of Barış Ozan Doğru, so the line-up may vary. Özge Durmuşcan is also assistant director, with lighting by Berker Selvi and costumes by Seda Kürtüloğlu. The tour takes in Istanbul's Asian side, Elazığ and Malatya as well as Antalya.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2027-01-09 at 20:00**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). Biletinial gives a running time of **70 minutes**, so expect to be out by about 21:15. The time comes from Biletix; Biletinial's pages show the date but not the time. The venue is at 734. Sokak No. 36, Pınarbaşı, Konyaaltı. Both sellers also list *Lady Macbeth*, a one-woman show by Özge Durmuşcan, at the same hall on 3 April 2027. Before then the hall hosts [Hamlet](/events/hamlet-2026-12-24-konyaalti/) on 24 December and [Aşk Listesi](/events/ask-listesi-2026-12-23-konyaalti/) the night before. Our [Konyaaltı district guide](/destinations/konyaalti/) covers the area.

## Tickets and rules

On 15 November 2026, Biletix listed tickets from 1,100 TL and Biletinial from 1,000 TL; prices change, so check before you buy. No age limit is published. Biletinial's rules:

- Tickets not used on the day and time are void, with no refund or exchange of the price or service fee.
- If you can't go for reasons other than force majeure or cancellation, contact Biletinial at least 48 hours before the start.
- The organiser may change your seat.
- Bring ID if you bought a discounted ticket.
- Biletinial is not responsible for getting latecomers in.
