**Ahududu** ("Raspberry") is a Turkish black comedy adapted and directed by **Nedim Saban**, now on what Biletinial calls its farewell tour. It plays **Türkan Şoray Kültür Merkezi** in Muratpaşa on **Monday 4 January 2027 at 20:30**. The play is in Turkish.

## Ahududu: the play

Biletinial describes two women who believe they have found a sure cure for loneliness and keep making raspberry mixtures "for people's happiness", without seeing that they are playing with fire. The writer is credited as **Joseph Kesselring**, the American playwright best known for *Arsenic and Old Lace*, and the page says the show has played to full houses for ten seasons.

The cast is led by **Suna Keskin** and **Melek Baykal** as the sisters Müşfike and Mürşide, with **Nedim Saban** as Zeki. Cem Güler, Birol Engeler, Özgür Yetkinoğlu, Batuhan Sezer, Müge Kement, Murat Turhan and Murat Ergür complete the company. It runs **130 minutes**, long for a touring comedy, and is for **ages 10 and up**.
