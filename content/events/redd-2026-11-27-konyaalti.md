## The band

Redd is a Turkish rock band from Istanbul. It started in 1996 under the name Ten, founded by singer Doğan Duru and guitarist Berke Hatipoğlu, and its seventh studio album, *Yersiz Göksüz Zamanlar*, came out in 2019.
