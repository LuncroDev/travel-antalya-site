**Auto World Expo Antalya** runs from **Thursday 8 to Sunday 11 April 2027** at the **ANFAŞ Expo Center** in Aksu, east of Antalya city.

## What the fair covers

ANFAŞ lists the show as a fair for cars, motorcycles, servicing and finance. The organiser, AYT Fuarcılık, describes its product groups as cars and motorcycles, service, maintenance and repair, spare parts, accessories, technology, and vehicle credit. Its site notes that the fair is organised under Law No. 5174 under the oversight of the Union of Chambers and Commodity Exchanges of Türkiye (TOBB). No edition number or exhibitor list has been published yet.

## The dates and venue

This listing covers **2027-04-08 to 2027-04-11**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). ANFAŞ gives its address as Soğucaksu, 25001 Sokak No. 1/4, Aksu. The centre is on the airport side of the city, so it is easy to combine with a stay in [Lara](/hotels/?area=Lara%20%26%20Kundu) or Belek. Our [Aksu district guide](/destinations/aksu/) covers the area, including [Perge Ancient City](/things-to-do/perge-ancient-city/), a short drive away.
