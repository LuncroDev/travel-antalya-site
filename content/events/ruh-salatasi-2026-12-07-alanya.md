The comedy **Ruh Salatası** ("soul salad") comes to **Alanya Kültür Merkezi** on **Monday 7 December 2026 at 20:00**. The play is in Turkish.

## The play

The set-up is pure farce. A mafia boss, his girlfriend and two other people end up sharing a house in the mountains. A mysterious cassette tape swaps their souls, and the chaos gets worse when a rival turns up. **İrfan Kangı** wrote and directs it, and the cast listed by the Oggusto event guide is **Ferit Aktuğ, Giray Mert Özdenoğlu, Zeynep Beşerler, Bestemsu Özdemir and Mete Deran**. There are no subtitles listed, so it suits Turkish speakers best.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2026-12-07 at 20:00**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). No running time or end time is published. Alanya Kültür Merkezi is the town's main cultural centre; iTicket gives a map pin but no street address, so check the location on the ticket page before you set off. If 7 December doesn't suit, the same play is listed at **Manavgat Atatürk Kültür Merkezi on 8 December 2026**, closer to [Side](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/).

On 17 November 2026, iTicket sold one category at 1,090 TL, service fee included. Its FAQ says tickets for this event are non-refundable, so be sure of the date. No age limit is given.
