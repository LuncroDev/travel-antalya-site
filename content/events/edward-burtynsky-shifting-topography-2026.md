**Edward Burtynsky: Shifting Topography** is at **Akra Antalya’s Side Ballroom from 9 to 20 November 2026**, open **09:00–22:00** with **free admission**.

## What to see

The COP31 selection includes twelve photographs from the artist’s *Erosion* series and the 22-minute multimedia work *In the Wake of Progress*. Curated by Dr. Kumru Eren, it examines landscapes changed by human activity.

## Plan your visit

Follow the [organizer’s exhibition page](https://www.borusancontemporary.com/en/cop31-edward-burtynsky-shifting-topography_2563) for updates. Ask reception for the Side Ballroom on arrival at [Akra Antalya](/hotels/akra-hotel/). “Side” is the room name here, not an instruction to travel to Side town.
