The Golden Orange (Altın Portakal) Film Festival has taken place in Antalya since 1963 and is one of Turkey's most important film events. Press reports in June 2026 announced the 63rd edition for 24 to 31 October 2026, with competition sections and the Antalya Film Forum.

The festival was cancelled in 2023 after a dispute over a documentary, then returned. Venues and the screening schedule are announced by the organisers, so check the festival's official website for the current programme and ticket details.
