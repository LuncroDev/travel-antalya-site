**Cornelia Golf Club** in Belek runs its **Cornelia Pro-Am** from **Saturday 27 March to Saturday 3 April 2027**. It is a package event: you stay a week at the resort and play six rounds, ending with a prize-giving and gala night.

## The programme

Cornelia publishes this schedule for the week:

- **Sat 27 March:** arrival.
- **Sun 28 March:** first practice round, registration, professionals' briefing and welcome cocktail.
- **Mon 29 March:** second practice round.
- **Tue 30 March:** Texas scramble.
- **Wed 31 March and Thu 1 April:** pro-am days one and two.
- **Fri 2 April:** individual pro and amateur day, then the awards ceremony and gala night.
- **Sat 3 April:** departure.

The page doesn't say which holes are used or how the rounds are scored. Cornelia's course is a 27-hole Nick Faldo design; see our [Cornelia Golf Club profile](/golf/cornelia-golf-club/).

## Packages and prizes

As listed on 16 November 2026:

- **Amateur package:** €2,880 per person sharing a double room, €3,260 single. It covers 7 nights all-inclusive at Cornelia Diamond Golf Resort & Spa (Standard Garden View rooms), 6 rounds, range tokens, trolley and a shared buggy, a halfway snack, airport and course transfers, welcome gifts, the cocktail and the gala dinner.
- **Non-golfer package:** €1,380 sharing or €2,070 single, with the same stay, cocktail and gala dinner.

Staying at the resort is compulsory to play. Professionals play free if they bring three amateurs in their team. The winning professional receives €5,000, and Cornelia also promises professionals a guaranteed €1,500 prize. You register through a form on the tournament page; no deadline is given.

## Before you book

Cornelia's price page asks for a handicap certificate, with a maximum of 28 for men and 36 for ladies.
