**Ölünce Ara** ("Call Me When You're Dead") is a Turkish comedy starring **Pamela** and **Murat Ergür**. It plays **Türkan Şoray Kültür Merkezi** in Muratpaşa on **Tuesday 26 January 2027 at 20:30**. The play is in Turkish.

## The play

Biletinial describes a man whose partner catches him cheating. The play asks whether he can be forgiven and how she deals with the betrayal, and the page says the story carries on in the audience after the curtain. **Yunus Günçe** wrote it and **İrfan Kangı** directs; the production is by Cuk Fikir & Sufle Organizasyon. It runs **80 minutes** and is for **ages 15 and up**. The tour takes it to Osmaniye, Adana, Mersin, İzmir and Muğla in December 2026 and to Isparta on 27 January 2027, so Antalya has a single night.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2027-01-26**. Biletinial's event page shows the year in its session calendar, and its Antalya theatre list shows "26 Ocak 2027". The hall is at Çınarlı Caddesi No. 7, Yeşilbahçe, in [Muratpaşa](/destinations/muratpasa/). The next night, 27 January, the same venue has Ali Congun's stand-up show *Adliye Çayı*.

## Tickets and rules

On 21 November 2026 Biletinial sold four bands: 950 TL, 1,200 TL, 1,450 TL and VIP at 1,650 TL. Its rules:

- Tickets not used on the day and time are void, with no refund or exchange.
- For events linked to Anıl Medya, refunds are given only for health problems with a medical report.
- Bring ID: it is checked for the 15+ limit and for discounted tickets.
- Biletinial won't guarantee entry if you arrive late.
