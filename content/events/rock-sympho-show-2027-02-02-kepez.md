Prime Orchestra's **Rock Sympho Show** comes to Antalya on **2 February 2027 at 20:00**, at Mimar Sinan Kongre Merkezi. Antalya is the first stop on a short Türkiye tour. Biletix lists Ankara on 4 February, Bursa on 6 February and İzmir on 7 February.

## What the show is

Biletinial says the orchestra tours Türkiye every two years with its own rock programme, and calls this a brand-new programme. The ticket page promises rock arrangements, strong vocals and a light show. No setlist or running time is published, so don't count on a particular song.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2027-02-02 at 20:00**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). Biletinial lists the venue as Kepez Mimar Sinan Kongre Merkezi, in Kepez, the inland district north of the city centre, not in Kaleiçi. Plan a taxi or transfer for the ride home as well as the trip out. For the area, see our [Kepez district guide](/destinations/kepez/), which also covers the [Düden Waterfalls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/).

## Tickets and practical planning

See the ticket page for current prices. Biletinial lists an age limit of 3+, and you need ID at the door for age-restricted or discounted tickets. Biletinial's rules say unused tickets can't be refunded or changed.
