The Türkiye Tourism Promotion and Development Agency (TGA) lists the **Tourism and Cultural Heritage Day** for **Wednesday, 18 November 2026, 13:30–17:30 Antalya time**. Its programme places the opening and four panels in the COP31 **Green Zone**, not in the Blue Zone. The [17 November TÜROB invitation](https://www.turob.com/haberler/turizm-ve-kulturel-miras-gunu-programina-davet) gives the same hours.

## Programme in Antalya time (UTC+3)

- **13:30:** Opening.
- **13:45–14:30:** Türkiye’s model for more sustainable tourism.
- **14:45–15:30:** Investment in accommodation, infrastructure and innovation.
- **15:45–16:30:** Cultural heritage, food and sustainable tourism.
- **16:45–17:30:** How different parts of the travel industry can put sustainable tourism into practice.

## How to visit

The programme is at the **Nurol Holding Congress Centre in the COP31 Green Zone, D6, Ahmed Yesevi Halls 1, 2 and 3 (combined)**. Free Green Zone visitor registration is required. Use the official [registration page](https://cop31.tr/register-to-visit) and check your confirmation for the entry QR code. We could not verify live registration availability or room capacity. Check the [official programme calendar](https://cop31.tr/programme-calendar) for any last-minute change before travelling. Green Zone registration does not give Blue Zone access.

The [Governorship's venue map](https://www.gocop31.com/en/yesil-bolge) shows the site. For transport and conference access see our [COP31 overview](/events/cop31-antalya/) and [shuttle and tram guide](/blog/cop31-antalya-shuttles-trams-green-zone/).
