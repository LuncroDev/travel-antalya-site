Selçuk University lists a **Tourism and Cultural Heritage Day** at the COP31 Green Zone on **Wednesday, 18 November 2026, from 13:00 to 17:15** Antalya time. The provisional programme looks at how tourism destinations can respond to climate change. The university describes contributions from the tourism sector, researchers, public institutions and international organisations.

## How to visit

The programme is at the **Green Zone at Antalya EXPO Center in Aksu**, not at a city-centre hotel. Use the official [Green Zone registration page](https://cop31.tr/register-to-visit) and check your visitor account for the entry QR code. Registration is listed as free and required. We have not confirmed availability or separate session capacity, so check the latest [university programme notice](https://selcuk.edu.tr/birim/icerik/5337/cop31-turizm-ve-kulturel-miras-gunu-18-kasimda-antalyada-gerceklestirilecek-84859) before setting out. A Green Zone registration does not give Blue Zone access.

The [Governorship’s venue map](https://www.gocop31.com/en/yesil-bolge) lists approximate locations and walking routes from the gates. For transport and the distinction between conference and city access, see our [COP31 overview](/events/cop31-antalya/) and [shuttle and tram guide](/blog/cop31-antalya-shuttles-trams-green-zone/).
