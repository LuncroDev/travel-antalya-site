**Chris Potter Trio** plays **The 251 Soul**, the music stage inside the Akra Antalya hotel, on **Friday 27 and Saturday 28 November 2026**, starting at **21:00** each night.

## The show

Biletix describes Potter as one of modern jazz's most powerful and distinctive saxophonists, and the concert is part of the venue's **The 251 CORE** series. The tour agency lists the trio as Chris Potter on saxophone, Matt Brewer on bass and Kendrick Scott on drums. No set list or running time has been published.

## The date and venue

This listing covers **2026-11-27 and 2026-11-28**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). Doors open at 20:00 and the music starts at 21:00, according to the ticket page. The 251 Soul is inside [Akra Antalya](/hotels/akra-hotel/), which stands on Lara Caddesi in Şirinyalı, east of the old town. From [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/) it is a short taxi ride along the coast road past [Karaalioğlu Park](/things-to-do/karaalioglu-park/).

Akra Hotels says guests who stay at Akra Antalya on the night of 27 or 28 November can watch the concert free of charge. The offer needs a minimum one-night stay and places are limited, so ask the hotel when you book a room.

## Tickets and practical planning

Biletix showed both nights on sale when we checked; see the ticket page for current prices. The venue rules on the same page matter here: guests must be **18 or older**, seating is **unreserved** (first come, first served), advance table reservations are not possible, and you may be asked to share a table. Outside food and drink and professional recording equipment are not allowed. Arrive close to 20:00 if you want a good seat.
