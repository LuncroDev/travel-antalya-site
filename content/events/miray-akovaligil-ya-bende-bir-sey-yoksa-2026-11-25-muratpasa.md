Stand-up comedian **Miray Akovalıgil** brings her show **Ya Bende Bir Şey Yoksa?** to the **Aspendos hall of the Atatürk Kültür Merkezi (AKM)** on **Wednesday 25 November 2026 at 20:00**. The show is in Turkish.

## The show

The title asks, roughly, "What if there's nothing wrong with me?". Both ticket pages sum it up the same way: Akovalıgil takes the usual measures of success, in work, in love and in health, and questions them one by one. Neither Biletix nor Biletinial lists writing or directing credits or a support act. Antalya is one stop on a national tour; Biletix shows the same show in Denizli on 17 November and in Ankara the following night. Stand-up lives on spoken Turkish, so it suits fluent speakers best.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2026-11-25 at 20:00**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). Both ticket sites give a running time of about **90 minutes**. AKM's address is Sakıp Sabancı Bulvarı No. 7 in Meltem, inside the Atatürk Kültür Parkı. The same hall stages the play [Hadi Öldürsene Canikom](/events/hadi-oldursene-canikom-2026-11-21-muratpasa/) four nights earlier and a [Kirill Richter](/events/kirill-richter-2027-03-14-muratpasa/) piano concert in March. Our [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/) covers the area.

## Tickets and practical planning

Tickets were on sale on Biletix and Biletinial when we checked; see the ticket pages for current prices. Biletinial sets an age limit of **16+** and says ID is checked at the venue. Biletix publishes no age limit, so carry ID either way. Biletix's rules are strict:

- No one is let in once the show has started.
- Outside food and drink, cameras and audio recorders are not allowed.
- Tickets cannot be cancelled, refunded or changed, and lost tickets are not reissued.

Biletix also says the AKM box office opens on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00 and on event days, and takes cash only.
