The **17th Antalya Book Fair (Antalya Kitap Fuarı)** is announced for **1–10 October 2027**. Antalya Metropolitan Municipality published these dates after the previous edition, and DHA separately reported the announcement.

## What is confirmed

The date range and edition number are supported by the municipal announcement. These are announced future dates, not a guarantee that arrangements will remain unchanged. Recheck the organizer before booking travel specifically for the fair.

## Plan your visit when details are released

The sources reviewed do **not confirm the 2027 venue, daily opening hours, admission conditions or programme**. Author appearances, signings and any children's activities should be checked against the forthcoming 2027 schedule rather than assumed from a previous edition.

The municipality's article describes the **2026** event at Cam Piramit. That retrospective information is not being used here as confirmation of the **2027** location. Wait for event-specific venue details before choosing transport or nearby accommodation.
