**Çizmeli Kedi Müzikali** is a children's musical of *Puss in Boots* by **F Prodüksiyon**, at the **Konyaaltı Belediyesi Nazım Hikmet Fuar ve Kongre Merkezi** on **Sunday 24 January 2027**. It is in Turkish, runs about 60 minutes and is recommended for ages 3 and up.

## The show

The old fairy tale becomes a family musical comedy: a loyal friend, a pair of boots and a string of clever schemes, ending on the idea that real wealth is measured in kindness, not gold. Biletinial promises live songs, dances and colourful characters, and the credits include a puppet designer, Özgür Mehmet Sakallı. Fatih Çağırtekin directs and adapted the story with dramaturg Ceyhun Yağmurlar. Hamdi Akatay wrote the music, Lara Tülü did the choreography, and the team includes a pedagogue, Nurhayat Çağırtekin. The cast of twelve includes Buğse Karacan, Erdinç Kılıç, Miyaser Soypak and Tolga Ilık. The words are Turkish, but small children who don't speak it may still enjoy a story they know told through song.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2027-01-24**. Biletinial's venue page gives the date with the year but no start time, and the session times on the event page couldn't be read on 18 November 2026, so check the time when you book. Don't mix it up with *Çizmeli Kedi*, a different Puss in Boots production by Reverans at the same hall on 6 December. The venue is at 734. Sokak No. 36, Pınarbaşı, Konyaaltı.

The hall's January list has more for children: *Kırmızı Başlıklı Kız ve Yalancı Kurt* on 9 January (ages 2+) and *Yaramazlar Sirki* on 31 January 2027.
