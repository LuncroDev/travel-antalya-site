**Ezginin Günlüğü** plays **Holly Stone Antalya** in Kaleiçi on **Wednesday 9 December 2026 at 21:00**.

## Who they are

The band's official history says it was formed in Istanbul in 1982 and gave its first concert in January 1983. Its repertoire mixes traditional folk songs with its own music set to poems by Nazım Hikmet, Orhan Veli, Omar Khayyam and others. Its first studio album, *Seni Düşünmek*, came out in 1985.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2026-12-09 at 21:00**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). iTicket gives the address as Selçuk Mah., Uzun Çarşı Sk. No. 42, in the old town.
