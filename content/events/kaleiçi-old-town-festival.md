The International Kaleiçi Old Town Festival, organised by Antalya Muratpaşa Municipality since 2016, brings together representatives of historic cities from several continents to share experience in protecting and living in ancient places. According to the organisers, it is a free public event.

The 11th edition took place on 8 to 11 October 2026 around Kaleiçi, Antalya's old town, at the end of the tourism season. The festival's website carries the programme and past declarations; if you are planning for 2027, look for the next dates there.
