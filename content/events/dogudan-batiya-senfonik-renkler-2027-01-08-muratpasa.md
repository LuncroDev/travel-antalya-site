**Doğudan Batıya Senfonik Renkler** ("Symphonic Colours from East to West") is a concert by the **Antalya State Symphony Orchestra** (Antalya Devlet Senfoni Orkestrası) at the **Aspendos Hall of Antalya's Atatürk Cultural Centre (AKM)** on **Friday 8 January 2027 at 20:30**.

## The concert

Biletinial names **Fuad İbrahimov** as conductor and **Diyang Mei** as viola soloist. The organiser is the orchestra's own directorate, with DenizBank as main sponsor. The ticket page doesn't list the works yet, so the programme behind the title, a journey from east to west, is still to be announced. If you want to know what you'll hear, check the page again closer to the date. Our guide to the [Antalya State Symphony Orchestra's 2026–27 season](/blog/antalya-state-symphony-orchestra-2026-27/) explains how the season works.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2027-01-08**. The event page's detail line calls the day Thursday ("Perşembe"), but its own session list says Friday, which matches the calendar. The start time is 20:30. AKM is in the Meltem area of [Muratpaşa](/destinations/muratpasa/) and has several halls, so make sure you head for the Aspendos Salonu.

## Tickets and rules

On 21 November 2026 Biletinial showed a full price of ₺430.01 and a discounted price of ₺355.01, plus a starting price of ₺215.01 that it doesn't explain. Its rules:

- Tickets not used on the day and time are void, with no refund or exchange, and refunds can't be requested through Biletinial.
- Bring ID if your ticket is discounted or age-limited.
- Biletinial won't guarantee entry if you arrive late.
