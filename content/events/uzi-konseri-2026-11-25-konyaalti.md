**Uzi**, the Turkish rapper Utku Cihan Yalçınkaya, plays the **Konyaaltı Nazım Hikmet Congress Centre** on **Wednesday 25 November 2026 at 20:30**. It is a standing show.

## The show

iTicket's Turkish listing calls Uzi one of the most energetic names in Turkish rap and names songs including "Krvn", "Umrumda Değil", "Siz Sevmeyin", "Arasan Da" and "Gecenin İçine Gir". The seller's tour page says he is playing 12 concerts in Türkiye this autumn. Lyrics are in Turkish, so the night is best for fans who already know the songs.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2026-11-25 at 20:30**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). No door time or running time is given. The hall is at Pınarbaşı, 734. Sokak No. 36, in [Konyaaltı](/destinations/konyaalti/). One caution: the ticket page's web address still says "3 Kasım" (3 November), and an older line on iTicket's tour page placed the Antalya show at the open-air Açıkhava venue. The event page itself is clear about 25 November at Nazım Hikmet, but check your ticket.

## Tickets and entry

On 19 November 2026 iTicket sold two options, service fee included: general admission standing at 1,000 TL, and a two-person ticket at 900 TL. Tickets can't be refunded, transferred or reprinted. Expect an ID check and a bag search at the door; outside food and drink, professional cameras, selfie sticks and pets are not allowed, and the organiser films the show.
