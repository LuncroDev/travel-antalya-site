The **Staysure Legends Tour**, the professional tour for golfers aged 50 and over, holds its 2027 Qualifying School at **Gloria Golf Resort in Belek** from **Saturday 16 to Tuesday 19 January 2027**. Practice days are Thursday 14 and Friday 15 January. Last year's edition was also at Gloria, on 17–20 January 2026.

## The format

The tour describes a single stage of four rounds. The first two are played across Gloria's **Old and New Courses**, with room for up to 84 players on each course per day. After 36 holes the field is cut to the top 72 and ties. A second cut after the third round keeps only those within ten shots of fourth place.

The prize is a tour card. The **top four after 72 holes** earn Category 8 membership for the 2027 season, which the tour says gets them into its regular events with 60-player fields. If one of those places goes unused, it passes to the best-placed finisher outside the top four.

## Who can enter

- Male professionals who are 50 on or before 31 July 2027.
- Male amateurs who meet the same age rule and have a Handicap Index of no more than 1.4 when they enter.

Entry costs **£800**, through the European Tour Group Player Portal or the My Tour app, and closes on **Friday 8 January 2027 at midday UK time**. Places are limited. Players must stay at the official hotel or pay a daily resort fee; the terms and conditions give the details.

## Watching and the venue

The organiser publishes no spectator information, and Gloria's own pages don't mention the event.
