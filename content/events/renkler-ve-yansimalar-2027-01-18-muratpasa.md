**Renkler ve Yansımalar** ("Colours and Reflections") is a chamber concert at **Re22 Kültür Sanat** in Kaleiçi, Antalya's old town, on **Monday 18 January 2027 at 20:00**. It is the fourth of five concerts in the second International Antalya Chamber Music Festival.

## The concert

Three players share the stage:

- **Aslıhan And Say**, flute
- **Can Özhan**, violin
- **Müge Hendekli**, piano

Biletinial lists the programme by composer only: works by **Bonis, Doppler, Andriessen, Ibert, Kreisler and Martinů**, with no individual pieces named yet. The page doesn't give a running time. The concert is for **ages 8 and up**.

## The festival

Biletinial's festival pass page describes the **II. Uluslararası Antalya Oda Müziği Festivali** as five concerts plus masterclasses in Kaleiçi between **5 December 2026 and 21 January 2027**, under the artistic direction of violinist Can Özhan and organised by Re22 Kültür Sanat. The pass covers all five concerts but not the masterclasses. The festival closes three nights later with [Floransa Anıları](/events/floransa-anilari-2027-01-21-muratpasa/), Tchaikovsky's *Souvenir de Florence*.

## The venue and tickets

Re22 Kültür Sanat Konser Salonu is at Paşa Cami Sokak No. 22, in the Tuzcular quarter of Kaleiçi, Muratpaşa. The event page doesn't say whether seats are numbered. Biletinial showed a starting price of ₺1,232 on the event page (₺1,100 on the venue list).
