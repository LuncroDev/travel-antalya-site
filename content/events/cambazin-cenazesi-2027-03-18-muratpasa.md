**Cambazın Cenazesi** ("The Tightrope Walker's Funeral") is a Turkish comedy-drama by **Firuze Engin**, staged by Batı Performans Sanatları. It plays **Türkan Şoray Kültür Merkezi** in Muratpaşa on **Thursday 18 March 2027 at 20:00**. The play is in Turkish.

## Cambazın Cenazesi: the play

Biletinial bills it as a comedy and a drama at once. The death of a tightrope walker sets off the story, which looks at what we lose and what we gain in life "in an entertaining and touching way". The play is based on Engin's prize-winning script and is directed by **Eyüp Emre Uçaray**.

It is a two-hander, with **Ünal Yeter** and **Bülent Seyran** in the cast. The show runs **75 minutes** and is for **ages 11 and up**, so older children who follow Turkish can come too.

## Date and venue

This listing is for **2027-03-18**. The year appears on Biletinial's session line under "Mart 2027", and Biletix's venue page lists the same date and time. Antalya is the last stop of a six-date run that starts in December. The hall is in Yeşilbahçe, [Muratpaşa](/destinations/muratpasa/), a short taxi ride from [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/) and the Lara hotels.
