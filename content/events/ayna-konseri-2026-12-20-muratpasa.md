Turkish rock band **Ayna** plays the **Türkan Şoray Kültür Merkezi** in Yeşilbahçe on **Sunday 20 December 2026 at 20:00**. The songs are in Turkish.

## The band

Biletix describes Ayna as a rock band formed in the 1990s that mixes rock with Anatolian melodies and sings mostly about love and parting. Biletinial points to the songs most people know, "Ölünce Sevemezsem Seni", "Severek Ayrılanlar" and "Anlatmalıymış Meğer", and promises an energetic live show. Neither seller names the line-up or a set list. Antalya is one stop on a long winter run that Biletix groups as a Türkiye tour: Bursa, Istanbul, Ankara and six other cities come first in December, with more dates in Ankara, Eskişehir and Istanbul in early 2027. With a 20:00 start, it suits anyone who wants a Turkish rock night without a late club gig.

## The date and venue

This listing is for **2026-12-20 at 20:00**, local time (Europe/Istanbul). No running time is published. Biletix gives the address as 1455. Sokak No. 8, Yeşilbahçe. Muratpaşa Municipality built the centre and opened it on 26 October 2018. In 2019 it described a 667-seat multipurpose hall, with about two-thirds of the building set underground, plus a café and an amphitheatre. The same stage hosts [Erkek Aklı Oksimoron](/events/erkek-akli-oksimoron-2026-12-15-muratpasa/) on 15 December and [Özel Bir Rica](/events/ozel-bir-rica-2026-12-22-muratpasa/) two nights after Ayna. Our [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/) covers the area.

## Tickets and practical planning

On 14 November 2026 Biletix listed tickets from 1,200 TL, and Biletinial also sells them; prices change, so check before you buy. Biletinial lists the concert as **12+** and may ask for ID at the door for age-limited and discounted tickets. Its other rules:

- Tickets not used on the day and time are void, with no refund or exchange.
- The organiser may move you to a different seat.
- Biletinial is not responsible for getting latecomers in, so arrive early.
