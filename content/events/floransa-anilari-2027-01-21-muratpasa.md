**Floransa Anıları** ("Souvenir de Florence") is the closing concert of the second International Antalya Chamber Music Festival, at **Re22 Kültür Sanat** in Kaleiçi on **Thursday 21 January 2027 at 20:00**. The whole evening is built around one work: **Tchaikovsky's *Souvenir de Florence***, written for string sextet.

## The players

A sextet needs two violins, two violas and two cellos. Biletinial lists:

- **Cihat Aşkın**, violin, and **Gözde Yaşar**, cello
- **Cameratalia:** **Can Özhan**, violin; **Sevinç Duran Yalaz** and **Sonat Serbest**, violas; **Pelin Baysal Ateşok**, cello

Can Özhan is also the festival's artistic director. The concert is for **ages 8 and up**; Biletinial gives no running time.

## The festival

Biletinial's festival pass page describes five concerts plus masterclasses in Kaleiçi from **5 December 2026 to 21 January 2027**, organised by Re22 Kültür Sanat. The pass is valid for all five concerts; masterclasses are not included. Three nights earlier, flute, violin and piano play [Renkler ve Yansımalar](/events/renkler-ve-yansimalar-2027-01-18-muratpasa/) in the same hall.

## The venue and tickets

Re22 Kültür Sanat Konser Salonu is at Paşa Cami Sokak No. 22, Tuzcular, in Kaleiçi (Muratpaşa). Biletinial showed a starting price of ₺1,232 on the event page and ₺1,100 on its venue list on 22 November 2026, so check the price when you book.
