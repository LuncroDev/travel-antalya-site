**Lumera Trio** brings an evening of **Sezen Aksu** songs to the **Aspendos hall of Antalya's Atatürk Kültür Merkezi (AKM)** on **Wednesday 16 December 2026 at 20:30**. It is an instrumental, candlelit concert: clarinet, guitar and cello, with no singer on the bill.

## Lumera Trio: Sezen Aksu songs by candlelight

Sezen Aksu is the most loved songwriter in Turkish pop, and this show rearranges her songs for three instruments. Biletinial lists the genre as instrumental pop and says the hall is lit by hundreds of candles. The songs it names are "Gülümse", "Firuze", "Tükeneceğiz", "Kutlama", "Seni Kimler Aldı" and "Keskin Bıçak".

Neither ticket seller names the three musicians. With no lyrics, this is one of the easier Turkish-music nights for visitors to enjoy. For more winter options, see our [live music guide](/blog/live-music-antalya-winter-venues/).

## The date and venue

AKM sits inside Atatürk Kültür Parkı on Sakıp Sabancı Bulvarı in Meltem, Muratpaşa, a short ride west of Kaleiçi. The concert starts at 20:30. No running time or age limit is published. December is a busy month for the hall; our [December 2026 week-by-week guide](/blog/antalya-december-2026-week-by-week/) lists what else is on, and the [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/) covers the area.
