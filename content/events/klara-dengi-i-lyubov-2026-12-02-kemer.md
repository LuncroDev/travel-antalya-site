**Klara, Money and Love** (*Клара, деньги и любовь*) is a Russian-language play staged by the **Otrazheniya** ("Reflections") theatre company at **Kemer Belediyesi Kültür Salonu**, the municipal culture hall, on **Wednesday 2 December 2026 at 19:30**. It is performed in Russian with Turkish subtitles.

## The play

The company calls it a memory play, "about my mother and me". It moves between childhood and first love, family secrets, old grievances and tenderness, and it is about growing up and the things that stay with us. iTicket describes it as warm and lively rather than heavy, and recommends it for ages 8 and up. The writer, director and cast aren't named on the ticket page.
