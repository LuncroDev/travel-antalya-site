**Romantizmin İki Ustası** ("Two Masters of Romanticism") brings violist **Marc Sabbah** to **Re22 Kültür Sanat** in Kaleiçi on **Tuesday 22 December 2026 at 20:00**. It is the second of five concerts in the second International Antalya Chamber Music Festival, and it pairs two string quintets written for two violas.

## The programme

- **Felix Mendelssohn Bartholdy:** String Quintet No. 2 in B-flat major, Op. 87
- **Johannes Brahms:** String Quintet No. 1 in F major, Op. 88

Biletinial frames the evening as Mendelssohn's lively storytelling set against Brahms's dense harmonies. Two works for the same five instruments make an easy evening to follow, even if you only know the composers by name.

## The players

- **Olgu Kızılay**, first violin
- **Can Özhan**, second violin
- **Marc Sabbah** and **Sevinç Duran Yalaz**, violas
- **Pelin Baysal Ateşok**, cello

The ticket page presents Sabbah as the festival's guest, a soloist, teacher and chamber musician. It describes Kızılay as a leading Turkish chamber player with ties to the Borusan Quartet. Özhan is the festival's artistic director. The concert is for **ages 8 and up**; no running time is given.

## The festival and tickets

The pass page lists five concerts plus masterclasses from 5 December 2026 to 21 January 2027, organised by Re22 Kültür Sanat. The festival opens with [Bir Başyapıt: Dvořák](/events/bir-basyapit-dvorak-2026-12-05-muratpasa/). Two nights after this one, on 24 December, Can Özhan plays with three young violinists. January brings [Renkler ve Yansımalar](/events/renkler-ve-yansimalar-2027-01-18-muratpasa/) and [Floransa Anıları](/events/floransa-anilari-2027-01-21-muratpasa/).

The hall is at Paşa Cami Sokak No. 22, Tuzcular, in Kaleiçi. On 23 November 2026 Biletinial showed ₺1,232 on the event page and ₺1,100 on its venue list, so check the price at checkout.
