**Hayatta Kalma Çizgileri: Yaşamak, Uyum Sağlamak, Yarını Hayal Etmek** brings together work by African artists in an exhibition presented in collaboration with Africa Soft Power. The first stop was at Bülent Ecevit Kültür Merkezi. This listing is for the second stop, at the **Antalya Metropolitan Municipality service building foyer from 16 to 27 November 2026**.

## Before you go

The [municipality’s programme](https://mcmsmedia.antalya.bel.tr/MediaFolder/6770156cf267483097290291/76c6fc3a-d703-4bd7-ba37-a45101c45a1d.pdf) lists the dates and venue but does not specify daily visiting hours or whether any fee or advance registration applies. Check with the municipality before making a special trip. This is not at the Antalya EXPO Center and is separate from admission to the COP31 conference.

See the [COP31 city-events guide](/events/cop31-city-events-2026/) for the wider programme. The foyer exhibition is scheduled to continue after the city programme’s 20 November end date.
