Hospitality equipment suppliers and industry buyers meet at this professional trade fair in Aksu.

## Dates and venue

The **37th International Hotel Equipment & Hospitality Trade Fair** is scheduled for **12–15 January 2027** at **ANFAŞ Expo Center, Aksu, Antalya**. The organizer publishes visiting hours of **10:00–18:00**, local Antalya time (Europe/Istanbul). Check for day-specific changes before travelling.

Hotel Equipment and Food Product are separate fairs taking place at the same venue on the same dates.

## Who can attend

**This is a trade event, not a public visitor attraction.** The organizer describes its audience as industry decision-makers with purchasing authority and explicitly excludes the general public.

The organizer offers [free visitor registration](https://anfashotelequipment.com/en/visitor-form). Its form asks for company and contact details plus a business-card photo. Fair entry details will be emailed nearer the event. Free registration does not establish automatic approval or guaranteed admission.

No event-wide minimum age was stated in the public pages reviewed. Confirm any relevant age, accessibility and entry requirements with the organizer before making travel plans.
