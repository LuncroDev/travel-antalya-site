İclal Aydın brings **Ama Değil, Rağmen** ("Not 'But' — 'Despite'") to the **Türkan Şoray Kültür Merkezi** on **Friday 27 November 2026 at 20:30**. The play is in Turkish.

## The show

This is a one-woman play in a single act, written and performed by Aydın herself; Biletinial credits Setart Booking and Vişne Production as producers. The setting is her study, filled with novels, books, sculptures, paintings and photographs. As she talks, the story moves out from her own life into a shared memory of the country, travelling from eastern Anatolia through Cappadocia and the Aegean to Ankara and Istanbul. The ticket page frames it as a piece about the values that keep people connected. Expect storytelling more than plot, and a lot of spoken Turkish: it suits fluent speakers best.
