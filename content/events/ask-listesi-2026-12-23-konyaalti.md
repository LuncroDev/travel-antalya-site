Poetika Yapım's comedy **Aşk Listesi** plays the **Konyaaltı Belediyesi Nazım Hikmet Fuar ve Kongre Merkezi** on **Wednesday 23 December 2026 at 20:00**. The play is in Turkish.

## The play

It starts with a question: if you could choose every quality of your dream partner, what would you write on a blank sheet of paper? Oktay and Kenan are close friends. Oktay's gift for Kenan's 50th birthday changes both their lives: the moment Kenan writes down ten qualities of his ideal woman, Melis turns up to find him. The play is Norm Foster's, translated by Eylül Aktürk, and adapted and directed by Serkan Üstüner. **Hakan Bilgin, Sevinç Erbulak and Yosi Mizrahi** are the cast, with sets by Serkan Kavurt. Biletinial says the production won the comedy production of the year prize at the 25th Direklerarası Audience Awards.

Biletinial lists the play as **16+**. On 15 November 2026, Biletix listed tickets from 1,413.50 TL and Biletinial from 1,285 TL; prices change, so check before you buy. Biletinial's rules:
