**Reverans Sanat** brings Anton Chekhov's comedy **Bir Evlenme Teklifi** (*A Marriage Proposal*) to the **Konyaaltı Belediyesi Nazım Hikmet Fuar ve Kongre Merkezi** on **Sunday 6 December 2026 at 20:00**. The play is in Turkish.

## The play

A marriage proposal should take a minute. In Chekhov's comedy it keeps getting lost in pride, stubbornness and one argument after another. Biletinial calls Reverans Sanat's version a warm, playful look at very familiar human failings, and Biletix promises humour and a lively pace. Fulya Selvi directs and also handles the dramaturgy, with Özge Durmuşcan as assistant director. Berker Selvi, Zehra Azra Kalfa and Kenan Pekdiker make up the cast of three. No translator is credited. The production is touring: Biletix shows it in Isparta the night before, then in Samsun and Ordu on 21 and 22 December. It suits fluent Turkish speakers best.
