Antalya Metropolitan Municipality has announced a **9–20 November 2026 city programme** alongside COP31, with cultural, sporting and environmental activities at several city locations. This listing covers the city programme, **not admission to the COP31 conference**.

## Selected upcoming performances

Times below are **Antalya local time (Europe/Istanbul, UTC+3)**. These are scheduled start times, not venue opening hours.

- **11 November, 17:00, Hıdırlık:** İsmail Baha Sürelsan Conservatory Polyphonic Choir, conducted by Prof. Dr. Seval Köse.
- **12 November, 17:00, Hıdırlık:** Antalya Metropolitan Municipality Band and City Orchestra.
- **12 November, 17:00, Üç Kapılar (Hadrian's Gate):** the conservatory's Turkish Folk Music ensemble and Folk Dance Group.

These selections are listed on pages 4–5 of the official PDF. Consult the complete programme for other dates and locations; the date range does not mean every activity runs every day.

## City events are separate from conference access

The [COP31 conference listing](/events/cop31-antalya/) explains accreditation and participant information for the conference site. A city performance is not a conference ticket and does not grant access to restricted conference areas. Conversely, this guide does not assume that conference accreditation is required for a city activity: **check the conditions of the specific event**.

The reviewed city programme does not establish ticket prices, registration requirements, age restrictions or accessibility arrangements for each activity. Confirm these with the municipality before travelling. Do not assume all activities are free or open without registration. Outdoor plans and timings may change.
