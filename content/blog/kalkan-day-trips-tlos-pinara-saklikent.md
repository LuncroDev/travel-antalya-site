Kalkan sits at the western edge of Antalya province, and some of the best day trips from Kalkan lie just over the border in Muğla. Three of them make a natural set: **Tlos**, a Lycian hill city with a castle on its acropolis; **Pınara**, a cliff riddled with rock tombs that, on the ministry's ticket page, costs nothing to enter; and **Saklıkent**, a narrow gorge you can wade into. This guide uses the Ministry of Culture and Tourism's Turkish Museums pages for Tlos and Pınara, read on 28 November 2026. For Saklıkent we could not open the official fee tariff, so we say what is and isn't confirmed.

One warning before you set off: **Saklıkent Gorge** is not the Saklıkent ski centre in the mountains above Antalya. That is a different place with the same name; see our [Saklıkent ski day trip](/blog/saklikent-ski-day-trip-from-antalya/) if snow is what you want.

## Tlos: the city on the rock

**Tlos** stands on a steep rocky hill at the south-western foot of the Akdağlar range, above the Xanthos valley. The ministry's page places it in Yaka village, Seydikemer district, Muğla, at the 25th km of the Fethiye–Antalya road.

The page tells a long story in a few lines. Tlos peaked in the 5th and 4th centuries BC, received Alexander the Great in 333 BC and was one of the six cities of the Lycian League that held **three votes** each. It became part of the Roman province under Claudius in AD 43 and was the seat of a bishop into the 12th century. In the 19th century a local ağa, Kanlı Ali Ağa, built a castle on the acropolis. Tlos was added to UNESCO's **World Heritage Tentative List** on 6 February 2009.

Excavations led by Prof. Taner Korkut of Akdeniz University have concentrated on the **theatre, stadium, Great Bath, the temple of Kronos, a basilica and the rock tombs**, the ministry says. The tombs, cut into the acropolis cliff, are the classic view; our guide to [Lycian rock tombs](/blog/lycian-rock-tombs/) explains the different types.

**Hours:** summer 08:30–19:30, winter 08:30–17:30, with the ticket office closing at the same time. The Tlos page prints the same date range (1 April–1 November) against both seasons, which is clearly a slip; the Pınara page gives winter as 1 November–1 April.

**Tickets:** €3 for foreign adults and children aged 8 and over, ₺200 for Turkish citizens; free for under-8s. The page has a MuseumPass The Aegean banner (seven days, €95, for Muğla, İzmir, Aydın and Denizli sites) but does not say whether Tlos is included, so check before relying on it. Facilities listed: a shop and toilets.

## Pınara: a cliff full of tombs

**Pınara** is the quieter, wilder site. The ministry places it in Minare village, Seydikemer, **40 km from Fethiye** and 5 km west of the Fethiye–Kaş road, reached by a 2 km stabilised (unsurfaced) road from the village. The village takes its name, Minare, from the ancient city.

Lycian inscriptions call the city **Pinale**. Strabo, quoting Artemidoros, lists it among the six three-vote cities of the Lycian League — so in its day it ranked with Tlos. The ministry's page describes a bath, theatre, agora, odeon, rock tombs and two acropolises.

The sight that stops people is the **upper acropolis**: its steep eastern face is cut with **hundreds of rock tombs**, most of them in the house-type style. On the lower acropolis are the odeon, agora, temples and pillar tombs. One tomb has a pediment relief of a walled city — thought to be Pınara itself — and the ministry suggests it may have belonged to the prince of Pınara.

**Hours:** summer (1 April–1 November) 08:30–19:30, winter (1 November–1 April) 08:30–17:30.

**Tickets:** the ministry's page lists every category, foreign adults included, as **free** ("Ücretsiz"). That is unusual for a site of this size, so don't be surprised if it changes. Only toilets are listed as facilities; bring water and food.

## Saklıkent Gorge

**Saklıkent** means "hidden city". The gorge is a national park in Seydikemer, Muğla; Wikipedia's article gives its establishment date as 6 June 1996, its area as 1,643 hectares, and its distance as 50 km from Fethiye and 65 km from Kaş. The same article says the canyon is 18 km long and 300 m deep, but also calls its full length 16 km, and says about **4 km is walkable after April**, when the water drops. A new wooden bridge was built across the stream in 2024.

We could not read the official fee table: the Directorate of Nature Conservation and National Parks' tariff page was closed to our check. Pay at the gate and ask for the current price there, and wear shoes you can wade in.

## How to plan the day from Kalkan

The three sites are close enough to each other to pair, but not all three in one comfortable day:

- **Tlos + Saklıkent:** the classic pairing. Ruins in the morning, the gorge in the afternoon heat.
- **Pınara on its own, or with Xanthos and Letoon:** Pınara lies off the same Fethiye–Kaş road as the Xanthos valley sites in our [Letoon and Xanthos day trip](/blog/letoon-and-xanthos-day-trip/). See also [Xanthos Ancient City](/things-to-do/xanthos-ancient-city/).
- **A gentler day:** [Patara](/things-to-do/patara-ancient-city/) and its beach, closer to Kalkan and in Antalya province, covered in our [Patara beach and ruins guide](/blog/patara-beach-and-ancient-city/).

None of the official pages gives a driving time from Kalkan, so check your route the night before. A hire car or a private driver is far easier than public transport for these sites; the last stretch to Pınara is unsurfaced.

## Seasons

**Spring and autumn** are the best time for Tlos and Pınara: both are exposed hillsides with little shade. In **summer**, start early and save Saklıkent for the afternoon. In **winter** the ruins are quiet and pleasant to walk, but Wikipedia describes the gorge as walkable only after April, so in the cold months it may be little more than a viewpoint; ask locally before you go.

## FAQ

### Is Pınara really free to enter?

The ministry's Turkish Museums page lists all ticket categories as free on 28 November 2026. Tlos, nearby, charges €3 for foreign adults.

### Can I do Tlos, Pınara and Saklıkent in one day?

It is possible by car, but rushed. Tlos and Saklıkent pair well; Pınara fits better with Xanthos and Letoon.

### Are these sites in Antalya province?

No. Tlos, Pınara and Saklıkent are all in Seydikemer, Muğla province, just across the border from Kaş district.

### What should I bring?

Water, sun protection and walking shoes for the ruins; water shoes and a change of clothes for Saklıkent.
