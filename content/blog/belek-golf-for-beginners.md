Belek golf for beginners is a real option, not just a sales line. Several clubs run academies with starter courses, there is a nine-hole course, and some clubs publish handicap limits generous enough for newer players. The catch is that most 18-hole courses still expect a handicap certificate. This guide explains how a first golf holiday in Belek can work: where to learn, where to play your first holes, and what to check before you book.

## Can beginners play golf in Belek?

Yes, but usually in stages. A complete beginner starts at an academy: range sessions, short-game practice and a lesson out on the course with a professional. A player who already has a handicap, even a high one, can book tee times on courses whose published limits allow it.

The limits matter. Each club sets its own maximum handicap, and some are strict. When we checked on 10 November 2026, the clubs' own pages gave these limits:

| Course | Holes | Published handicap limit |
|---|---|---|
| Gloria **Verde** | 9 (par 35, 2,923 m) | Men 36, ladies 40 |
| Antalya Golf Club, **Pasha** | 18 (par 72, 5,731 m) | Men 28, women 36 |
| Antalya Golf Club, **PGA Sultan** | 18 (par 71, 6,477 m) | 24 for all |
| **Cullinan Links** | 36 holes | Men 36, ladies 44 |

The Pasha and Sultan figures are from the golf page of Kempinski The Dome, the hotel beside Antalya Golf Club. Other clubs publish their own limits; see our profiles of [National Golf Club](/golf/national-golf-club/), [Carya](/golf/carya-golf-club/), [Cornelia](/golf/cornelia-golf-club/) and [Montgomerie Maxx Royal](/golf/montgomerie-maxx-royal-golf-club/) for each club's rules.

If you have never played, don't book an 18-hole round on day one. Book lessons first, then a playing lesson, then a short course.

## Step 1: learn at a golf academy

### Gloria Golf Training Academy

Gloria has the most detailed beginner offer of the clubs we checked. Its academy page lists 48 covered driving-range bays with TrackMan screens, five putting greens, seven pitching and chipping greens and bunker practice greens, across an academy area of 3,500 m².

The key product for new players is the **4 Day Beginners Package**. Gloria says it covers an introduction to golf, swing basics, putting, chipping, the full swing, how to use a full set of clubs, playing on the course and an introduction to the rules. Sessions run four hours a day for groups of two to six. There are matching packages for children.

Single lessons last 50 minutes, for one person, a couple or a small group, and Gloria says each lesson includes practice balls, equipment and video analysis. If you cancel at least 12 hours ahead, there is no charge; later cancellations cost half the fee. Gloria also says guests who take eight hours or more of lessons can receive a Gloria certificate from its Director of Golf. That is not the same as an official handicap: Gloria says a handicap certificate requires club membership.

The academy lists its teaching staff by name, with PGA and Turkish PGA (TPGA) qualifications. One of them, the page says, has been Gloria's golf director since 2008. Lessons are offered in Turkish, English, German and Russian, depending on the coach.

### Sueno Golf Academy

Sueno, home of the [Sueno Golf Club](/golf/sueno-golf-club/) and its Pines and Dunes courses, says its academy teaches every level, from beginners to experienced players. It offers group and individual lessons and programmes for junior golfers. Sueno calls its practice area one of the largest in Belek, but its academy page gives no figures, so ask what you will be using.

### Antalya Golf Club academy

The academy at [Antalya Golf Club](/golf/antalya-golf-club/) offers individual or group lessons, playing lessons, specialist tuition packages, junior training and video coaching. Kempinski The Dome, next door, says it sells golf-lesson packages designed for beginners; ask the hotel for the details.

### Cullinan Links Golf Academy

The academy at [Cullinan Links Golf Club](/golf/cullinan-links-golf-club/) sells individual and group lessons of 50 minutes, for groups of two to four, in packages of one, three or six lessons. It also offers a **nine-hole playing lesson**. That includes a bag, tees and balls, but not the green fee or the buggy. Juniors under 13 get a 20% discount. The academy highlights a 1,500 m² putting green, and the club's general page says lessons for all levels are taught by PGA professionals.

## Step 2: play your first holes on a short course

Gloria's **Verde course** is the obvious first course in Belek. It has nine holes, par 35, over 2,923 m, and its published handicap limit (36 for men, 40 for ladies) is among the more open of the courses above. Gloria also runs **Verde Select**, which joins the Verde nine to the front nine of its Old or New course for a full 18. It is a combined routing, not a separate course.

The Verde page lists 2026 maintenance dates. Before you book, ask whether the course is open on your dates.

## Step 3: take a playing lesson

A playing lesson is the bridge between the range and a real round. You play a few holes with a professional, who shows you how to move round the course, keep up the pace and follow etiquette. Gloria offers playing lessons over 9 or 18 holes, and Cullinan offers a nine-hole version. For a nervous beginner, nine holes is plenty.

## What a first-timer should know before booking

### You need proof of handicap for most rounds

A handicap is issued at home, through a golf club or national federation. Bring proof of it to Belek. Without one, plan to stay on the academy and short course.

### Hire equipment is available

You don't need to fly with clubs for a first try. Cullinan's pro shop rents left- and right-handed sets, and Gloria's lessons include equipment. Once you are playing full rounds, ask the club about rental sets and shoes.

### Buggies have rules

Cullinan says its 150 buggies may only be driven on the marked paths, to protect the course. Expect similar rules elsewhere, and ask about the minimum age for drivers if you are travelling with teenagers.

### Lessons and green fees are separate

A playing lesson is not a green fee. Cullinan, for example, says its nine-hole playing lesson does not include the green fee or the buggy. Ask for a full quote before you go.

## When to come

Kempinski The Dome says the best months for golf in Belek are March to May and September to November, when it is warm but not hot. Those are also the busiest months on the tee sheet. Winter can suit a beginner too: some clubs' rates drop, and long range sessions are comfortable without the summer heat. Our guide to the [Belek golf season](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/) explains how prices, maintenance closures and the weather change through the year.

## A simple first-week plan

- **Days 1–2:** academy sessions on the range and short-game area.
- **Day 3:** a nine-hole playing lesson with a professional.
- **Days 4–5:** more practice, then nine holes on the Verde course if your handicap and the course's rules allow it.
- **Day 6:** a rest day, or a trip to Aspendos.
- **Day 7:** your best nine, or Verde Select if you are ready for 18.

## FAQ

### Do I need a handicap certificate to play golf in Belek?

For most 18-hole rounds, yes. Clubs publish maximum handicaps, from 24 on the PGA Sultan course to 44 for ladies at Cullinan. Complete beginners should start with lessons at an academy.

### Is there a nine-hole course in Belek?

Yes. Gloria's Verde course has nine holes, par 35, over 2,923 m. Gloria also combines it with the front nine of its Old or New course as Verde Select.

### How long is a beginner golf course in Belek?

Gloria's Beginners Package runs over four days, with four-hour sessions for two to six people. Single lessons at Gloria and Cullinan last 50 minutes.

### Can children learn golf in Belek?

Yes. Gloria runs four-day packages for children, Sueno and Antalya Golf Club offer junior training, and Cullinan's academy gives under-13s a 20% discount on lessons.
