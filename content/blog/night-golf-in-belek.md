Night golf in Belek is not a gimmick. Four clubs on this stretch of coast sell rounds under floodlights, from a lit back nine to a full 18 holes. In summer that means you can play after the heat of the day has gone; in winter it stretches a short afternoon into a full round. This guide explains which courses are lit, what each club actually sells, and what to ask before you book. All details come from the clubs' own websites, checked on 10 November 2026.

If you are still planning the trip itself, start with our guide to the [Belek golf season](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/). New to the game? Read [Belek golf for beginners](/blog/belek-golf-for-beginners/) first, because floodlit rounds follow the same handicap rules as daytime ones.

## Carya: the full 18 under lights

Carya is the strongest choice for a proper night round. The club says it was the first in Europe to offer night golf under floodlights on all 18 holes, and that the lights are set high enough for players to follow the flight of the ball. Turkey's official tourism portal, GoTürkiye, even titles its Carya page "Night Golf in Progress!".

The course is a par 72; the scorecard lists the back tees at 6,605. Carya sells both an 18-hole and a 9-hole floodlight round, and its online tee-time request form has three slots to choose from: morning, afternoon or floodlight.

### Carya's floodlighting times

The green-fee page publishes a table headed "Golf Course Floodlighting Start Times". The times shift through the year:

- **October:** 14:33
- **November:** 14:24
- **December:** 14:15
- **January:** 14:30
- **February:** 15:09
- **March:** 15:36
- **April:** 16:03
- **May to August:** 16:30
- **September:** 15:36

The page does not say whether these are tee times for a floodlit round or the time the lights come on, so ask when you request your slot. Either way, the pattern is clear: in winter, a floodlit round starts in the early afternoon.

Carya's own advice is to use the lights to avoid the heat. It says an early-morning or late-evening round is more comfortable in July and August, and that in the busy season the golfing day stretches to almost 18 hours. Buggies must be pre-booked through the reservation office, and a reserved buggy cannot be cancelled on the day. Trolleys, clubs and range tokens are hired at the club reception. See our [Carya Golf Club profile](/golf/carya-golf-club/) for the course itself, and [Regnum Carya](/hotels/regnum-carya/) for the resort associated with it.

## Montgomerie Maxx Royal: a floodlit back nine

The Montgomerie course is an 18-hole par 72 of 6,522 metres, designed by Colin Montgomerie with European Golf Design. Its homepage states plainly: floodlit holes 10–18. That makes it a good option if you want to start in daylight and finish under the lights.

There is one conflict to be aware of. The Maxx Royal resort's golf page describes "illuminated 18 holes" and a floodlit track for night rounds, while the golf club's own homepage says only the back nine is lit. We go with the club's more specific wording, but ask which holes you will play in the dark.

The club hires out buggies, pull trolleys and sets of clubs. Its homepage lists a bookings line (+90 242 444 62 99), and the contact page gives the golf club's number. The enquiry form has no night option, so mention floodlit golf in your message. Our [Montgomerie Maxx Royal profile](/golf/montgomerie-maxx-royal-golf-club/) lists the published handicap limits, and [Maxx Royal Belek](/hotels/maxx-royal-belek-golf-resort/) is the resort that promotes the course on its golf page.

## National: nine holes after dark

National Golf Club has been open since 1994. Its night-golf page says the club's night experience "has grown even bigger" with lighting added to the last four holes, and its rate tables include a **"National 9 Holes (Floodlight)"** round in every season. The page does not say exactly which holes now have lights, so ask which nine the floodlit round uses.

National's rules apply at night too: a valid handicap certificate is required (maximum 36 for men and women), buggies must be reserved in advance and cannot be cancelled on the day, only guests aged 18 and over may drive a golf cart, and children may only ride in a buggy with an adult. Trolleys, rental clubs and range tokens are hired at the club reception. The tee-time form offers morning or afternoon, with no night option. More on the course in our [National Golf Club profile](/golf/national-golf-club/).

## Cullinan Links: floodlit rounds for groups

Cullinan's hotel site says the club was redesigned with 36 holes and "night-lights" by European Golf Design. Its green-fee page lists **"18 Holes Floodlight"** and **"9 Holes Floodlight"** rates in every period from September 2026 to March 2028.

The catch is the group size: Cullinan says **a minimum of eight golfers** is required for a floodlight reservation, and they must check in at the club an hour beforehand. Sharing a buggy is mandatory on all rounds, and the published handicap limits are 36 for men and 44 for ladies, the most generous of the four clubs. The club's course pages list a third 18-hole layout, Termessos, alongside Aspendos and Olympos, but none of the pages we opened says which holes are lit. Our [Cullinan Links profile](/golf/cullinan-links-golf-club/) covers the courses, and [Cullinan Belek](/hotels/cullinan-belek/) is the resort associated with the club.

## When night golf makes sense

- **High summer.** Carya itself recommends an early-morning or late-evening round in July and August. A late-afternoon start that finishes under the lights avoids the hottest hours.
- **Winter.** Days are short from November to February. A floodlit round lets you play a full 18 after a slow morning, or fit golf around a family day at [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/) or the beach.
- **Group trips.** Cullinan's eight-golfer minimum suits a society or a stag group better than a couple.
- **Mixed groups.** A lit back nine at Montgomerie or a floodlit nine at National is a short, social outing for players who do not want a full round.

## How to book a floodlit round

Night golf is booked like any other tee time, but ask more questions:

1. **Say "floodlight" in your request.** Carya's form has a floodlight slot. National's and Montgomerie's forms do not, so write it in the message.
2. **Ask which holes are lit**, and whether your round starts in daylight.
3. **Confirm the start time** for your month. Carya publishes a table; the others do not.
4. **Book the buggy in advance.** Carya and National both say reserved buggies cannot be cancelled on the day, and Cullinan makes shared buggies compulsory.
5. **Carry your handicap certificate.** Every club in this guide requires one, with limits from 36 to 44.
6. **Check the group size.** At Cullinan, you need at least eight players.
7. **Ask about equipment.** None of the clubs publishes rules on ball type or dress for night play, so ask whether they recommend particular balls.

None of the clubs publishes a last floodlit tee time, and none describes a floodlit driving range on the pages we opened. Warm up in daylight if you can.

## FAQ

### Can you play golf at night in Belek?

Yes. Carya sells floodlit rounds over all 18 holes, Montgomerie Maxx Royal lights its back nine, National sells a floodlit nine-hole round, and Cullinan Links sells floodlit 9- and 18-hole rounds for groups of at least eight.

### Which Belek golf course is fully floodlit?

Carya says all 18 of its holes are floodlit, and calls itself the first course in Europe to offer that. The Maxx Royal resort also describes illuminated 18 holes, but the Montgomerie club's own homepage says holes 10–18.

### What time does night golf start in Belek?

It depends on the month. Carya publishes floodlighting start times from 14:15 in December to 16:30 from May to August. The other clubs do not publish times, so confirm when you book.

### Do I need a handicap certificate for night golf?

Yes, the same rules apply as for daytime rounds. Carya and National set a maximum of 36 for men and women; Cullinan allows 36 for men and 44 for ladies.
