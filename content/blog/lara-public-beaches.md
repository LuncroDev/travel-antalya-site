Lara public beaches come in two very different kinds. Between the city centre and the sand, Muratpaşa Municipality runs small swimming platforms at the foot of the cliffs, with no sand at all. Further east, the public sections of Lara's sandy shore give you the beach you probably pictured. Beyond them, in Kundu, Aksu Municipality opened its own public beaches this year. This guide covers all three: who runs each one, what the official pages say about flags, facilities and hours, and what we could not confirm. We opened every source on 18 November 2026, after the summer season, so we also say what the municipalities publish about winter. For the main sandy beach in depth, read our [Lara Beach page](/things-to-do/lara-beach/).

## Lara public beaches at a glance

- **Cliff beaches (Muratpaşa):** five small platform beaches under the cliffs, 5–7 km from the centre on TÜRÇEV's figures. Rock and wooden decking, no sand.
- **Blue Flag 2026 on the cliffs:** Erenkuş, İnciraltı, Mobil and Konserve Koyu, according to TÜRÇEV, which runs the Blue Flag in Türkiye. We could not find a 2026 entry for Erdal İnönü Kent Parkı.
- **Sandy sections (Lara):** the ministry's Lara Halk Plajı 1 and the metropolitan municipality's Lazlo Beach, both flagged for 2026, about 15 km from the centre.
- **Kundu (Aksu):** four municipal public beaches, called Huzur, Turunç, Perge and Kurşunlu. Huzur opened on 3 May 2026, and Aksu says Turunç earned the Blue Flag in August.
- **Season:** TÜRÇEV's bathing season runs from 15 May to 30 September. The cliff beaches' published hours are 08:00–20:00 in summer.
- **Winter:** Aksu Municipality says its public beaches keep serving through the winter. We found nothing from Muratpaşa on winter opening.

## The Lara cliff beaches (falez plajları)

TÜRÇEV's pages describe the cliffs as a first-degree natural protected site. Muratpaşa has built its beaches as wooden platforms where the rock meets the sea. Antalya Metropolitan Municipality's beaches page lists five of them, all run by Muratpaşa: İnciraltı, Engelsiz Kafe, Erdal İnönü City Park, Erenkuş and Konserve Bay.

Muratpaşa's own news uses slightly different names: Erenkuş, Erdal İnönü, İnciraltı, Mobil and Konserve Koyu. In May 2024 it placed Mobil just below its Engelsiz Kafe (the "barrier-free café"), so the two lists appear to describe the same five spots.

Expect platform swimming. TÜRÇEV records rock and wooden decking at each flagged cliff beach, and no sand or pebbles. You swim from the decks rather than wading in from a shore. The Lower Düden waterfall also pours off this cliff line into the sea; see our [Düden Waterfalls page](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/).

### Erenkuş

Of the four beaches TÜRÇEV lists, this is the closest to town: 5 km from the centre, about 15 minutes away, and 16 km from the airport. The platform measures 25 m by 20 m, and Muratpaşa describes a 500 m² sunbathing area. Erenkuş has flown the Blue Flag every year since 2018, 2026 included, and the municipality held its 2025 flag ceremony here.

### İnciraltı

Choose this one if steps are a problem. Muratpaşa calls it the district's only beach with a lift, and describes a 625 m² wooden platform with municipal sunbeds, umbrellas and a snack kiosk (*büfe*). It opened in 2016 on the old Lara avenue. TÜRÇEV gives 6 km to the centre, 15 km to the airport and Blue Flags every year from 2017 to 2026.

### Mobil

Mobil was among Muratpaşa's first Blue Flag beaches in 2017, and TÜRÇEV shows the flag every year since. It is also the smallest of the four TÜRÇEV-listed platforms: 25 m by 7 m, or 175 m² of decking in the municipality's figure, 6 km from the centre.

### Erdal İnönü Kent Parkı

This beach takes its name from the Prof. Dr. Erdal İnönü city park. Muratpaşa describes a 126 m² sunbathing area and says it first won the Blue Flag in 2017. The municipality listed it among its five flagged beaches in July 2025. We could not open a TÜRÇEV page for it, so its 2026 status is unconfirmed.

### Konserve Koyu

The biggest platform: 1,800 m² of decking by Muratpaşa's count, and 60 m by 30 m on TÜRÇEV's page. It is also the furthest from the centre of the four TÜRÇEV lists, at 7 km (about 20 minutes), and 14 km from the airport. Blue Flags from 2019 to 2026.

In September 2024 the municipality also said a sixth spot, Bambus, had newly opened to the public. It is not on the July 2025 flag list, and we found no current details.

## Hours, fees and facilities on the cliffs

- **Hours:** TÜRÇEV's pages for Erenkuş, İnciraltı, Konserve Koyu and Mobil all give 08:00–20:00 every day in the summer season. In May 2023 the municipality said İnciraltı and Erdal İnönü opened at 06:00, with its own services starting at 08:00.
- **Fees:** none of the TÜRÇEV pages mentions an entrance fee. The latest fee notice from Muratpaşa that we could open dates from May 2023. Entry was free that year, and sunbed-and-umbrella sets were charged. Ask at the gate for current prices.
- **Food and drink:** Muratpaşa says it runs five beaches, four cafés and one restaurant along the cliff coast. İnciraltı has its own kiosk.
- **Lifeguards:** no page we opened says when lifeguards are on duty at the cliff beaches.
- **Accessibility:** İnciraltı's lift is the only step-free way down that the municipality mentions. TÜRÇEV marks none of its listed accessibility features, such as accessible toilets, ramps or ramped sea entry, at the four flagged cliff beaches.
- **Water quality:** TÜRÇEV publishes the test results. Mobil, for example, was sampled roughly every two weeks from 5 May to 20 October 2026.
- **Questions:** Muratpaşa's phone line is 444 80 07.

## Lara's sandy public beach sections

Past the cliffs, the coast flattens into sand. Our [Lara Beach page](/things-to-do/lara-beach/) covers this stretch in full, including Sandland and the facilities run by TURAŞ. Here is just the public-beach picture from TÜRÇEV as of today.

- **Lara Halk Plajı 1 (Ministry of Culture and Tourism):** a 100 m × 50 m sand beach, 15 km (about 20 minutes) from the centre and 10 km from the airport. Blue Flags in 2011–2017 and 2019–2026. It is the most accessible public beach in this guide: TÜRÇEV ticks accessible toilets, a ramp, accessible sunbeds and umbrellas and a floating sunbed. It also lists beach sports and water sports.
- **Lazlo Beach (Antalya Metropolitan Municipality):** 200 m × 50 m of sand at the same distances, with sunbed and umbrella service, a restaurant, free Wi-Fi, a market and caravan camping. TÜRÇEV lists Blue Flags in 2016 and every year from 2019 to 2026.
- **Lara Halk Plajı 2 (Antalya Metropolitan Municipality):** TÜRÇEV's page lists 2025 and 2026 but also states that the beach holds no Blue Flag certificate. Don't count it as flagged.

### What the sand is really like

TÜRÇEV records sand and no pebbles at both flagged sections. Some older guides, including a few posts on this site, promise soft golden sand. GoTürkiye, as quoted on our Lara Beach page, describes darkish sand with some small pebbles instead. Pack water shoes.

## Kundu's public beaches in Aksu

Kundu is where the resort strip carries on east into Aksu district. Several hotels with "Lara" in their names stand here, [Liberty Lara](/hotels/liberty-lara/) among them. On 3 May 2026 Aksu's mayor, İsa Yıldırım, said the municipality runs four public beaches: Huzur, Turunç, Perge and Kurşunlu.

### Huzur Plajı by the Kopak stream

Huzur ("peace") Beach opened on 3 May 2026. Aksu Municipality places it in the Kemerağzı–Kundu area, right beside the Kopak stream (Kopak Çayı). The opening notice lists no facilities, hours or fees, so treat it as a basic public beach until the municipality says more.

### Turunç, Perge and Kurşunlu

Aksu's website shows a photo gallery from the opening of Turunç Public Beach, dated 16 May 2026. On 21 August 2026 the municipality announced that Turunç had earned the Blue Flag. It said the beach had lifeguard service, that seawater was sampled every 15 days and that endemic plants were protected. We could not find Turunç on TÜRÇEV's website today, so check there before relying on the flag.

In May the mayor said Perge and Kurşunlu beaches would also be modernised and opened before summer. No official page we opened gives their exact location, opening date or facilities.

### Kumköy and the sea turtles

Aksu's coast is turtle country too. On 17 August 2026 the municipality called Kumköy beach one of the important nesting sites of the loggerhead turtle (*Caretta caretta*). It removed the gazebos that stood on the sand, and municipal officers and police patrol through the nesting season. Visitors are asked not to drive onto the sand or cast fishing lines in nesting zones.

The same notice says a public beach for Kumköy is being planned for next summer around the nests. The beach was cleaned in a joint clean-up on 21 October, and on 6 November the mayor called on everyone to keep it clean. Our [sea turtle beaches guide](/blog/sea-turtle-beaches-in-antalya/) explains the national rules.

## Visiting in November and winter

We're publishing in mid-November, after the Blue Flag season. Here is everything the sources say about the off-season:

- **TÜRÇEV's season** for every beach in this guide ends on 30 September. The cliff beaches' 08:00–20:00 hours are given for the summer season only.
- **Water testing ran on into autumn.** The latest results on the TÜRÇEV pages are dated 20 October 2026.
- **Aksu** posted a headline on 13 November 2026 saying its public beaches carry on serving in the winter season. We could not open the full item, so we can't say which services continue.
- **Muratpaşa** publishes nothing we could find on winter opening of the cliff platforms. Call 444 80 07 before you go.

For sea temperatures month by month, read our guide to [swimming in Antalya in winter](/blog/can-you-swim-in-antalya-in-winter-weather-sea-temperature-guide/).

## Getting there

TÜRÇEV says public transport serves every beach in this guide, but no official page we opened names bus lines or stops. Plan the trip in the AntalyaKart app; our [AntalyaKart guide](/blog/antalyakart-guide/) explains how to pay. Distances from TÜRÇEV:

- **Cliff beaches:** 5–7 km from the centre, 15–20 minutes.
- **Lara Halk Plajı 1 and Lazlo:** 15 km from the centre (about 20 minutes) and 10 km from the airport.
- **Kundu:** Aksu publishes no distances for its beaches.

## FAQ

### Are Lara's public beaches free?

None of the official pages we opened today states an entrance fee. Muratpaşa last confirmed free entry to its cliff beaches in May 2023, when only sunbed sets were charged. For Lara Halk Plajı 1, see our Lara Beach page.

### Which Lara cliff beaches have the Blue Flag in 2026?

TÜRÇEV lists Erenkuş, İnciraltı, Mobil and Konserve Koyu for 2026. Erdal İnönü Kent Parkı held the flag in 2025, according to the municipality, but we could not confirm 2026.

### Are the beaches open in November?

The Blue Flag season ended on 30 September. Aksu says its public beaches keep serving in winter. Muratpaşa has published no winter hours for the cliff platforms, so call 444 80 07 first.

### Which beach suits wheelchair users or buggies best?

Lara Halk Plajı 1. TÜRÇEV ticks accessible toilets, a ramp and accessible sunbeds there. On the cliffs, İnciraltı is the one with a lift.
