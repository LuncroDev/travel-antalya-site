Antalya's concert calendar does not stop when the beach season ends. From late November through the holidays, a handful of clubs and municipal halls book Turkish pop, rock, rap and jazz almost every weekend. This guide covers the venues we have confirmed shows at, with real dates from ticket sellers' pages, and what to expect from the nights. Most headliners are Turkish artists; that matters for what you will get out of a concert, and we say so where it applies.

## How the concert scene works

Three ticket sites cover almost everything: **Biletix**, **Biletinial** and **iTicket**. Event pages usually give the date, venue and start time, and many say whether ID is checked at the door. Evening club shows start late, at 21:00 or 22:00. Municipal halls start at 20:00 or 20:30.

Most acts in our listings are Turkish. If you do not speak Turkish you can still enjoy the music, but spoken stand-up and theatre are another matter (see [theatre and comedy for non-Turkish speakers](/blog/antalya-theatre-comedy-non-turkish-speakers/)).

## The venues

### The Bar Kaleiçi: club nights in the old town

The Bar is in [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/), which makes it the easiest place to combine a concert with dinner in the old town. Our listings show:

- **4 December:** [Madrigal](/events/madrigal-konseri-2026-12-04-muratpasa/), 22:00
- **5 December:** [Dedublüman](/events/dedubluman-konseri-2026-12-05-muratpasa/), 21:00
- **12 December:** [Emre Aydın](/events/emre-aydin-konseri-2026-12-12-muratpasa/), 21:00
- **19 December:** [Emir Can İğrek](/events/emir-can-igrek-konseri-2026-12-19-muratpasa/), 22:00
- **20 January:** [Gökhan Türkmen](/events/gokhan-turkmen-2027-01-20-muratpasa/), 22:00

### Holly Stone Performance Hall: rap, rock and pop

Our listings place Holly Stone Antalya in the old-town area, and it books younger acts. Confirmed nights include [Allame, Hidra and Bossy](/events/allame-hidra-bossy-konseri-2026-12-03-muratpasa/) on 3 December, [Yung Ouzo](/events/yung-ouzo-2026-12-04-muratpasa/) on 4 December, [Ezginin Günlüğü](/events/ezginin-gunlugu-konseri-2026-12-09-muratpasa/) on 9 December at 21:00 and [Paptırcem](/events/paptircem-konseri-2026-12-25-muratpasa/) on 25 December at 21:00.

### The Sponge Performance Hall, Konyaaltı

On the western side of the city, The Sponge has [Yeni Türkü](/events/yeni-turku-konseri-2026-12-10-konyaalti/) on 10 December at 22:00 and the [Türkçe Pop Fest](/events/turkce-pop-fest-2026-12-13-konyaalti/) on 13 December at 22:00.

### The 251 Soul at Akra Antalya: jazz

For a different evening, The 251 Soul is a music stage inside the Akra Antalya hotel on Lara Caddesi. Saxophonist Chris Potter plays there on [27 and 28 November](/events/chris-potter-trio-2026-11-27-muratpasa/) at 21:00, doors at 20:00. Biletix says guests must be 18 or older, seating is unreserved and you may be asked to share a table; outside food and drink are not allowed. Akra Hotels says guests staying at the hotel on those nights can attend free, with limited places, so ask when booking. See our [Akra Antalya hotel page](/hotels/akra-hotel/).

### Municipal halls: bigger names, earlier starts

The Konyaaltı Nazım Hikmet centre hosts [Birsen Tezer](/events/birsen-tezer-2026-12-12-konyaalti/) on 12 December at 21:00. The Türkan Şoray cultural centre has [Efkan Şeşen](/events/efkan-sesen-2026-12-08-muratpasa/) on 8 December at 20:30 and [Ayna](/events/ayna-konseri-2026-12-20-muratpasa/) on 20 December at 20:00. In Kepez, [Wegh](/events/wegh-konseri-2026-12-11-kepez/) plays Antalya Aktüel Sahne on 11 December at 21:00.

### Classical and orchestral

The Antalya State Symphony Orchestra plays about 25 concerts this season at the Atatürk Cultural Center, usually on Fridays at 20:30, according to Hürriyet Daily News. Our guide to the [2026–27 season](/blog/antalya-state-symphony-orchestra-2026-27/) lists the special dates. Further ahead, [Rock Sympho Show](/events/rock-sympho-show-2027-02-02-kepez/) plays Mimar Sinan Kongre Merkezi in Kepez on 2 February 2027 at 20:00.

## Practical tips

1. **Check the ticket page the week before.** Times and acts change. Where two sites disagree on a time we use the ticket seller's own page, and we flag the difference in the event listing.
2. **Bring ID.** Several pages say age-restricted or discounted tickets need ID at the door.
3. **Plan the ride home.** Many shows end late. Taxis from the old town are easy; from Kepez or Konyaaltı you may want to pre-arrange a pick-up.
4. **Dress for the season.** December evenings can be wet and cool; our [December weather guide](/blog/is-december-too-cold-in-antalya-weather-rain-sea-conditions/) helps.
5. **Browse the full list.** Our [concerts calendar](/events/category/concerts/) is updated as new dates are confirmed.

## FAQ

### Where can I buy tickets for concerts in Antalya?

Mainly Biletix, Biletinial and iTicket. Each event page on our site links to the seller we checked.

### Are concerts in Antalya in English?

Most headliners in our listings are Turkish artists performing Turkish music. Jazz and orchestral concerts need no translation.

### What time do club concerts start?

Our listings show 21:00 or 22:00 starts for clubs and 20:00 or 20:30 for municipal halls.
