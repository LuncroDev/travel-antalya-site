The **Antalya Piano Festival 2026** — officially the 26th International Antalya Piano Festival — runs from **Friday 27 November to Sunday 13 December 2026**. All eleven concerts start at **20:30** in the Aspendos Hall of Antalya's Atatürk Cultural Centre (AKM), and Biletix lists tickets from **₺100**. At those prices it makes an easy evening out from a hotel in Konyaaltı or the city centre.

We built this guide from the festival's own website and 2026 programme booklet, Antalya Metropolitan Municipality's festival page and Biletix's AKM listings, all read on 26 November 2026.

## The festival at a glance

- **Dates:** 27 November – 13 December 2026, eleven concerts.
- **Time:** every concert at 20:30.
- **Venue:** AKM Aspendos Salonu. The booklet says all festival concerts are held there this year.
- **Tickets:** on Biletix. On 26 November its AKM page listed most concerts "from ₺100".
- **Event listing:** our [Antalya Piano Festival event page](/events/antalya-piano-festival/) has the dates in brief.
- **Organiser:** the booklet calls the festival one of Antalya Metropolitan Municipality's most important arts events, held with its support. The municipality's festival page credits the composer and pianist **Fazıl Say** as artistic director, without saying for which edition.

## The Antalya Piano Festival 2026 programme

Titles are given as the festival prints them, with an English gloss where the title is Turkish.

### Week one: 27–29 November

- **Friday 27 November — Anna Malikova (opening concert).** "Romantizmin İki Yüzü: Chopin & Schumann" — the two faces of Romanticism, one pianist, two composers.
- **Saturday 28 November — Duo Fidelis.** "Aşk ve Ölüm" (Love and Death), with Zeynep Keleşoğlu and Orçun Orçunsel.
- **Sunday 29 November — Hugues Leclère.** "Vive la France", a French programme.

### Week two: 1–5 December

- **Tuesday 1 December — Piano Next.** "Yeni Kuşağın Piyanistleri", the new generation of pianists: Can Erkekli, Derin Zehra Erdem and Serdar Yekta Elmas.
- **Thursday 3 December — Bosphorus Trio.** "Bosphorus D'light", with Özgecan Günöz, Çağlayan Çetin and Özgür Ünaldı.
- **Friday 4 December — Can Saraç.** "Virtüözite Sanatı", the art of virtuosity.
- **Saturday 5 December — Çağdaş Özkan.** "Anatolian Piano".

### Week three: 7–13 December

- **Monday 7 December — İsmail Baha Sürelsan Conservatory.** "Genç Piyanistler", seven young pianists from Antalya's conservatory: Can Bali, Deniz Kantaş, Azra Arkış, Elif Naz Durusoy, Eray Ünal, İsmail Aras Sağlam and Sündüz Su Efe. This concert is in the festival programme but was not on Biletix's AKM venue list on 26 November, so check how tickets are issued.
- **Tuesday 8 December — Salih Can Gevrek.** "Orkestrasız Konçertolar", concertos without orchestra.
- **Thursday 10 December — Burak Çebi.** "Tuşlarda Neşenin Işıltısı", the sparkle of joy on the keys.
- **Sunday 13 December — Szymon Nehring (closing concert)**, with conductor **Michał Nesterowicz** and the **Antalya State Symphony Orchestra**. Biletix showed no price for this one on 26 November. Our guide to the [Antalya State Symphony Orchestra's 2026–27 season](/blog/antalya-state-symphony-orchestra-2026-27/) covers the orchestra's other dates.

## Which concerts to pick

If you can only go once, three evenings stand out:

- **The opening night (27 November)** sets the tone with a well-known Romantic programme, easy listening if you don't know the repertoire.
- **The closing concert (13 December)** is the only evening with a full orchestra, which makes it the festival's biggest night.
- **Çağdaş Özkan's "Anatolian Piano" (5 December)** is the one to choose if you want something you won't hear at home: a Turkish pianist on a Turkish theme.

The conservatory evening on 7 December is a chance to hear local students at the start of their careers. Families with older children may like the Piano Next and young pianists' nights best.

## Tickets and prices

The festival website says simply "Biletler Biletix'te": tickets are on Biletix. On 26 November 2026, Biletix's page for AKM's Aspendos Hall listed the concerts from 27 November to 10 December **from ₺100**, all on sale and none marked sold out. The closing concert was on sale without a price shown.

No discounts, free concerts or outdoor events appear on the festival's pages this year. The municipality's page says visiting artists also run workshops and play for school students, but gives no dates, and these are not public concerts.

The festival's contact page and Biletix give the hall's address as Meltem Mahallesi, Sakıp Sabancı Bulvarı, inside Atatürk Kültür Parkı, AKM No:7, Muratpaşa. The festival lists two phone numbers for the hall: +90 242 248 90 22 and +90 242 243 92 82.

## FAQ

### When is the Antalya Piano Festival 2026?

From 27 November to 13 December 2026. There are eleven concerts, all at 20:30 at AKM's Aspendos Hall.

### How much are tickets?

Biletix listed most concerts from ₺100 on 26 November 2026. The closing concert with the Antalya State Symphony Orchestra had no price shown yet.

### Who is playing?

Anna Malikova opens the festival and Szymon Nehring closes it with the Antalya State Symphony Orchestra. In between come Duo Fidelis, Hugues Leclère, Piano Next, the Bosphorus Trio, Can Saraç, Çağdaş Özkan, the İsmail Baha Sürelsan Conservatory's young pianists, Salih Can Gevrek and Burak Çebi.

### Can I buy tickets at the door?

The festival's website points only to Biletix and publishes no box-office hours. If you want to try on the night, call the hall first on +90 242 248 90 22.

### Do I need to speak Turkish?

No. These are instrumental concerts, so the music needs no translation. Some titles and the booklet are in Turkish, but performers' names and the dates are easy to follow on Biletix.
