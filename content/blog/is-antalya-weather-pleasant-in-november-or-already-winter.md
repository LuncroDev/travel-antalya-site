Antalya is one of Turkey’s most loved travel destinations, famous for its sunny coastline, turquoise sea, ancient ruins, and relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle. While summer attracts the biggest crowds, more travelers are now choosing autumn months like November for a calmer and more comfortable experience. November is a transition period in Antalya, when the intense summer heat fades, autumn colors begin to appear, and the city feels quieter and more peaceful. The weather is no longer hot, but it is not wintery either, making it suitable for sightseeing, outdoor walks, and cultural exploration. Understanding [**Antalya weather in November**](https://travel-antalya.com/) helps travelers plan better, pack appropriately, and enjoy beach walks, historical sites, and local life without unexpected weather changes.

In this detailed guide, we will explain what Antalya weather is really like in November. We will cover temperatures, rainfall, sea conditions, sunshine, outdoor activities, crowds, prices, packing tips, and whether November is a good time to visit. Everything is explained in easy language so every reader can understand clearly.

## Understanding Antalya’s Climate

Antalya has a typical Mediterranean climate, with long, hot, and dry summers followed by mild, wetter winters. Unlike many European destinations, winters in Antalya are gentle, and freezing temperatures are very rare. Snow rarely falls in the city itself, although the nearby Taurus Mountains may receive some snowfall, creating beautiful distant views. November is a transition month between autumn and winter. By this time, the intense summer heat has completely passed; days feel cooler and more comfortable, evenings become fresher, and rainfall slowly begins to increase. Even so, Antalya weather in November is still pleasant and far from cold, making it suitable for sightseeing, outdoor walks, and cultural activities. Thanks to this mild climate, Antalya remains a year-round destination, and November is especially appealing for travelers who want a peaceful, relaxed holiday without summer crowds.

## Antalya Weather in November: Monthly Overview

November brings noticeable changes in Antalya’s weather, but it remains pleasant and manageable for travel. The days are cooler than October, but still warm enough for outdoor activities.  
Typical weather conditions in November include:

* **Daytime temperature:** 18°C to 23°C

* **Nighttime temperature:** 10°C to 14°C

* **Average rainfall:** 80–100 mm

* **Sunshine hours:** 6–7 hours per day

* **Sea temperature:** 21°C to 23°C

These numbers show that November is not winter in Antalya. Instead, it feels like a mild autumn month with warm days and cooler evenings.

## Daytime Weather in November

During the day, Antalya weather in November is warm, mild, and very comfortable for travel. Daytime temperatures usually stay between 18°C and 23°C, which feels pleasant rather than hot, especially for visitors coming from cooler countries. The sun still shines regularly, but it is much softer than in summer, so you can walk, explore, and stay outdoors for long hours without feeling exhausted. This weather is ideal for sightseeing, city walks, visiting beaches, and enjoying outdoor cafés. Popular attractions such as Kaleiçi Old Town, Hadrian’s Gate, Perge, and Aspendos are far more enjoyable in November because there is no intense heat and far fewer tourists. Many travelers find this month perfect for relaxed exploration, as the comfortable temperatures allow you to fully enjoy Antalya’s history, culture, and natural beauty at an easy pace.

## Evening and Night Weather in November

Evenings in November in Antalya are cooler and refreshing, with temperatures usually ranging between 10°C and 14°C. It is not cold, but the air feels crisp, especially near the coast where gentle sea breezes are common. A light jacket or sweater is more than enough for going out at night. These cooler evenings are perfect for relaxed activities such as walking along the marina, enjoying quiet dinners at seaside restaurants, or spending time in cozy local cafés. Unlike summer nights, there is no heat or humidity, which makes the atmosphere more comfortable and helps visitors sleep better. Overall, November evenings in Antalya feel calm, peaceful, and pleasant, adding to the relaxed charm of visiting the city during this time of year.

## Rainfall in November

November is one of the months when rainfall starts to increase in Antalya, but it rarely causes serious problems for travelers. The average rainfall is around 80–100 mm, yet rain usually comes in short, light showers rather than lasting all day. It is common to see sunshine before or after rainfall, which means plans for sightseeing, walking, or exploring the city can still continue with little disruption. Many days remain dry, mild, and comfortable, especially during the daytime hours. The rain also brings positive changes to the environment. Gardens, parks, and forests become greener, flowers look fresher, and nearby waterfalls flow more strongly, creating beautiful scenery for nature lovers and photographers. A light rain jacket or small umbrella is usually enough to stay comfortable. Overall, even though rain is more frequent than in summer, [**Antalya weather in November**](https://travel-antalya.com/) remains pleasant, balanced, and suitable for relaxed travel, outdoor activities, and sightseeing.

## Sunshine and Daylight Hours

November days in Antalya are shorter than in summer, but the city still enjoys around 6–7 hours of soft, pleasant sunshine each day, which makes spending time outdoors very enjoyable. The weather is mild during the day, usually ranging between 15°C and 20°C, which is perfect for sightseeing, walking along the coastline, visiting historic sites, or relaxing at outdoor cafés without feeling too hot or cold. Mornings and early afternoons are the best times to explore the city when the light is bright and the temperatures are comfortable. Even with shorter daylight hours, there is still enough time to experience Antalya’s main attractions, take scenic walks, and enjoy the natural beauty of the area. The combination of gentle sunlight, calm weather, and fewer tourists makes November a peaceful and comfortable month for travel, giving visitors a chance to enjoy the city at a relaxed pace while experiencing its charm and culture.

## Sea Temperature and Swimming in November

One of the biggest advantages of visiting Antalya in November is that the sea remains pleasantly warm, with temperatures usually ranging between 21°C and 23°C, making it comfortable for swimming. Many travelers take advantage of sunny days to enjoy the sea, and the beaches are much quieter than in summer, offering a more peaceful and relaxing experience. The water feels refreshing without being too cold, perfect for a dip or simply wading along the shore. In addition, most hotels and resorts have heated pools, allowing guests to swim comfortably even when the air is cooler. Visitors can also enjoy other water activities such as boat tours, coastal walks, or simply relaxing by the water while soaking in the mild November weather.

## Can You Enjoy Beaches in November?

Yes, you can still enjoy Antalya’s beaches in November, but the experience is quite different from the busy summer months. The beaches are much quieter and less crowded, creating a peaceful and relaxing atmosphere. While you might not spend the whole day sunbathing, walking along the shore, sitting by the sea, or enjoying short swims is very pleasant, especially on sunny days when the water remains warm. For travelers who prefer a calm and more private beach experience without the summer crowds, Antalya in November offers ideal conditions, combining mild temperatures, gentle sunshine, and a tranquil coastal setting.

## Outdoor Activities in November

November is one of the best months for outdoor activities in Antalya. The cooler, milder temperatures make physical activities much more comfortable. Popular outdoor activities include:

* **Sightseeing and city tours:** Explore the city’s landmarks and attractions at a relaxed pace.

* **Hiking and nature walks:** Discover scenic trails and enjoy Antalya’s beautiful landscapes.

* **Visiting waterfalls and parks:** Spend time in nature without the summer crowds.

* **Cycling along coastal paths:** Enjoy long rides along the coastline with pleasant weather.

* **Exploring ancient ruins:** Visit historical sites while avoiding the peak-season heat.

The mild November weather allows you to stay active and enjoy these activities without feeling tired or overheated.

## Nature and Scenery in November

Nature in Antalya becomes even more colorful and lively in November. Trees slowly change their leaves to warm autumn shades, while gardens and parks look fresh and greener after the seasonal rainfall. The surrounding mountains, often dusted with morning mist, create stunning scenic views that are perfect for enjoying and photographing. This makes November an ideal time for travelers who love capturing landscapes, city views, and historical or cultural sites without the crowds of summer. The peaceful and calm atmosphere also provides a relaxing experience for nature lovers and slow travelers. It allows them to enjoy scenic walks, quiet moments by the sea, or simply soak in the beauty of Antalya’s natural surroundings during this serene time of year.

## Cultural Experiences in November

Antalya’s cultural life continues in November, even though it is off-season. Museums, historical sites, and local markets remain open, allowing you to enjoy:

* **Exploring Kaleiçi Old Town:** Wander through the charming narrow streets and historic architecture.

* **Visiting museums without crowds:** See exhibitions at your pace, without waiting in long lines.

* **Shopping at local markets:** Browse traditional crafts, souvenirs, and fresh produce.

* **Trying traditional Turkish food:** Taste local specialties in cozy restaurants and cafés.

* **Attending small local events:** Experience music, art, or cultural festivals in a more intimate setting.

Cultural experiences feel more authentic in November because there are fewer tourists, giving you a true taste of Antalya’s history and local life.

## Tourist Crowds in November

November is considered the off-season in Antalya, and tourist numbers drop significantly compared to summer and early autumn. This means:

* **Less crowded attractions:** You can explore popular sites without the usual hustle.

* **Shorter waiting times:** Restaurants, tours, and transport are quicker and easier to access.

* **More peaceful city atmosphere:** Streets, beaches, and public spaces feel calm and relaxing.

* **Better service in hotels and restaurants:** Staff can give more attention, making your stay more enjoyable.

If you prefer a calm and relaxed travel experience, Antalya in November offers the perfect combination of good weather and fewer crowds.

## Hotel Prices and Travel Costs in November

November is one of the most budget-friendly months to visit Antalya, making it a great choice for travelers looking to enjoy the city without spending too much. After the busy summer, hotel prices drop significantly, and many resorts and guesthouses offer special discounts or packages, giving visitors the chance to stay in comfortable or even luxury accommodations at much lower prices than during peak months. Flights to Antalya are also often cheaper in November, making the overall trip more affordable for both short stays and longer vacations. For budget travelers, this month provides excellent opportunities to find great deals on hotels, tours, and activities while still enjoying the city’s attractions, beaches, and natural beauty. Overall, visiting Antalya in November offers a perfect combination of pleasant weather, fewer crowds, and excellent value for money.

There are many benefits to visiting Antalya in November:

* **Pleasant daytime temperatures:** The weather is mild and comfortable, perfect for walking, sightseeing, and enjoying outdoor cafés.

* **Warm sea for swimming:** The Mediterranean remains warm enough for swimming and beach activities even as summer ends.

* **Fewer tourists:** With the busy season over, attractions and beaches are less crowded, giving a more relaxed experience.

## Possible Drawbacks of November Travel

While November in Antalya is generally pleasant, there are a few things to keep in mind:

* **Evenings can feel cool:** Nights and early mornings may be chilly, so bringing a light jacket is recommended.

* **Rainfall is higher than in summer:** Showers can occur, though they are usually short and do not last all day.

* **Some beach activities may be limited:** Water sports or sunbathing may not be as frequent as in peak summer months.

However, these are minor issues and rarely affect travelers’ enjoyment, as the weather and atmosphere still make Antalya a great destination in November.

## Is November Already Winter in Antalya?

No, November is not winter in Antalya. Winter usually begins in late December or January, so November still feels very much like autumn. The temperatures are mild, generally ranging from 18°C to 23°C during the day, and there is plenty of gentle sunshine to enjoy outdoor activities. For most travelers, Antalya in November feels comfortable, calm, and very enjoyable, rather than cold or harsh. The weather allows for sightseeing, beach walks, exploring nature, and relaxing without the crowds and heat of summer, making it a perfect time for a peaceful autumn visit.

## Comparing November With Other Months

* **Summer (June–August):** Very hot, crowded, and expensive, best for those who enjoy lively beach vacations.

* **September–October**: Warm and pleasant, ideal for beaches, sightseeing, and outdoor activities.

* **November:** Mild, peaceful, affordable, and the sea is still swimmable, making it a great choice for a relaxed trip.

* **December–February:** Cooler and rainier, less suitable for beach activities but still fine for exploring the city.

Overall, November offers the perfect balance between good weather, fewer crowds, and affordable travel, making it one of the best months to visit Antalya.

## Conclusion

November in Antalya is still very pleasant and far from the cold of winter. Daytime temperatures usually range between 18°C and 23°C, making sightseeing and outdoor activities comfortable and enjoyable. The sea remains warm enough for swimming, while evenings are cooler but still manageable with light layers. Rainfall increases slightly compared to the summer months, but it rarely disrupts travel plans, and short showers often refresh the air and scenery. With fewer tourists, quieter beaches, lower accommodation prices, and a calm, peaceful atmosphere, [**Antalya in November**](https://travel-antalya.com/) offers an excellent opportunity for relaxed and stress-free travel. Whether you want to explore historical sites, enjoy scenic nature walks, spend time along the coast, or simply unwind and soak in the mild weather, visiting Antalya in November can be a truly enjoyable and memorable experience.
