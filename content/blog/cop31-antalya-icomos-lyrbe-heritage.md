ICOMOS Türkiye and Antalya Bilim University are scheduled to present their cultural heritage and climate work on **Friday, 20 November 2026, from 16:00 to 16:30 Antalya time (UTC+3)**. The university lists **Screen 1 in the shared Council of Higher Education (CoHE/YÖK) area**, in the **COP31 Green Zone at Antalya Expo Center in Aksu**. This is a screen presentation, not a field visit.

## Why Lyrbe, Side and Termessos are on the programme

The presentation looks back at the university’s 11–16 November Cultural Heritage in Climate Action programme. Its published agenda included fieldwork at Lyrbe and Side and a separate visit to Termessos. Those locations connect the conference’s heritage agenda to archaeological sites in Antalya province. The [Ministry of Culture and Tourism lists Lyrbe as temporarily closed](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/turkiye/antalya/gezilecekyer/lyrbe-seleukeia-antik-kenti); the archival photo is not an invitation to visit.

## Before going to the Green Zone

Use the official [Green Zone registration page](https://cop31.tr/register-to-visit) and check the entry QR code in your account. Live capacity and any separate admission rule for the screen area have not been independently confirmed. See the [organiser’s event listing](https://cop31.antalya.edu.tr/en/events/cop31-antalya-icomos-cultural-heritage-and-climate-change-activities) and the [earlier field programme](https://cop31.antalya.edu.tr/en/announcements/cultural-heritage-in-climate-action-programme-in-antalya-on-11-16-november). The 20 November presentation is separate from the 18 November Tourism and Cultural Heritage Day.
