This Kaş meze and meyhane guide compares seven places to eat in town, using the menus, hours and contact details each restaurant publishes itself, all read on 26 November 2026. A meyhane night here means a table of cold meze, a few hot starters, grilled fish and a bottle of rakı, ideally within sight of the harbour. Prices below are in Turkish lira exactly as the restaurants print them; none of the menus carries a date, so treat them as a guide.

## How a meyhane evening works

A meyhane is a Turkish tavern built around food that is shared and drink that is sipped slowly. You start by choosing cold meze — olive-oil vegetables, yoghurt dips, cured fish — often from a display. Hot starters (ara sıcak) such as fried calamari or pastry rolls come next, then grilled fish or seafood. Rakı is the classic drink, mixed with water and ice. Plan on two to three hours. Our guide to [ocakbaşı and meyhane nights in Antalya](/blog/ocakbasi-and-meyhane-nights-in-antalya/) explains more of the etiquette.

## The restaurants

### Ehl-i Keyf: the long meze list

[Ehl-i Keyf](/restaurants/ehl-i-keyf-meyhane-kas/) is on Hükümet Caddesi No:3 in Andifli, "under olive trees" in the heart of Kaş, and opens **every day 11:00–02:00**, the longest hours in this guide. It prints the most detailed menu and rakı list.

- **Cold meze:** mostly ₺425–445, such as babagannuş, beetroot, Greek-style cacık and the house meze; octopus salad ₺650, marinated sea bass ₺600.
- **Hot dishes:** fried calamari ₺985, garlic-butter shrimp ₺995, grilled octopus ₺1,350.
- **Fish:** grilled sea bream ₺985, sea bass ₺1,025, swordfish ₺1,250. The daily sea fish is sold by the kilo, with no price printed. A seafood platter for two is ₺5,750.
- **Rakı:** a single ₺450, a double ₺600; 35 cl bottles ₺1,700–2,300 and 70 cl ₺2,750–3,600, depending on the brand.

The paçanga pastry costs ₺600 on the meze menu and ₺650 on the food menu. Phone +90 536 729 22 18 (also WhatsApp) or +90 553 701 46 69.

### Bahçe: meze on Uzunçarşı

[Bahçe](/restaurants/bahce-restaurant-kas/) is on Uzunçarşı, the old shopping street. Its menu pages were updated through 2026, but the site prints two street numbers (No. 33 on the Turkish page, No. 29 on the English one) and no opening hours.

- **Olive-oil meze:** fava, hummus and şakşuka ₺330, Cretan ezme ₺370, artichoke hearts ₺420.
- **Yoghurt meze:** haydari ₺330, mütebbel ₺340, "atom" (hot pepper in yoghurt) ₺330.
- **Seafood meze:** marinated sea bass, smoked anchovy and dried mackerel ₺500, lakerda ₺530.
- **Hot starters:** fried shrimp or calamari ₺880, fried octopus ₺1,200.
- **Fish:** grilled sea bass or bream ₺850, swordfish in paper ₺1,100, grilled octopus ₺1,200.

Bahçe's drinks page lists wine, rakı and beer but prints no prices. Call +90 242 836 23 70.

### Elia: sunset meze on the peninsula

[Elia](/restaurants/elia-restaurant-kas/) sits on the Çukurbağ Peninsula (Beyhan Cenkçi Caddesi No.7) and sells itself on sunset sea views. It opens **every day 14:00–00:00**.

- **Meze:** a yoghurt meze selection ₺300, olive-oil selection ₺350, seafood selection ₺500 — the closest thing to a set meze plate in town.
- **Hot starters:** fried calamari ₺850, butter shrimp ₺800, octopus with Elia's sauce ₺1,298, grilled octopus ₺1,789.
- **Fish:** grilled bream, sea bass or salmon ₺1,000 each; jumbo shrimp ₺1,200.
- **Drinks:** rakı by the bottle only — 35 cl ₺2,100, 70 cl ₺3,500 — local Likya Patara wine ₺450 a glass or ₺2,000 a bottle, beer ₺400.

Book by phone or WhatsApp on +90 505 987 89 00.

### Etna Fish House: fish by the portion

[Etna Fish House](/restaurants/etna-fish-house-kas/) is a family fish restaurant on Öztürk Sokak No:17 in Andifli, with tables out on the street. It opens **daily 11:00–00:00**, but says hours may vary by season. Mains come with a free salad bar.

- **Meze:** hummus and Cretan herb paste ₺350, samphire ₺390, lakerda ₺840.
- **Fish:** mackerel ₺740, grilled sole ₺890, bream ₺1,150, sea bass or fried red mullet ₺1,200. A fish wrap is ₺590.
- **Drinks:** the cheapest rakı in this guide — Yeni Rakı ₺350 a single, ₺450 a double; draught beer ₺240.

The menu notes that prices can change with the season and the catch. Call +90 531 402 04 04 to book.

### İnceden Bir Meyhane: fasıl on the water

İnceden Bir Meyhane, at Necipbey Caddesi No:15 in Andifli, is the one for music. It describes a seafront setting and **live fasıl nights** that start at sunset; its own guide, updated on 13 October 2026, says the schedule changes with the season and asks you to call ahead. It publishes no hours or prices. Book by WhatsApp or phone on +90 532 297 30 20.

### Redbox Meze & Cafe: small and early

[Redbox](/restaurants/redbox-meze-cafe-kas/) is a small café-restaurant opened in 2022, with a vine-shaded courtyard in its photos. It lists **16:00–22:00 daily**, so it suits an early evening. Meze start at ₺250 (fava, hummus, topik), shrimp saganaki is ₺700 and the fish of the day 900 TL. Like Elia, it gives its address as No. 7 on Beyhan Cenkçi Caddesi, so ask for directions. Call or WhatsApp 0552 508 26 95.

### Naturel: an open-air alternative

[Naturel](/restaurants/naturel-restaurant-kas/) is not a meyhane but a Mediterranean restaurant on Gürsoy Sokak No:6, off Uzun Çarşı, with open-air tables among the old stone houses. It opens **12:00–22:00 daily**. Starters run from ₺370 (falafel) to ₺800 (fried calamari or shrimp), and grilled sea bass is ₺850. It lists no rakı. The footer still says © 2019, but a menu image was uploaded in 2026.

## When to go

Every restaurant here prints year-round hours, but none mentions winter closing days. İnceden's guide calls May to October the liveliest time, and Etna warns that hours vary by season. Between November and April, phone before you walk over, especially early in the week.

In summer, book a day ahead for a sunset table. Kaş evenings fit neatly after a day at [Kaputaş Beach](/things-to-do/kaputas-beach/), on a [Kekova boat trip](/blog/kekova-boat-trips/) or [diving](/blog/diving-in-kas/).

## FAQ

### How much does a meze dinner in Kaş cost?

Cold meze cost about ₺250–650 a plate on the menus we read, grilled fish ₺850–1,200 a portion, and rakı ₺350–600 a glass. Our sample dinner for two with a small bottle of rakı came to about ₺6,400–7,100.

### Which Kaş meyhane has live music?

İnceden Bir Meyhane advertises live fasıl nights starting at sunset, with a schedule that changes by season. Call +90 532 297 30 20 to ask which nights.

### Which restaurant is open latest?

Ehl-i Keyf lists 11:00–02:00 every day. Elia and Etna close at midnight; Naturel and Redbox at 22:00.

### Are Kaş restaurants open in winter?

None of these restaurants publishes winter hours or closing days. Phone ahead from November to April.
