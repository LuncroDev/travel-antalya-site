Köprülü Canyon rafting is the most popular active day out from Antalya's resorts: a few hours on a cold, green mountain river between cliffs and cypress forest, with a lunch stop by the water. Most trips are gentle enough for first-timers and families, but it is still white water, and the rules on who may run a trip are stricter than many visitors realise. This guide explains the river, the rafting section, the season, the legal side and how to fit in the ruins of Selge. We checked the Ministry of Culture and Tourism, Manavgat Municipality and the relevant regulation on 19 November 2026. There is no official price list for rafting, so prices here are operators' own.

## Köprülü Canyon rafting at a glance

- **Where:** Köprülü Kanyon National Park, in the Taurus foothills between Serik and Manavgat. The ministry places the park 49 km northeast of Antalya; Manavgat Municipality puts it 60 km from Manavgat.
- **The river:** the Köprüçay, the ancient Eurymedon.
- **The water:** the ministry describes the beginners' section as class 2–3.
- **The season:** the Antalya culture and tourism directorate says rafting is possible for about eight months a year. No official source names the months.
- **Who runs it:** licensed operators only, under a 2011 tourism-sport regulation (see below).
- **Price:** no official tariff. The operator listings we saw started from €20 for children and €27 for adults.

## The national park

Köprülü Kanyon was declared a national park on 12 December 1973, according to the national park table in the Ministry of Environment's state-of-environment report. The Ministry of Culture and Tourism gives its area as 36,000 hectares, including part of the Köprüçay and the ancient city of Selge; Manavgat Municipality says 37,000.

The ministry calls the gorge, at 14 km, the longest canyon in Türkiye, with walls up to 100 m high. Pine, cypress and cedar forests cover the slopes, and the ministry singles out a 400-hectare forest of Mediterranean cypress as the park's most important plant community. It lists deer, mountain goats, bears, foxes, wolves, rabbits and badgers among the wildlife, though it adds that hunting has reduced their numbers, and says the side streams of the river hold plenty of trout. Our [Köprülü Canyon page](/things-to-do/koprulu-canyon/) gives the short version.

## The river and the rafting section

The Köprüçay rises in the Taurus Mountains and reaches the Mediterranean south of Serik. Further downstream it passes [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/), and the Antalya directorate's Aspendos page uses its ancient name, the Eurymedon.

The Ministry of Culture and Tourism describes the rafting route like this:

1. **The put-in.** Less experienced groups usually start at the Oluk Bridge. About 100 m upstream of the bridge the river widens into a calm pool.
2. **The first canyon.** It is about 3 km long, with class 2–3 water.
3. **The take-out for beginners.** A concrete bridge stands about 10 km downstream, and the ministry says novices should finish just before it.
4. **The second canyon.** The ministry says it is strictly off limits.

The Antalya directorate adds that parts of the course suit professional paddlers, while the route as a whole suits a wide range of ages.

Operators' figures for a standard trip are longer than the ministry's 10 km. Two listings on a booking platform described 14 km and 15 km routes. One said the trip starts between the Oluk Bridge and the Karabük Bridge, with its base camp at Karabük; the other gave 2.5–3 hours on the water. Ask your operator exactly where you put in and take out.

## What a rafting day looks like

Most trips are sold as a day out from the resorts, with hotel pick-up, a safety briefing at a riverside base, the river run and lunch. That pattern comes from the operators, not from any official source, so read what your booking includes. The two operator listings we read included insurance and lunch, provided helmets and life jackets, and charged extra for a wetsuit.

Things worth knowing before you book:

- **Age.** There is no legal minimum age in the regulation. Operators set their own: one listing said seven and over and asked for swimming ability, another said there was no age restriction. If you're bringing children, ask.
- **Swimming.** Even on class 2–3 water you can end up in the river. Tell the guide if anyone in the boat can't swim.
- **The water is cold.** The river comes out of the mountains, and the canyon is shaded. No official source publishes water temperatures, so take the wetsuit if it's offered.
- **What to bring.** Shoes that can get wet and stay on, a change of clothes, sun cream and a towel. Leave phones and valuables at the base unless you have a waterproof case.

## The rules: who may run a rafting trip

Rafting in Türkiye is covered by the *Turizm Amaçlı Sportif Faaliyet Yönetmeliği*, the Ministry of Culture and Tourism's regulation on sporting activities for tourism, published in the Official Gazette on 23 February 2011 (no. 27855). We read it from a copy published by TÜRSAB, the travel agents' association, and did not confirm later amendments. In summary, it says:

- Rafting is one of the listed tourism sporting activities.
- Operators need a permit from the provincial governorate for each type of activity, valid for one year.
- Activities may only take place in areas that the governorates set and announce each year.
- Staff need a licence issued by the relevant sports federation, and operators must carry first-aid equipment.
- Operators must insure both staff and participants against accidents and liability.
- Running an activity without a permit, or outside the designated area, is prohibited.

The regulation doesn't list specific kit such as helmets or life jackets, or set a guide-to-boat ratio; those details come from the federation's rules and each operator's practice. The Turkish Canoe Federation's statute, published in the Official Gazette on 29 June 2025, lists rafting among its branches.

In practice, that gives you three questions to ask any operator: Is your company licensed for rafting by the governorate? Are your guides licensed? Is participant insurance included?

## When to go

The Antalya directorate says rafting is possible for about eight months a year, and one operator listing ran its 2027 season from 13 April to 6 October, Monday to Saturday. Book ahead in the summer holidays. In winter, which is now, check with operators before planning a trip, and see our guide to [Antalya in winter](/blog/antalya-in-winter/) for other ideas.

## Getting there

The ministry describes two road approaches:

- **From Antalya:** east via Serik, then inland via Taşağıl to Beşkonak.
- **From Manavgat:** north via Taşağıl to Beşkonak.

The river forms a valley between the villages of Bolasan and Beşkonak. Most visitors come on an operator's transfer from [Side](/hotels/?area=Side), Belek or Antalya; if you're driving yourself, arrange a meeting point with the operator first.

## Add Selge to the day

The Oluk and Büğrüm bridges carried an ancient stone road that linked Selge with the Pamphylian plain, the ministry says. Beyond the Oluk Bridge, a mountain road climbs to Selge, in the village of Altınkaya (Zerk) at about 950 m. The ministry gives the distance as 12 km; the Antalya directorate says 13 km.

Selge's history goes back to the end of the second millennium BC, and it was the first place in Pisidia to mint coins. The remains include city walls, a theatre, a temple of Zeus, agoras, a colonnaded street, baths, cisterns and basilicas. No official opening hours or fee are published. ## FAQ

### Is Köprülü Canyon rafting suitable for beginners?

The ministry says the first canyon, from the Oluk Bridge, has class 2–3 water and that novices should stop before the concrete bridge about 10 km downstream. Operators run this stretch for first-timers, with a briefing and a guide in each boat.

### How old do children have to be?

No legal minimum age is set in the regulation. Operators decide: of two listings we read, one took children from seven, the other set no limit.

### When is the rafting season?

About eight months a year, according to the Antalya culture and tourism directorate. An operator's 2027 season ran from mid-April to early October.

### Is there an entry fee for the national park?

We couldn't find a current official fee for Köprülü Kanyon. The national parks authority's pages could not be opened when we checked, so ask your operator whether a park fee is included.
