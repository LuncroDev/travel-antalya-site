Adults-only hotels in Antalya come in two flavours: 16+ and 18+. Most set the line at 16, which suits couples and also lets you bring an older teenager. A few go further and accept only adults. Then there are big family resorts that keep one corner, a block of suites or a pool, for grown-ups. On 21 November 2026 we opened the official pages of the hotels below and noted exactly what each says about age, board and facilities. **We list a hotel as adults-only only when its own website says so.** Rules can change from season to season, so confirm the age limit when you book, especially if you're travelling with a 16- or 17-year-old.

## 16+ or 18+: what the difference means

At a 16+ hotel, a family with teenagers of 16 or 17 can book, and you may share the pool with them. At an 18+ hotel such as Robinson Çamyuva, everyone is an adult. Neither type takes younger children. If you want a hotel with no families at all, the 18+ rule is the stricter one. If you're a parent with a 16-year-old who wants a quieter holiday, a 16+ hotel lets them come too. Hotels can ask for ID, so book in the names of the people actually travelling.

## Kemer: Robinson Çamyuva (18+)

ROBINSON's Çamyuva club, south of Kemer, is the only hotel on our list that sets the limit at 18. Its page says "FOR ADULTS ONLY" and describes guests from 18 focusing on sport and relaxation. It is all-inclusive, with 332 rooms on two floors, three restaurants and four bars, about 240 m from a pebble beach. Sport is the point: tennis, padel, pickleball and beach volleyball. The WellFit Spa is about 210 m² and there is one freshwater pool, 19 × 14 m. ROBINSON gives Kemer centre as 10 km and Antalya Airport as 65 km, about 1 hour 15 minutes. When we checked, its booking offer showed a week from 16 April 2027, and our [Robinson Çamyuva page](/hotels/robinson-camyuva/) has the season details. ## Side and Kumköy: three 16+ choices

Three of the adults-only hotels on our list are in and around [Side](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/).

**[TUI BLUE Side](/hotels/tui-blue-side/)** states that the hotel "is for guests aged 16+ only". It is all-inclusive, with 234 rooms, a sandy beach, an outdoor pool of 56 × 13 m and a heated indoor pool of 18 × 8 m. Its spa covers more than 2,000 m². Three à la carte restaurants serve set dinners on given nights, and the evenings run to live music rather than big shows. TUI gives Antalya Airport as about 60 km and Side Old Town as about 7 km.

**[Trendy Palm Beach](/hotels/trendy-palm-beach/)** calls itself a 16+ hotel and says its rooms are for guests aged 16 and older. It is all-inclusive, in Kumköy, with rooms of 18 to 35 m². This is a livelier choice: the page describes indoor and outdoor pools with water slides and an animation team, plus Italian and world-cuisine à la carte restaurants and a spa.

**[Trendy Side Beach](/hotels/trendy-side-beach/)** is the town option, near the Temple of Apollo and the shops of Side. Trendy bills it as 16+ too, with rooms of 18 to 32 m², a Blue Flag private beach, indoor and outdoor pools, a spa, two à la carte restaurants and evening shows. It's the base on this list closest to the ruins.

Browse all [Side hotels](/hotels/?area=Side) or read our [Side day trip guide](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/) for the sights.

## Alanya: Sunprime C-Lounge (16+)

**[Sunprime C-Lounge](/hotels/sunprime-c-lounge/)** in the Oba area of Alanya advertises a "+16 adult only" concept on its home page, and its amenities list says "Adult only". It opened in 2015. Meals are open buffets, and the hotel lists two pools, a private beach area, a spa and fitness centre, and an airport shuttle at extra charge, without naming the airport. Its website still shows a 2023 copyright, so check the details directly. If you're flying in, see our [Gazipaşa Airport guide](/blog/gazipasa-alanya-airport-guide/) as well as Antalya; for the town, try [Alanya in a day](/blog/alanya-in-a-day/).

## Kalkan: Lures Hotel (16+)

**[Lures Hotel](/hotels/lures-hotel-kalkan/)** is the boutique choice, on Kalamar Bay at Kalkan, far to the west of Antalya. Its website says "Adults Only 16+", and its FAQ says it welcomes guests aged 16 and over. Unlike the others, it's bed and breakfast, not all-inclusive. There are six room types, from Superior rooms to a Duplex Villa. Junior Suites, Senior Suites and the villa have private pools, and the Penthouse has its own jacuzzi. The hotel has a private beach with cabanas, an infinity pool for in-house guests, an indoor spa pool, hammam and sauna, and an à la carte restaurant, Asedo, open 19:00–23:00. The hotel says Kalkan is reached through Dalaman or Antalya airport. Compare [Kalkan hotels](/hotels/?area=Kalkan).

## Resorts with adults-only areas

Some family resorts keep part of the hotel for adults. These are not adults-only hotels, so expect children around the grounds.

- **[Paloma Grida](/hotels/paloma-grida/), Belek:** its Adults-Only Lake Suites have direct pool access and private decks. The rest of the 529-room resort, 35 km from Antalya Airport, is aimed at families and has a kids' club.
- **[ROBINSON Nobilis](/hotels/robinson-nobilis/), Belek:** a family golf club with a relaxation pool for guests aged 16 and over, heated from about November to April, and an adults-only spa.
- **[Paloma Finesse Side](/hotels/paloma-finesse-side/):** the hotel's own pages call it a place for families with children, though its Palomaqua park has 12 adult-only slides.

If a hotel isn't on this list, check before you assume. [Barut Hemera](/hotels/barut-hemera/) near Side, for example, lists a children's club for ages 4–12, so it isn't adults-only.

## Adults-only in winter

None of these hotels publishes winter 2026–27 opening dates on the pages we read. ROBINSON's Çamyuva offer started in mid-April 2027. TUI BLUE Side lists its outdoor pool hours as all year and says its indoor pool is heated, while two of its restaurants close from the end of October. The Trendy hotels and Lures list indoor pools without saying whether they're heated. If you want sun and quiet between November and March, start with our guide to [Antalya hotels with heated pools](/blog/antalya-hotels-with-heated-pools-winter/) and then check the age rule.

## Before you book: a checklist

- **Read the rule on the hotel's own page.** A label on a booking site isn't enough, because some resorts keep only part of the hotel for adults.
- **Check the age line:** 16 or 18, and whether it applies to the whole hotel.
- **Ask about animation.** Adults-only doesn't always mean quiet: some 16+ hotels have slides and evening shows.
- **Check the board.** Most here are all-inclusive; Lures is bed and breakfast.
- **Check the season.** None of the hotels here published winter dates on the pages we read.

## FAQ

### Are there adults-only hotels in Antalya?

Yes. On their own websites, Robinson Çamyuva (18+), TUI BLUE Side, Trendy Palm Beach, Trendy Side Beach, Sunprime C-Lounge and Lures Hotel (all 16+) describe themselves as adults-only.

### What age is "adults only" at Antalya hotels?

Usually 16. Of the hotels we checked, only Robinson Çamyuva sets the limit at 18.

### Can I bring a 16-year-old to an adults-only hotel?

At a 16+ hotel, yes; at an 18+ hotel such as Robinson Çamyuva, no. Book in the names of everyone travelling, as ages can be checked.

### Are adults-only hotels in Antalya open in winter?

The hotels on our list don't publish winter 2026–27 dates on the pages we read. TUI BLUE Side gives all-year pool hours and has a heated indoor pool; check opening dates with any hotel before you book a winter stay.
