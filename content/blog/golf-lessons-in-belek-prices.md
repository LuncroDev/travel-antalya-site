Golf lessons in Belek cost €70 to €100 for a single private session at the clubs that publish a price, and a beginner course runs from €199 to €700 a person. The gap is wider than it looks, because clubs sell different units: most count 50 minutes, while Cornelia and Sueno count a full hour. On 17 November 2026 we opened the official lesson pages of every Belek club we could reach. Seven publish prices. All figures below are **as listed on 17 November 2026, in euros; confirm when you book**. Few clubs put a date on their lesson prices, so treat these as the rates going into winter 2026–27.

This guide is about the money. For how a first golf holiday works step by step, read [Belek golf for beginners](/blog/belek-golf-for-beginners/). Children's rates are in [junior golf in Belek](/blog/junior-golf-in-belek/).

## Private lessons: €70 to €100

- **ROBINSON Nobilis:** €70 for a 50-minute VOLVO Private Lesson, the lowest price we found. Content is tailored to you, subject to the professional's availability.
- **Sueno:** €80 for 60 minutes.
- **Cornelia:** €80 an hour with the Teaching Professional, €90 with the Head Professional. Every individual lesson uses TrackMan, and a video analysis follows by email.
- **Gloria:** €95 for 50 minutes with a PGA or TPGA professional. Practice balls, equipment and video analysis are included.
- **Cullinan Links, National and Carya:** €100 for 50 minutes. National and Carya belong to the Regnum group and print identical lesson tables.

Cullinan also lists a "PGA Professional Master Program by Cahit Kaya" at €200 for 50 minutes, twice its standard rate. The page says nothing else about it, so ask what you get for the extra.

Per minute, Cornelia's Teaching Professional and Sueno come out cheapest: €80 for a full hour is less than ROBINSON's €70 for 50 minutes.

## Lesson packs: where the discounts are

Most clubs cut the price when you buy several lessons, by very different amounts.

- **Gloria:** three 50-minute lessons for €240 or five for €400. Both work out at €80 a lesson, so there is no extra saving after three.
- **Cullinan:** three for €270 or six for €540, which is €90 a lesson either way.
- **National and Carya:** €275 for three, €525 for six, €750 for nine and €925 for twelve. The twelve-lesson pack comes to about €77 a lesson, the deepest volume discount on a 50-minute lesson among the clubs we checked.
- **Cornelia:** €255 for three hours, €400 for five, and a "6-hour bulk buy" of four 90-minute sessions for €450, all with the Head Professional. With the Teaching Professional the same packs cost €225, €350 and €390. The bulk buy works out at €75 or €65 an hour.
- **Sueno:** five 60-minute "Advanced Golf Lessons" for €320, or €64 an hour.

## Beginner courses and golf schools

### Gloria: four-day packages

Gloria's academy lists a **4 Day Beginners Package** at €700 and a **4 Day Advanced Package** at €800, per person. Both run four hours a day for four days, in groups of two to six, with equipment and range balls included. That is 16 hours, so the beginners' course costs about €44 an hour by our sums.

The beginners' course covers swing basics, putting, chipping, using a full set of clubs and playing on the course. The advanced course adds full-swing video analysis, a short-game clinic, trouble shots and course management. Gloria's academy page says the beginners' package ends with a Gloria Golf Resort certificate signed by David Clare. Gloria's separate four-day packages page shows no prices.

### Cornelia: golf schools at four pupils per pro

Cornelia runs its schools with no more than four pupils per instructor:

- **Four-day school, 8 hours** (two hours a day, morning or afternoon): €480 with the Head Professional or €440 with the Teaching Professional for one pupil, falling to €360 or €320 in a group of four.
- **Five-day school, 12 hours** (ten hours of tuition, then a two-hour playing lesson on the Faldo course on the last day): €600 or €540 for one pupil, €420 or €360 in a group of four.

A five-day, 10-hour version sits between them at €550 or €500 for one pupil. The page lists prices for one to four people without saying whether they are per person or per group. Ask before you pay.

### ROBINSON Nobilis: VOLVO courses

ROBINSON's courses are the cheapest we found:

- **VOLVO Beginners:** €199 per person for six hours of tuition in 50-minute sessions, in groups of three or more. Grip, address, backswing, follow-through and course etiquette are on the syllabus. Balls and clubs are provided.
- **VOLVO Intermediate:** €199 per person, same format, covering irons, woods and the short game.
- **VOLVO Golf License:** €495 per person for twelve training sessions plus three on the course, 50 minutes each, ending in a theory and practical exam and a certificate.

There is also a free 60-minute clinic at least once a week. The catch is timing: the same page dates its golf packages, the licence course included, to travel between 21 June and 15 November 2026. Ask whether these prices hold for winter.

### National and Sueno

National runs a six-day intensive course: two lessons a day, twelve in all, for up to four people. Equipment, unlimited range balls and a certificate are included; the nine-hole green fee is not. National publishes no price for it.

Sueno's factsheet says beginners must take lessons before they play. It lists a "PRO Package Program" of nine holes and ten hours for €899, without explaining what it covers.

## Playing lessons: check what's included

Prices look similar until you read what they include.

- **Cornelia:** the only club that says its price includes a green fee, for nine holes on the Faldo course. One person pays €255 with the Head Professional or €235 with the Teaching Professional; the price per person falls to €195 or €185 for two, and €175 or €165 for three. Buggy and trolley are extra.
- **Gloria:** €180 per person for nine holes, €300 for eighteen. The pages we opened don't say whether the green fee is included.
- **Sueno:** €180 for one, €140 for two and €110 for three, over nine holes. The factsheet doesn't say whether that is per person.
- **National and Carya:** €200 for nine holes with one golfer, €300 with two and €350 with three; €400, €600 and €700 for eighteen. National adds green fee and buggy on top, and allows up to 2 hours 15 minutes for nine holes and 4 hours 30 minutes for eighteen. Carya doesn't say what its price covers.
- **Cullinan:** €300 for one, €400 for two, €500 for three and €600 for four, over nine holes. A bag, tees and balls are included; the green fee and buggy are not.

To compare Cullinan or National with Cornelia, add a nine-hole green fee. Our [Belek green fees for winter 2026–27](/blog/belek-green-fees-winter-2026-27/) guide has those rates.

## Couples and group lessons

Sharing a pro cuts the cost:

- **Two people, one lesson:** €100 at Sueno (60 min), €140 for a couple at Gloria, €150 at National and Carya, €180 at Cullinan (all 50 min). Cornelia lists €85 or €75 at two people, but doesn't say whether that is each or for the pair.
- **Gloria groups of three to six:** €55 per person for one 50-minute lesson. Its individual-lessons page prices three group lessons at €120 per person and five at €230. Oddly, that makes the shorter pack the better deal per lesson.
- **National and Carya groups:** €70 per person for two lessons with five to ten golfers, €65 with eleven to fifteen and €60 with sixteen or more.
- **Sueno groups:** €30 per person for a 60-minute lesson with four to nine people, €20 over ten and €25 over 25. That last step goes up, not down, so ask.

## Studio time and club fitting

Gloria publishes the most detail. Its academy has 48 covered range bays with TrackMan screens, and it rents its **TrackMan Performance Studio** for €40 and its **PuttView** room for €60, both per 50 minutes. PuttView X costs €30 a session. Club work is priced "from": €10 for putter loft and lie or wedge grinding, €20 a club for regripping and €60 a shaft for reshafting.

Carya's pros teach from a TaylorMade Performance Lab, and National's club fitters are trained by TaylorMade, but neither publishes a fitting price.

## Before you book

1. **Compare by the minute.** A €80 hour is better value than a €95 lesson of 50 minutes.
2. **Ask whether group prices are per person.** Cornelia, Cullinan and Sueno don't say.
3. **Ask what a playing lesson includes.** Only Cornelia says it includes the green fee.
4. **Check the cancellation rule.** Gloria charges 50% if you cancel less than 12 hours ahead.
5. **Confirm winter prices at ROBINSON**, whose golf packages are dated to 15 November 2026.
6. **Give children's ages.** Cullinan takes 20% off for under-13s, and National and Carya charge €75 for an under-14's lesson.

## FAQ

### How much does a golf lesson cost in Belek?

As listed on 17 November 2026, a private lesson costs €70 for 50 minutes at ROBINSON Nobilis, €80 to €90 an hour at Cornelia, €80 an hour at Sueno, €95 for 50 minutes at Gloria and €100 for 50 minutes at Cullinan, National and Carya.

### What is the cheapest beginner golf course in Belek?

ROBINSON Nobilis lists VOLVO Beginners at €199 per person for six hours in groups of three or more. Cornelia's four-day, eight-hour school costs €440 to €480 for one pupil, and Gloria's four-day package is €700 for 16 hours.

### Is the green fee included in a playing lesson?

Cornelia is the only club that says so: its playing lessons include a nine-hole green fee on the Faldo course. Cullinan and National charge the green fee and buggy on top. Gloria, Carya and Sueno don't say.

### Do I get a handicap after taking lessons in Belek?

Not from lessons alone. Gloria gives its own certificate after eight or more hours, but says a handicap certificate requires club membership. National and ROBINSON also award course certificates; ask what they let you play, and read [Belek golf handicap limits and dress codes](/blog/belek-golf-handicap-limits-and-dress-codes/).
