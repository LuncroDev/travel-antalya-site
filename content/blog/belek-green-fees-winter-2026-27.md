Belek green fees drop sharply in winter, but we could read current prices for only four of the eleven clubs in our directory. We opened every club's official site on 12 November 2026 and noted the 2026–27 winter rates at Carya, Cullinan Links, National and Cornelia. For the rest, we say what each club does publish. All prices below are **published rack rates checked 12 November 2026; confirm when you book**, because every club reserves the right to change them without notice.

This guide is about the money. For the weather, maintenance dates and the shape of the golf year, read our [Belek golf season guide](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/). For floodlit rounds, see [night golf in Belek](/blog/night-golf-in-belek/).

## Belek green fees at a glance: winter 2026–27

Each club draws its own winter dates. All four start the cheap season on 1 December 2026, but they end it on different days in February.

| Club | Winter rate period | 18 holes (rack) | 9 holes (rack) | Floodlit | Hotel-guest 18 holes | Buggy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Carya](/golf/carya-golf-club/) | 1 Dec 2026 – 10 Feb 2027 | €120 | €85 | 18 holes €130, 9 holes €95 | €95 (Regnum guests) | Extra: €60 for 18 holes |
| [Cullinan Links](/golf/cullinan-links-golf-club/) | 1 Dec 2026 – 11 Feb 2027 | €140 | €100 | 18 holes €170, 9 holes €120 | €130 (Cullinan and Titanic guests) | Shared buggy included |
| [National](/golf/national-golf-club/) | 1 Dec 2026 – 10 Feb 2027 | €95 | €65 | 9 holes €75 | €75 (Regnum guests) | Extra: €60 for 18 holes |
| [Cornelia](/golf/cornelia-golf-club/) | 1 Dec 2026 – 13 Feb 2027 | €120 | €95 | Not listed | Not shown separately | Extra: €50 for 18 holes |

National is the lowest published 18-hole rack rate in winter. Cullinan is the highest, but it is the only one of the four whose green fee includes a buggy.

Here is the 18-hole rack rate either side of the winter band:

| Club | Autumn 2026 | Winter 2026–27 | Spring 2027 starts |

The change-over dates matter if your trip straddles mid-February: a week from 8 to 15 February would pay winter rates for the first rounds and spring rates for the rest.

## Club by club: what the published rates include

### Carya Golf Club

Carya's rate card covers 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027, although its heading still reads "2025-2026", so check the season when you book. Guests of Regnum hotels, part of the same group, pay the lower "Regnum rack rate".

The green fee is for the round alone. A golf car costs €60 for 18 holes or €40 for nine. A trolley is €12, hire clubs €40 a day, and unlimited range balls €50 for non-Regnum guests (a single token is €6). A reserved buggy cannot be cancelled on the day.

Regnum guests with green fees eat and drink at the clubhouse on the all-inclusive concept (not at the buggy bars or halfway house), and tea and coffee are free. Under-16s get 50% off green fees and packages, calculated from the rack rate. The handicap limit is 36 for men and women. Our [Regnum Carya hotel page](/hotels/regnum-carya/) covers the resort.

### Cullinan Links Golf Club

Cullinan has the most detailed rate card we found, and it already shows next winter too. Its table has two columns: rack rate, and a guest rate for Cullinan and Titanic hotel guests.

What is included: one shared buggy per two players and one range token per person per day. Sharing a buggy is compulsory; a single buggy, or a non-golfer riding along, costs a €30 supplement in winter (€40 in autumn and spring). The range fee is €20, and free for Cullinan Belek and Titanic guests.

Winter extras:

- **Packages:** two 18-hole rounds for €270 (guest €250), three for €390 (guest €360). They are paid in full at reception before the first round and cannot be changed or refunded.
- **Floodlit rounds:** €170 for 18 holes or €120 for nine. They need at least eight golfers, check-in an hour before, and come with a free food and drink menu until 18:00.
- **Food and drink:** the club says its all-inclusive concept applies to green fees in the winter season only, and not from 16 June to 31 August.

Juniors up to 16 get 50% off green fees, but not on the packages. The handicap limits are 36 for men and 44 for ladies, and under-18s may not drive a buggy. Planning further ahead? The card shows €150 rack for 1 December 2027 to 11 February 2028. See [Cullinan Belek](/hotels/cullinan-belek/) for the hotel.

### National Golf Club

National's 2026–27 winter rates are on its night-golf page. Several other pages on the club's site still carry an older table that runs only from January to September 2026, so make sure any quote you get uses the 2026–27 dates.

From 1 December 2026 to 10 February 2027, 18 holes cost €95 (Regnum guests €75) and nine holes €65 (€60). The floodlit nine-hole round is €75 (€70). A golf car is €60 for 18 holes or €40 for nine.

Regnum Carya guests with green fees get clubhouse food and drink on the all-inclusive plan. Under-16s pay half the rack rate. A handicap certificate is required, with a maximum of 36. One conflict to note: the night-golf page says only over-18s may drive a buggy, while the club's "About" page says under-16s may not.

### Cornelia Golf Club

Cornelia's price page sets out three seasons, A, B and C, each marked "Rack Rate". The cheapest, C, covers 1 December 2026 to 13 February 2027 (and 16 June to 31 August 2027):

- 18 holes €120, nine holes €95
- Golf car €50 for 18 holes, €25 for nine
- Trolley €7, electric trolley €25
- Range token (30 balls) €4, hire clubs €40
- All-day ticket €170

Season A, from 16 September to 30 November and from 14 February to 31 May, is €190 for 18 holes. The page also says special rates are only for guests staying at Cornelia, without showing them. Staying elsewhere? Ask if you can book, and at what price. The handicap limits are 28 for men and 36 for ladies, and only soft spikes are allowed. See [Cornelia Diamond](/hotels/cornelia-diamond-golf-resort-spa/) for the resort.

## Clubs that don't publish green fees online

Seven clubs showed no current green-fee table on the official pages we opened. Book through the club and ask for a written quote.

- **[Antalya Golf Club](/golf/antalya-golf-club/)** (PGA Sultan and Pasha): the club's site has a green-fee page, but it would not open for our check. The golf pages of Kempinski The Dome, the hotel next door, give no rates and ask you to contact the resort.
- **[Gloria](/golf/gloria-golf-club/)**: the golf and course pages carry no prices. Gloria says its hotel guests get preferred green-fee rates, and its Glorian Member Club lists green-fee discounts of 10% and 20% (and 25% and 50% off buggy hire) for its Diamond and Platinum tiers.
- **[Kaya Palazzo](/golf/kaya-palazzo-golf-club/)**: no rates on the course page; it gives a club phone number. The handicap limits are 28 for men and 36 for women.
- **[Montgomerie Maxx Royal](/golf/montgomerie-maxx-royal-golf-club/)**: no prices on the club or resort pages. The contact form has an "Only Green Fee" enquiry option.
- **[ROBINSON Nobilis](/golf/robinson-nobilis-golf-club/)**: the resort page names a single green fee, a twilight green fee for afternoon tee times and three- to five-round packages, but shows no prices, and their travel period ends on 15 November 2026. Packages are booked through ROBINSON's service centre.
- **[Sueno](/golf/sueno-golf-club/)**: neither the site nor its 2025 factsheet lists green fees; tee times are requested by form. The factsheet says guests in Sueno Deluxe's Island Villas get "unlimited green fee conditions".
- **[Lykia Links](/golf/lykia-links-antalya/)**: the golf page has no prices. Read the closure warning on our profile before planning a winter round there.

## Twilight, nine-hole and floodlit rates

None of the four winter rate cards we could read lists a twilight rate, and ROBINSON's twilight package ends on 15 November. What the published cards offer instead:

- **Nine holes:** all four published cards price a nine-hole round, from €65 at National to €100 at Cullinan. Cullinan and National both say an 18-hole fee cannot be split into two nines.
- **Floodlit rounds:** the winter premium is €10 at Carya (€130 for 18 holes) and at National (€75 for nine), and €30 at Cullinan (€170 for 18).

Carya's floodlights come on early in winter: its table gives 14:15 in December. Our [night golf guide](/blog/night-golf-in-belek/) covers start times and booking.

## What a winter round really costs

Cullinan's €140 includes a shared buggy and a range token; Carya, National and Cornelia charge for the buggy separately. If those golf-car prices are per cart, two players sharing would each pay roughly:

- **National:** €95 + €30 = **€125**
- **Cullinan:** **€140**, buggy included
- **Cornelia:** €120 + €25 = **€145**
- **Carya:** €120 + €30 = **€150**

None of the cards says whether the golf-car price is per cart or per player, so ask, and ask if you may walk with a trolley instead. None of the four mentions hotel transfers.

## Booking tips for winter green fees

1. **Check which band your dates fall in.** The winter rate ends on 10, 11 or 13 February depending on the club.
2. **Ask for the hotel-guest rate.** It is €25 less at Carya and €20 less at National for Regnum guests, and €10 less at Cullinan for Cullinan and Titanic guests.
3. **Carry your handicap certificate.** The limits run from 28 for men at Cornelia to 44 for ladies at Cullinan.
4. **Book the buggy with the tee time.** Carya's card says a reserved buggy can't be cancelled on the day.
5. **Mention juniors.** Carya, Cullinan and National give under-16s half the rack rate, with conditions. Cornelia's card has no junior rate.
6. **Read package terms.** Cullinan's multi-round packages are prepaid and non-refundable.

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest green fee in Belek this winter?

Of the clubs that publish prices, National is cheapest: €95 for 18 holes from 1 December 2026 to 10 February 2027, or €75 for Regnum guests. Add €60 for a golf car.

### Are Belek green fees cheaper in winter?

Yes, by a lot. Published 18-hole rack rates fall by €70 to €80 between November and December: Carya from €195 to €120, Cullinan from €220 to €140, National from €170 to €95 and Cornelia from €190 to €120.

### Do Belek green fees include a buggy?

At Cullinan Links, yes: one shared buggy per two players and a range token. Carya, National and Cornelia charge for a golf car separately, at €50 to €60 for 18 holes.

### Which Belek golf clubs don't publish green fees?

Gloria, Kaya Palazzo, Montgomerie Maxx Royal, ROBINSON Nobilis, Sueno and Lykia Links showed no current green-fee table on the pages we opened. Antalya Golf Club has a green-fee page, but it would not load for us. Ask the club for a written quote.
