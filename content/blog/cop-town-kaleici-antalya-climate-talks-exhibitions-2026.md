Muratpaşa Municipality’s COP TOWN brings a separate programme of climate discussions and exhibitions to Kaleiçi during COP31. These listings are in Antalya’s old town, not at the conference venue in Aksu. Three afternoon talks close the programme, alongside exhibitions that the municipality lists through Friday, 20 November.

## Last three talks

All times are Antalya local time (TRT).

- **Wednesday, 18 November, 14:00–16:00 | Hıdırlık Terrace.** The municipality scheduled a conversation with historian and actor Pelin Batu, actor Ahmet Mümtaz Taylan and Muratpaşa mayor Ümit Uysal, moderated by Zeynel Lüle.
- **Thursday, 19 November, 14:00–16:00 | Hadrian’s Gate (Üç Kapılar).** The climate-talk series turns to water, with moderator Güray Doğan and speakers İbrahim Ethem Karadirek and Mine Aydoğan.
- **Friday, 20 November, 14:00–16:00 | Hadrian’s Gate (Üç Kapılar).** The final climate talk looks at city life, with moderator Güray Doğan and speakers Senem Atvur and Erdem Vardar.

The talk listings do not specify prices, registration, or interpretation. Check the municipality’s latest announcement if those details will affect your visit.

## Pair the talks with an exhibition

Arif Durukan’s sculptures at Atelya Art Hotel (Civelek Sokak No. 21) use locally quarried Limra stone to portray faces inspired by ancient Lycia. The official programme lists the exhibition through 20 November. At Hadrian’s Gate, Serkan Türk’s video exhibition explores how objects become waste, also through 20 November. Özgül Kahraman’s “Silent Contact”, at the Suna İnan Kıraç Kaleiçi Museum (Kocatepe Sokak No. 22), uses the fingerprint as a metaphor for the mark people leave on nature. The municipality’s brochure lists the exhibition dates, not daily gallery hours; confirm directly before setting off.
