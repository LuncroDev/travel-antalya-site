December is one of Antalya's busiest months for events outside the beach season. This calendar lists what we could confirm for December 2026 on an organiser's or ticket seller's own page on 20 November, week by week, with links to the full listing for each. It is a working list: promoters add dates through the month, so check our [events calendar](/events/) for updates. For weather and packing, read [is December too cold in Antalya?](/blog/is-december-too-cold-in-antalya-weather-rain-sea-conditions/). For Christmas and New Year, our [Christmas in Antalya](/blog/christmas-in-antalya/) guide goes into detail.

## 30 November – 6 December

- **2 Dec:** [Klara, Money and Love](/events/klara-dengi-i-lyubov-2026-12-02-kemer/), a Russian-language play with Turkish subtitles, Kemer, 19:30. [Mahşer-i Cümbüş](/events/mahser-i-cumbus-2026-12-02-muratpasa/) at Türkan Şoray, 20:30. [Murda Tour](/events/murda-tour-2026-12-02-muratpasa/) at The Bar Kaleiçi, 22:00. [İlker Gümüşoluk](/events/ilker-gumusoluk-stand-up-2026-12-02-konyaalti/) stand-up in Konyaaltı, 20:30 (Turkish).
- **3 Dec:** [Women's EHF EURO 2026, Day 1](/events/womens-ehf-euro-2026-day-1/) at Antalya Sports Hall, 18:30. [Allame, Hidra and Bossy](/events/allame-hidra-bossy-konseri-2026-12-03-muratpasa/) at Holly Stone, 22:00.
- **4 Dec:** [Madrigal](/events/madrigal-konseri-2026-12-04-muratpasa/) at The Bar Kaleiçi, 22:00. [Yung Ouzo](/events/yung-ouzo-2026-12-04-muratpasa/) at Holly Stone, 22:00.
- **5 Dec:** [Women's EHF EURO 2026, Day 2](/events/womens-ehf-euro-2026-day-2/), 18:30. [Dedublüman](/events/dedubluman-konseri-2026-12-05-muratpasa/) at The Bar Kaleiçi, 21:00.
- **6 Dec:** [Bir Evlenme Teklifi](/events/bir-evlenme-teklifi-2026-12-06-konyaalti/), theatre, Konyaaltı, 20:00. It is also St Nicholas Day, the anniversary the Orthodox Church marks on 6 December; see our [Demre and St Nicholas notes](/blog/christmas-in-antalya/) and the [Myra and St Nicholas Church guide](/blog/myra-and-st-nicholas-church-demre/).

Golf: the [Regnum Carya Pro-Am](/blog/belek-golf-tournaments-winter-2026-27/) runs 29 November to 6 December and the Maxx Royal Golf Cup final starts on 6 December.

## 7 – 13 December

- **7 Dec:** [Women's EHF EURO 2026, Day 3](/events/womens-ehf-euro-2026-day-3/), 18:30. [Ruh Salatası](/events/ruh-salatasi-2026-12-07-alanya/), theatre in Alanya, 20:00.
- **8 Dec:** [Efkan Şeşen](/events/efkan-sesen-2026-12-08-muratpasa/), concert, Türkan Şoray, 20:30.
- **9 Dec:** [Ezginin Günlüğü](/events/ezginin-gunlugu-konseri-2026-12-09-muratpasa/) at Holly Stone, 21:00. [Fazlı Polat](/events/fazli-polat-saka-saka-2026-12-09-muratpasa/), comedy, 20:30.
- **10 Dec:** [Yeni Türkü](/events/yeni-turku-konseri-2026-12-10-konyaalti/) at The Sponge, 22:00. [Bu Hikaye Senden Uzun Osman](/events/bu-hikaye-senden-uzun-osman-2026-12-10-muratpasa/), theatre, 20:30.
- **11 Dec:** [Emrah Kaman](/events/emrah-kaman-yuksek-gelirim-2-2026-12-11-muratpasa/), comedy, 20:30. [Wegh](/events/wegh-konseri-2026-12-11-kepez/), Kepez, 21:00.
- **12 Dec:** [Birsen Tezer](/events/birsen-tezer-2026-12-12-konyaalti/), 21:00. [Emre Aydın](/events/emre-aydin-konseri-2026-12-12-muratpasa/) at The Bar Kaleiçi, 21:00. [Rumba](/events/rumba-2026-12-12-kemer/), Russian-language theatre, Kemer, 19:00.
- **13 Dec:** [Türkçe Pop Fest](/events/turkce-pop-fest-2026-12-13-konyaalti/), 22:00. [Sefa Doğanay](/events/sefa-doganay-tek-mi-sandiniz-2026-12-13-muratpasa/), comedy, 20:00.

Golf: the Turkish Amateur Open (10–12 December, Kaya Palazzo) and the Golf Mad Turkish Junior Open (from 13 December, Gloria) are on the TGF calendar; see our [tournament guide](/blog/belek-golf-tournaments-winter-2026-27/).

## 14 – 20 December

- **14 Dec:** [Shirley Valentine](/events/shirley-valentine-2026-12-14-muratpasa/), theatre, Türkan Şoray, 20:30.
- **15 Dec:** [Erkek Aklı Oksimoron](/events/erkek-akli-oksimoron-2026-12-15-muratpasa/), theatre, 20:30.
- **18 Dec:** the Antalya State Symphony Orchestra's İsmet İnönü Commemoration Concert, per Hürriyet Daily News; see our [orchestra guide](/blog/antalya-state-symphony-orchestra-2026-27/).
- **19 Dec:** [Emir Can İğrek](/events/emir-can-igrek-konseri-2026-12-19-muratpasa/) at The Bar Kaleiçi, 22:00.
- **20 Dec:** [Ayna](/events/ayna-konseri-2026-12-20-muratpasa/), concert, Türkan Şoray, 20:00.

## 21 – 31 December

- **21 Dec:** the start of winter, and the date GoTürkiye gives for the *nar kırma* pomegranate tradition; see [Antalya's winter citrus](/blog/finike-oranges-antalya-winter-citrus/).
- **22 Dec:** [Özel Bir Rica](/events/ozel-bir-rica-2026-12-22-muratpasa/), theatre. Biletix lists 20:30 and iTicket 20:00, so check.
- **23 Dec:** [Aşk Listesi](/events/ask-listesi-2026-12-23-konyaalti/), theatre, Konyaaltı, 20:00.
- **24 Dec:** [Hamlet](/events/hamlet-2026-12-24-konyaalti/), theatre, Konyaaltı, 20:00.
- **25 Dec:** [Paptırcem](/events/paptircem-konseri-2026-12-25-muratpasa/) at Holly Stone, 21:00, and the orchestra's New Year's Special Concert.
- **31 Dec:** New Year's Eve. Resort galas dominate; see [Christmas in Antalya](/blog/christmas-in-antalya/) for how they work. We have not confirmed a public street celebration for 2026.
