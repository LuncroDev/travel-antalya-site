In winter, when the beach is quiet, the best evenings in Antalya often happen at a grill or a meze table. Two Turkish formats do most of the work: the **ocakbaşı**, where meat cooks over charcoal and you can often watch it, and the **meyhane**, a meze-and-drinks house built for a long, shared evening. This guide explains how both work and lists five Antalya places whose own websites we checked in November 2026.

## Ocakbaşı or meyhane?

An **ocakbaşı** (literally "by the hearth") is a grill house. You order meze to start, then one or two grilled dishes such as Adana (spiced minced lamb), Urfa (milder), lamb shish or liver. **Közz Liman**'s site describes meat grilled over embers and meze made fresh each day.

A **meyhane** is built around the table: you order several cold and hot meze, then fish or a main, and drink rakı, the aniseed spirit, or wine as the evening runs on. Ayar Meyhanesi in Kaleiçi says it has served in the old town since 2017 and focuses on seafood and meze.

The two overlap: Közz Liman calls itself a grill house "run with a meyhane's love of meze and rakı".

## How the evening runs

1. **Meze first.** Order a few cold ones to share. Typical Antalya choices include *hibeş* (a tahini, cumin and lemon dip), *atom* (strained yoghurt with dried pepper and garlic) and Girit ezmesi (cheeses with walnut and herbs).
2. **Hot starters.** *İçli köfte* (stuffed bulgur shells) and *yaprak ciğer* (thin-sliced liver) are common.
3. **The grill.** Adana, Urfa, shish or *ali nazik* (lamb mince on smoky aubergine and yoghurt).
4. **Dessert.** *Kabak tatlısı* (candied pumpkin with tahini and walnuts) is the local favourite.
5. **Pace yourself.** Orders arrive in stages, and the table is yours for the evening.

Rakı is usually served with water and ice, and people mix it themselves. If you do not drink alcohol, ayran or tea is normal.

## Five places to start

### Közz Liman Ocakbaşı & Meze, Konyaaltı

Open daily 14:00–00:00, with a full menu in Turkish, English and Russian. Prices on 13 November 2026: cold meze ₺285 each, Adana or Urfa ₺800, lamb shish ₺950, ali nazik ₺1,000, mixed grill for one ₺2,750; a single rakı started at ₺360. Book by WhatsApp or phone on +90 532 485 67 07. [Profile](/restaurants/kozz-liman-ocakbasi-konyaalti/).

### Çatı Ocakbaşı, Şirinyalı

Open daily 14:00–01:00 and promising sea views, with about 90 cold meze. On 13 November: Adana or Urfa ₺950, beyti or ali nazik ₺1,070, küşleme ₺1,300. The site's home-page prices for some dishes are lower than its menu's, so confirm. Call 0549 242 72 72. [Profile](/restaurants/cati-ocakbasi-antalya/).

### Şişçi İbo, Lara and Konyaaltı

A family grill house serving Antalya's *şiş köfte* with tahini *piyaz* since 1974. The site says the meat is male goat raised in the Beydağları and served in 180 g portions. It publishes no hours or reservation system, so call the branch. Read [the piyaz guide](/blog/antalya-piyaz-tahini-bean-dish/). [Profile](/restaurants/sisci-ibo/).

### Ertan Usta, Kepez

A no-frills lunch place for charcoal köfte and piyaz, served as set plates. Closed on Fridays. It is the local-lunch end of the same food culture. [Profile](/restaurants/ertan-usta-aksu-kofte-piyaz-kepez/).

### Ayar Meyhanesi, Kaleiçi

A meyhane on Hesapçı Sokak in the old town. Its site mentions the İncili gastronomy guide, which is not a Michelin guide. Ask about the evening's music programme and table area when you reserve. [Profile](/restaurants/ayar-meyhanesi/).

## Tips

- **Prices change.** The lira prices above were published on 13 November 2026 and will move; use them to compare, not to budget.
- **Ask about the card machine.** Some sites do not publish payment details.
- **Book at weekends.** Winter is quiet, but local groups fill grill houses on Friday and Saturday nights.
- **Combine with a walk.** [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/) before dinner works well; see our [Kaleiçi walking route](/blog/kaleici-walking-route/).

## FAQ

### What is an ocakbaşı?

A Turkish grill house where meat is cooked over charcoal, often at a counter you can see.

### What is the difference between a meyhane and a restaurant?

A meyhane is built around meze and drinks, and meals last longer.

### Is ocakbaşı food spicy?

Adana is spiced; Urfa is milder. Ask the waiter.
