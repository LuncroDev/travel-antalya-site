Gazipaşa–Alanya Airport (GZP) is the small airport on the coast east of Alanya. If you're staying in Alanya, Mahmutlar or Kestel and a flight from home lands there, it can save you a long drive from Antalya. The catch is choice: far fewer airlines fly to Gazipaşa, and several of its routes come and go with the seasons. This guide covers where the airport is, who flies there, how to get to your hotel and what you'll find in the terminal. **Everything here comes from the airport's own website, opened on 21 November 2026.** The airport is run by TAV Airports.

## Where Gazipaşa Airport is

The airport is 7 km from the town of Gazipaşa, in [Gazipaşa district](/destinations/gazipasa/) east of Alanya. Its own table of road distances:

| Demirtaş | 20 |
| Kargıcak | 25 |
| Mahmutlar | 30 |

So the airport is closest to Alanya's eastern resorts and to Gazipaşa itself, and it also serves Anamur and Bozyazı further east.

## Who flies to Gazipaşa

The airport's destinations page, headed "scheduled and charter", sums up 14 countries, 20 international destinations and 12 airlines. As listed on 21 November 2026:

- **Germany:** Cologne, Nuremberg and Düsseldorf (Corendon).
- **Scandinavia and Finland:** Copenhagen (SAS, Norwegian, Sunclass, Corendon Dutch, Copenhagen Air Taxi), Billund and Aalborg (Corendon Dutch), Stockholm-Arlanda (SAS, Sunclass), Oslo (SAS, Norwegian, Sunclass) and Helsinki (Finnair).
- **Netherlands and Belgium:** Amsterdam and Groningen (Corendon Dutch), Brussels (Corendon).
- **Central and eastern Europe:** Warsaw, Bucharest, Cluj, Bratislava and Budapest (Corendon), Vilnius (GetJet, Avion Express) and Moscow Vnukovo (Pobeda).
- **Iran:** Tehran (Freebird, Taban, Corendon).
- **Domestic:** Istanbul Sabiha Gökçen (Pegasus) and Istanbul Airport (Turkish Airlines).

The page doesn't say which routes run in which season. The airport's 2025 press release on Copenhagen Air Taxi, for example, described flights from the end of May to 18 October that year. In winter, check your airline's own timetable before you plan around GZP. If there's no direct flight from home, the two Istanbul routes are the ones to look at for a connection.

## Gazipaşa or Antalya Airport?

Use the airport's distance table as your guide:

- **Mahmutlar, Kestel and eastern Alanya:** Gazipaşa wins easily, at 30–40 km.
- **Alanya centre, the castle and [Cleopatra Beach](/things-to-do/cleopatra-beach/):** Gazipaşa is 40 km away. Worth it if the flight suits.
- **Konaklı, Avsallar and Okurcalar:** 55–75 km from Gazipaşa. Compare flight times and prices with Antalya before you decide.
- **Side and Manavgat (100 km), Belek (150 km), Antalya city (180 km):** fly to Antalya. Our [Antalya Airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) covers that side.

If your flight options are similar, the shorter road usually wins: less time in a minibus after a long flight, and a cheaper taxi.

## Getting from the airport to your hotel

### Havaş shuttle

Havaş runs the airport bus. The airport's Havaş page sends you to Havaş's own website for departure points and times; we couldn't read a Gazipaşa timetable there today, so check havas.net before you travel and don't rely on a late-night bus.

### Taxi

The airport names a taxi provider: the **Gazipaşa Airport Taxi Drivers Cooperative**, on +90 533 632 5988, which runs its own website. The airport pages give no fares, and the cooperative's fare page wouldn't open for us, so agree or check the price before you set off.

### Car hire

Three firms have desks at the exit of the International Arrivals Hall:

- **Fly Rent a Car:** +90 545 417 66 72 or +90 532 417 66 72.
- **Garenta:** +90 542 302 99 78.
- **Europcar:** +90 533 311 19 29.

### Hotel transfers and parking

If your hotel or tour operator offers a transfer, book it before you fly. For drivers, the car park offers subscriptions on a first-come, first-served basis and takes no reservations; vehicles of disabled drivers park free for up to 15 days per entry. Parking prices come from a calculator on the website rather than a printed table.

## In the terminal

The airport lists:

- An information desk in the centre of the terminal, an exchange office, and Denizbank and Garanti ATMs at the international arrivals exit.
- A baby care room, a prayer room and a Biz Klinik health point in the arrivals hall.
- Two places to eat: Brewmark, a pub, and Kantin, a self-service café. Lounges are listed without details.
- A visa office before passport control, open 24 hours.
- Free Wi-Fi. One page says it covers the whole terminal; the FAQ says two hours free on the "Gazipasa - Alanya_Belediyesi" network, after registering with a Turkish (+90) number by SMS.

There's no left-luggage service and no hotel at the airport, so don't plan on leaving bags there for a last day in [Alanya](/blog/alanya-in-a-day/).

### Help for passengers with reduced mobility

The airport lists reserved parking, accessible toilets, Braille signs and ramps, and a Fast Track lane for a passenger with reduced mobility and one companion, with no documents required. Wheelchairs come through your airline or its ground handler, or you can call for help at the assistance points in the terminal. Tell your airline when you book.

### Lost property

TAV's information desk handles lost property around the clock: +90 242 582 71 26 (extensions 4444 or 4381) or gzptim@tav.aero. Items are kept for one year. Lost passports and ID cards go to the airport police, extension 1114.

## When to arrive for your flight

The English FAQ asks you to arrive at least two hours before domestic flights and three hours before international ones; the Turkish FAQ says 120 minutes. Go with the longer figure for international flights. Check-in is at the airline counters; if you've checked in online, go to the bag drop.

A site-wide notice also mentions works running between January and December 2026, so leave a little extra time and follow the signs.

## Making the most of an Alanya trip

Landing at Gazipaşa puts Alanya 40 km away. Start with [Alanya Castle](/things-to-do/alanya-castle/) and the [Alanya Museum](/things-to-do/alanya-museum/), then the beach below the castle. On Alanya's eastern coast, the hillside ruins of [Syedra](/things-to-do/syedra-ancient-city/) make a quiet stop. Browse [Alanya hotels](/hotels/?area=Alanya) or read our [Alanya district guide](/destinations/alanya/).

## FAQ

### How far is Gazipaşa Airport from Alanya?

The airport's own table gives 40 km to Alanya, 30 km to Mahmutlar and 35 km to Kestel.

### Is there a bus from Gazipaşa Airport to Alanya?

Havaş runs the airport shuttle. Its routes and times are on the Havaş website, which the airport links; check them before you fly, as we couldn't confirm a timetable today.

### Which airlines fly to Gazipaşa?

The airport lists 12 airlines, including Corendon, SAS, Norwegian, Sunclass, Finnair, Pobeda, Pegasus and Turkish Airlines. The airport doesn't mark which routes are seasonal, so check your dates with the airline.

### Should I fly to Gazipaşa or Antalya for Alanya?

If you're staying in Alanya or east of it, Gazipaşa is much closer. For Side, Belek or Antalya city, fly to Antalya.
