Çıralı is a quiet beach village south of Kemer, where the Olympos valley meets the sea. It has an ancient city in a forest, a turtle-nesting beach, a ridge where natural gas burns in the rock, and the long-distance Lycian Way close by. It suits an overnight stay better than a rushed day trip. This guide explains how the pieces fit together and what we could verify, with winter in mind.

## What is where

- **Çıralı Beach:** a long bay below Tahtalı Mountain, and a nesting site for loggerhead turtles according to the Ministry's Turkish Museums portal. Sources disagree on length (about 4 km on one listing, 3.2 km in another). See [Çıralı Beach](/things-to-do/cirali-beach/) and our [sea turtle guide](/blog/sea-turtle-beaches-in-antalya/).
- **Olympos:** an ancient Lycian city with temples, tombs, houses and a theatre scattered among trees, with a stream running through the site. See [Olympos and the Chimaera flames](/things-to-do/olympos-and-the-chimaera-flames/). Our page says the ruins are about 80 km from Antalya, 1.5 to 2 hours by road.
- **The Chimaera (Yanartaş):** small flames burning from vents in the hillside, fed by natural gas. The best time is after dark, via a short, steep path from the car park; bring a torch and sturdy shoes.
- **Tahtalı cable car:** on the way, near Kemer. Check its operating status; it is weather-dependent.

## Fees and hours in winter

Our Çıralı Beach page, using the Turkish Museums portal, gives the Olympos ruins at **€10 in winter, 08:00–17:00** when checked on 17 November 2026. Fees and hours change, and the Chimaera area has its own conditions, so confirm before you go.

## Where to stay: Olympos Lodge

Olympos Lodge is a small hotel in Çıralı with a guest-only garden that runs down to the beach beside a stream. Rooms are listed from 35 m² to 105 m², most with a fireplace, and the site mentions a sauna in the garden but no swimming pool. Its English page says 14 rooms and its About page says 17, and it publishes no child policy, check-in time or season dates, so ask about those, and whether breakfast is included. [Full profile](/hotels/olympos-lodge-cirali/).

Çıralı is not Kemer town: it lies on its own bay reached down a valley road. For a wider choice of beds, see [Kemer hotels](/hotels/?area=Kemer) and our [Kemer guide](/destinations/kemer/).

## Eating

We have not verified individual restaurants in Çıralı for this guide, and Olympos Lodge's site names no restaurant or hours. Ask your accommodation, and phone ahead in winter when some places close.

## Walking: the Lycian Way

The Lycian Way passes through here, so you can walk a stretch of it. Read [the Lycian Way near Antalya](/blog/lycian-way-near-antalya/) for how to choose a section, and wear sturdy shoes. In winter, mountain paths can be wet and slippery.

## A realistic plan

1. **Day 1:** drive the coast road from Kemer using [Kemer to Olympos coast road](/blog/kemer-to-olympos-coast-road/), visit Phaselis and the cable car if it is running, then settle in at Çıralı.
2. **Day 2:** walk the ruins in the morning, take a beach walk, and visit the Chimaera at dusk.
3. **Rain plan:** keep the Chimaera for a dry evening; the path is steep and rocky.

## Practical tips

- **Winter weather:** it is cooler and wetter than the summer beach season; read [Antalya in winter](/blog/antalya-in-winter/).
- **Turtles:** the beach has night-time rules in nesting season (May to autumn), which our turtle guide explains.
- **Cash:** small places may not take cards.
- **Transfers:** see [Antalya airport transfers](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/).

## FAQ

### How far is Çıralı from Antalya?

Our Olympos page puts the ruins about 80 km away, 1.5 to 2 hours by road.

### Is Olympos open in winter?

The portal lists winter hours of 08:00–17:00 at €10. Confirm before you travel.

### Can I see the Chimaera flames in the day?

They burn all the time but show best after dark.
