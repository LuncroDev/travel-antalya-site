This Gloria Old Course hole-by-hole guide covers all 18 holes of the Old Course at Gloria Golf Club in Belek. It opened in 1997 and plays to **par 72 and 6,529 metres from the white tees**. We built it from the club's own course planner and course pages, read on 25 November 2026, and we flag one problem up front: the "Select" scorecard Gloria publishes for the Old Course does not match the planner.

For photos and booking basics, see our [Gloria Golf Club profile](/golf/gloria-golf-club/). The series also covers [Carya](/blog/carya-golf-club-hole-by-hole/), [Cullinan Links](/blog/cullinan-links-hole-by-hole/) and [Montgomerie Maxx Royal](/blog/montgomerie-maxx-royal-hole-by-hole/).

## The Old Course at a glance

Gloria calls itself Türkiye's first and only 45-hole golf facility: the Old Course, the New Course (18 holes, 6,523 m) and the Verde nine (par 35, 2,923 m). The Old Course page gives these facts:

- **Opened:** 1997.
- **Par and length:** 72, 6,529 m.
- **Grass:** Bermuda on greens and fairways.
- **Water:** six lakes covering 60,000 m².
- **Area:** 772,000 m² on the course page; the planner says 700,000 m².

Gloria names no designer, and publishes no bunker count, floodlit holes, course rating or slope rating for the Old Course.

### Which tee is which

The planner uses four tees: **White, Yellow, Blue and Red**. There is no black tee. It says plainly that **all distances are in metres**, measured to the centre of the green, and that you add 10% to get yards. That makes this card easier to read than Carya's or Cullinan's, which print no unit.

Neither matches the holes, which add up to 6,529.

## The scorecard mix-up

Gloria's course page also links a "Select" scorecard for the Old Course. It does not show the Old Course as the planner and the course page do:

- Its holes 1–9 are the **Verde nine**, and its holes 10–18 are the **planner's holes 1–9**. The Old Course's real back nine is missing.
- It totals 6,492 m, not 6,529 m.
- Its first hole is a par 4, while an image caption on the course page calls the 1st tee a par 5, as the planner does.

The New Course and Verde "Select" cards have the same oddity: both start with the Verde nine. We follow the planner, which agrees with the course page, and suggest you ask the starter for the card in use on the day.

**Stroke index:** the planner prints none. The "Select" card does, but for its own mixed routing. On it, the planner's 8th hole carries stroke index 1. Don't rely on that for the Old Course as laid out in the planner.

### Front-nine notes

- **The 5th and 9th are the big ones.** Together they are more than a kilometre from the white tees.
- **Watch the 7th from white.** It is 182 m, the longest of the five par 3s, while the yellow tee cuts it to 143 m.
- **The 8th barely shrinks from yellow.** Only 11 m separate white and yellow, then 46 m drop away to blue.

### Back-nine notes

- **The 15th is the toughest-looking par 4 by length:** 449 m from white, 2 m longer than the 3rd.
- **The 16th is the birdie chance.** At 311 m from white and 220 m from red, it is the only par 4 under 370 m.
- **Red-tee players gain most on the 17th**, where the step from blue to red is 46 m, the largest on the back nine.

## Reading the card

- **Five par 5s, five par 3s.** Only eight holes are par 4s, so the scoring comes from the long and short holes.
- **The front nine is 609 m longer than the back** from the white tees.
- **The par 3s range from 141 to 182 m** from white, and three of them fall on the back nine.
- **Biggest tee spread:** the 5th, where white to red is 106 m. The smallest is the 10th, at 33 m.

## FAQ

### How long is the Gloria Old Course?

6,529 m from the white tees, par 72, according to both the course page and the planner. From the other tees it is 6,057 m (yellow), about 5,632–5,637 m (blue) and 5,197 m (red).

### Are the Gloria Old Course distances in metres or yards?

Metres. The planner says all distances are in metres to the centre of the green, and to add 10% for yards.

### Why does the Old Course scorecard show different holes?

The "Select" scorecard linked from the course page puts the Verde nine first and only the planner's front nine second, totalling 6,492 m. The planner and the course page both give 6,529 m with the routing shown above. Ask the starter which card is in use.

### Does the Gloria Old Course publish a stroke index?

Not in the planner. The "Select" scorecard has one, but for its own mixed routing, so it doesn't apply cleanly to the planner's holes.
