Belek golf courses run from a nine-hole par 35 to championship layouts of more than 6,500 metres, and every club publishes its facts a little differently. On 18 November 2026 we opened each club's own course pages and scorecards and set the numbers side by side: holes, par, length from the longest tees, designer and opening year. Where a club gives no figure, or two of its pages disagree, we say so. Each club name links to our full profile.

This guide is about the courses themselves. For what a round costs, see [Belek green fees for winter 2026–27](/blog/belek-green-fees-winter-2026-27/). For who is allowed on which course, see [Belek handicap limits and dress codes](/blog/belek-golf-handicap-limits-and-dress-codes/).

## How to read the numbers

- **Length** is the total from the longest tees each club lists, in metres unless we say otherwise. National's course map suggests adding 10% to turn metres into yards.
- **Par** is the club's published total. Antalya Golf Club's hole-by-hole pages give no total, so we added up the hole pars; the club's About page agrees with our sums.
- **Two clubs give no unit.** Carya's scorecard and Cullinan's hole pages print lengths without saying whether they are metres or yards. We show their figures as printed.
- **Length is not difficulty.** Lakes, trees, bunkers and wind often matter more than a few hundred metres. The notes below say what each club highlights.

## The long championship courses

Seven Belek layouts measure more than 6,400 metres from the back (counting Cornelia's Queen loop), and the top three are within 7 metres of each other.

- **Gloria Old (6,529 m).** Opened in 1997, with tree-lined fairways, views of the Taurus Mountains and six lakes covering 60,000 m² on a 772,000 m² site.
- **Gloria New (6,523 m).** Seven lakes over 57,000 m², pine trees all round, and a 700,000 m² site. Gloria says it has been popular with amateurs and professionals since 2005. Both Gloria courses have Bermuda grass greens and fairways.
- **Montgomerie Maxx Royal (6,522 m).** A seaside-parkland course on 104 hectares, with pine-lined fairways, elevation changes, eight lakes and about 7 hectares of bunkers, waste bunkers included. Holes 10–18 are floodlit.
- **PGA Sultan (6,477 m).** Water comes into play on several holes, including the 1st, 3rd, 9th and 10th. The 10th green has a cliff wall on its left, the 5th looks out to sea and the 18th to the Taurus Mountains.
- **Sueno Pines (6,413 m).** On the eastern side of the Sueno complex. The club describes Scottish parkland-style fairways and lists 128 bunkers across its two courses.
- **Cornelia (6,324–6,411 m).** Twenty-seven holes played as three 18-hole loops: King (holes 1–18), Queen (10–27) and Prince (19–27, then 1–9). With 27 holes, the club says it stays open all year, overseeding nine holes at a time.
- **National (6,403 m).** Gently rolling ground among mature pine and eucalyptus, on a 1.1 million m² site with ten lakes. The last nine holes are floodlit.

Carya belongs in this group too. Its scorecard prints 6,605 from the black tees without a unit. The club calls it a heathland course in pine forest, and Regnum's golf page describes it as "Europe's first and only fully illuminated golf course".

## Shorter courses and gentler rounds

- **Kaya Palazzo (5,942 m).** A pine-forest course with nine lakes and four bridges on 650,000 m². It has unusually full tee information: 5,604 m from the yellows, 5,239 m from the blues and 4,911 m from the reds, which the club marks for seniors, juniors and beginners.
- **Pasha (5,769 m).** Antalya Golf Club's second course has generous fairways, but also doglegs, bunkers and undulating greens.
- **Sueno Dunes (5,643 m, par 69).** The only par-69 course in Belek on our list, on the south-west side of the Sueno Golf Hotel. Both Sueno courses are listed as 50 metres from the hotel.
- **Cullinan Links (Aspendos 5,807, Olympos 5,407).** Both figures come from the hole pages, which give no unit. Olympos is a par 71, with six par 3s and five par 5s. The club says its 36 holes cover 110 hectares, and its course page mentions the Beşgöz River.
- **Gloria Verde (2,923 m, par 35).** Nine holes set around pine trees and two lakes. "Verde Select" adds the front nine of the Old or New Course to make 18.
- **ROBINSON Nobilis.** The resort's page calls it its own 18-hole parkland course, with old pines along every fairway, but publishes no par or length.

## Lykia Links: the odd one out

Lykia Links is the course that breaks the pattern. Its owner's page names Perry Dye as the designer, gives about 6,900 metres and describes a course shaped by sand dunes and coastal wind, with open sea views. It bills itself as the first and only true links course in Türkiye. Our [Lykia Links profile](/golf/lykia-links-antalya/) places it at Denizyaka, in Manavgat district, so budget extra transfer time from a Belek hotel.

## The designers behind Belek's courses

A few names turn up again and again:

- **David Jones** worked on three clubs: National, with David Feherty (opened 18 November 1994, the earliest opening date we found); Antalya Golf Club, as design consultant to European Golf Design; and Kaya Palazzo, with Golf Med Ltd.
- **European Golf Design** appears at Antalya Golf Club, at Cullinan and alongside Colin Montgomerie at Maxx Royal.
- **Thomson Perrett & Lobb** designed Carya, which opened in November 2008. Regnum credits Peter Thomson, the five-time Open champion, and his team.
- **Sir Nick Faldo** designed Cornelia's 27 holes.
- **International Design Group** designed both Sueno courses.
- **Perry Dye** designed Lykia Links.

Gloria's and ROBINSON's course pages name no designer. Our Gloria profile credits Michel Gayon from an earlier check, but we could not find the name on Gloria's pages today.

## Where the clubs' own figures disagree

- **PGA Sultan par.** Antalya Golf Club's About page and hole pars say par 72; the Kempinski page for the same club says par 71. Both give 6,477 m.
- **Pasha length.** The About page says 5,769 m. The Kempinski page says 5,731 m, which is also what the club's own hole-by-hole lengths add up to.
- **Cullinan's third course.** The home page speaks of 36 holes, while the general information page lists three 18-hole courses: Olympos, Aspendos and Termessos. Our profile, from an earlier check, describes Termessos as a floodlit routing of the two back nines, so confirm which course your tee time is on.
- **Gloria Old length.** 6,529 m on the course page and scorecard, 6,239 m in the planner's summary.

## Which course suits your game?

- **Low handicaps who want length:** the 6,400 m-plus group above. Note that Cornelia, Montgomerie Maxx Royal, Kaya Palazzo and ROBINSON Nobilis cap men at handicap 28; National, Carya and Sueno allow 36. Our [handicap guide](/blog/belek-golf-handicap-limits-and-dress-codes/) has every club's rule.
- **Mixed groups and higher handicaps:** Pasha, Sueno Dunes, Kaya Palazzo from the forward tees, or Gloria Verde for a short round. [Belek golf for beginners](/blog/belek-golf-for-beginners/) explains how a first golf week works.
- **After dark:** National's back nine, Maxx Royal's holes 10–18 and Carya are lit. See [night golf in Belek](/blog/night-golf-in-belek/).
- **In autumn:** several courses close for about two weeks in September or October to overseed. Gloria's three courses close in turn from 10 September to 14 October 2026, and Carya (15–30 September) and National (10–24 September) have already published 2027 dates. Our [Belek golf season guide](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/) covers the rest.

## FAQ

### How many golf courses are there in Belek?

We count ten clubs in Belek with 270 holes between them: 18-hole layouts at Carya, Kaya Palazzo, Maxx Royal, National and ROBINSON Nobilis; 27 holes at Cornelia; 36 each at Antalya Golf Club, Cullinan and Sueno; and 45 at Gloria. Lykia Links adds 18 more outside Belek.

### Which is the longest golf course in Belek?

Among courses published in metres, Gloria's Old Course is longest at 6,529 m, just ahead of Gloria New (6,523 m) and Montgomerie Maxx Royal (6,522 m). Carya's scorecard shows 6,605 but gives no unit. Lykia Links, at about 6,900 m, is longer but lies outside Belek.

### Which Belek golf course is easiest for beginners?

None is easy, but the shortest options are Gloria's nine-hole Verde (2,923 m), Sueno Dunes (5,643 m, par 69) and the Pasha (5,769 m). Kaya Palazzo's red tees, at 4,911 m, are marked for beginners.

### Who designed Belek's golf courses?

David Feherty and David Jones designed National, Nick Faldo designed Cornelia, Thomson Perrett & Lobb designed Carya and International Design Group designed Sueno. European Golf Design worked on Antalya Golf Club, Cullinan and, with Colin Montgomerie, Maxx Royal. Kaya Palazzo is by Golf Med Ltd with David Jones.
