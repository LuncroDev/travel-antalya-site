Aspendos and Side in one day is the classic road trip east of Antalya: the best-preserved Roman theatre on the coast in the morning, then a seaside ancient town and a temple at sunset. It works well by car, and in winter the order matters. Aspendos stops selling tickets at 16:30, while Side's museum stays open an hour later and its temple has no published closing time. So do Aspendos first. This plan uses hours and prices from the Ministry of Culture and Tourism and its Turkish Museums portal, distances from the ministry, GoTürkiye and Manavgat Municipality, all checked on 16 November 2026.

## Aspendos and Side in one day: the essentials

- **Order:** Aspendos in the morning, Side from lunchtime to sunset.
- **Aspendos:** open every day 08:00–17:00 in winter, ticket office closes 16:30. €15 for foreign adults.
- **Side Museum:** every day 08:30–17:30 in winter. €5. The Side theatre is closed for restoration.
- **Distances:** Aspendos is 48 km east of Antalya (ministry). Side is 75 km from Antalya and 7 km from Manavgat (Manavgat Municipality).
- **Drive between them:** GoTürkiye puts Side about 40 minutes from Aspendos.
- **Tickets for the day:** about €20 per adult for Aspendos and the Side Museum; add €11 if you stop at Perge.
- **Children:** foreign children under 8 go free at ministry sites.

## Before you start

Rent a car or book a driver for the day; public transport can reach both places but not easily in one loop (our [Side day trip](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/) covers the minibus). Leave Antalya by about 08:30. If you're travelling before 20 November 2026, the ministry's Antalya office warns that city traffic is being rearranged for the COP31 climate conference, so allow extra time getting out of town.

Buy tickets at the gate or on the ministry's e-ticket site. If you're visiting several sites in a week, the MuseumPass The Mediterranean costs €90 for seven days from the first visit and covers Aspendos, Perge, the Side site and the Side Museum. For this day alone, single tickets are cheaper; our [MuseumPass guide](/blog/museumpass-the-mediterranean-antalya/) does the maths.

## 08:30 – Leave Antalya (optional stop at Perge)

Head east on the Antalya–Alanya road (D400). If you're up early, [Perge](/things-to-do/perge-ancient-city/) is on the way and opens at 08:00. The ministry lists it as open 08:00–17:00 with the ticket office closing at 16:30, and €11. Its theatre is closed for works, so an hour on the colonnaded street is enough before you drive on. Skip Perge if you want a relaxed lunch in Side.

## 09:30 – Aspendos

Manavgat Municipality's travel guide describes the approach: after passing Serik on the Antalya–Alanya road, turn north onto the 4 km Aspendos road. The ministry's Antalya office puts the site about 8 km east of Serik town, in Belkıs.

### The theatre

The [Aspendos theatre](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/) is why you came. The ministry notes that it hosted Türkiye's first theatre festival in 1959 and still holds the Aspendos Opera and Ballet Festival every summer. Climb as high as the open rows allow for the view down over the stage building, then walk around the outside to see how complete the outer wall is.

### Hours and tickets

- **Winter (1 November–30 April):** 08:00–17:00 every day, ticket office closes 16:30 (Turkish Museums and muze.gov.tr agree).
- **Summer 2026:** the ministry's summer list had Aspendos open until 21:00 from 1 May to 1 October.
- **Price:** €15 for foreign visitors aged 8 and over; free under 8. Turkish citizens pay 200 TL.

A ministry letter reproduced on a Turkish finance news site says museum prices will rise from 1 April 2027. The ministry hasn't published the new prices on its own pages yet, so check before a spring trip.

### The aqueduct and the bridge

Turkish Museums describes a 1 km line of arches that carried water to the city from the mountains to the north; the ministry's page says the water came from about 25 km north-west. No official page gives the walking distance from the theatre, so ask at the gate. On the theatre road, the ministry's Antalya office recommends stopping at the historic bridge, and GoTürkiye describes it as the Seljuk-era Eurymedon Bridge, resting on Roman foundations, over the Köprüçay river.

Allow about two hours at Aspendos with the aqueduct, then drive on by about 11:30.

## 12:15 – Arrive in Side and park

GoTürkiye gives about 40 minutes from Aspendos to Side. Manavgat Municipality's news archive explains how Side handles cars: the ancient town's entrance is closed to vehicles, there is a free 500-space car park in the bus station (otogar) area, and train-style shuttles run into the old town. That item dates from a 2019 council decision and its fares are long out of date, so take the shuttle or walk in and pay what's asked on the day.

Have lunch near the harbour. Our pages on [Zula Side](/restaurants/zula-side/), [Side Orfoz](/restaurants/side-orfoz-restaurant/) and [Karma Side](/restaurants/karma-side/) cover three sea-view options.

## 13:30 – Side Museum and the main street

The museum is in a Roman bath building in the old town. Its hours depend on which ministry site you read:

- **muze.gov.tr:** 08:30–17:30, ticket office closes 17:00, €5.
- **Turkish Museums:** winter (2 October–30 April) 08:30–17:30, ticket office 17:15; summer 08:30–21:00.

The ministry's fee directive also lists a combined Side ticket at €10, marked "theatre closed". With the theatre shut, ask at the desk whether your ticket covers anything else.

### The theatre, from outside

The [Side theatre](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/) has been closed to visitors for restoration since November 2024, with no reopening date. Turkish Museums says it closed on 29 November 2024; another ministry list says the 21st. You can still see its outer walls from the street.

### Night visits, this month only

The ministry's Antalya office announced night opening at the Side archaeological site from 9 to 20 November 2026: entry from 17:30, ticket office until 19:30, site closes 20:00. It quotes 200 TL for Müzekart holders and no foreigners' price. Check on the day if you're there before the 20th.

## 16:30 – The Temple of Apollo and Athena at sunset

Walk to the tip of the peninsula for the temple columns by the sea. No official page gives hours or a fee for the temple area, but the ministry's theatre page tells visitors not to miss the temples of Apollo and Athena, "particularly during sunset". In winter the sun goes down not long after the museum closes, so this is the natural finale. Then drive back to Antalya: 75 km, according to Manavgat Municipality.

## Short on time? Swap or add stops

- **Manavgat Waterfall:** Manavgat Municipality puts it 3 km from the town; no official fee is published. See [Manavgat Waterfall](/things-to-do/manavgat-waterfall/), or our [Manavgat river boat trip](/blog/manavgat-river-boat-trip/) for a longer day.
- **Köprülü Canyon:** north of Aspendos, better as a separate day. Read [Köprülü Canyon](/things-to-do/koprulu-canyon/).
- **Stay the night:** split the trip with a night in Side or Belek. Browse [Side hotels](/hotels/?area=Side) or [Belek hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek), and see the [Manavgat district guide](/destinations/manavgat/).

## Winter or summer?

In winter the plan is tight but the light is soft and the sites are quiet. In summer 2026 the ministry kept Aspendos, Perge and the Side Museum open until 21:00, so you can flip the day: beach in the morning, Side in the afternoon heat with the museum's air-conditioning, and Aspendos in the evening. The opera and ballet festival fills the theatre in September; the 2026 edition ran 12–26 September and no 2027 dates are published yet. ## FAQ

### Can you see Aspendos and Side in one day?

Yes, by car. Aspendos is 48 km east of Antalya and Side is about 40 minutes further on. Allow two hours at Aspendos and four at Side, and start at Aspendos in winter because its ticket office closes at 16:30.

### How much are Aspendos and Side tickets?

As of 16 November 2026, Aspendos costs €15 and the Side Museum €5 for foreign adults; under-8s are free. Prices are expected to rise from April 2027, so check before you go.

### Is the Side theatre open?

No. It has been closed for restoration since November 2024, with no reopening date published. The museum, main street and Temple of Apollo and Athena are still worth the trip.

### Where do you park in Side?

Manavgat Municipality says cars can't enter the ancient town and points visitors to a free car park in the bus station area, with shuttles into the old town.
