Belek golf tournaments fill the winter calendar, and if you are booking a golf week between December and April it helps to know which course is hosting what. Pro-ams, a national amateur championship, junior tours and, next spring, a DP World Tour event all land on the same handful of courses. This guide lists every Belek event we could confirm on an organiser's or club's own page on 15 November 2026, course by course, and explains what it means for visiting golfers.

One point up front: **no club page we read says its course closes to visitors during a tournament**, and none gives spectator information. A tournament still takes up tee times, so if your dates overlap an event below, ask the club when you book. For rates, read [Belek green fees for winter 2026–27](/blog/belek-green-fees-winter-2026-27/).

## How we built this list

We used four kinds of source:

- **The Turkish Golf Federation (TGF) domestic calendar.** Use the Turkish-language page: when we checked, the English version showed no Antalya events for November and December.
- **Club calendars.** [Carya](/golf/carya-golf-club/) publishes the fullest list. It covers events run by the Regnum group, which owns the Regnum Carya resort, but many rows name no course.
- **Tour and organiser pages:** the DP World Tour, the Legends Tour, U.S. Kids Golf and the Maxx Royal Golf Cup.
- **Clubs' own tournament pages**, such as Cornelia's pro-am page.

Where a row on the Carya calendar names no course, we say so rather than guess. The TGF also lists the third legs of its 2026–27 Türkiye Golf Tour and Development Tour for 25–27 December with the venue "Belirlenecek" (to be decided), so we have left them out.

## December 2026: the busiest month

### Regnum Carya Pro-Am, 29 November – 6 December

The 21st edition of Regnum's pro-am is on both the TGF calendar and Carya's own list, with Carya named as the course. It runs straight into the **10th Regnum Russian Club Championship & Winter Challenge** (6–10 December), which the calendar lists without a course. If you want a quiet week at Carya, these first ten days of December are the ones to check. Our [Carya profile](/golf/carya-golf-club/) and [Regnum Carya hotel page](/hotels/regnum-carya/) cover the course and resort.

### Maxx Royal Golf Cup final, 6–11 December

The [Montgomerie Maxx Royal](/golf/montgomerie-maxx-royal-golf-club/) home page says the 2026 Maxx Royal Golf Cup ends with "a grand finale from 6-11 December 2026 at Maxx Royal Belek Golf Resort". Players qualify through stages at partner clubs around the world; the page gives no dates for those stages.

### Turkish Amateur Open, 10–12 December

The 20th Turkish Amateur Open is the one national championship on this list. The TGF's announcement puts it at [Kaya Palazzo](/golf/kaya-palazzo-golf-club/), with a practice round on Wednesday 9 December and the championship from Thursday 10 to Saturday 12 December:

- **Format:** 54 holes of stroke play, 18 a day, with no cut.
- **Eligibility:** men and women with a Handicap Index of 9.0 or lower and a valid WHS handicap, through a federation recognised by the EGA, the R&A or WAGR. A tenth of the field is kept for the Turkish national team.
- **Entry:** €150. Registration opened on 1 September and closed on 15 November 2026.
- **On course:** caddies are allowed; buggies are banned.
- **Prize:** the men's champion gets a wild card into the 2027 Turkish Airlines Open.

### Golf Mad Turkish Junior Open, 13–20 December

The TGF lists the 17th Golf Mad Turkish Junior Open on [Gloria](/golf/gloria-golf-club/)'s Old and New courses, organised by Golf Mad Travel. Gloria also has the nine-hole Verde course, so ask whether it is open to visitors that week. Families with junior golfers should also read [junior golf in Belek](/blog/junior-golf-in-belek/).

### Fikret Öztürk Championship and the holiday events

From 17 to 20 December, Carya hosts the 12th Fikret Öztürk Club Championship and its Winter Challenge, two events on the TGF list run by Regnum. Then come the U.S. Kids Golf local tour days at [National](/golf/national-golf-club/) on 24 and 25 December, and the Golf Mad New Year Cup from 29 December to 5 January, which the Carya calendar lists without a course.

## January 2027: juniors and senior pros

### U.S. Kids Golf in Belek

U.S. Kids Golf runs a winter local tour in Antalya. Its page lists six one-day events: National on 24 and 25 December, National again on 9 and 10 January, and Carya on 23 January and 24 January, the second being the Tour Championship. Registration for each opened on 4 November, with a $15 surcharge for late entry. The page lists no base entry fees or age groups. Carya's calendar then shows a **U.S. Kids International Tournament** from 26 to 31 January, without naming a course.

### Legends Tour Qualifying School, 14–19 January

The Staysure Legends Tour, the tour for players over 50, holds its 2027 Qualifying School at Gloria Golf Resort. Practice days are 14–15 January and the tournament runs from Saturday 16 to Tuesday 19 January, with play on Gloria's Old and New courses. Entry is £800, and the deadline is midday UK time on Friday 8 January 2027. Players must be 50 on or before 31 July 2027; amateurs also need a Handicap Index of 1.4 or better. The page says nothing about spectators.

### Cornelia Pro-Am, 27 March – 3 April

[Cornelia](/golf/cornelia-golf-club/) publishes the most complete pro-am package. Players arrive on Saturday 27 March. Two practice days follow, then a Texas Scramble on 30 March, the pro-am on 31 March and 1 April, and an individual pro and amateur day on 2 April, closing with an awards gala. The amateur package is €2,880 per person in a double room or €3,260 single, for seven nights all-inclusive at [Cornelia Diamond](/hotels/cornelia-diamond-golf-resort-spa/) with six rounds, token, trolley and a shared buggy, a halfway snack and airport transfers. Non-golfing partners pay €1,380 (double) or €2,070 (single). Staying at the resort is compulsory. Professionals play free when they bring three amateurs, and the winning pro takes €5,000.

## Turkish Airlines Open 2027

The DP World Tour's 2027 schedule lists the **Turkish Airlines Open from 29 April to 2 May 2027 at National Golf Club, Belek**. That makes National the course to avoid as a visitor in the last days of April. We found no ticket prices or spectator details yet. The Turkish Amateur Open champion in December earns a wild card into the field.

## What the tournaments mean for your golf week

1. **Book tee times early around these dates.** No club says it closes, but a pro-am or a championship takes a lot of tee sheet.
2. **Ask your hotel.** Resorts with their own courses, such as [Regnum Carya](/hotels/regnum-carya/), [Kaya Palazzo](/hotels/kaya-palazzo-golf-resort-belek/) and [Gloria Golf Resort](/hotels/gloria-golf-resort/), know their event weeks first.
3. **Have a second course in mind.** Our [golf directory](/golf/) lists 11 clubs in and around Belek.
4. **Mind the extras.** Buggy rules can change on event days; the Turkish Amateur Open bans them outright. See [golf buggy hire in Belek](/blog/golf-buggy-hire-in-belek/) for the normal rules.
5. **Pick your season.** If you want empty fairways, our [Belek golf season guide](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/) weighs up the months.

## FAQ

### Are Belek golf courses closed during tournaments?

None of the club pages we read on 15 November 2026 announces a closure to visitors for a tournament. Events do take up tee times, so if your dates overlap one, ask the club or your hotel which tee times are free.

### When is the Turkish Airlines Open 2027?

The DP World Tour's 2027 schedule lists it from 29 April to 2 May 2027 at National Golf Club in Belek. No ticket prices had been published when we checked.

### Can amateurs play in Belek tournaments?

Yes, mostly through pro-ams and packages. Cornelia's pro-am is sold as a seven-night package. The Turkish Amateur Open needs a Handicap Index of 9.0 or better, and its 2026 entry has closed.

### Which Belek course is busiest this winter?

On the published calendars, Carya. It hosts the Regnum pro-ams, the Fikret Öztürk championship and U.S. Kids events, and many other Regnum events are on its list too. Gloria is busy for the Junior Open in December and the Legends Tour Qualifying School in January.
