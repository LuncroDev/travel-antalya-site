Myra and St Nicholas Church are the two reasons to stop in Demre, a town on the coast road between Finike and Kaş. One is a Lycian city with a cliff of carved rock tombs and a Roman theatre. The other is the church built over the tomb of the bishop who became Santa Claus. They are a few minutes apart, and together they make an easy half day. This guide covers what you will see, what is open in late 2026, and how to fit Demre into a trip from Antalya.

## Where Demre is

Demre is 140 km from Antalya by road, according to the Highways Directorate (KGM) distance table. The route follows the coast through Kumluca (94 km) and Finike (112 km). Kaş is 187 km from Antalya, so Demre sits roughly three-quarters of the way along. Our [Demre district guide](/destinations/demre/) covers the town and its coast.

Most visitors come on a long day trip along the coast or stop on the way to Kaş. Our [Antalya to Kaş day trip guide](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) sets out the whole drive. If you want more time at Myra, the half-day plan below works best from a base in Kaş.

## Myra: the Lycian city

### What Myra was

The Ministry of Culture and Tourism says Myra was one of the six leading cities of the Lycian League, each holding three votes, and for a while its capital. The city took its name from the Myros, the river now called Demre Çayı, which gave it water and the reason to exist. A canal cut into the rock at the edge of town carried the supply.

Myra's best years, the ministry says, came between 408 and 450 AD under Emperor Theodosius II, when it was the capital of Lycia and a trading centre with a port at Andriake. Arab raids between the 7th and 9th centuries hit the city hard, and it was captured in 809. The river's silt then buried much of it, and Myra lost its importance. In the Middle Ages it was known above all as the seat of its bishop, Nicholas.

### What to see at Myra

- **The rock tombs.** The ministry singles out the carved tombs and their reliefs, cut into the cliff above the theatre. A second group is known as the river necropolis. GoTürkiye describes limestone tombs set into a rock-hewn mountain wall.
- **The theatre.** The ministry dates the theatre to the Roman period and calls it one of the few ancient theatres to have survived to the present day, with the rock tombs rising above it. GoTürkiye says locals come here to watch the sunrise and sunset.

The tombs are best seen from the ground. Stay on marked paths, and don't climb into the cliff.

### Myra opening hours and tickets

When we checked on 10 November 2026, the ministry's page listed Myra as open every day from 08:00 to 19:00, with the box office closing at 18:30, and showed an entry fee of €13. An audio guide is offered. Winter hours are often shorter than summer ones, so check the [official Myra page](https://muze.gov.tr/muze-detay?DistId=MYR&SectionId=MYR01) before you set off. The address is Alakent Mahallesi, Dağ Dibi Mevkii.

## St Nicholas Church

### Who Nicholas was

The ministry's page says Nicholas was born in Patara around 300 AD, the son of a wealthy wheat merchant. He became Bishop of Myra and, according to the ministry, died on 6 December 365 at the age of 65. He came to be honoured as a protector of children and sailors, and he is the saint behind Santa Claus. Patara, his birthplace, is further west; see our [Patara guide](/blog/patara-beach-and-ancient-city/).

### What to see in the church

The people of Myra built a church over his tomb. GoTürkiye describes the building as an 11th-century structure, with frescoes, mosaics, a stone altar and four columns around it, and a corridor that leads to the sarcophagus. The ministry says the walls show scenes of the miracles attributed to Nicholas.

The ministry also describes a Roman sarcophagus decorated with fish scales and acanthus leaves, which is believed to be his. In 1087, merchants from Bari opened the tomb and carried off part of his remains. They are now in the Basilica of San Nicola in Bari. Some relics believed to be his were displayed at the Antalya Museum, which is closed for rebuilding; see our [Antalya Museum page](/things-to-do/antalya-museum/).

The church is run as a museum, the Aziz (St) Nikolaos Anıt Müzesi, but it is still a religious site. Dress modestly and keep your voice down.

### St Nicholas Church opening hours

The ministry lists the church as open every day from 08:30 to 19:00, with the box office closing at 18:30. Its page did not show an entry fee when we checked, so look at the [official St Nicholas page](https://muze.gov.tr/muze-detay?SectionId=STN01&DistId=STN) for the current price. The address is Gökyazı Mahallesi, Müze Caddesi, in the centre of Demre.

### 6 December in Demre

6 December, the anniversary of his death, is the saint's feast day. The news agency DHA reported that on 6 December 2024 a memorial service was held in the church, led by the Orthodox Metropolitan of Demre and attended by about 500 people. DHA said services have been held there for about 30 years. If you come on that date, expect crowds and arrive early. We have not seen an official programme for 2026.

## Andriake and the Lycian Civilisations Museum: closed

Andriake, at Çayağzı on the coast, was Myra's harbour. Its great granary was built in AD 129–130 under Emperor Hadrian and still has its roof, according to the Turkish Museums site. Today it houses the Lycian Civilisations Museum, with eight halls on the cities of the Lycian League.

**Both the archaeological site and the museum have been closed since 17 February 2025** for new displays and landscaping, and Turkish Museums gives no reopening date. Don't plan your day around them until the official page says they are open again.

The ministry's Myra page also mentions a bird area between Çayağzı and Andriake, home to 149 bird species, if you want a short walk by the water.

## A half-day plan for Demre

- **Arrive early.** Go to Myra first, before tour groups and, in summer, the heat. Allow an hour or more for the theatre and tombs.
- **Drive into town for the church.** St Nicholas Church is in the centre of Demre. Allow 45 minutes to an hour.
- **Lunch.** Eat in town, or drive on west towards Kaş.
- **Afternoon.** Continue along the coast. The [Antalya to Kaş guide](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) covers the stops between Demre and Kaş, including Kekova.

## Saving money with a museum pass

The ministry's list of sites covered by the **MuseumPass The Mediterranean** includes Myra, the St Nicholas Memorial Museum, Patara, Simena, Arykanda, Olympos, Phaselis, Perge, Aspendos and Alanya Castle. If you plan to see several of them over a few days, compare the pass price with separate tickets. Check the pass's current terms before you buy.

## FAQ

### How far is Myra from Antalya?

Demre, where Myra is, is 140 km from Antalya by road, according to the KGM distance table. Allow a long day for a return trip, or stay in Kaş.

### Is St Nicholas buried in Demre?

Nicholas was buried in Myra, where the church stands over his tomb. In 1087 merchants from Bari took part of his remains to Italy, where they are kept in the Basilica of San Nicola. The ministry says a Roman sarcophagus in the church is believed to be his.

### What are the opening hours of Myra and St Nicholas Church?

When we checked on 10 November 2026, the ministry listed Myra as open daily from 08:00 to 19:00 and the church from 08:30 to 19:00, both with the box office closing at 18:30. Hours change by season, so check the official pages on the day.

### Is the Lycian Civilisations Museum open?

No. Turkish Museums says the museum and the Andriake site have been closed since 17 February 2025 for new displays and landscaping, with no reopening date given.
