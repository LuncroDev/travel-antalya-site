Antalya is one of Turkey’s most loved holiday destinations, well known for its long coastline, beautiful beaches, ancient ruins, luxury resorts, and warm Mediterranean climate. While many travelers choose to visit during the busy summer months, October is becoming increasingly popular for those looking for a more relaxed experience. October falls between the hot summer and the cooler winter months, offering a pleasant balance of warmth and comfort. Some visitors wonder if it might be too cool for swimming, while others look forward to milder temperatures for sightseeing and outdoor activities. Understanding [**Antalya’s weather in October**](https://travel-antalya.com/) helps travelers decide whether this month suits their holiday plans and allows them to enjoy the city at a calmer, more comfortable pace.  
In this detailed guide, we will explore Antalya’s October weather in depth. We will cover temperature, rainfall, sea conditions, sunshine, crowds, activities, packing tips, and travel benefits. By the end, you will clearly understand whether October feels like summer or the start of autumn in Antalya.

## Understanding Antalya’s Climate

Antalya has a Mediterranean climate, which means hot, dry summers and mild, rainy winters. The city enjoys sunshine for most of the year, making it a popular destination in every season. Summer in Antalya usually lasts from June to September and is known for high temperatures and strong sunshine. Autumn arrives gradually, and October is the first true transition month. Unlike many European destinations where autumn comes suddenly, Antalya experiences a slow shift from summer to cooler weather, allowing warmth to linger longer. Because of this, Antalya’s weather in October often surprises visitors. Many expect cool autumn days but instead find warm temperatures, sunny skies, and comfortable sea conditions, making October an unexpectedly pleasant time to visit.

## October Climate Overview in Antalya

October in Antalya is best described as late summer mixed with early autumn comfort. The weather remains warm, but it is no longer extreme or exhausting.  
Average weather conditions in October include:

* Daytime temperatures between 25°C and 28°C

* Night temperatures around 15°C to 18°C

* Sea temperature around 24°C

* Sunshine for 7 to 8 hours per day

* Low to moderate rainfall, mainly toward the end of the month

The heat becomes softer, humidity decreases, and evenings feel fresh and pleasant. This balance makes October one of the most comfortable months for visiting Antalya.

## Daytime Weather in October

Daytime weather is one of the main reasons travelers love Antalya in October. The sun is still strong enough to give a summer-like feel, but the heat is no longer overwhelming. Most days are warm and bright, with temperatures often reaching 26°C to 27°C, which is perfect for enjoying the outdoors. This weather is ideal for sightseeing, walking around the city, relaxing on the beach, or visiting historical sites. Unlike July and August, visitors can comfortably explore during the day without feeling exhausted by extreme heat. The skies are usually clear, and a gentle sea breeze adds extra comfort. For many travelers, Antalya’s weather in October feels like the perfect, more relaxed version of summer—warm, sunny, and enjoyable without the crowds or intense heat.

## Evening and Night Conditions

Evenings in October in Antalya are noticeably cooler than summer, yet still very pleasant and comfortable. Temperatures usually drop to around 15°C to 18°C, which feels refreshing after a warm daytime. This weather is perfect for outdoor dinners, evening walks along the beach, and relaxing at open-air cafes without any discomfort. A light jacket or sweater is usually enough for nighttime outings. The cooler evenings clearly signal that autumn is slowly approaching, but they do not take away from the holiday experience. Instead, they add a sense of comfort, calm, and relaxation, making evenings in Antalya especially enjoyable during October.

## Rainfall Patterns in October

Rainfall in Antalya during October is generally low, especially in the first half of the month, when most days remain dry and sunny. As October moves forward, there is a slightly higher chance of rain, but any showers are usually short and light. Heavy or long-lasting rain is rare, and many visitors experience little to no rain at all during their stay. Because of this, [**Antalya’s weather in October**](https://travel-antalya.com/) is not risky for travel. Outdoor plans rarely need to be canceled, and when rain does occur, it often refreshes the air and cools the surroundings rather than causing inconvenience, allowing travelers to continue enjoying their activities comfortably.

## Sea Temperature and Swimming in October

One of the biggest surprises for visitors is how warm the sea remains in October. After absorbing heat throughout the summer, the Mediterranean Sea stays warm well into autumn, with average temperatures around 24°C, which is ideal for swimming. Many travelers actually find swimming more enjoyable in October than in peak summer, as the water feels refreshing rather than overly warm. Beaches are quieter, sunbeds are easy to find, and the overall beach atmosphere is calm and relaxed. For beach lovers, October in Antalya still feels very much like summer, offering warm water, sunny days, and a peaceful seaside experience without the crowds.

## Is October Still Beach Season in Antalya?

Yes, October is absolutely still beach season in Antalya. Many locals and tourists continue to swim, sunbathe, and enjoy water sports throughout the month. Resorts keep their beach facilities open, and boat tours and sea activities operate regularly. Unlike the peak summer months, the beaches are far less crowded, and the sun feels softer and more comfortable, making long beach days more enjoyable. This creates a peaceful and relaxing beach atmosphere, perfect for unwinding by the sea. For many travelers, Antalya’s weather in October offers one of the best beach experiences of the year, combining warm sea temperatures with calm surroundings and pleasant sunshine.

## Outdoor Activities in October

October is one of the best months for outdoor activities in Antalya. The moderate temperatures make it comfortable to stay active throughout the day without feeling tired or overheated. Visitors can enjoy long walks through Kaleiçi (Old Town), explore ancient ruins such as Perge and Aspendos, or visit beautiful waterfalls like Düden and Kurşunlu. Hiking in the Taurus Mountains is also much more enjoyable in October, as the lower heat allows for longer and more comfortable trails. Outdoor cafes, local markets, and coastal walking paths feel lively yet not overcrowded, creating a relaxed atmosphere. Overall, October’s weather perfectly supports both relaxation and adventure, making it an ideal time to explore Antalya at your pace.

## Nature and Scenery in October

Nature in Antalya remains beautiful in October, offering a different kind of charm compared to spring. While spring is known for colorful flowers, autumn brings warm tones, clear blue skies, and fresh, crisp air. The surrounding mountains, forests, and coastal landscapes appear calm and peaceful, creating a relaxing atmosphere for visitors. The soft autumn light is especially ideal for photography, providing clear views, balanced brightness, and gentle shadows. Because the weather is comfortable, nature-based activities such as walking, cycling, and sightseeing are very popular during October. Overall, the season allows travelers to enjoy Antalya’s natural beauty at a slower, more relaxed pace.

## Cultural and Sightseeing Experience

October is an excellent time for cultural tourism in Antalya. With lower temperatures and fewer crowds, historical sites become much easier and more enjoyable to explore. Visitors can take their time visiting ancient cities, museums, and landmarks without feeling rushed or overwhelmed by heat. Guided tours are more comfortable, and photography is easier with soft, natural light. For travelers who love history and culture, Antalya’s weather in October provides the perfect conditions for learning, exploring, and truly appreciating the city’s rich heritage.

One of the biggest advantages of visiting Antalya in October is the significantly reduced number of tourists. Restaurants often provide better service, and visitors have a clearer view of local life and culture. This tranquil environment makes October ideal for couples seeking a romantic getaway, families wanting a relaxed vacation, and solo travelers looking for a stress-free experience, offering a perfect combination of comfort, calm, and enjoyment.

## Hotel Prices and Travel Costs in October

October falls within the shoulder season in Antalya, which offers a great balance between comfort and affordability. Prices for hotels, flights, and resorts are generally lower compared to the busy summer months of July and August, while services and amenities remain fully available. Many hotels offer special discounts, and luxury resorts become more accessible, making it easier for travelers to enjoy top-tier accommodations. Budget travelers also find better deals during this time. Because Antalya’s weather in October is still excellent, visitors get high value for money, enjoying warm days, pleasant evenings, and plenty of activities without paying peak-season prices.

## What to Pack for Antalya in October

Packing for October is simple and flexible. You need clothing for warm days and slightly cool evenings.

* Light summer clothes such as t-shirts, shorts, dresses, and swimwear are still needed. A light jacket or sweater is useful for evenings.  
    
* Comfortable walking shoes are important for sightseeing. Sunglasses and sunscreen are still necessary because the sun remains strong.  
    
* You do not need heavy winter clothes, which makes traveling lighter and easier.

Some travelers may worry that October in Antalya could be unpredictable, but in reality, the city is very stable during this month. The weather remains warm, rainfall is limited, and sea conditions are safe for swimming and water activities. There are no extreme weather risks, and all services and infrastructure operate normally, ensuring a smooth travel experience. From the perspective of safety, comfort, and convenience, Antalya’s weather in October is reliable, making it suitable for families, couples, solo travelers, and adventure seekers alike. Visitors can enjoy their holiday with confidence, knowing that mild and pleasant conditions will support both relaxation and exploration.

Compared to July and August, October in Antalya feels much more comfortable. The heat is milder, humidity is lower, and crowds are significantly smaller, making outdoor activities far more enjoyable. Visitors can still take part in swimming, sightseeing, beach walks, and outdoor dining, but with less stress and more comfort than during the busy summer months. Many travelers find that exploring the city, relaxing on the beach, or enjoying cultural sites in October is more pleasant and relaxing. Both seasons have their charms, but for travelers who enjoy beaches, swimming, and water sports, October is generally considered the better choice, providing the perfect combination of warm sea, pleasant weather, and peaceful surroundings.
