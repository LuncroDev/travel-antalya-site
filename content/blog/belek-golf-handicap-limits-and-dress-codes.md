Belek golf handicap limits are the first thing to check before you pack your clubs. Most of the region's courses ask visitors to prove their handicap, and the maximum allowed varies from 24 to 44 depending on the club, the course and whether you're a man or a woman. Dress rules are simpler but stricter than at many home clubs: proper golf clothing, no denim and soft spikes only. We opened each club's own pages on 16 November 2026 and set out what they say, club by club, with the gaps and contradictions flagged.

## What counts as proof

No club page we read names a specific scheme such as the World Handicap System. National's own handicap table still uses "EGA handicap" column headings and runs up to 54. In practice, bring your handicap certificate or a membership card or app screen from your home club that shows your current index. National says plainly that it's "advisable to bring your certificate or a copy of it" and that it can refuse anyone who can't prove their handicap.

If you don't have a handicap yet, see the beginners section below, and our [Belek golf for beginners](/blog/belek-golf-for-beginners/) guide.

## Dress codes and shoes

Only four clubs publish a dress rule, and they agree on the basics:

- **National:** "Proper golf attire, no denim; soft-spiked shoes required."
- **Cornelia:** "Appropriate golf attire is required on the course, and only soft-spike golf shoes are permitted."
- **Kaya Palazzo:** "Golfing clothing must be worn" and "only soft spike golf shoes are allowed".
- **Carya:** the club "uses non-metal spikes"; it publishes no clothing rule.

None of the pages we read spells out collars, shorts length or caps. Gloria's FAQ covers dress only for its restaurants, and Cullinan publishes no dress rule. The safe default everywhere is a collared or golf shirt, tailored shorts or trousers (no jeans, no beachwear) and soft-spike or spikeless golf shoes.

## Club by club

### National Golf Club

National is the most detailed. Its home, about, services and academy pages all say a valid handicap certificate is required, with a maximum of 36 for men and 36 for ladies. One undated page (/course) still says 28 for men, so if your handicap sits between 29 and 36, mention it when you book. National's etiquette rules:

- A fourball should take **no more than 4 hours 30 minutes**, keeping pace with the group in front.
- Spend **no more than 3 minutes** looking for a ball.
- Players who fall behind may be asked to leave the course without a refund; marshals enforce this.
- Twosomes and single players may be paired with others.
- Play stops when the lightning siren sounds.

Children may only ride in a buggy with an adult. National's rates section says buggy drivers must be 18, while its etiquette section still says 16; our [buggy hire guide](/blog/golf-buggy-hire-in-belek/) covers that. Full profile: [National Golf Club](/golf/national-golf-club/).

### Carya Golf Club

Carya's green-fee page says a handicap certificate is required, maximum 36 for men and ladies, and its course page gives the same numbers. Oddly, its tee-time and floodlight booking forms include an "I Don't Have a Handicap" option, so ask before you assume you can play without one. Carya uses non-metal spikes. Since 1 October 2025 its junior discount applies to under-16s rather than under-18s. Profile: [Carya Golf Club](/golf/carya-golf-club/).

### Antalya Golf Club (Pasha and PGA Sultan)

Kempinski's golf page gives a limit of 24 for men and women on the PGA Sultan course, and 28 for men and 36 for women on the Pasha. That makes the Sultan the strictest course in this list. The same page has a garbled line saying players with a "low" handicap can't play, which almost certainly means high. It publishes no dress code. Confirm with the club when you book. Profile: [Antalya Golf Club](/golf/antalya-golf-club/).

### Gloria Golf Club

Each of Gloria's three course pages (Old, New and the nine-hole Verde) lists "Handicap: Ladies 40, Men 36". None of them says a certificate is required. Gloria's academy says you can get a handicap certificate by joining Gloria Golf Club, and that after eight or more hours of lessons the Director of Golf issues a "Gloria" certificate. Its four-day beginners package includes time on the course and rules training with a DP World Tour referee. Profile: [Gloria Golf Club](/golf/gloria-golf-club/).

### Cornelia Golf Club

Cornelia's price page reads: "A handicap certificate is required (maximum handicap: Men 28, Ladies 36)." Its course page gives the same limits. Buggies, trolleys, hire clubs and range tokens can only be hired from the golf club itself. The same rules apply to the [Cornelia Pro-Am](/events/cornelia-pro-am-2027-03-27-serik/) in spring 2027. Profile: [Cornelia Golf Club](/golf/cornelia-golf-club/).

### Cullinan Links

Cullinan asks for a handicap certificate and sets the most generous limits for women: 36 for men and 44 for ladies. Its other rules:

- Twosomes and singles may be grouped with other players.
- Sharing a buggy is compulsory; a single buggy costs €40 extra per round on today's terms.
- Under-18s may not drive a buggy, and children may only go on the course by buggy with an adult.
- Spectators and caddies can walk with you for an extra fee.
- For floodlit rounds you need at least eight golfers and must check in an hour ahead.

Profile: [Cullinan Links](/golf/cullinan-links-golf-club/).

### Kaya Palazzo Golf Club

Both of Kaya's official pages give "Handicap Men 28 / Women 36", soft spikes only and golf clothing required. They disagree on the red (senior, junior and beginner) tees: 4,716 m par 71 on one, 4,911 m par 72 on the other. Profile: [Kaya Palazzo Golf Club](/golf/kaya-palazzo-golf-club/).

## No handicap? Your options

Belek clubs are set up for learners, but the championship courses expect proof of ability:

1. **Book a beginners' course first.** National runs a six-day intensive course of 12 lessons, maximum four people per group, with a certificate at the end. It doesn't say whether that certificate gets you onto the course.
2. **Ask about Gloria's route.** Its academy certificate after eight hours of lessons, and the course time in its beginners' package, make it the clearest path for a first-timer.
3. **Use a nine-hole or academy course.** Gloria's Verde is a nine-hole course with the same published limits. Our [beginners' guide](/blog/belek-golf-for-beginners/) explains the steps, and [golf lessons in Belek](/blog/golf-lessons-in-belek-prices/) compares lesson prices.

## Juniors and families

- **National:** under-16s pay half the rack rate, but juniors can't be more than 10% of the players in a group booking on the same day.
- **Carya:** under-16s get 50% off green fees and packages from the rack rate.
- **Cullinan:** juniors up to 15.99 get 50% off green fees, but not on two- or three-round packages.

## FAQ

### Do I need a handicap certificate to play golf in Belek?

At most clubs, yes. National, Carya, Cornelia and Cullinan all say a certificate is required. Gloria and Kaya Palazzo publish limits without saying so, but bring proof anyway; National says it can turn away players who can't show their handicap.

### What is the maximum handicap at Belek golf courses?

It ranges from 24 (men and women on Antalya Golf Club's PGA Sultan, per Kempinski) to 44 for women at Cullinan Links. Most clubs set 28 or 36 for men and 36 for women; Gloria allows 40 for women.

### Can I wear shorts on Belek golf courses?

No club page we read bans tailored golf shorts. National bans denim, and National, Cornelia and Kaya Palazzo require golf clothing. Wear golf shorts or trousers and a golf shirt, not beachwear.

### Are metal spikes allowed?

No. National, Cornelia and Kaya Palazzo allow soft spikes only, and Carya uses non-metal spikes. Spikeless golf shoes are the easy choice.
