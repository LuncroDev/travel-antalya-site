Antalya piyaz is a bean dish with a generous tahini-based dressing, not simply a white-bean salad with an optional spoonful of sesame paste. Order it by its full name, **Antalya piyazı**, or ask for **tahinli piyaz**. Those few words make your intention clear: you want the city's local version.

This guide is for choosing and ordering a plate, rather than following a home recipe. It explains the defining sauce, the toppings you can ask about and one restaurant menu checked directly on 17 November 2026. The photograph shows real tahinli Antalya piyaz photographed by Basak in September 2024. It illustrates the dish, not the current serving at any restaurant named below.

## What makes Antalya piyaz different?

The [TÜRKPATENT registration, number 315](https://ci.turkpatent.gov.tr/Files/GeographicalSigns/315.pdf), identifies the combination of small beans and tarator sauce as distinctive. Its sauce uses tahini, garlic, lemon juice, vinegar and salt; some makers also incorporate a little cooked bean. The registration describes serving the beans while lukewarm, dressed with the sauce and finished with tomato, parsley and olive oil. Onion and boiled egg are optional in the registered preparation.

This is why a photograph of a dry-looking bean-and-onion salad is not a reliable illustration of the Antalya speciality. Look for the dressing as well as the beans. The registration records the dish on 29 December 2017 and places it within Antalya province and its districts. It is a description of the regional product, not a promise that every restaurant displaying the word piyaz follows exactly the same preparation.

[Antalya's provincial culture and tourism directorate](https://antalya.ktb.gov.tr/TR-296660/antalya-piyazi-ve-piyazcilik.html) also documents the local combination of beans, tahini, vegetables and boiled egg. Its account treats piyaz as a dish in its own right rather than only a small meze. That distinction is useful when you are deciding how much else to order.

## Build your order around the plate

Our suggestion for a first taste is to start with one piyaz and decide whether you also want a grill dish. Ask about the portion before ordering several starters. If you are sharing, say so when you order rather than assuming that the kitchen will divide the dish between plates.

Do not assume that piyaz is automatically included with köfte. Look for separate entries on the menu and ask what comes with your main course. The same applies to bread, drinks and anything brought to the table before your order. A simple question about what is included is more useful than a guessed holiday budget.

For two people trying several dishes, a shared piyaz can be a sensible starting point, provided the restaurant confirms the serving size. For someone who wants only the beans, ask whether the portion is appropriate as a meal. Neither suggestion requires ordering meat, and neither means that all restaurants will offer a half portion.

## One current menu example, not a citywide ranking

The official [Şişçi İbo Lara menu](https://www.sisciibo.com.tr/lara) lists piyaz with beans, tarator, tomato, onion, egg and parsley. It also lists şiş köfte and a spicy onion version of the skewered meat dish. That makes the menu a useful concrete example of how to assemble a piyaz-and-grill order, rather than a reason to claim it is Antalya's best restaurant.

The same menu includes hibeş and a pumpkin dessert, kabak tatlısı, described with tahini and walnuts. If your aim is to explore sesame across a meal, those are other menu entries to ask about. Do not order all three simply because they are local: choose the dishes you actually want and check portions first.

## Useful Turkish phrases at the table

You do not need to deliver a long explanation. Start with the dish name, then ask one clear question. You can show these phrases on your phone:

- **Tahinli piyaz var mı?** Is there piyaz with tahini?
- **Bir porsiyon piyaz, lütfen.** One portion of piyaz, please.
- **Yumurtasız olabilir mi?** Can it be without egg?
- **Soğansız olabilir mi?** Can it be without onion?
- **İki kişi paylaşacağız.** Two of us will share it.
- **Köftenin yanında piyaz dahil mi?** Is piyaz included with the köfte?
- **Güncel menüyü görebilir miyim?** May I see the current menu?

Make topping requests before the dish is prepared. A request is not a guarantee that the kitchen can change its preparation. If the answer is unclear, ask the server to confirm with the kitchen rather than relying on the photograph or a translated menu heading.

For a vegan order, do not treat removing the visible egg as a complete check of a restaurant's preparation. Explain the requirement and ask about the whole dish. For any food allergy, tell the restaurant directly and ask it to confirm ingredients and preparation; this article is not an allergen declaration for a particular kitchen. Tahini is sesame paste, so a tahini-based sauce is not a sesame-free alternative.

## Quick questions

### Is Antalya piyaz just a salad?

The local speciality has a substantial sesame-based dressing. Official tourism descriptions distinguish it from piyaz served as a salad elsewhere and describe it as a main dish in Antalya. How you order it still depends on appetite and the restaurant's portion.

### Does it always come with egg?

A restaurant may serve egg as standard, as the checked Lara menu does. Ask for it to be omitted before ordering if that is your preference. Do not assume that the menu photograph settles the question.

### Do I need to order köfte too?

No. Decide whether you want the bean dish alone or alongside another dish. Ask what is included and how large the portions are before ordering both.
