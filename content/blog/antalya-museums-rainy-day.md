Antalya museums are the obvious plan for a rainy day, but in late 2026 the list looks different from the guidebooks. The city's great archaeological museum is closed while a new building goes up, and two other big names are shut for works. Plenty is still open: a fine small museum in the old town, Atatürk's house, a museum inside a Roman bath at Side, and Alanya's museum and castle towers. This guide covers what is open, what is closed, and how to build a wet day around it.

Our guide to [rainfall in Antalya by month](/blog/does-it-rain-a-lot-in-antalya-monthly-rainfall-explained/) explains when wet days are most likely, and [Antalya in winter](/blog/antalya-in-winter/) covers the rest of the low season.

## First, what is closed

### Antalya Museum

**The Antalya Museum is closed.** The news agency DHA reported in September 2025 that the museum shut to visitors on 16 July 2025. Its 1972 building had been judged unsafe in an earthquake, and the Ministry of Culture and Tourism ordered it demolished. DHA said about 100,000 objects were packed and moved into sealed, climate-controlled containers in the museum garden before the demolition began. The report gave November 2026 as the target for finishing the new museum.

That target has not become an opening date. When we checked on 10 November 2026, the [Ministry’s museum listing](https://www.muze.gov.tr/muze-detay?DistId=ANT&SectionId=ANT01) explicitly marked it closed. Our [Antalya Museum page](/things-to-do/antalya-museum/) will change when the ministry announces a reopening.

### Lycian Civilisations Museum and Andriake

The museum in Hadrian's granary at Andriake, near Demre, and the Andriake site itself have been closed since 17 February 2025 for new displays and landscaping, according to the Turkish Museums site. No reopening date is given.

### Side Theatre

The ministry's page for the Roman theatre at Side says it is closed to visitors for restoration. The Side Museum next to it is open; see below.

## Open museums in Antalya city

### Kaleiçi Museum

The best rainy-day museum in Antalya is in the old town. The **Kaleiçi Museum** is run by Koç University's research centre AKMED and has two parts.

- **A traditional Antalya house.** A two-storey 19th-century house with staged scenes of Turkish domestic life and customs.
- **The Church of St George (Aya Yorgi).** A protected monument in Kaleiçi, now used to show Çanakkale ceramics from the Suna and İnan Kıraç collection.

The museum's page lists opening hours of 08:30 to 17:30. It is **closed on Mondays**, on 1 January and on the first day of the Ramadan and Sacrifice holidays. When we checked, the adult ticket was 80 TL, with reduced prices for some groups. Entry is free for children aged 14 and under, visitors aged 65 and over, and people with disabilities plus one companion. The address is Barbaros Mahallesi, Kocatepe Sokak No. 22.

The museum is a short walk from [Hadrian's Gate](/things-to-do/gate-of-hadrian/). Our [Kaleiçi Old Town guide](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/) has the rest of the quarter.

### Antalya Atatürk House Museum

Mustafa Kemal Atatürk stayed in this house during his visits to Antalya in 1930 and 1935, according to the ministry. The original house was pulled down in 1984, rebuilt, and opened as a museum in 1986. The first floor tells the story of his Antalya visit in news cuttings, photographs and a documentary. The second floor has a room of his personal belongings and a display of coins minted since the Republic was founded.

The ministry lists it as open every day from 08:30 to 19:00, with the box office closing at 18:30. It is on Işıklar Caddesi in Haşim İşcan Mahallesi. It is a quick visit, so pair it with the Kaleiçi Museum.

## Side Museum

The **Side Museum** is inside a Roman bathhouse. The ministry says the bath was built in the 2nd century AD, altered in the 5th and 6th centuries, and turned into a museum between 1959 and 1961. It holds Hellenistic, Roman and Byzantine finds from the excavations at Side led by Professor Arif Müfid Mansel. The ministry adds that it was the first museum in Türkiye to open in a village.

The ministry lists it as open every day from 08:30 to 17:30, with the box office closing at 17:00. The address is Side Mahallesi, Liman Caddesi No. 1. The museum and theatre pages show different ticket prices and say one ticket covers both, but the theatre is closed, so check what you are paying for at the desk.

Our [Side Ancient City guide](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/) covers the temple by the sea and the rest of the peninsula. Side is in Manavgat district, on the way to Alanya.

## Alanya Museum and the castle museums

The **Alanya Museum** has been open since 1967, according to Turkish Museums. Its star piece is a bronze statue of Heracles, 52 cm high and dated to the 2nd century AD, shown in its own hall with a mosaic of Hylas. The museum also displays an inscription dated to 625 BC and finds from Alanya Castle. Winter hours, from 2 October to 30 April, are 08:30 to 17:30. The address is Saray Mahallesi, İ. Hilmi Balcı Caddesi No. 2.

When the rain stops, go up to the castle. The ministry says the ground floor of the **Red Tower**, built in 1226, holds an ethnography museum, and the Seljuk **shipyard** beside it is now a ship and maritime museum. Our [Alanya Castle guide](/things-to-do/alanya-castle/) has hours, tickets and the cable car.

## St Nicholas Memorial Museum, Demre

The church of St Nicholas in Demre is run by the ministry as the **Aziz (St) Nikolaos Anıt Müzesi**. Most of a visit is under a roof, which helps on a wet day. The ministry lists it as open every day from 08:30 to 19:00. Our [Myra and St Nicholas Church guide](/blog/myra-and-st-nicholas-church-demre/) explains the saint's story and pairs the church with the rock tombs of Myra, a short drive away.

## Elmalı Museum

Inland, in the mountain town of Elmalı, the **Elmalı Museum** tells the story of the Elmalı Treasure, a famous hoard of ancient coins. The ministry says the museum shows copies of the coins and that the originals were displayed at the Antalya Museum. It lists Elmalı Museum as open every day from 08:30 to 17:30. See our [Elmalı district guide](/destinations/elmali/) for the area. In heavy rain, check the mountain roads before you drive.

## A rainy-day plan in Antalya city

- **Morning:** the Kaleiçi Museum. Allow an hour or so for the house and the church.
- **Lunch:** stay in the old town. [Seraser](/restaurants/seraser-fine-dining/), [Hasanağa](/restaurants/hasanaga-restaurant/) and [Mermerli](/restaurants/mermerli-restaurant/) are all in Kaleiçi.
- **Afternoon:** the Atatürk House Museum, then a café near the harbour.
- **Evening:** a concert or a play. See [concerts in Antalya](/events/category/concerts/) and [theatre in Antalya](/events/category/theatre/).

Remember that the Kaleiçi Museum is closed on Mondays. On a Monday, swap the order and look at the events listings first.

## Museum passes

The ministry's list for the **MuseumPass The Mediterranean** includes the Antalya Museum, Alanya Castle and Alanya Museum, the Antalya and Alanya Atatürk houses, the Side Museum and Side Theatre, Elmalı Museum, the St Nicholas Memorial Museum, and open-air sites such as Perge, Aspendos and Myra. The Kaleiçi Museum is not on the list; it is run by Koç University, not the ministry. Some sites on the pass are closed for now, so count only the ones you can visit before you decide whether a pass is worth it.

## FAQ

### Is the Antalya Museum open?

No. DHA reported that it closed on 16 July 2025 and that its old building was demolished to make way for a new museum, with completion targeted for November 2026. When we checked on 10 November 2026, the Ministry explicitly listed the museum as closed. Check our [Antalya Museum page](/things-to-do/antalya-museum/) for updates.

### What is the best museum in Antalya to visit now?

In the city, the Kaleiçi Museum. Its old Antalya house and the Church of St George are both indoors, and it is in the old town, close to cafés and restaurants. Remember it is closed on Mondays.

### Which Antalya museums are open on Mondays?

The ministry lists the Atatürk House Museum in Antalya, the Side Museum and the St Nicholas Memorial Museum as open every day. The Kaleiçi Museum is closed on Mondays.
