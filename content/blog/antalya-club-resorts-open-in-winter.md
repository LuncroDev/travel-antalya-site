Club resorts in Antalya are built for summer: kids' clubs, slides, beach sport and a full evening programme. In winter most of them close, and the ones that stay open run a smaller, quieter version of the club. If you want an all-inclusive club holiday between November and March, the question is simple: **which clubs are actually open?** On 22 November 2026 we opened the official pages of the two big club brands with resorts on the Antalya coast, **ROBINSON** and **TUI MAGIC LIFE**, and noted each club's published season, its winter pools and how far it is from the airport.

Short answer: **TUI MAGIC LIFE Jacaranda** near Side runs a winter season, **ROBINSON Nobilis** in Belek is open all year, **TUI MAGIC LIFE Masmavi** opens for part of the winter, and **TUI MAGIC LIFE Belek** is closed until April. For hotels more widely, see our guide to [Kemer and Belek hotels open in winter](/blog/kemer-belek-hotels-open-winter-2026-27/).

## TUI MAGIC LIFE Jacaranda: the full winter club

Jacaranda is the only club in this group with a dedicated **winter season**, from 8 November 2026 to 1 May 2027. It sits on a gently sloping sandy beach about 3 km from Çolaklı and 12 km from Side, an hour's transfer from Antalya Airport. TUI says it opened in April 2014.

What winter looks like there, from TUI's page:

- **Pools:** seven in all, but only the **indoor pool and indoor baby pool are heated**, from November to April. The outdoor pools and the three shared lagoon pools in the bungalow area are unheated.
- **Slides:** the four-slide aqua park runs only from April to October.
- **Food and drink:** the Magico buffet plus seven speciality restaurants and nine bars, with a 24-hour Wunderbar.
- **Children:** MyMo SwimClub lessons for children from age 4.
- **Times:** rooms ready from 15:00; check-out by 12:00. Late check-out costs extra, with no published price.

Read the full profile: [TUI MAGIC LIFE Jacaranda](/hotels/tui-magic-life-jacaranda/). For the area, see our [Manavgat district guide](/destinations/manavgat/).

## ROBINSON Nobilis: open all year, with golf

ROBINSON describes Nobilis as open all year. It is a big club, **404 rooms in 13 categories**, on a river east of Belek, 8 km from the centre. You cross a bridge to reach the beach, which ROBINSON calls a 20 km shingle-and-sand beach.

Its winter pool set-up, as published:

- **Indoor pool:** 360 m², heatable, with a water slide that runs from November to March.
- **Relaxation pool:** for guests aged 16 and over, heated from about November to April.
- **Main pool (950 m²) and 25 m sports pool:** not heatable.
- **Children's pool:** 240 m², with a slide from April to early November; there are two heatable baby pools.

Nobilis has its own 18-hole course, which makes it the obvious club for a [winter golf break](/blog/winter-golf-in-antalya/); our [ROBINSON Nobilis Golf Club](/golf/robinson-nobilis-golf-club/) page has the course details. Rooms are ready from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00; late check-out costs €55 until 15:00 or €90 after. The club lists German, English and Turkish as its languages, and pets aren't allowed.

## TUI MAGIC LIFE Masmavi: open in shoulder months

Masmavi, on a sandy beach about 3 km from Kadriye and 5 km from Belek, publishes two winter windows: **9 to 29 November 2026** and **27 February to 30 April 2027**. TUI's page lists nothing between 30 November and 26 February, so don't plan a December or January stay there.

When it is open in winter, the **Relax Pool (16+)** is open all year and heated in winter, and the Island Pool is listed as open all year. The 50 m Activity Pool and the Yakamoz Pool run from April to October. TUI lists 13 pools and 5 water slides in all. Its page gives the airport as about 35 km, a 40-minute drive; it also mentions a 40-minute transfer from Dalaman Airport, which looks like a template slip, since Dalaman is much further away. See our full [TUI MAGIC LIFE Masmavi](/hotels/tui-magic-life-masmavi/) page.

## TUI MAGIC LIFE Belek: closed until April

TUI MAGIC LIFE Belek, also near Kadriye, publishes summer seasons only: 11 April to 8 November 2026 and **17 April to 14 November 2027**. It is closed now. It has seven pools and ten water slides, but TUI marks every pool as unheated, so even its April opening is a sea-and-sun bet. For a spring break, it suits families who want slides; see [TUI MAGIC LIFE Belek](/hotels/tui-magic-life-belek/).

## ROBINSON Çamyuva and Pamfilya

ROBINSON runs two more clubs in Antalya: the adults-only **[ROBINSON Çamyuva](/hotels/robinson-camyuva/)** near Kemer and the family club **[ROBINSON Pamfilya](/hotels/robinson-pamfilya/)** near Side. Their club pages could not be opened on 22 November 2026. ROBINSON's Turkey overview page that day showed bookable travel windows of 22 November 2026 to 7 November 2027 for Çamyuva and 22 April to 7 November 2027 for Pamfilya. These are offer windows, not season dates: Nobilis, which is open all year, showed a window starting only on 30 April 2027. When we last read Çamyuva's own page, on 18 November, it gave a season starting 16 April 2027, so ask ROBINSON before booking either club for winter.

## How to choose a winter club

- **For a family break in December or January:** Jacaranda is the only club here with a published winter season and a heated indoor pool for children. Ask which kids' clubs and age groups run during your week.
- **For golfers:** Nobilis, with its own course and an all-year season. Belek's other courses are a short drive away; compare them in our [Belek golf courses guide](/blog/belek-golf-courses-compared/).
- **For couples:** both Nobilis and Masmavi have heated 16+ pools in winter, but Masmavi is open only in its two winter windows.
- **For warm-water swimming:** no club heats its main outdoor pools in winter. If a heated outdoor pool matters, read our [heated pools guide](/blog/antalya-hotels-with-heated-pools-winter/) instead.

Whichever you choose, the winter sea is cool and the days are short; our guide to [Antalya with kids in winter](/blog/antalya-with-kids-in-winter-2026-27/) has ideas for days out, and the [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) covers getting to Belek and Side.

## What to check before you book

1. **The exact dates.** Seasons are set by each club and published on its own page. Check that your nights fall inside one.
2. **Which pools are heated.** "Seven pools" in winter may mean one indoor pool.
3. **Kids' clubs and entertainment.** Winter programmes are usually smaller; ask what runs during your week.
4. **Language.** Nobilis lists German, English and Turkish. Ask any club which languages its kids' clubs and shows use.
5. **Times and extras.** Check-out is by 11:00 at Nobilis and by 12:00 at Jacaranda and TUI MAGIC LIFE Belek, and late check-out is charged at all three. Each also lists some restaurants or drinks outside the all-inclusive, so check what's included.

## FAQ

**Which club resorts in Antalya are open in winter 2026–27?**
TUI MAGIC LIFE Jacaranda (winter season 8 November 2026–1 May 2027) and ROBINSON Nobilis (open all year). TUI MAGIC LIFE Masmavi opens for part of the winter: 9–29 November 2026 and from 27 February 2027.

**Are the pools heated at Antalya's club resorts in winter?**
Only some. Jacaranda heats its indoor pools from November to April; Nobilis lists its indoor pool as heatable and heats its 16+ relaxation pool from about November to April; Masmavi heats its 16+ Relax Pool in winter. Outdoor main pools are unheated.

**When does TUI MAGIC LIFE Belek reopen?**
On 17 April 2027, for a season to 14 November 2027.

**Is ROBINSON Nobilis open in January?**
Yes. ROBINSON describes the club as open all year.
