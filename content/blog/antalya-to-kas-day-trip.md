An Antalya to Kaş day trip is one of the great drives on the Turkish coast. It is also a long one. Kaş is 187 km from Antalya by road, according to the Highways Directorate (KGM) distance table, and most of that is a winding two-lane coast road through Kemer, Kumluca, Finike and Demre. This guide sets out the route, the stops worth your time, and a timetable that gets you home before midnight. It also makes the case for staying a night.

## The route at a glance

The KGM table gives these road distances from Antalya city centre:

| Town | Distance from Antalya |
|---|---|
| Kemer | 44 km |
| Kumluca | 94 km |
| Finike | 112 km |
| Demre | 140 km |
| Kaş | 187 km |
| Kalkan | 213 km |

The road follows the coast for most of the way, with hilly sections between the towns. There is no motorway, and you pass through town centres and plenty of bends. No official source publishes a driving time. As a planning estimate, allow at least three hours each way without stops, and check a live navigation app on the morning you leave. That is six hours or more in the car for the round trip, which is why we recommend an overnight stay if you can.

## By car, tour or bus

**By car** is the most flexible way to do it as a day trip. You choose your stops and can turn back early.

**By organised tour** is the easiest, because someone else drives. Before you pay, check exactly which sites are included and whether entry fees are covered.

**By bus** works for a one-way trip or an overnight stay, but not for a same-day return with stops. Batı Antalya, whose "About" page says it has carried passengers since 2000 on its Antalya–Kaş–Fethiye–Marmaris route, sells tickets from Antalya to stops including Kumluca, Finike, Demre, Kekova, Kaş and Kalkan. It does not publish a timetable or journey time online, so call its 24-hour line on (0242) 331 40 81 or check at the bus station.

## Stop 1: Myra and its rock tombs (Demre)

Myra is the headline ancient site on the route, 140 km from Antalya. The ministry's page describes a Roman theatre built below the cliff of rock-cut tombs, and calls it one of the few ancient theatres that have survived. The Antalya Culture and Tourism Directorate calls it the region's largest theatre. The house-like tomb façades carved into the cliff are the reason most people come. The ministry also points out the reliefs on the tombs, including those in the river necropolis.

**Opening and entry:** When we checked on 9 November 2026, the ministry listed Myra as open every day from 08:00 to 19:00, with the box office closing at 18:30. The fee was shown as €13. Hours change seasonally, so check the [official Myra page](https://muze.gov.tr/muze-detay?sectionId=MYR01&amp=&distId=MYR) before you go.

## Stop 2: St Nicholas Church (Demre)

A few minutes away in Demre town is the Church of St Nicholas, built over the tomb of the bishop whose legend inspired Santa Claus. The ministry's page says his relics are now in Bari, Italy. It also notes a Roman sarcophagus decorated with fish scales and acanthus leaves. GoTürkiye highlights the church's frescoes and mosaics.

**Opening and entry:** The ministry listed the church as open every day from 08:30 to 19:00, box office until 18:30, at €17 when we checked. The ministry's "MuseumPass The Mediterranean" list includes Myra, the St Nicholas Museum, Simena, Arykanda, Limyra and Patara. If you plan several sites over a few days, compare the pass price with the separate tickets.

The old harbour of Andriake, near Demre, now houses the Lycian Civilisations Museum, according to the Antalya Culture and Tourism Directorate. It's a good extra stop if you have time. See our [Demre district guide](/destinations/demre/) for more.

## Stop 3: Kekova, Üçağız and Simena

The sunken city of Kekova is seen from the water. The Antalya Culture and Tourism Directorate says boats hired at Üçağız, 36 km from Kaş, take visitors to Kaleköy (ancient Simena) and Kekova Island. Kekova is a protected area, and entering the water over the sunken ruins is banned. You look at the walls and stairs from the boat. Simena, reached only by sea, has a castle above the village and what the directorate calls the smallest theatre in Lycia.

Add a boat trip to six hours of driving and the day becomes too long, so Kekova belongs on an overnight trip or an organised tour. If you're driving and want to see it, consider staying the night in Kaş or Üçağız.

## Stop 4: Kaputaş Beach

Between Kaş and Kalkan, the coast road crosses the mouth of a gorge. Below it is Kaputaş, the turquoise cove you will recognise from photos. Antalya Metropolitan Municipality says it is reached by 187 steps from the road, about 20 km from Kaş and 7 km from Kalkan. GoTürkiye puts it about 20 minutes' drive from Kaş. The beach is small, so go early or late in summer. Our [Kaputaş Beach guide](/blog/kaputas-beach-travel-antalya/) has more, and the [best beaches in Antalya](/blog/best-beaches-in-antalya/) guide compares it with Patara further west.

## Stop 5: Kaş town

Kaş was the ancient Lycian city of Antiphellos. The municipality's history page says its earliest name was Habesos. Three things are worth an hour or two:

- **The King's Tomb.** A 4th-century BC Lycian sarcophagus carved from a single block, with an eight-line Lycian inscription. It stands on Uzun Çarşı, the old market street, and locals call it the King's Tomb.
- **The ancient theatre.** The culture directorate dates the theatre of Antiphellos to the 1st century BC and says it has 26 rows of seats and room for about 4,000 people.
- **The harbour.** GoTürkiye calls Kaş Antalya's diving centre. Even if you don't dive, the harbour is where the evening happens.

For a meal, try [Naturel Restaurant](/restaurants/naturel-restaurant-kas/) on Uzun Çarşı or [Bi Lokma](/restaurants/bi-lokma-restaurant/) in Andifli. For more ideas, see our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/).

### Can you add Meis (Kastellorizo)?

Not on a day trip from Antalya. The Kaş municipality says Meis lies only 2.7 km off the Kaş coast, and ferry companies run crossings from Kaş harbour. But it is a separate border crossing into Greece. The Meis Express rules say to arrive at the port an hour before departure, and passport and visa rules depend on your nationality. Leave it for a day based in Kaş.

## A realistic Antalya to Kaş day trip timetable

This is a sample plan for a self-drive day in the long-daylight months, assuming no long delays. Treat the times as estimates.

- **07:00** Leave Antalya. Pick up breakfast in Kemer or Kumluca.
- **10:00** Myra rock tombs and theatre (1 hour).
- **11:15** St Nicholas Church in Demre (45 minutes).
- **12:30** Lunch in Demre, or drive on and eat in Kaş.
- **14:00** Kaş town: King's Tomb, theatre, harbour (2 hours).
- **16:30** Kaputaş Beach for a swim (1 hour), then head back the same way.
- **21:00–22:00** Back in Antalya, depending on traffic and stops.

In winter, daylight is much shorter and site hours may close earlier. Cut the plan to Myra, St Nicholas and Kaş, and drive the mountain stretches in daylight. Our [Antalya in winter](/blog/antalya-in-winter/) guide explains what to expect.

## Make it an overnight trip instead

If you can spare a night, the trip gets much better. Drive down with stops at [Phaselis](/things-to-do/phaselis-ancient-city/) and [Olympos](/things-to-do/olympos-and-the-chimaera-flames/), sleep in Kaş, and do Kekova or Kaputaş the next morning. Small hotels in town include [Casa Bi](/hotels/casa-bi-kas/) on the peninsula and [Hideaway Hotel](/hotels/hideaway-hotel/). Further west, [Villa Mahal](/hotels/villa-mahal/) is in Kalkan.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far is Kaş from Antalya?

187 km by road from Antalya city centre, according to the Highways Directorate (KGM) distance table. The route follows the coast through Kemer, Kumluca, Finike and Demre.

### Is an Antalya to Kaş day trip worth it?

Yes, if you accept a long day in the car and pick only two or three stops. Myra, St Nicholas Church and Kaş town fit comfortably. Kekova and Meis are better on an overnight trip.

### Can I take a bus from Antalya to Kaş?

Yes. Batı Antalya sells Antalya–Kaş tickets with stops including Finike and Demre. It doesn't publish a timetable online, so check departures by phone or at the bus station. Buses suit a one-way trip or an overnight stay better than a same-day return.

### Do I need tickets for Myra and St Nicholas Church?

Yes, both charge entry. The ministry listed Myra at €13 and St Nicholas at €17 when we checked on 9 November 2026. Both are on the ministry's MuseumPass The Mediterranean list. Check the official pages for current hours and fees.
