Konyaaltı is Antalya's long western beach, backed by the Bey Mountains, and it is the best-placed part of the city for a winter stay: you can walk the seafront, reach the old town by taxi or tram, and use a hotel with an indoor pool when the weather turns. This guide compares four hotels on the Konyaaltı seafront using what their own websites say, as checked in mid-November 2026. We have not stayed at any of them.

For the area itself, see our [Konyaaltı district guide](/destinations/konyaalti/) and the [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/) page.

## Porto Bello Hotel Resort & Spa

A large beach hotel built in 2002 and restored in 2019, in the Liman quarter. It has a 150 m private Blue Flag beach, a 1,000 m² outdoor pool, a 100 m² heated indoor pool "pitched as a year-round option", a hammam, a sauna and a mini club. The main restaurant seats 450 for buffets, and there is a 60-seat à la carte restaurant.

**Caveats:** the site never names the board basis (only "the selected concept") and gives no distances, not even to the airport. The room pages list central heating but do not mention air conditioning. [Full profile](/hotels/porto-bello-hotel-resort-spa-konyaalti/).

## SeaLife Family Resort Hotel

A five-star family hotel on GMK Bulvarı with three connected blocks. It lists four pools: a slide pool, an outdoor "Relax" pool, a heated 79 m² indoor pool and a children's pool. The outdoor pools open 1 April to 31 October and beach service 1 May to 15 October, weather permitting, so in winter the indoor pool is the one to count on. The hotel puts itself 4 km from the city centre and 20 km from the airport.

**Caveats:** the site gives 239 and 240 rooms on the same page, never names the board basis, and does not say how far the sand is from the hotel. [Full profile](/hotels/sealife-family-resort-konyaalti/).

## Crowne Plaza Antalya

IHG's 194-room hotel on Akdeniz Bulvarı is described as a business hotel, but it works for winter holidays. The heated indoor pool runs 09:00 to midnight, the spa 10:00 to midnight, and the 10th-floor Neapolis Fine Dining has a terrace. Konyaaltı Beach is across the boulevard; the Tünektepe cable car is next door.

**Caveats:** IHG lists no private beach, sunbeds or airport shuttle. It gives the airport as 23 km in one place and 21 km in another. Minimum check-in age is 18. [Full profile](/hotels/crowne-plaza-antalya/).

## Hotel SU

Our page lists it as a design hotel at the eastern end of the Konyaaltı beach area, with its official address in Meltem, Muratpaşa. We could not confirm pool, spa or winter details from its site. Older listings call it "SU & Aqualand", so ask about any waterpark access and operating dates directly. [Full profile](/hotels/hotel-su-aqualand/).

## How to choose

- **Families who want a beach in summer and a pool in winter:** SeaLife or Porto Bello.
- **Couples or business travellers who want a city base:** Crowne Plaza.
- **Anyone:** ask what the rate includes, because two of the four hotels never state the board basis online.

## Getting around from Konyaaltı

The seafront is walkable in dry weather. For the old town, a taxi is simple; our [AntalyaKart guide](/blog/antalyakart-guide/) covers the tram and bus. In wet weather, read [Antalya museums for a rainy day](/blog/antalya-museums-rainy-day/).

## FAQ

### Which Konyaaltı hotels have a heated indoor pool?

Porto Bello (100 m²), SeaLife (79 m²) and Crowne Plaza (heated, 09:00–midnight) according to their own websites.

### Is Konyaaltı good in winter?

It is a good base for walking, sightseeing and day trips, but not for beach swimming. See [Antalya in winter](/blog/antalya-in-winter/).
