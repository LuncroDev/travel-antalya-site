The Montgomerie Maxx Royal golf course is the Belek layout that Colin Montgomerie designed with European Golf Design. It opened on 1 September 2008, runs to **6,522 metres, par 72**, and both starts and finishes with a par 5. Its back nine is floodlit, so it is one of the few places in Antalya where you can still be on the course after dark. This guide walks through all 18 holes using the club's own hole-by-hole page and championship fact sheet, which we read on 22 November 2026. Lengths are in metres, as the club prints them.

For the club's profile, booking notes and photos, see [Montgomerie Maxx Royal Golf Club](/golf/montgomerie-maxx-royal-golf-club/). To compare it with its neighbours, read our [Belek golf courses compared](/blog/belek-golf-courses-compared/).

## The course at a glance

The championship page describes 104 hectares (260 acres) of seaside parkland and forest, with **8 lakes** and about **7 hectares of bunkers**, waste bunkers included. Greens average about 600 m². The back nine, holes 10 to 18, has floodlights.

The hole-by-hole page gives four sets of tees: **Black, Silver, Gold and Red**. It prints no totals, so we added up the club's hole figures:

| Tee | Front nine | Back nine | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black | 3,204 m | 3,318 m | **6,522 m** |
| Silver | 2,846 m | 3,016 m | **5,862 m** |
| Gold | 2,674 m | 2,853 m | **5,527 m** |
| Red | 2,335 m | 2,587 m | **4,922 m** |

The black total matches the 6,522 m on the championship page. Par is 35 out and 37 home.

### Slope ratings

The championship page gives slope ratings of **143 (black), 136 (white) and 125 (yellow) for men**, and **142 (yellow) and 139 (red) for women**. Note that it names white and yellow tees, while the hole-by-hole page uses Silver and Gold, so ask the starter which colour matches your card.

For context: Carya's own slope page lists 145 from its black tees and 127 from its red, and Sueno's Pines course document shows slopes from 134 to 148. Under the World Handicap System, 113 is the slope of a course of standard difficulty, so all of these are demanding from the back tees.

## The front nine, hole by hole

Lengths are Black / Red; the stroke index (SI) shows how the club ranks each hole's difficulty, 1 being the hardest.

1. **Par 5, 522 m / 410 m (SI 7).** Several hazards from the first tee. The club's advice is simple: hit an accurate drive and get off to a steady start.
2. **Par 3, 148 m / 107 m (SI 17).** A short hole and an early birdie chance, or a place to recover from the first.
3. **Par 4, 367 m / 253 m (SI 13).** A long drive that cuts the corner is rewarded. A deep bunker guards the right of the green.
4. **Par 5, 527 m / 378 m (SI 9).** Risk and reward: a long, accurate tee shot can set up a go at the green in two.
5. **Par 3, 205 m / 88 m (SI 11).** A long carry over water from the back tee; keep your eye on the flag. The red tee is far shorter.
6. **Par 4, 441 m / 336 m (SI 1).** The hardest hole on the card: an undulating fairway and a long approach.
7. **Par 4, 420 m / 321 m (SI 5).** You need a good drive to carry the water, then an approach to a raised, well-defended green.
8. **Par 3, 166 m / 121 m (SI 15).** A stream crosses the fairway; the club calls it a "magic magnet for golf balls".
9. **Par 4, 408 m / 321 m (SI 3).** Place the drive well for a better angle into a green guarded by a bunker.

## The back nine, under the lights

These are the floodlit holes. If you are booking a twilight or night round, this is the stretch you will play in the dark; our [night golf in Belek guide](/blog/night-golf-in-belek/) explains how evening rounds work across the resort.

10. **Par 4, 331 m / 246 m (SI 16).** Downhill, and the club calls it an easy start to the second nine.
11. **Par 5, 521 m / 428 m (SI 10).** Aim at the almost unreachable central bunker. The green is raised, so the approach decides the hole.
12. **Par 4, 448 m / 340 m (SI 2).** One of the toughest par 4s on the course; the bunker on the left sits well short of the green.
13. **Par 5, 516 m / 421 m (SI 8).** A dogleg with two tall pines standing in the middle of the fairway.
14. **Par 3, 161 m / 119 m (SI 14).** Water to carry. The club asks for "a courageous mid-iron".
15. **Par 4, 308 m / 235 m (SI 6).** The shortest par 4, but the fairway narrows the further you hit it, so a long drive isn't automatically better.
16. **Par 3, 165 m / 109 m (SI 18).** A good view from the tee; the club says it is one of Colin Montgomerie's favourite holes.
17. **Par 4, 358 m / 286 m (SI 12).** Slopes, a bunker and pines. The club says the slopes are the main difficulty.
18. **Par 5, 510 m / 403 m (SI 4).** The clubhouse is in sight, but there is a long fairway to cover first, with deep bunkers on the left.

## Strategy notes

Reading the card as a whole, three things stand out:

- **Water on the short holes.** Three of the five par 3s (5, 8 and 14) involve water or a stream, so take enough club.
- **The par 5s are scoring holes for long hitters.** Holes 4 and 11 reward a big drive; 13 and 18 punish a wayward one with trees and bunkers.
- **The hard holes come in pairs.** SI 1 and SI 3 (holes 6 and 9) close the front nine; SI 2 (12) comes early on the back.

## Practical information

- **Handicap limits:** men 28, women 36, per the championship page. Our [handicap limits and dress codes guide](/blog/belek-golf-handicap-limits-and-dress-codes/) compares every Belek club.
- **Shoes:** soft spikes only.
- **Practice:** a double-ended driving range with 25 bays, a putting green of about 1,010 m², a 750 m² chipping green and a 600 m² bunker green. Lessons are available seven days a week.
- **Buggies and hire:** golf cars, pull trolleys and rental clubs are available at the golf reception.
- **Clubhouse:** two floors with a 30-metre viewing tower over the 18th, the à la carte Golf Club Restaurant, Bueno Steak House and the Ryder Bar.

## FAQ

**How long is the Montgomerie Maxx Royal course?**
6,522 m from the black tees, par 72. Adding up the club's hole figures gives 5,862 m from silver, 5,527 m from gold and 4,922 m from red.

**What handicap do I need?**
The club publishes a maximum of 28 for men and 36 for women.

**Which holes are floodlit?**
Holes 10 to 18, the back nine, according to the championship page.

**Which is the hardest hole?**
The 6th, a 441 m par 4 from the black tee, is stroke index 1. The 12th (SI 2) and the 9th (SI 3) are next.
