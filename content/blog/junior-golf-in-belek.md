Junior golf in Belek is better organised than most parents expect. Three clubs charge under-16s half the rack-rate green fee, five publish junior lesson prices or children's packages, and one has run a junior academy since 2006. The catch: every club draws its own age line. A 14-year-old is a junior for lessons at Cornelia but pays the adult price at National. We opened every club's official pages on 13 November 2026, and all prices below are **published rates checked 13 November 2026; confirm when you book**.

Adults learning from scratch should read [Belek golf for beginners](/blog/belek-golf-for-beginners/). For adult rates this winter, see [Belek green fees for winter 2026–27](/blog/belek-green-fees-winter-2026-27/).

## Junior green fees: half price at three clubs

National, Carya and Cullinan all halve the rack rate for under-16s. Applied to their published winter rack rates (1 December 2026 to 10 February 2027 at National and Carya, to 11 February at Cullinan), that gives:

| Club | Adult rack, 18 holes | Junior, 18 holes | Adult rack, 9 holes | Junior, 9 holes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| National | €95 | €47.50 | €65 | €32.50 |
| Carya | €120 | €60 | €85 | €42.50 |
| Cullinan Links | €140 | €70 | €100 | €50 |

The junior figures are our arithmetic from each club's rule, not prices printed on the cards.

### The small print

- **National** treats players aged 15.99 and younger as juniors. Juniors may not make up more than 10% of the players in a group booking on the same day.
- **Carya** applies the 50% to green fees *and* packages, always from the rack rate, so a junior pays less than the €95 winter Regnum guest rate. Its group-booking condition is worded differently from National's, so ask. Carya's junior limit was under 18 until 30 September 2025 and has been under 16 since.
- **Cullinan** uses the same 15.99 cut-off. The discount does not apply to its 2- and 3-round packages; for several rounds, it is worked out from the 18-hole rack rate. The fee includes a shared buggy for every two players, which matters for children (see below).

Cornelia's two price pages show no junior green fee, and the other clubs publish none. Ask for one in writing.

## Junior academies and lessons, club by club

### National: Belek's long-running junior academy

National has the most developed junior programme we found. Its academy page says the NGC Junior Academy started in 2006 with 12 children aged 7 to 9 and now holds about 80 pupils aged 7 to 19, at least 15 of whom have played for the Turkish national team. Training covers rules, etiquette, course management, fitness, nutrition, sports psychology and custom-fitted clubs.

The page gives no training days, fees or entry rules, and doesn't say whether visiting children can join. Ask.

For holiday lessons, National prints a junior price list for under-14s: €75 for one 50-minute lesson (adults €100), rising to €725 for 12. Two juniors sharing pay €125. The page also offers under-14s 20% off package prices, without saying whether that comes on top of the junior table. The PGA professionals teach in Turkish, English, Russian and German.

### Carya: the same junior table

Carya, National's sister club in the Regnum group, prints exactly the same lesson prices, including the under-14 junior row. Lessons are taught from a TaylorMade Performance Lab, and Carya's pros between them speak Turkish, English and Russian. The resort is [Regnum Carya](/hotels/regnum-carya/).

### Cornelia: junior rates for 14 and under

Cornelia publishes the fullest junior price list. It defines a junior as 14 years of age and under and prints a junior price beside every adult one. All lessons use TrackMan, and a video of the lesson follows by email.

- **One-hour lesson, one pupil:** €65 with the Head Professional (adult €90), €60 with the Teaching Professional (adult €80).
- **Four-day school, 8 hours:** two hours a day, morning or afternoon, four pupils per instructor. Junior €380 with the Head Professional or €340 with the Teaching Professional (adults €480 and €440).
- **Five-day school, 12 hours:** ten hours of tuition plus a two-hour playing lesson on the Faldo course. Junior €510 or €450 for one pupil.

One oddity: for a group of four on the 12-hour school, junior and adult prices are the same (€420 or €360). The page shows no season or year, so confirm before you pay. Head Professional Uğur Dinler lists a Leadbetter Kids certification. [Cornelia Diamond](/hotels/cornelia-diamond-golf-resort-spa/) is the resort.

### Gloria: four-day packages for children

Gloria sells two children's packages over four days, with four-hour sessions in groups of two to six. Both include equipment and range balls:

- **Child Beginner Package:** €550 per person, for children who want to learn.
- **Child Golfer Package:** €700 per person, for children who already play. It covers video analysis, a short-game clinic, trouble shots and course management, and ends with a Gloria certificate signed by Director of Golf David Clare.

Gloria gives no ages for either package, and its single-lesson price list has no junior rate. Its free one-hour taster lessons for hotel guests, which it pitches at families, run only from 15 June to 31 August (Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays at 10:00). See [Gloria Golf Resort](/hotels/gloria-golf-resort/).

### Cullinan Links: 20% off for under-13s

Cullinan's academy gives junior golfers under 13 a 20% discount. Its package table starts at €100 for one 50-minute individual lesson, so by our sums an under-13 pays about €80. The same page lists a "PGA Professional Master Program by Cahit Kaya" at €200 for 50 minutes, without saying whether the discount applies. There is no separate children's programme. The resort is [Cullinan Belek](/hotels/cullinan-belek/).

### ROBINSON Nobilis: VOLVO Golf Kids

The ROBINSON club runs paid children's courses for ages 8 and up in the main school holidays (on request at other times). Sessions last 50 minutes, in groups of six or more, with balls and clubs provided; the course totals four hours. One line says "children over 4", which conflicts with the minimum age of 8, so ask. No price is shown.

### Sueno and Antalya Golf Club: ask for details

Sueno's academy page mentions junior programmes designed for children, but gives no ages or prices. Its undated factsheet says beginners must have lessons first and lists a 60-minute lesson at €80 for one, €100 for two or €120 for three, with no junior rate. Antalya Golf Club lists junior training among its academy services, without details.

## Why the age bands don't match

Here is what "junior" means on the pages we opened:

- **Under 13:** Cullinan lessons.
- **Under 14:** National and Carya lessons. **14 and under:** Cornelia lessons.
- **Under 16 (15.99 and younger):** green fees at National, Carya and Cullinan.
- **Under 18:** the buggy-driving cut-off at National and Cullinan.
- **7 to 19:** National's junior academy.
- **8 and up:** ROBINSON's children's courses.

So a 15-year-old pays adult lesson prices everywhere but half the green fee at three clubs. Give each child's age when you book.

## Buggies and teenagers

Cullinan says children under 18 may not drive golf carts and may only be on the course in a buggy with an adult. National says the same: drivers must be 18 or over, and children ride only with an adult. Sueno's factsheet bars under-16s from driving. Carya and Cornelia say nothing on the pages we opened.

This matters most at Cullinan, where the green fee comes with a shared buggy. As written, two teenagers playing together would need an adult in the cart. Ask how the club handles junior pairs, and whether they can walk with a trolley.

## When can a junior play the course?

Children face the same handicap rules as adults. National asks for a handicap certificate with a maximum of 36, Carya sets 36 for men and ladies, and Cullinan allows 36 for men and 44 for ladies. Cornelia sets 28 for men and 36 for ladies. Kaya Palazzo marks its red tees, 4,911 m at par 72, for senior, junior and beginner players. ROBINSON prints a stricter handicap table for every Belek club than several clubs' own pages, so go by the club.

A child without a handicap should start at the academy. Gloria awards its own certificate after more than eight hours of lessons and says a handicap certificate comes with Gloria Golf Club membership. National gives a certificate after 12 lessons. Neither certificate is a handicap.

## Booking checklist for families

1. **Give each child's exact age**, because the cut-offs differ by club and by product.
2. **Ask for the junior green fee in writing.** Only three clubs publish one.
3. **Check what a playing lesson includes.** Cornelia's include a nine-hole green fee; Cullinan's and National's exclude the green fee and buggy.
4. **Plan buggy pairings** for anyone under 18.
5. **Bring the handicap certificate** if your child will play the course.

## FAQ

### Do children pay green fees in Belek?

Yes, but less at some clubs. National, Carya and Cullinan Links charge under-16s 50% of the rack rate. This winter that works out at €47.50 for 18 holes at National, €60 at Carya and €70 at Cullinan.

### What age is a junior golfer in Belek?

It depends on the club and the product. For green fees it is under 16 at National, Carya and Cullinan. For lessons it is under 14 at National and Carya, 14 and under at Cornelia, and under 13 at Cullinan.

### Which Belek club has a junior golf academy?

National's NGC Junior Academy has run since 2006 and holds about 80 pupils aged 7 to 19. For holiday lessons, Cornelia, Gloria, National, Carya and Cullinan publish junior prices or children's packages.

### Can a teenager drive a golf buggy in Belek?

Not at National or Cullinan, where drivers must be 18. Sueno bars under-16s from driving. At National and Cullinan, children may only ride in a buggy with an adult.
