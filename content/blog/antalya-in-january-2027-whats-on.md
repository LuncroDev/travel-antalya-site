January is quiet on the Antalya coast, but it is not empty. The beach clubs are shut and the big resorts are thin on guests, yet the city's theatres, trade-fair halls and Belek's golf courses keep a steady calendar. This guide lists what we could confirm for January 2027 on an organiser's or ticket seller's own page on 20 November 2026. It is not a complete list: January schedules fill in during December, so check back.

For the weather side of the question, read [is January a bad time to visit Antalya?](/blog/is-january-a-bad-time-to-visit-antalya-weather-explained-honestly/). This page is about what you can do.

## Theatre, concerts and family shows

The municipal Nazım Hikmet centre in Konyaaltı has the most theatre in January. Our listings show *Hastalık Hastası* on 9 January at 20:00 (about 70 minutes per Biletinial) and *Haybeden Gerçeküstü Aşk* on 31 January, with the start time not yet published. Both are in Turkish. If you do not speak the language, read our [guide to theatre in Antalya for non-Turkish speakers](/blog/antalya-theatre-comedy-non-turkish-speakers/) before you book.

For children, the hall's list includes *Çizmeli Kedi Müzikali* (Puss in Boots) on 24 January, a 60-minute Turkish musical for ages 3 and up, with tickets from 500 TL on Biletinial when we checked. See [Antalya with kids in winter](/blog/antalya-with-kids-in-winter-2026-27/) for more.

Live music is lighter in January than in December. Our list has Gökhan Türkmen at The Bar Kaleiçi on 20 January at 22:00. More venues are covered in [live music in Antalya this winter](/blog/live-music-antalya-winter-venues/).

## Golf in January

January is the month for junior and senior golf in Belek. The Turkish golf calendars we read list:

- **U.S. Kids Golf local tour days** at National on 9 and 10 January and at Carya on 23 and 24 January.
- **Legends Tour Qualifying School** at Gloria's Old and New courses. Practice days are 14–15 January; the four rounds run 16–19 January. It is a qualifying tournament for players over 50, and the organiser's page says nothing about spectators.
- **U.S. Kids Golf Antalya Turkish Open** at Carya on 30–31 January, with registration and practice from 28 January.

None of the club pages we read says a course closes to visitors during these events, but they take up tee times. Read [Belek golf tournaments, winter 2026–27](/blog/belek-golf-tournaments-winter-2026-27/) and [Belek green fees for winter](/blog/belek-green-fees-winter-2026-27/) before you book.

## Trade fairs and hotel demand

The two ANFAŞ fairs, food products and hotel equipment, run side by side from 12 to 15 January at the ANFAŞ Expo Center in Aksu, from 10:00 to 18:00. They are trade events, so the public is unlikely to be admitted without a registration. Fairs like this bring business visitors to the city, so rooms near Aksu and Lara can be busier than the season suggests. Check the organiser's page for entry rules.

## Things to do in Antalya in January when nothing is "on"

- Walk [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/) and the harbour. January light is soft and the crowds are gone.
- Visit ancient sites with space to yourself: [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/), [Perge](/things-to-do/perge-ancient-city/) and [Termessos](/things-to-do/termessos/). See [Aspendos and Side in one day](/blog/aspendos-and-side-in-one-day/) for winter hours.

- Go up to the snow: [Saklıkent ski day trip](/blog/saklikent-ski-day-trip-from-antalya/), if the season has opened.
- Pick a hotel with a heated pool: [hotels with heated pools for winter](/blog/antalya-hotels-with-heated-pools-winter/).

## FAQ

### Are there festivals in Antalya in January?

We could not confirm a large public festival for January 2027. Most of the month's confirmed listings are theatre, concerts, trade fairs and golf events.

### Is 1 January a public holiday?

Yes. Our [Christmas in Antalya](/blog/christmas-in-antalya/) guide explains the holiday and what is open around it.

### Can visitors watch the Legends Tour Qualifying School?

The organiser's page we read does not mention spectators, so ask Gloria or the Legends Tour before you plan a visit.

### Is it worth visiting Antalya in January?

If you want quiet roads, cheap rooms and sightseeing in cool, clear weather, yes. If you want beach weather, no. See our [January guide](/blog/is-january-a-bad-time-to-visit-antalya-weather-explained-honestly/) for the honest version.
