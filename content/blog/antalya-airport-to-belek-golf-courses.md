Antalya Airport to Belek's golf courses is a short hop: the clubs that publish a figure put themselves 25 to 45 minutes away by road. That's close enough to play on the afternoon you land, if your flight is early and your bag arrives with you. On 21 November 2026 we opened the official pages of Belek's golf clubs and noted how far each says it is from the airport, where it sits, and what it says about shuttles, transfers and bag storage. **Every figure below is as the club published it.** Clubs measure in different ways, some to the course and some to their resort, and several publish nothing at all.

For the airport itself, terminals and public transport, see our [Antalya Airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/). For getting your clubs onto the plane, read [flying to Antalya with golf clubs](/blog/flying-to-antalya-with-golf-clubs/).

## How the clubs measure

Read the table as a rough guide, not a league. Kaya Palazzo's figure, 27 km, comes from its golf club's own course page. Sueno gives 30 km and a 30-minute transfer, but to its hotel next door. Carya and National both say 25 minutes' drive, in the same words. Yet National's accommodation page says "about 30 minutes", so the club itself gives two figures. Gloria's 30–35 minutes is written for the resort. ROBINSON's 40 km and 45 minutes are for the club at Acısu, east of the main hotel strip. The 20-minute claim on Cullinan's home page is for Belek as a whole.

Every figure the clubs publish is under an hour. The gap between them is smaller than the gap between a quiet morning arrival and a long wait at the bag carousel.

## The Kadriye cluster: Kaya Palazzo, Carya, National and Sueno

Four clubs give Kadriye or Belek addresses close together, and between them they publish the most transfer detail.

**Kaya Palazzo** gives the most precise location: 27 km from Antalya Airport, 35 km from Antalya city centre and 2.5 km from Kadriye, which it calls the nearest town centre. Its course page lists men's and ladies' locker rooms and a bag store room, without a price.

**Carya and National** belong to the Regnum group. Each club's "how to go" text says it is 25 minutes from the airport and 40 km from the old city of Antalya. National's accommodation page adds the most useful line in this guide: Regnum provides complimentary transfers between its hotels and the two courses, and the Regnum The Crown resort runs a free shuttle to both clubs. The same page puts National five minutes and Carya three minutes away by road. If you plan to play both, a stay at [Regnum Carya](/hotels/regnum-carya/) removes most of the driving.

**Sueno's** 2025 fact sheet gives Antalya Airport as 30 km and a 30-minute transfer to the hotel, Belek 5 km and Antalya 40 km. Free buggy shuttles link [Sueno Hotels Golf Belek](/hotels/sueno-hotels-golf-belek/) and the club, and a free shuttle runs to the Sueno Deluxe. The club also has a separate golf-bag storage room, free of charge.

## Gloria, Cornelia and the Belek seafront

**Gloria's** FAQ puts the resort about 30–35 minutes from Antalya Airport by car. It says private luxury transfers can be arranged through the reservation team, without a price, and that internal shuttles run between the resort's facilities. Its pages still show a 2024 copyright, so treat the details as indicative. See [Gloria Golf Resort](/hotels/gloria-golf-resort/) for the hotel.

**Cornelia** publishes no airport distance. Its golf pages say the course is two minutes from [Cornelia Diamond](/hotels/cornelia-diamond-golf-resort-spa/), with a free shuttle from the Cornelia hotels, and list locker rooms and bag storage.

**Montgomerie Maxx Royal** gives an address on İskele Caddesi in Belek but no distance or transfer information. Neither does **Antalya Golf Club**, whose pages link to [Kempinski The Dome](/hotels/kempinski-hotel-the-dome-belek/) and Sirene Belek under "privileges". **Cullinan Links** describes itself as a club by the sea and the Beşgöz river.

## ROBINSON Nobilis: the eastern edge

[ROBINSON Nobilis](/hotels/robinson-nobilis/) is the furthest of the published figures: 40 km and about 45 minutes from the airport, and 8 km from Belek's centre. Its course is in the club's own grounds, and ROBINSON lists a golf shuttle to Belek's other courses for a fee. If you want to play several courses from Nobilis, build the shuttle cost into your budget.

## Taxi, hotel transfer or hire car?

None of the clubs sells an airport transfer at a published price. Your realistic options are a taxi from the rank, a transfer booked through your hotel, or a hire car. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) covers each.

- **Staying at a golf resort?** Book the transfer through the hotel and tell them you have a golf bag. A big bag needs a big boot.
- **Playing several clubs?** Look first at hotels with free shuttles to the courses, such as the Regnum and Cornelia groups, before you hire a car.
- **Driving yourself?** A car makes sense if you're staying away from the courses or want to mix golf with days out to [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/) or [Side](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/).

## Your golf bag on arrival

Your airline sets the rules until the carousel; after that it's down to the club. Three clubs say they store bags: Sueno (free), Cornelia and Kaya Palazzo (no price given). Ask your own club before you book, and check whether you need a deposit or a tag. If you'd rather not fly with clubs at all, hire sets are listed in our [golf buggy and hire guide](/blog/golf-buggy-hire-in-belek/).

- **Arrival day:** an early flight and a 25–35-minute transfer leave time for nine holes or the range, but book a late tee time and keep it flexible.

## FAQ

### How far is Belek from Antalya Airport?

It depends on the course and on how the club measures. Kaya Palazzo gives 27 km. Carya and National say 25 minutes' drive, Sueno 30 km and 30 minutes, and Gloria 30–35 minutes. ROBINSON Nobilis, further east, gives 40 km and about 45 minutes.

### Which Belek golf club is closest to Antalya Airport?

On published distances, Kaya Palazzo (27 km) and Sueno (30 km) are the closest, with Carya and National quoting 25 minutes' drive. Clubs measure differently, so allow some leeway.

### Is there a shuttle between Belek golf courses?

Some hotel groups run one. Regnum hotels offer free transfers between their hotels and Carya and National. Cornelia runs a free shuttle from its hotels to its course. Sueno runs free buggy shuttles between its golf hotel and the club. ROBINSON Nobilis lists a paid shuttle to other courses.

### Can I store my golf bag at the club?

Sueno has a free golf-bag room. Cornelia lists bag storage and Kaya Palazzo a bag store room, without prices. Other clubs publish nothing, so ask when you book.
