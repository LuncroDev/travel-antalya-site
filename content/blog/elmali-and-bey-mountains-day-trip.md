Elmalı is a highland district in the Bey Mountains, inland from Finike and Kaş, where Antalya's coastal climate gives way to cold winters and apple orchards. It has a small museum with replicas of one of the great Lydian coin hoards, painted Lycian tombs, and a quiet old centre. It is a long day from Antalya, and in winter the weather needs checking. This guide sets out what the published sources say and what to confirm.

## Getting there and the climate

Elmalı is about 111 km from Antalya according to one listing. A Turkish newspaper feature says the drive on the D635 takes about an hour and a half from the city. The same feature gives the town's altitude as 1,100 metres. Wikipedia describes it as having "an inland climate of cold winters and hot summers" and notes it is much cooler than the coast, with a cedar forest near Lake Avlan. So even when Antalya is mild, Elmalı can be cold or snowy; check the forecast and the road before you set out.

## Elmalı Museum

The Antalya Elmalı Museum was created from the former Government House in the town. The Ministry of Culture and Tourism's museum plan describes burial chambers from Karaburun and Kızılbel, "one of the most important discoveries in the Elmalı area", whose murals can be seen on the first floor. The museum also holds copies of the Elmalı Treasure: the originals are in the Antalya Museum. Because the main Antalya Museum building is currently closed (see [Antalya museums for a rainy day](/blog/antalya-museums-rainy-day/)), the Elmalı copies are one of the few places to see the story told.

The local tourism site lists finds from Karataş Semahöyük, the Hacımusalar Tumulus and other excavations, and says the Kızılbel and Karaburun chamber paintings are reconstructed at original size in halls 4 and 8 on the first floor. A Turkish newspaper feature calls the Elmalı Treasure among the richest hoards of Lydian-era coins in the world.

**Hours:** sources conflict. A Wikivoyage extract lists daily 08:30–20:00, while a museums portal lists ticket-office closing at 17:30. The museum's phone is +90 242 618 4442 (as listed). Phone before you drive.

## Muhammed Hamdi Yazır City Museum

Elmalı was the birthplace of the scholar Elmalılı Muhammed Hamdi Yazır (1877–1942). A city museum dedicated to him has operated since 2013, with works and objects about his life, according to the local tourism site. Wikivoyage notes it is in a mansion he never lived in.

## The old centre and the mosque

Wikivoyage describes Elmalı as small enough to cover on foot, with the Seljuk-era Kesik Minaret across the street from the Ömer Paşa Mosque. The local tourism site recommends not leaving without seeing the Ömer Paşa Mosque. Opening conditions for the mosque are not listed, so respect prayer times and dress modestly.

## Nature: Lake Avlan and the Bey Mountains

The local tourism site mentions Avlan Lake and its endemic plants. In winter the lake and mountain roads may be wet or snowy, so treat this as a summer or spring add-on unless you have local advice.

## Food and wine

A Turkish feature says Elmalı's altitude suits high-altitude vineyards, and recommends booking tastings in advance. We have not verified individual wineries.

## Where to stay, if you do not go back

An older travel guide says Elmalı has a couple of small hotels but that it makes more sense to stay in Finike, and that hourly buses link the two. That information may be out of date. For Finike, see our [Finike district guide](/destinations/finike/) and [Finike, Limyra and Arykanda in one day](/blog/finike-limyra-arykanda-one-day/); in winter, see [Finike oranges](/blog/finike-oranges-antalya-winter-citrus/).

## A sensible plan

1. Leave Antalya early, by about 08:00.
2. Phone the museum before leaving.
3. See the Elmalı Museum, then the old centre.
4. Eat locally and drive back before dark. Our [sunset times](/blog/sunrise-sunset-daylight-hours-antalya/) give the day length.
5. Bring winter clothes and, if snow is forecast, ask about chains. See the [Saklıkent guide](/blog/saklikent-ski-day-trip-from-antalya/) for the same mountain-road caution.

## FAQ

### How far is Elmalı from Antalya?

About 111 km by one listing and around 1.5 hours by road, according to a Turkish feature.

### Is Elmalı worth a day trip in winter?

Only if you check the weather and the museum's hours. It is cold and can be snowy.

### Where can I see the Elmalı Treasure?

The originals are in the Antalya Museum, according to the Ministry's plan; the Elmalı Museum shows copies.
