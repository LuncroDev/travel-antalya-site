This is a dated guide for the **9–20 November 2026** COP31 programme in Antalya. The conference venue is at Antalya EXPO Center in **Aksu**. For a Green Zone visit, plan the ride and the walk inside the grounds as separate steps. The archive image shows a city tram in 2022, not the conference shuttle fleet.

## Choose a route before leaving

The [Antalya Governorship’s shuttle and tram directory](https://www.gocop31.com/en/servis-hatlari) lists stops, departure times and route details. From the city centre, look for the **E110 between Meydan and the COP31 venue** and read its [line timetable](https://www.gocop31.com/en/servis-hatlari/e110), including the direction. The directory also distinguishes **T1A towards the airport** from **T1B towards Expo**. Check the destination before boarding. Do not assume that all shuttles and trams have the same intervals or that every departure shown serves your stop.

The Governorship describes the shuttle lines as free and says its line data does not specify an accreditation requirement. We have not verified boarding conditions for every Green Zone visitor or specific stop. Check the [official transport FAQ](https://www.gocop31.com/en/sss) or ask staff before depending on a shuttle.

## Check the gate and your registration

[Register to visit the Green Zone](https://cop31.tr/register-to-visit) and look for the entry QR code in your visitor account. The [Governorship’s venue map](https://www.gocop31.com/en/yesil-bolge) shows approximate venue locations and suggested walking routes from Gates 1, 2 and 3. Use it to estimate the walk, then follow signs and staff at the site. Green Zone registration and Blue Zone accreditation are separate.

## Expo Boulevard vehicle restriction

The [Governorship’s dated traffic notice](https://www.gocop31.com/en) says Expo Boulevard and its junction with Turizm Yolu are reserved for COP31 shuttles **14–21 November 2026**, with exceptions for vehicles of residents and businesses on the boulevard. Don't assume a taxi or hire car can drop you at the gate. Ask your driver or accommodation for the current drop-off point. This is a specific approach-road restriction, not a statement that the D-400 is closed.
