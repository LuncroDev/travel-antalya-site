Seventeen restaurants in Antalya province earned at least one toque in the Gault&Millau Türkiye 2026 guide, announced in December 2025. This Gault&Millau Antalya list runs from 7 Mehmet, the province's only three-toque table, to a jazz meyhane in Çağlayan, a kebab house by the Kepez wholesale market and a tasting-menu kitchen in Beycik village, Kemer. Below: how the scoring works, the full list with toques and areas, and how to plan a dinner around it.

First, a clarification. Gault&Millau (G&M) is a separate guide from Michelin, with its own inspectors, scores and symbols. The [2026 MICHELIN Guide Türkiye](https://www.michelin.com/en/publications/products-and-services/the-michelin-guide-turkiye-reveals-its-2026-restaurant-selection) covers Istanbul, İzmir, Muğla and Cappadocia, and Antalya is not part of it. A toque is not a star, and none of the restaurants below is in the Michelin restaurant selection. Our [note on Michelin hotels and Keys](/blog/michelin-key-hotel-guide-antalya/) explains the difference on the hotel side.

## How Gault&Millau scores restaurants

Henri Gault and Christian Millau, two food critics, published the first guide in Paris in 1969. The Türkiye edition is much younger. The 2026 guide is its third, and the results were presented at Çırağan Palace Kempinski in Istanbul on Monday 8 December 2025, then posted on [G&M Türkiye's site](https://www.gault-millau.com.tr/gaultmillau-turkiye-2026-gastronomi-rehberi-odulleri-sahiplerini-buldu-2/) on 11 December. Inspectors covered 11 provinces, Antalya among them, and the cover of the printed guide promises more than 350 restaurant ratings and 30 special award categories.

Each restaurant gets a score out of 20. The quality and flavour of the food come first, but G&M says it also weighs service, price and atmosphere. Eleven points earns the first toque, the chef's hat that is the guide's symbol:

| Score | Toques | G&M's label |
|---|---|---|
| 10–10.5 | None | Original Tables (recommended) |
| 11–12.5 | 1 | Gourmet Tables |
| 13–14.5 | 2 | Chefs' Tables |
| 15–16.5 | 3 | Outstanding Tables |
| 17–18.5 | 4 | Prestigious Tables |
| 19–19.5 | 5 | Exceptional Tables |

Two notes. G&M Türkiye's [About page](https://www.gault-millau.com.tr/gault-millau-hakkinda/) still describes one to four toques, while the 2026 guide prints five bands; we follow the guide. The same page says restaurants cannot pay to be listed. And the half-points matter: a 12.5 and an 11 both carry one toque, but the score shows where each sits in the band.

## Special awards with an Antalya address

Three Antalya names took special awards on the night:

- **7 Mehmet** won a Second-Generation Business Award, credited to Mehmet Akdağ. G&M's profile calls him the executive chef and the third generation of the family brand.
- **Flow Manavgat Şelale** received a Distinctive Establishment Award. It has no toque in the Antalya list.
- **Nirvana Cosmopolitan**, the hotel that houses Chayote, was named Best Convention Hotel.

The headline prizes went elsewhere: Restaurant of the Year to Teruar Urla in İzmir, and Chef of the Year to Aret Sahakyan of Maçakızı in Bodrum.

## City centre and Kaleiçi

**7 Mehmet** stands in Atatürk Kültür Parkı in Meltem, west of the old town. G&M reads it as a Mediterranean restaurant giving regional flavours a modern treatment: seasonal meat, poultry, vegetables and fruit cooked simply, with local producers supplying Antalya's own ingredients. Book through the form on [its website](https://7mehmet.com/en/reservation), which lists hours of 11:00 to midnight. The brand also has an Istanbul restaurant; the three toques belong to the Antalya one.

**Parlak** has cooked in the Zincirli Han, opposite the Clock Tower at Kalekapısı, since 1954, and three generations of the family have run it. Its own site calls charcoal-grilled chicken the house speciality and takes bookings by phone on +90 242 241 65 53, with hours of 12:00 to midnight. Parlak has appeared in all three Turkish editions, its score rising from 11 to 11.2 to 11.5.

**Seraser Fine Dining** is at Paşa Cami Sokak No:14 in Kaleiçi. G&M files the neighbourhood as Selçuk and the restaurant's site says Tuzcular, but the street and number match. G&M's review describes a wide-ranging menu, from Mediterranean greens and Italian-style plating to Far Eastern aromatics, with a list of world wines. The restaurant opens evenings only, 16:00 to midnight, and recommends booking online. Walk our [Kaleiçi route](/blog/kaleici-walking-route/) before dinner.

**Lara Balık**, despite the name, is rated at its Tevfik Işık Caddesi address in Gençlik. G&M points to seasonal fish and close to a hundred meze.

## Lara side: Şirinyalı, Çağlayan and Kemerağzı

Three of the four seafood restaurants on the list are on the Lara side of the city:

- **Antalya Balıkevi** (Çağlayan): seasonal Mediterranean fish, olive-oil meze and both grilled and pan-cooked dishes in a relaxed garden setting, says G&M. Executive chef İmam Bilgin.
- **Göz Balık** (Şirinyalı): founder and chef Necati Doğrusoy. We found no 2026 review text beyond the listing, so check the restaurant's own menu.
- **İskele Balık Lara** (Lara Caddesi No:199): G&M places it between fine dining and a classic fish house, running from meze and hot starters to fresh fish off the grill.

Two more Lara-side picks are not seafood houses. **Asmani** occupies the upper floors of the Akra hotel with wide sea views; G&M mentions a sushi section and a seasonal, farm-to-table approach. **Zaruri**, on a side street in Çağlayan, is a jazz-themed meyhane that reworks the classic meze evening; our [meyhane guide](/blog/ocakbasi-and-meyhane-nights-in-antalya/) explains how those nights run.

Further east at Kemerağzı, **Chayote** sits inside the Nirvana Cosmopolitan hotel. It takes its name from the local *dikenli kabak*, and chef Tuncay Gülcü draws on Antalya and Muğla roots for Mediterranean and Aegean cooking with a modern edge. G&M also notes a refreshed dining room and a new wine offer. For daytime plans nearby, see our [Lara beaches guide](/blog/lara-public-beaches/).

## Konyaaltı and Kepez

No restaurant on the 2026 Antalya list has a Konyaaltı address. G&M's earlier review of Lara Balık says it also serves guests in Konyaaltı, but the rated address is in Gençlik.

Inland in Kepez, **Volkan Şişçi Kadir'in Yeri** on Altınova Caddesi, by the Yeni Toptancı Hal wholesale market, is the list's plainest table. G&M praises tender meat, cacık made with the house's own yoghurt, and quick, friendly service. Its most-ordered dishes, G&M says, are şiş köfte, tahinli piyaz and kabak tatlısı. Our [Antalya piyaz guide](/blog/antalya-piyaz-tahini-bean-dish/) and [local dishes guide](/blog/what-food-is-antalya-known-for-local-dishes-to-try/) cover what to expect.

## Belek and Kadriye

**Nemo** and **Asil** are both SWOT Hospitality restaurants at The Land of Legends in Kadriye ([Serik district](/destinations/serik/)). G&M describes Nemo as aquarium-themed, pairing European seafood with Pan-Asian flavours, cocktails and Japanese sake. Asil puts Turkish, Lebanese and Moroccan cooking on one menu.

Nemo's contact page lists lunch from 13:00 and dinner from 18:00 to midnight. It gives two different last food orders, 22:45 and 23:15, so ask when you book via SevenRooms or on +90 505 178 13 46. Asil opens 14:00 to midnight, with last food orders at 23:00, and books online or on +90 507 634 08 34.

## Side

**Karma Restaurant Side** shares the top one-toque score in Antalya with Nemo, at 12.5. G&M likes its stylish plates, vegetarian options, seasonal menus and local, regional produce, on the waterfront in the centre of ancient Side. Karma's own site describes a restaurant by day and nightlife after dark, takes online bookings for two to seven people and lists +90 537 440 15 04. It publishes no hours. G&M gives the address as Turgut Reis Caddesi No:60 and Karma's site as No:160. More in our [Side old town food guide](/blog/eating-in-sides-old-town/) and [Side day trip](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/).

## Further afield: Kemer, Alanya and Kaş

**The Rabbit Hole** is inside Olympos Mountain Lodge in Beycik village, near Beydağları National Park ([Kemer district](/destinations/kemer/)). Chef-founder Mehmet Ali Börtücene cooks a casual fine-dining menu that changes nightly. The lodge's page says dinner is a tasting menu only, no à la carte, served 19:00 to 21:00 with the last sitting at 20:30. Non-residents are welcome with at least one day's notice, by phone or WhatsApp on 0533 042 20 62. The page listed 4,500 TL per person when we checked. This is not Olympos Lodge in Çıralı, which is a [different property](/hotels/olympos-lodge-cirali/). Our [Kemer to Olympos coast road](/blog/kemer-to-olympos-coast-road/) covers the drive.

**Seasons Restaurant** in Alanya is chef Mutlu Şevket Yılmaz's place. G&M notes a major renovation into a minimal, modern room, a bigger wine list, and an à la carte menu built on regional flavours plus a sushi menu and an omakase option. Pair it with [Alanya in a day](/blog/alanya-in-a-day/).

**Capra Çukurbağ** is Serra Beklen's restaurant in Çukurbağ village, Kaş. The menu changes weekly on a farm-to-table basis, and the drinks follow a vineyard-to-table idea. See our [Antalya to Kaş day trip](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) for the road.

## How to book a Gault&Millau dinner in Antalya

1. **Use the restaurant's own channel.** For Asil, Karma and The Rabbit Hole, G&M's profile and the restaurant's site list different phone numbers. We have given the restaurant's own.
2. **Watch the hours.** The Rabbit Hole's last sitting is 20:30, and Seraser opens from 16:00. Karma publishes no hours at all.
3. **Give notice for tasting menus.** The Rabbit Hole needs a day's notice from non-residents. Seraser also offers a seasonal tasting menu.
4. **Ask about access.** Chayote and Asmani are hotel restaurants, and Nemo and Asil sit inside The Land of Legends. Ask how non-residents get in and where to park.
5. **Flag allergies early.** 7 Mehmet's booking form has an allergy field, and Seraser asks for dietary needs when you book.
6. **Check the edition.** G&M keeps older profiles online. The 2026 awards post is the reference for current toques.

## FAQ

### Does any Antalya restaurant have a Michelin star?

No. The 2026 MICHELIN Guide Türkiye restaurant selection covers Istanbul, İzmir, Muğla and Cappadocia, not Antalya. The awards on this page are Gault&Millau toques, from a different guide.

### What does one Gault&Millau toque mean?

A score of 11 to 12.5 out of 20, which G&M calls a Gourmet Table. Two toques start at 13 and three at 15.

### Which is the highest-rated Gault&Millau restaurant in Antalya?

7 Mehmet, with three toques and 15/20 in the 2026 guide. Chayote and The Rabbit Hole follow with two toques each, both at 13/20.

### When is the next Gault&Millau Türkiye guide?

The 2026 guide was presented on 8 December 2025. When we checked on 24 November 2026, G&M Türkiye's site had not announced a 2027 edition or a ceremony date. If you are reading this after December 2026, check G&M Türkiye's site for newer scores.
