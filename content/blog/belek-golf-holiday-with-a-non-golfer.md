A Belek golf holiday with a non-golfer works when the hotel does as much for the partner who doesn't play as the course does for the one who does. Turkey's official tourism portal, GoTürkiye, counts 15 golf courses along Belek's 17 km of coast, and many of the big resorts sit on a course or run a shuttle to one. This guide compares what seven golf resorts publish for non-golfers (spas, beaches, pools and kids' clubs), what the clubs say about partners who want to come along on the course, and the best days out nearby. Everything comes from the resorts' and clubs' own pages, checked on 11 November 2026.

If you are still choosing dates, start with our [Belek golf season guide](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/). If your partner fancies a first lesson, read [Belek golf for beginners](/blog/belek-golf-for-beginners/).

## Can a non-golfer go out on the course in Belek?

Only one club we checked publishes a clear rule. **Cullinan Links** says spectators or caddies may accompany players on the course for an extra fee. Its green-fee table lists an "Additional Non Golfer/Spectators/Single Buggy Supp." at €40 in most periods of the 2026–27 season and €30 from 1 December 2026 to 11 February 2027. Shared buggies are compulsory at Cullinan, and under-18s may not drive and may only ride with an adult.

The pages we opened for Carya, National, Sueno, Cornelia, Gloria, Montgomerie Maxx Royal and Kaya Palazzo say nothing about walkers or buggy passengers. What they do publish:

- **Buggy driving ages.** National and Cullinan set the limit at 18 (National's "About" page, checked 13 November 2026); Sueno does not let under-16s drive.
- **Handicap limits.** Carya and Sueno set a maximum of 36 (Sueno says it may lower this to 28 in busy seasons). Kaya Palazzo allows 28 for men and 36 for women; Cullinan allows 36 for men and 44 for women.
- **Dress.** Sueno asks for golf attire and soft spikes, and Kaya Palazzo allows soft spikes only.

If your partner wants to ride along for a few holes, ask the club, and the price, when you book the tee time.

## Gloria Golf Resort: try a free lesson

Gloria has the most golf, with 45 holes, and it is the easiest place for a curious non-golfer to have a go. Its free taster lessons for hotel guests run from **15 June to 31 August**, on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays at 10:00, for an hour; the brochure caps them at 40 people and says there are no free lessons in winter. Hotel guests get preferred green-fee rates.

For the partner who would rather not swing a club, **La Source Spa** (800 m²) has 15 therapy rooms, and the saunas, hammam, steam room and a 113 m² indoor pool are free for guests. Under-16s may not use the sauna, hammam or steam room. The 500-metre sand and pebble beach has a pier and a pier bar. There is an aquapark, tennis on four courts with free floodlighting by reservation, and free classes such as yoga, Pilates and Zumba. The outdoor pools close in winter. See our [Gloria Golf Club profile](/golf/gloria-golf-club/) for the courses.

## Regnum Carya: a walking trail around the course

Regnum Carya suits a couple where one person plays and the other wants to keep moving. The resort's summer factsheet lists a **4.6 km hiking trail around the Carya course**, and a free shuttle runs to both Carya and [National Golf Club](/golf/national-golf-club/). Carya's rate card shows a lower green fee for Regnum guests.

The **Revive** spa covers 5,000 m², and its hammam, steam room, saunas, ice fountain and indoor and outdoor pools are free; treatments cost extra. The 450-metre white-sand beach has a pier, but the factsheet gives the official beach season as **1 May to 31 October**. There is also an 18+ adult pool of 945 m² with its own bar, a 20,000 m² waterpark, and tennis, padel and pickleball for an extra charge. In the winter season (1 November to 31 March), pools are heated depending on the weather. Read our [Carya Golf Club profile](/golf/carya-golf-club/) for the course.

## Cornelia Diamond: spa and a short shuttle

At Cornelia, the 27-hole course designed by Nick Faldo is two minutes from the hotel by free shuttle, and it can be played as three different 18-hole loops. The driving range is free for hotel guests.

The **Crassula Spa** covers 5,000 m² on two floors, with traditional and "Sultan" hammams; its relax area is free for hotel guests. There is even a massage aimed at golfers. The beach measures 250 by 55 metres, with a 1,150 m² pier. For families, **Cornie Kids World** covers 5,000 m² and opens from 10:00 to 22:00, with a baby area for ages 1–3, a teen club, an 800 m² water park and three cinemas. See our [Cornelia Golf Club profile](/golf/cornelia-golf-club/).

## Sueno Hotels Golf Belek: golf and beach in one place

Sueno's two courses, Pines and Dunes, sit beside the hotels, and buggy shuttles run between the hotels and the course. The club's academy says beginners must take lessons before they play, and a 60-minute lesson costs €80 on its 2025 factsheet. Hotel guests eat free at the Birdie clubhouse restaurant, which is charged to outside players.

For the non-golfer, the beach is about 220 metres of sand, and Sueno calls its pier of about 1,800 m² the largest in the Belek region; it opens from 10:00 to 19:00. The spa has a hammam, sauna, steam room, jacuzzi and five massage rooms, and the Exclusive SPA, shared with Sueno Deluxe, adds a 40°C thermal pool and a salt room. The spa lists a 15% discount on treatments for pro golf groups. See our [Sueno Golf Club profile](/golf/sueno-golf-club/).

## Cullinan Belek: the biggest spa and a ride-along rule

Cullinan suits a partner who wants to ride along: its club is the only one we found that sells a non-golfer supplement. Off the course, the **C'espace Spa** covers 8,000 m², with a Turkish bath and indoor and outdoor pools. The hotel describes an 800-metre private sandy beach and 13 types of pool, including an aquapark with 13 slides.

The **Upupa Kids Club** takes ages 1–3 and 4–12, with a teen club for 13–16. Its hours run from 09:30 to 02:00 in summer and 09:30 to midnight in winter, and parents can track children with a wristband. Read our [Cullinan Links profile](/golf/cullinan-links-golf-club/) for the courses.

## Kempinski The Dome and Maxx Royal

**Kempinski The Dome** stands within [Antalya Golf Club](/golf/antalya-golf-club/), and twelve Lagoon Golf Suites sit on the course grounds. A 2024 press release describes a 3,600 m² Sanitas spa with Ayurvedic treatments. The Aladdin Castle Kids Club takes ages 4–12, with children's cooking classes and a heated indoor pool in winter; it closes at 17:30 in winter. See our [Kempinski The Dome hotel page](/hotels/kempinski-hotel-the-dome-belek/).

**Maxx Royal Belek** has its own floodlit course, Montgomerie Maxx Royal. For the partner, the **Maxx Wellbeing Spa** has a hammam, double massage rooms with jacuzzis and a spa suite, and the resort lists ten pools. Its Maxxi Land club runs Smiling Babies (1–3), a Mini Club (4–7) and a Junior Club (8–12), plus a game centre with bowling. See our [Montgomerie Maxx Royal profile](/golf/montgomerie-maxx-royal-golf-club/).

## Days out for the non-golfing partner

- **[Aspendos theatre](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/).** The ministry lists the site as open from 08:00 to 17:00, with the ticket office closing at 16:30. Kempinski puts it about 20 km from the hotel.
- **[Perge](/things-to-do/perge-ancient-city/).** Open 08:00–17:00 with the box office closing at 16:30, according to the ministry. Its list of temporarily closed sites shows Perge's theatre closed for restoration, so you will see the colonnaded streets but not the theatre.
- **[Side](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/).** The ministry's ticket table marks Side's theatre as closed, but the town, the harbour and the Temple of Apollo make a good half-day.
- **[Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/).** Kempinski gives the old town as 35 km away. Our [Kaleiçi walking route](/blog/kaleici-walking-route/) takes you from Hadrian's Gate to the old harbour.

If you plan several ruins, check whether a [MuseumPass](/blog/museumpass-the-mediterranean-antalya/) pays for itself.

## Winter or summer?

Winter is the cheaper season for the golfer. Carya's rack rate for 18 holes falls from €195 in October and November to €120 from 1 December 2026 to 10 February 2027, and Cullinan's falls from €220 to €140. The trade-off is for the partner: Gloria's outdoor pools close, Regnum's beach season has ended, kids' clubs keep shorter hours, and Gloria's free taster lessons stop. In winter, choose a resort with a big indoor spa and a heated pool. In summer, teen clubs and taster lessons are running, but the heat pushes golf to early morning and evening; our guide to [night golf in Belek](/blog/night-golf-in-belek/) explains the floodlit options.

## A booking checklist for mixed couples

1. **Ask whether a non-golfer may ride in the buggy**, and what it costs. Only Cullinan publishes a fee.
2. **Check buggy driving ages** if teenagers will come along: 18 at National and Cullinan, 16 at Sueno.
3. **Ask which spa areas are free.** At most of these resorts, saunas and pools are included and treatments cost extra.
4. **Check kids' club ages and seasons.** Teen clubs often run in summer only.
5. **Compare golf privileges with packages.** Some resorts offer guest green-fee rates; others sell nights and rounds together.
6. **Confirm the beach and outdoor-pool season** if you travel between November and April.

## FAQ

### Can a non-golfer ride in a golf buggy in Belek?

Sometimes. Cullinan Links sells a non-golfer or spectator supplement (€30–€40 in the 2026–27 season) and makes buggy sharing compulsory. The other clubs we checked publish no rule, so ask when you book.

### Which Belek golf resort is best for a non-golfer?

It depends on what the non-golfer wants. For the biggest spa, Cullinan's covers 8,000 m². For walking, Regnum Carya has a 4.6 km trail around the course. For trying golf, Gloria runs free taster lessons for its guests in summer.

### Can my partner try golf for free in Belek?

Gloria offers its hotel guests free one-hour taster lessons from 15 June to 31 August on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays at 10:00, for up to 40 people. Other academies charge; Sueno lists €80 for a 60-minute lesson.

### Is Belek good for non-golfers in winter?

Yes, if you pick the right resort. Choose one with a large indoor spa and heated pools, because outdoor pools and beach services wind down after October at some hotels.
