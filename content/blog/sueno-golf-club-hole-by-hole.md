This Sueno Golf Club hole-by-hole guide covers all 36 holes of Belek's two Sueno courses: the par-72 **Pines** and the shorter par-69 **Dunes**. We took every par, length and stroke index from the club's own 2025 scorecard and fact sheets, read on 26 November 2026. The club has not yet published any 2026 or 2027 documents, so treat the practical details as a starting point and check them when you book.

For photos and booking notes, see our [Sueno Golf Club profile](/golf/sueno-golf-club/). The other guides in this series cover [Carya](/blog/carya-golf-club-hole-by-hole/), [Cullinan Links](/blog/cullinan-links-hole-by-hole/), [Gloria's Old Course](/blog/gloria-old-course-hole-by-hole/) and [Montgomerie Maxx Royal](/blog/montgomerie-maxx-royal-hole-by-hole/).

## The courses at a glance

The fact sheets credit International Design Group, with Andrew Craven as head designer, and give **October 2007** as the opening date. The two courses share the same ground around the Sueno hotels in Kadriye, east of Belek:

- **Pines:** par 72, 6,413 metres, on the eastern side of the complex. The club calls it its championship course.
- **Dunes:** par 69, 5,643 metres, on the south-west side of the hotel.
- **Water:** the fact sheets say 19 artificial lakes cross the courses.
- **Bunkers:** the course page lists 71 fairway, 48 greenside and 9 waste bunkers, a total of 128 for each course. One fact sheet instead says 128 bunkers on both courses plus 9 waste bunkers, so the totals don't quite agree.
- **Grass:** Bermuda Tifdwarf, overseeded with perennial ryegrass and Poa trivialis. Greens average about 700 m².
- **Tees:** White (pros and single-figure handicaps), Yellow (18 and below), Blue (27 and below) and Red (over 27).

The scorecard prints **one length per hole** and does not say which tee it is measured from. The totals below are the club's, and our own sums of the hole figures agree.

### Reading the Pines card

The club publishes no hole descriptions on its course page, so these notes come from the numbers alone.

- **A gentle start, a hard finish to the front nine.** The 541 m 1st is a par 5 rated only 15th hardest. The 9th, a 391 m par 4, is stroke index 1.
- **Long par 3s.** The 7th (195 m) is stroke index 3, harder than most of the par 4s. The 13th (191 m) and 16th (181 m) are also long; only the 3rd, at 157 m, is a short iron for most players.
- **Four par 5s**, at the 1st, 8th, 15th and 17th. The 15th (499 m) and 17th (485 m) come back to back with the 181 m 16th between them, so the round can swing late.
- **The 18th is stroke index 2**, a 378 m par 4. Save something for it.

### Reading the Dunes card

- **Only one par 5**, the 533 m 13th. The rest is thirteen par 4s and four par 3s.
- **Short is not the same as easy.** The 232 m 17th is a par 3 and the hardest hole on the card. The 377 m 5th is stroke index 2.
- **Birdie chances** come early: the 122 m 4th is the shortest hole at Sueno, and the 270 m 3rd is a short par 4, though it is rated sixth hardest.
- **A good course for a second round in a day**, or for higher handicaps: 770 metres shorter than Pines.

## Course rating and slope

The club's 2025 rating sheets each print four blocks of course rating, par and slope, but the blocks carry **no tee or gender labels**. The only tee information is a footer listing White, Yellow, Blue and Red. Here they are in the order printed:

- **Pines (2025):** 74.5 / 144, 71.0 / 138, 75.2 / 135, 72.3 / 125
- **Dunes (2025):** 75.8 / 144, 72.8 / 138, 70.6 / 135, 67.5 / 125

The 2024 Pines sheet had the same ratings but higher slopes for three blocks (148, 138, 144 and 134). The 2025 Pines and Dunes slopes are an identical set, which may be a copying error. Use the starter's card or the federation's figures for official handicap play. ## Practical notes

- **Handicap limit:** 36 for men and women, per the course page. The fact sheets add that the club can lower it to 28 in busy periods.
- **Buggies:** 110 for hire; players under 16 may not drive. No hire prices are published.
- **Dress and etiquette:** golf attire and soft spikes. The fact sheets say mobile phones are prohibited and practice swings on the tee are not allowed.
- **Practice:** a 300 × 120 m range with 50 grass bays and 40 mats, and a 3,500 m² putting green. The chipping green is 1,700 m² in the fact sheets and 1,000 m² on the course page.
- **Lessons:** the September 2025 fact sheet lists €80 for a 60-minute private lesson, €320 for five, and €180 for a nine-hole playing lesson, with no validity date. Compare them in our [Belek golf lessons guide](/blog/golf-lessons-in-belek-prices/).
- **Food:** the Birdie Restaurant serves lunch and snacks (12:00–16:00 in one fact sheet, 12:00–17:00 in winter in another), and the Birdie Bar sits on a small lake looking towards the island greens.
- **Maintenance:** the 2025 overseeding calendar is colour-coded and its closed days cannot be read as text. No 2026–27 calendar is online; see our [Belek maintenance guide](/blog/belek-golf-course-maintenance-2027/).
- **Getting there:** the club gives 30 km (about 30 minutes) from Antalya Airport and 5 km from Belek. The [Sueno Hotels Golf Belek](/hotels/sueno-hotels-golf-belek/) sits on the course.

No green fees appear in any club document we could open, so ask for the current rate when you book a tee time. For context, our [winter green fees guide](/blog/belek-green-fees-winter-2026-27/) compares other clubs' published rates.

## FAQ

### Which Sueno course is harder, Pines or Dunes?

Pines is longer (6,413 m, par 72) and the club calls it its championship course. Dunes is 5,643 m and par 69, but its long par 3s, especially the 232 m 17th, keep it from being a pushover.

### What is the hardest hole at Sueno Golf Club?

On Pines, the 391 m par-4 9th is stroke index 1. On Dunes, the English 2025 card gives stroke index 1 to the 232 m par-3 17th, though an older fact sheet gives it to the 8th.

### What handicap do I need to play Sueno?

The course page sets a limit of 36 for men and women on both courses. The club says it may lower it to 28 in busy periods, so carry your handicap certificate.

### Is Sueno near Antalya Airport?

Yes. The club gives 30 km, about 30 minutes by car. For transfer options, see our [airport-to-Belek golf guide](/blog/antalya-airport-to-belek-golf-courses/).
