Many Antalya beach resorts close between November and March, and those that stay open do not all offer the same thing. This guide covers three Kemer and Belek hotels whose own websites publish a winter programme or say they operate all year. For each one we give what is open, what is not, and the points to confirm before you book. It is not a ranking, and it is not a complete list of winter-open resorts.

A word on method: we read each hotel's own pages and factsheets on 11–12 November 2026. We did not stay at any of them, we found no live availability, and rates change, so treat the figures as published claims.

## What "open in winter" usually means

Outdoor pools and beach services run on fixed dates, and most hotels close them from late autumn. What stays on is the indoor pool, the spa, the restaurants and the activity programme, often in a reduced form. If you want to swim outside, read [can you swim in Antalya in winter?](/blog/can-you-swim-in-antalya-in-winter-weather-sea-temperature-guide/) and [Antalya in winter](/blog/antalya-in-winter/) first. For a wider list of hotels with warm water, see [hotels with heated pools in winter](/blog/antalya-hotels-with-heated-pools-winter/).

## Fame Residence Kemer & Spa: open 1 November to 31 March

The hotel's winter factsheet, headed "Fame Style All Inclusive" for 2026–2027, runs from **1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027**. It is a 246-room beach hotel in the centre of Kemer, listed at 0 km from the beach, 1 km from the bus station and 50 km from Antalya Airport.

- **Pools:** a 195 m² indoor pool is the winter option; the 270 m² outdoor pool and outdoor activities close in winter, according to the factsheet.
- **Spa:** the hammam, sauna, indoor pool and fitness centre are free under the winter concept. Massages and scrubs cost extra.
- **Food:** winter meals are buffets: breakfast 07:00–10:00, lunch 12:30–14:00 and dinner 18:30–20:30.
- **Children:** a Mini Club takes ages 4 to 12.
- **Watch out for:** the à la carte restaurant is listed both as included and as a €20-per-person supplement, so confirm it.

Full profile: [Fame Residence Kemer & Spa](/hotels/fame-residence-kemer-spa/). For the town, see our [Kemer guide](/destinations/kemer/) and the [Kemer to Olympos coast road](/blog/kemer-to-olympos-coast-road/).

## Ela Excellence Resort Belek: open all year

Ela's winter factsheet says the resort opened in 2007, was fully renovated in 2022 and opens all year. Its 583 rooms are on 110,000 m² of grounds at İskele, Belek, 35 km from the airport. The website lists a Christmas package for 21–28 December 2026.

- **Pools:** four heated pools, including a 865 m² semi-Olympic outdoor pool and a 216 m² indoor pool. The hotel says heated pools stay open through winter, but opening days depend on weather and occupancy.
- **Spa:** the 4,500 m² Elazen Spa has 14 massage rooms and Turkish baths, open 09:00–20:00. Saunas, steam room, heated pools and gym are included; treatments cost extra.
- **Children:** Everland Kids World covers 5,000 m².
- **Watch out for:** suite sizes and kids' club age bands differ between the website and the factsheet, and which à la carte restaurants cost extra changes by season.

Full profile: [Ela Excellence Resort Belek](/hotels/ela-excellence-resort-belek/). For the area, see our [Serik and Belek guide](/destinations/serik/).

## Bellis Deluxe Hotel, Kadriye: heated aquaparks "in all four seasons"

Bellis is a family resort on 135,000 m² in the Kadriye part of Belek. Its site says seven pools include two heated aquaparks, one for adults and one for children, that work in all four seasons. The spa covers 1,000 m² with a hammam, sauna, steam room and indoor pool, and the grounds include a private zoo and horse riding.

- **Watch out for:** the site publishes no season dates, no airport distance, no room count and no kids' club ages, and its villa sizes conflict. Ask all of these before you book.

Full profile: [Bellis Deluxe Hotel](/hotels/bellis-deluxe-hotel-belek/).

## Why golfers care

Belek's golf season runs September to May, according to a hotel-managers' association official quoted by Hürriyet Daily News, and he said golf lets many hotels stay open longer than the beach season alone would. That is why Belek has more winter-open resorts than most of the coast. Read [Belek golf season: when to play](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/) and [winter golf in Antalya](/blog/winter-golf-in-antalya/) if you are planning a golf week, and [Belek hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek) for the wider list.

## Questions to ask any winter hotel

1. Which pools are heated, and are they open on my dates?
2. Is the beach, the spa and the à la carte restaurant included or extra?
3. Is the kids' club open in winter, and for which ages?
4. Is there an airport transfer, and what does it cost? See [Antalya airport transfers](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/).
5. What does the room have for heating? Winter evenings can be cold; see [is December too cold in Antalya?](/blog/is-december-too-cold-in-antalya-weather-rain-sea-conditions/).

## FAQ

### Which Kemer hotel is open in winter?

Fame Residence Kemer & Spa publishes a winter all-inclusive programme from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027.

### Which Belek resorts stay open all year?

Ela Excellence says it opens all year, and Bellis says its heated aquaparks work in all four seasons. Confirm dates with each hotel.

### Can you swim outdoors in these hotels in winter?

Fame closes its outdoor pools in winter. Ela and Bellis publish heated outdoor pools, but opening depends on weather and occupancy.
