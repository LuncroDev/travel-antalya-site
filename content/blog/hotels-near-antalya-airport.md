Hotels near Antalya Airport save you a dawn taxi ride before an early flight, or a late drive after a midnight landing. The airport (AYT) is only 13 km from the city centre, according to the airport authority DHMİ, so "near the airport" here covers the Aksu and Kepez roadside hotels, the beach hotels of Lara and a few city-centre options. We read each hotel's own website on 25 November 2026 and list only what it publishes: the distance or drive time to the airport, shuttles, check-out times and the details that matter for one night.

Two warnings first. Several hotels give two different distances on their own pages, and we show both. And none of the hotels below publishes an early "breakfast box" for dawn departures, so ask at check-in.

## The closest: right by the airport

### IC Hotels Airport

The only hotel here that publishes a distance under a kilometre. Its fact sheet says it is "only 800 meters away from the airport" and ten minutes from the city centre, at Güzelyurt Mevkii, Havaalanı Yolu, Çalkaya, Aksu. It lists a **free airport transfer**, a free city-centre shuttle at set hours, free parking, indoor and outdoor pools, a hamam, sauna and gym, and says it is open all year.

- **Rooms:** 198, from 32 m² Standard rooms to 50 m² Senior Suites, with an accessible room type.
- **Meals:** breakfast 07:00–10:30, lunch 12:00–14:00, dinner 19:00–22:00; the à la carte restaurant runs 09:00–24:00 and the bar 08:00–24:00. Room service is 24 hours, at extra cost.
- **Times:** check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00; late check-out is extra and on request.

The catch: the only fact sheet we could open is headed summer 2022, and the hotel's main page would not load. Confirm the free transfer before you rely on it.

### Hampton by Hilton Antalya Airport

On Serik Caddesi in Altınova Sinan, Kepez, this 150-room hotel suits a no-frills night. Breakfast is a free hot buffet (under-18s eat free), but no hours are published. Parking is free, covered and uncovered. There is a gym but no pool, and **no airport shuttle**. Pets are allowed for a TRY 2,000 fee, up to 30 kg. Check-in is 14:00, check-out 12:00.

Hilton's own pages can't agree on the distance: 5 km under "Airports", 0 km under "What's nearby", and 1.61 miles (about 2.6 km) on Hilton's airport search page. Whichever is right, it is a short taxi ride.

## Lara: beach hotels 5–17 km away

Lara is the beach district closest to the terminals, so it works if you want a last swim or a resort night before flying. Browse all [Lara hotels](/hotels/?area=Lara%20%26%20Kundu).

### Four Points Flex by Sheraton Antalya Lara

A 52-room city hotel on Bülent Ecevit Bulvarı, Çağlayan, and the best fit for a very early flight: breakfast starts at **06:30** (until 10:00, at extra cost). The front desk is staffed 24 hours. Guests must be 18 or over. There is no parking, no shuttle and no pets; the hotel offers a taxi to the airport for €20 on request. Check-in is 15:00, check-out 11:00. The page says "about 5 km" to the airport in one place and "7.0 km" in another.

### Holiday Inn Antalya – Lara

At Güzeloba, 10.6 km from the airport by IHG's figure. Free parking has 15 outdoor spaces and two electric-car spaces (charging extra). The third-floor outdoor pool is closed from 1 November 2026 to 30 April 2027. Guests must be 18 or over; check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00. No shuttle.

### Wyndham Garden Lara and Titanic Deluxe Lara

**Wyndham Garden Lara**, in Kemerağzı, says it is 12 km from the airport. It is all-inclusive, with a 24-hour front desk, free parking, breakfast 07:00–10:00 plus a late breakfast until 11:00, and 24-hour room service at extra cost.

**[Titanic Deluxe Lara](/hotels/titanic-deluxe-lara/)** says it is "just 12 km" from the airport on its main page and "only 10 minutes away" on its contact page. It is all-inclusive, with heated indoor pools and an outdoor pool heated in winter, but publishes no check-in times or transfers.

### Fame Residence Lara & Spa and Liberty Lara

**[Fame Residence Lara & Spa](/hotels/fame-residence-lara-spa/)** gives 14 km to the airport and 16 km to the city. It has 559 rooms and a 205 m² indoor pool; the outdoor pools close in winter. All-inclusive runs from 14:00 on arrival to 12:00 on departure.

**[Liberty Lara](/hotels/liberty-lara/)** puts the airport "around 17 minutes" away in its FAQ (its COP31 page says about 17 km and 20 minutes) and estimates a taxi at 490–600 TL. Airport transfers are available for a fee. Reception is open 24/7; early check-in and late check-out are paid extras.

- **[Crowne Plaza Antalya](/hotels/crowne-plaza-antalya/):** in Konyaaltı, 23 km by its description and about 21 km by its FAQ. A city hotel, not an airport one; weekend breakfast runs 07:00–11:00.

## Getting from your hotel to the right terminal

Antalya Airport has **International Terminal 1, International Terminal 2, the Domestic Terminal and a CIP terminal**. The airport operator says terminals are assigned by airline, not by destination, so check your booking.

- **Between terminals:** T1 and the Domestic Terminal are connected and you can walk between them. T2 is about 2.5 km away. The operator's transport page describes a free shuttle every 20 minutes between Domestic/T1 and T2, stopping in front of Departures; its buses page says a public bus or taxi links them instead.
- **By car:** the operator says about 20 minutes from the city centre.
- **Taxis:** ranks are outside every arrivals exit, with a fare board beside the rank.
- **Tram:** Antalya Ulaşım's AntRay line runs between the airport, the city centre, the intercity bus station and Fatih; the AntalyaKart FAQ names it the "Fatih – Havalimanı – Expo" line. A full fare is 42.00 TL with an AntalyaKart, or 50.00 TL by contactless bank card (no transfers or discounts), and a card costs 100.00 TL. A temporary "Havalimanı – COP31" ring line ran until 25 November 2026. Our [AntalyaKart guide](/blog/antalyakart-guide/) explains the card.
- **Buses:** city buses run to the centre and the bus station; Antalya Ulaşım publishes times in the AntalyaKart app or on 0242 606 07 07, but no official page we opened gives line numbers.
- **Parking:** the airport's example tariff is 140 TL for an hour, and the first 15 minutes are free only in the Domestic open car park.

## Tips for an early flight

- **Book a hotel with a 24-hour desk.** Four Points Flex, Wyndham Garden Lara and Liberty Lara say theirs is; most others don't say.
- **Ask about breakfast before 07:00.** Only Four Points Flex publishes a 06:30 start.
- **Confirm the shuttle in writing.** IC Hotels Airport is the only hotel here that lists a free airport transfer, and that is on a 2022 fact sheet.
- **Know your terminal.** T2 is a separate building about 2.5 km from T1 and Domestic, so tell your driver which one.
- **Check when all-inclusive ends.** At Fame Residence Lara it stops at 12:00 on departure day, so plan lunch for a late flight, and ask other resorts the same.

## FAQ

### Is there a hotel inside Antalya Airport?

The airport operator lists VIP and CIP lounges but no hotel inside the terminals. The nearest hotel publishing a distance is IC Hotels Airport, which says it is 800 m away.

### Which hotel is closest to Antalya Airport?

By the hotels' own figures, IC Hotels Airport (800 m), then Hampton by Hilton Antalya Airport, whose Hilton pages give 0 km, 5 km or 1.61 miles.

### How far is Antalya Airport from the city centre?

DHMİ, the airport authority, gives 13 km, and the airport operator says about 20 minutes by car.

### Can I take the tram from the airport to my hotel?

The AntRay tram links the airport with the city centre and the intercity bus station. It suits city-centre hotels; for Lara and the Aksu roadside hotels, a taxi or the hotel's own transfer is simpler.
