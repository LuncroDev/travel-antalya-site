Antalya in February and March 2027 is a quieter, cheaper city than in summer, but it is far from empty. The two municipal stages in Muratpaşa and Konyaaltı have a full run of Turkish plays and concerts, professional cycling comes to the coast roads, and Belek's golf courses are in their busiest weeks. This guide lists what is confirmed, with the year printed on the organiser's or ticket seller's page, as of 28 November 2026. Programmes grow over the winter, so check back before you book.

## Theatre and concerts at Türkan Şoray

**Türkan Şoray Kültür Merkezi** in Yeşilbahçe, Muratpaşa, is the busiest stage of the late winter. Biletix's venue page, read on 28 November 2026, lists every date with the year, and all were on sale:

- **Rammstein Symphonic Experience**, Wednesday 3 February, 20:00, from ₺2,190 — the highest starting price on this list.
- **Kocamın Karısı** ("My Husband's Wife"), Thursday 25 February, 20:00. A comedy about a ship's captain who sails between the two sides of the Aegean and keeps a wife on each, until Leda and Leyla find out about each other. Serkan Budak adapts and directs a story by Leda Sotirios, with Selda Özbek Orpak, Ebru Karanfilci and Hakkı Ergök. Biletinial sold it from ₺1,072 and Biletix from ₺1,430.
- **Olağanüstü Bir Gece**, Friday 26 February, 20:30, from ₺750 on Biletix.
- **[Cambazın Cenazesi](/events/cambazin-cenazesi-2027-03-18-muratpasa/)**, Thursday 18 March, 20:00: Firuze Engin's comedy-drama about a tightrope walker's funeral, with Ünal Yeter and Bülent Seyran.
- **Soytarılar**, Thursday 25 March, 20:00, from ₺780 on Biletix.
- **Boşanma Teklifi** ("A Divorce Proposal"), Wednesday 31 March, 20:30. Barbaros Uzunöner wrote it and plays opposite Merve Sevi: a man who backs the government and a woman who backs the opposition run their marriage like a quarrelsome coalition, until one asks the other for a divorce. It runs 75 minutes and is for ages 13 and up. Biletinial started at ₺900 and Biletix at ₺750.

Prices on the two sites differ, sometimes by several hundred lira, because each runs its own discounts. Compare both before you pay.

## Theatre in Konyaaltı

The **Nazım Hikmet** congress and culture centre in Konyaaltı shows the year only on its 2027 dates, which makes them easy to trust. Biletinial's venue page lists:

- **Bir Evlilik Müzikali**, Saturday 13 February (the venue page gives the date but no details yet).
- **Fehim Paşa Konağı**, Sunday 28 February, from ₺660.
- **Süper Patiler**, Saturday 27 March, from ₺400 — a children's show, handy for a family trip.

A few days later the same stage has [Lady Macbeth](/events/lady-macbeth-2027-04-03-konyaalti/) on 3 April. If you are in town at the end of January, Sergen Deveci's [Ketenpere / Olan](/events/sergen-deveci-ketenpere-olan-2027-01-29-konyaalti/) stand-up plays here on 29 January.

Almost everything on these stages is in Turkish. Our guide to [theatre and comedy for non-Turkish speakers](/blog/antalya-theatre-comedy-non-turkish-speakers/) explains what works without the language: concerts and music shows such as Rock Sympho and Kirill Richter are the safest bets.

## Cycling: the Tour of Antalya and the Grand Prix races

Late winter is road-racing season on the Antalya coast. Two reports on the 2027 calendar — a Türkiye Today article of 4 October 2026 and a CyclingUpToDate round-up of the UCI calendar from 30 September 2026 — agree on these dates:

- **Grand Prix Antalya**, 13 February
- **Grand Prix Alaiye**, 20 February, and **Grand Prix Alanya**, 21 February, both in Alanya
- **Grand Prix Pedalia**, 27 February
- **Tour of Antalya**, **4–7 March 2027**

The two reports disagree about the end of March: Türkiye Today lists a Grand Prix Antalya on 27 March and the Grand Prix Apollon Temple on 3 April, while CyclingUpToDate puts Apollon Temple on 27 March and adds a Grand Prix Syedra Ancient City on 3 April. Check the organiser's site nearer the time.

For scale: the 2026 Tour of Antalya ran 12–15 March over four stages, with 29 teams and 174 riders from 14 countries, according to Fanatik. Stage routes for 2027 have not been published. Watching from the roadside costs nothing; on race days, check local traffic notices if you have an airport transfer booked.

## Golf in February and March

These are peak weeks in Belek: the weather is mild and the courses are green, so tee times and golf hotels fill early. Book well ahead, and read our [Belek golf season](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/) guide before choosing dates.

Two things to plan around:

- **Maintenance.** Some clubs aerate and overseed in spring; our [Belek golf course maintenance 2027](/blog/belek-golf-course-maintenance-2027/) guide lists the dates clubs have published.

## Planning tips

- **Buy tickets from the official sellers.** Biletix and Biletinial sell the municipal halls, and age limits are checked at the door, so bring ID.
- **Arrive early.** Türkan Şoray and Nazım Hikmet are both outside the old town, so leave time for a taxi in evening traffic.
- **Watch for duplicates.** Some tours appear on both sites with different prices and slightly different start times. Trust the time printed on your ticket.
- **Combine with day trips.** Quiet ruins and museums are at their best now; try [winter day trips from Antalya](/blog/winter-day-trips-from-antalya/).

## FAQ

### Is Antalya worth visiting in February or March?

Yes, if you want city sights, ruins, golf and theatre rather than the beach. Prices are lower and sites are quiet. Bring a jacket for evenings.

### When is the Tour of Antalya 2027?

Two reports on the 2027 UCI calendar give 4–7 March 2027. The organiser had not published stages when we checked on 28 November 2026.

### Are there English-language shows?

Very few. The plays listed here are in Turkish. Concerts are the best choice if you don't speak the language.

### What's on in April?

[Lady Macbeth](/events/lady-macbeth-2027-04-03-konyaalti/) on 3 April, the [Runtalya Marathon](/events/runtalya-marathon/) on 4 April and [Auto World Expo](/events/auto-world-expo-antalya-2027/) from 8 to 11 April are already on our calendar.
