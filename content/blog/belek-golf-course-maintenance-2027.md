Belek golf course maintenance follows a yearly rhythm: greens are cored and sanded in summer, and in September and October the courses are overseeded so they stay green through the winter. While that happens, some courses close completely, others ban buggies, and the rest play on with patchy surfaces. If you're booking a golf week from June to October, these dates matter more than the green fee. On 19 November 2026 we opened every Belek club's own pages and noted what each publishes for 2027, or the latest year it gives. Only three clubs have 2027 dates so far.

## Why Belek courses close in autumn

Belek's courses are built on warm-season grass. Gloria lists Bermuda on the greens and fairways of all three of its courses, and Kaya Palazzo gives Bermuda 419 on its fairways and tees and TifDwarf Bermuda on its greens. Bermuda thrives in the heat but loses colour in winter, so clubs sow a winter grass into it each autumn. That's overseeding. While the new grass takes, the course needs rest: no play at all at some clubs, no buggies on the fairways at others. The summer work, hollow-coring or aeration, opens up the greens and is usually less disruptive, but the putting surfaces are slower and bumpier for a while afterwards.

No club we checked names the winter grass it sows, says when the overseeded turf is at its best, or offers lower green fees during maintenance.

## National Golf Club

National posted its 2027 dates on 30 September 2026, and its About page has been updated to match:

- **Hollow-core aeration:** 20 June to 31 August 2027. National calls it scheduled maintenance and doesn't say the course closes.
- **Overseeding:** 10 to 24 September 2027 inclusive, with the course "temporarily closed".
- **Buggies on paths only:** 25 September to 4 October 2027. In 2026 the same rule ran from 25 September to 5 October.

One oddity: National's 2026–27 green-fee table charges its top rate (€170 rack, €145 for Regnum guests) from 11 to 30 September 2027, even though the course is closed until the 24th, and the cheaper 1–10 September band includes the first closed day. National's lowest rate, €85 rack, applies from 1 July to 31 August 2027, during the aeration period. For what a round costs now, see [Belek green fees for winter 2026–27](/blog/belek-green-fees-winter-2026-27/).

## Carya Golf Club

Carya is run by Regnum, like National, and its page on regnumhotels.com lists:

- **Hollow-core aeration:** 1–10 July and 1–10 August 2027. Closure isn't mentioned.
- **Overseeding:** 15 to 30 September 2027, course temporarily closed.
- **Buggies on designated paths only:** 1 to 10 October 2027.

National and Carya overseed at different times, so in 2027 one of the two Regnum courses is open on every day of September except the 15th to the 24th, when both are shut.

## Sueno Golf Club

Sueno's documents page links an "OverSeeding 2027" calendar, uploaded in February 2026, for both the Pines and the Dunes courses. Its key has three states: course completely closed, open but no buggies, and open under light maintenance. The days are marked only by colour, so download the PDF and read it yourself; we couldn't extract the dates as text. Sueno's home page still links only the 2025 and 2026 calendars.

Separately, Sueno says its driving range and putting and chipping green are under maintenance from 19 September to 12 October and open with limited capacity. That line has no year and appears in the 2026 calendar too, so it may be a standing rule.

## Gloria Golf Club

Gloria's three course pages still show 2026 dates only. They are worth knowing because they show how Gloria staggers the work so that, in 2026, two of its three courses were open on all but three days (4–6 October, when the New and Verde courses overlapped):

- **Old Course:** summer periods 9–22 June and 21 July–3 August; overseeding 10–21 September, reopening on the 22nd.
- **New Course:** summer periods 23 June–6 July and 4–17 August; overseeding 22 September–6 October, reopening on 7 October.
- **Verde Course:** summer periods 7–20 July and 18–31 August; overseeding 4–14 October, reopening on the 15th.

After each overseeding reopening, Gloria bans buggies for seven days. The pages don't say what the summer work is, or use the word "closed", but each period has an "opening" date. Wait for Gloria's 2027 dates before booking around them.

For winter, Gloria is promoting a "Golf in Winter 2026–2027" offer at Gloria Verde Resort, from 20 November 2026 to 1 February 2027, with complimentary green fees on stays of at least 14 nights.

## ROBINSON Nobilis

ROBINSON says Nobilis is overseeded every year and stays playable, though course quality and play may be restricted. Its 2026 dates were 13 September to 6 October, with buggies banned from 20 September to 6 October. A green-fee note on the same page gives the same days with the year 2025 and says tee times start at 11:30 during overseeding. No 2027 dates are published yet.

## Cornelia Golf Club

Cornelia's course information page says it overseeds nine holes at a time, so 18 holes stay open, and that buggies are allowed during overseeding. The page's dates are for 2024, and it doesn't say which of the King, Queen and Prince nines are closed when. Ask the club for the 2027 plan.

## How to plan a golf week around maintenance

- **Safest months:** the winter and spring months have no published maintenance at any club. Our [Belek golf season guide](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/) explains the trade-offs of each month.
- **Mid-September:** in 2027 both Regnum courses are closed from 15 to 24 September. If you're coming then, check Sueno's calendar and ask the other clubs.
- **Late September and early October:** expect path-only buggy rules at National, Carya, Gloria and Nobilis. If you can't walk 18 holes, read our [golf buggy hire guide](/blog/golf-buggy-hire-in-belek/) and ask before booking.
- **Summer:** aeration at National and Carya overlaps the cheapest green fees. Fast greens aren't guaranteed.
- **Get it in writing:** green fees aren't reduced during maintenance at any club we checked, so ask your hotel or tour operator to confirm which courses will be open on your tee dates.

## FAQ

### When are Belek golf courses closed for overseeding in 2027?

National is closed from 10 to 24 September 2027 and Carya from 15 to 30 September 2027. Sueno's 2027 calendar is published as a colour-coded PDF. The other clubs haven't published 2027 dates yet.

### Can I still play golf in Belek in September?

Yes, but choose carefully. Gloria's staggered schedule kept at least one of its three courses open every day in September and October 2026, and two on all but three days; Nobilis stays open during overseeding, and Cornelia overseeds nine holes at a time.

### Are green fees cheaper during maintenance?

No club we checked offers a maintenance discount. National's fee table even charges its peak rate for part of its closed period.

### Which grass do Belek courses use?

Gloria lists Bermuda on all three courses, and Kaya Palazzo lists Bermuda 419 on fairways and tees and TifDwarf Bermuda on greens. The other clubs don't say.
