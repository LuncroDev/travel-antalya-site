Antalya city centre hotels come into their own between November and March. Beach service and outdoor pools wind down for the season, but the old town is there to explore all winter. A city hotel puts you a walk or a short ride from Kaleiçi, and the better ones add an indoor pool or a hammam for cold evenings. On 23 November 2026 we opened each hotel's own website and noted what it publishes about pools, parking, distances and winter. We have not stayed at any of them. Where a hotel prints no figure, we say so.

For the season itself, read [Antalya in winter](/blog/antalya-in-winter/). Most of these hotels are in [Muratpaşa](/destinations/muratpasa/), the old town's district.

## The big city hotels

These five sit outside the old walls. You get pools, spas and car parks, and trade them for a taxi, bus or longer walk into Kaleiçi. Browse every [Antalya city hotel](/hotels/?area=Antalya%20city) for more.

### DoubleTree by Hilton Antalya City Centre

The address is Adnan Menderes Bulvarı No. 61 in Etiler, Muratpaşa. Hilton puts the city centre at 0 km, the MarkAntalya shopping centre at 1 km and the airport at 15 km. It calls Kaleiçi "very close" but gives no distance.

Hilton lists an indoor pool and an outdoor pool, captioned in the gallery as a rooftop pool, but doesn't say either is heated or give hours. Photo captions show a spa with a sauna, and there is a fitness centre.

- **Parking:** self-parking and valet are free, covered and secured. No EV charging, no airport shuttle.
- **Evenings:** the lobby Ocean Bar is open 24 hours.
- **Breakfast:** the buffet costs extra, but the offers page lists a "Breakfast Included" rate.
- **Times:** check-in from 14:00, check-out by 12:00. No pets.

### Rixos Downtown Antalya

Accor's page calls this "an urban resort in the centre of Antalya", on Sakıp Sabancı Bulvarı in the Falez area of Muratpaşa. Guests take a lift down to the hotel's area at Konyaaltı Beach Park. Rixos says it is 1 km from the city centre and 18 km from the airport, a 20–25 minute drive. The distance to Kaleiçi depends on the page: Accor gives 2 km, a 30-minute walk or 10-minute drive, while a Rixos FAQ gives 3 km.

The catch for winter: Rixos lists **two outdoor pools and no indoor pool**, a 701 m² adults' pool (16+) and a 153 m² children's pool. Neither is described as heated. Your warm option is the Anjana Wellness & Spa, with a sauna and free Turkish bath access; treatments cost extra.

- **Open all year:** Rixos says so, though some restaurants, bars and beach services are summer-only.
- **Dinner:** Accor says the à la carte dinner moves to the 24-hour lounge from November to April, 19:00–23:00.
- **The Land of Legends:** entrance and the shuttle are included. Rixos puts the park 45 km away, 45–50 minutes on the shuttle.
- **Children:** the Rixy Kids Club takes ages 4–12, 10:00–23:00.
- **Parking:** both FAQs promise free valet. One gives 276 open spaces and two EV chargers; the other gives open-air parking for 188 cars.

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out by 11:00. One Rixos FAQ refers to an all-inclusive concept, while the Accor page states no board basis, so confirm what your rate includes.

### Akra Antalya

Akra Antalya is at Lara Caddesi No. 24 in Şirinyalı. It has 471 rooms and has been open since 2014; it was Akra Barut until it left Barut Hotels in 2023. Akra says it is open year-round.

Akra lists six pools, including a semi-Olympic indoor pool on a "special heated system" linked to the outdoor area, and says its pools are heated to 28°C in winter. The same page says the outdoor pools, the cliff-side platform beach (reached by a lift) and their bars run from April to November, so in mid-winter plan to swim indoors. The Turkish bath and sauna are open 06:00–22:00.

- **Getting there:** about 20–25 minutes by car from the airport, says Akra. Its travel guide calls Hadrian's Gate "moments away" but prints no distance.
- **Parking:** not stated on the pages we could open.
- **Board:** depends on the rate you book.
- **Offers:** the offers list includes "New Year 2027 at Akra Antalya", with no dates or price shown.

### Akra V

Akra V is the four-star sister across the road, at Metin Kasapoğlu Caddesi No. 281 in Yeşilbahçe, about 300 m from the sea. It has been under the Akra brand since 2023. Rates are room-only or bed and breakfast.

It has no pool or spa of its own on its pages, but Akra says Akra V guests can use "all the services offered by Akra Antalya". Ask whether that includes the heated indoor pool on your dates. The car park is free and open 24 hours, and a paid VIP transfer runs to the airport or the bus station. Akra V also has pet-friendly rooms for cats and dogs up to 11 kg.

### Crowne Plaza Antalya

Our [Konyaaltı beach hotels comparison](/blog/konyaalti-beach-hotels-compared-winter/) covers this one, so here are only the city-break points. It is on Akdeniz Bulvarı in Gürsu, Konyaaltı, with the beach in front. IHG says the old city is a 15-minute drive and that buses pass the hotel every five minutes.

The heated indoor pool is open 09:00 to midnight at no charge, and the Velvet Aura spa runs 10:00 to midnight with a Turkish bath and Finnish sauna. Parking is free for guests, with 100 outdoor spaces and EV charging for two cars. IHG gives the airport as 23 km in one place and 21 km in another, and lists no shuttle. The minimum check-in age is 18.

## Staying inside Kaleiçi

Sleeping inside the walls means you can walk to dinner, the harbour and our [Kaleiçi walking route](/blog/kaleici-walking-route/) without a taxi. The trade-off in winter: none of these three lists an indoor pool, and cars are harder. See all [Kaleiçi hotels](/hotels/?area=Kalei%C3%A7i), and our [Kaleiçi old town](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/) page for what to see.

### Alp Paşa Hotel

Alp Paşa has 155 rooms across 18th- and 19th-century Ottoman mansions, restored by Mehmet Alp in 1995. The hotel puts [Hadrian's Gate](/things-to-do/gate-of-hadrian/) 150 m away, Mermerli Beach a five-minute walk and the airport 15 km.

It is the only one of our three old-town picks to promise parking: free for guests, subject to availability. You leave your keys at reception, or the car may be towed. Its two outdoor pools open 10:00–18:00 under the pool rules, and it doesn't mention heating, an indoor pool or a spa. In winter, dinner changes from an open buffet to a set menu, served 19:00–21:00 from November to March.

### Casa Sur Antalya

Casa Sur has six rooms in a house that once belonged to the painter Muhittin Selamet. It is at Kandiller Sokak No. 3, and its terrace bar looks over Hadrian's Gate. The hotel says its garden's orange and mandarin trees fruit mainly from December to March.

Driving in is possible. Casa Sur's directions take you through the "Acar Taxi gate" to a garage door on Kandiller Sokak, but it gives no details on spaces or fees. Without a car, take the tram to İsmetpaşa and walk about six minutes through Hadrian's Gate. Its pages mention no pool and list spa only among the activities it arranges, with yoga and meditation. It gives no airport distance.

### Doğan Hotel

Doğan is a garden hotel at Mermerli Sokak No. 2, where breakfast is served outdoors. It has a seasonal outdoor pool and a children's pool, with no heating mentioned, so don't plan a winter swim. It puts the privately run Mermerli Beach a three-minute walk away and the free Konyaaltı public beach 3 km away. You can book room-only, bed and breakfast or half board. Parking isn't mentioned, so ask before you drive in.

## How to choose for a winter city break

- **You want a warm swim every day:** Akra Antalya (heated indoor pool, 28°C in winter) or Crowne Plaza (heated, open to midnight). Akra V guests may be able to use Akra's pool; ask first.
- **You want to walk everywhere:** stay inside Kaleiçi. Alp Paşa has pools and parking; Casa Sur, with six rooms, is the smallest.
- **You're driving:** DoubleTree, Rixos, Crowne Plaza and Akra V publish free parking. Inside the walls, only Alp Paşa promises it.
- **You're travelling with children:** Rixos Downtown has a kids' club and includes The Land of Legends, but its pools are outdoors and not described as heated.
- **You're bringing a dog or cat:** Akra V has pet-friendly rooms. DoubleTree and Rixos don't allow pets.

## Getting around without a car

Kaleiçi is best on foot. From the big hotels, a taxi is simplest and the tram is the cheap option: Casa Sur's directions use the İsmetpaşa stop by Hadrian's Gate, and our [AntalyaKart guide](/blog/antalyakart-guide/) explains fares. For a sunset stroll, see [Karaalioğlu Park](/things-to-do/karaalioglu-park/). On a wet day, try our [rainy-day museums guide](/blog/antalya-museums-rainy-day/); on a clear one, our [winter day trips](/blog/winter-day-trips-from-antalya/).

## What to check before you book

1. **Pool heating and hours on your dates.** Only Akra and Crowne Plaza say their indoor pools are heated.
2. **Parking and car access.** For Kaleiçi, ask for driving directions and whether a space is guaranteed.
3. **What the rate includes.** Breakfast costs extra at DoubleTree, Akra's board depends on the rate, and Rixos's sources describe its concept differently.
4. **Distances.** Rixos and Crowne Plaza each publish two figures, so check the trip to Kaleiçi on a map.
5. **Winter changes.** Rixos and Alp Paşa both say some services differ between summer and winter.

## FAQ

### Which Antalya city centre hotels have a heated indoor pool?

Akra Antalya (heated system, 28°C in winter) and Crowne Plaza Antalya (heated, 09:00 to midnight). DoubleTree City Centre has an indoor pool but doesn't say it is heated. Rixos Downtown lists only outdoor pools. For resorts further out, see our [heated pools guide](/blog/antalya-hotels-with-heated-pools-winter/).

### Which hotel is closest to Kaleiçi?

The three inside the walls: Alp Paşa, Casa Sur and Doğan. Of the bigger hotels, Rixos Downtown gives 2 km or 3 km depending on the page. DoubleTree calls the old town "very close" without a figure.

### Do Antalya city hotels have free parking?

DoubleTree, Crowne Plaza, Akra V and Rixos Downtown say so. Alp Paşa offers free parking subject to availability. Akra Antalya and Doğan don't say on the pages we opened, and Casa Sur has a garage but publishes no details.

### Are these hotels open in winter?

Akra says Akra Antalya and Akra V are open all year, and Rixos says Rixos Downtown operates year-round. Hilton and IHG publish no seasonal closures for DoubleTree or Crowne Plaza. Alp Paşa publishes a November–March dinner menu.
