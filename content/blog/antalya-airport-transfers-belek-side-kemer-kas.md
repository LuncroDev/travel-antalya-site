Antalya Airport transfers are simple once you know three things: which terminal you land at, how far your resort is, and whether you want door-to-door or the cheapest option. Belek is close, Kemer is about an hour away, Side a little further, and Kaş is a long drive west. This guide sets out every option for the trip to Belek, Side, Kemer and Kaş: taxis, hotel transfers, car hire, the AntRay tram, city buses and minibuses. We use the airport operator's own pages, the Ministry of Culture and Tourism, Antalya's transport authorities and hotels' published distances, all checked on 11 November 2026.

For flights and the trip into Antalya city itself, see our guide on [how to get to Antalya](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/) and our [plan your trip](/plan-your-trip/) page.

## First, which terminal?

The airport operator, Fraport TAV Antalya, says that **all international flights have used Terminal 2 since 27 October 2025**. Domestic flights use the Domestic terminal, which is linked on foot to International Terminal 1. The two international terminals are about 2.5 km apart, around five minutes by car, and a free shuttle runs every 20 minutes between the Domestic/T1 area and T2, from stops in front of Departures. If someone is meeting you, tell them your terminal; it saves a 2.5 km mistake.

## Your Antalya Airport transfer options at a glance

| Option | Best for | What the official pages say |
|---|---|---|
| **Taxi** | Belek, Lara, the city, late arrivals | Ranks at the arrivals exits of all terminals; drivers wear airport ID |
| **Hotel or private transfer** | Families, Side, Kemer, Kaş | Arranged by the hotel or an operator in advance |
| **Car hire** | Kaş, touring the coast | Ten rental firms have desks at the airport |
| **AntRay tram and city buses** | The city, the bus station | Pay with AntalyaKart or a contactless bank card |
| **Bus station minibuses** | Side, Alanya, Kemer on a budget | Change at the Otogar (or the Kemer stop) |

## Taxi

Taxi ranks stand in front of the arrivals exits at every terminal, the airport says. Drivers wear airport ID tags, a fare board stands near the rank, and the taxi cooperative also takes bookings online. The airport also confirms a 24-hour taxi service at International Terminal 1. Official pages do not publish fares to the resorts, so agree the route and confirm the price at the rank before you set off. A taxi is the easiest choice for Belek and Lara, which are close to the airport.

## Hotel and private transfers

Many resorts arrange their own transfers, and they are worth asking about when you book the room. [Ela Excellence Resort Belek](/hotels/ela-excellence-resort-belek/), for example, sells a VIP transfer and makes it free for suite and villa guests on stays of five nights or more. In Kaş, [Suna Sun Hotel](/hotels/suna-sun-hotel-kas/) says it can arrange private transfers from both Antalya and Dalaman airports if you send your flight details. A pre-booked transfer makes the most sense for the longer runs to Kemer, Side and Kaş, and for families with car seats or lots of luggage.

## Car hire

The airport lists ten car rental companies with offices on site, including Avis, Budget, Europcar, Enterprise and Garenta; desk locations are shown on the terminal floor plans. A car pays off if you are heading for Kaş or want to tour the Lycian coast; our [Antalya to Kaş day trip](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) describes the road.

## Public transport: AntRay, buses and AntalyaKart

The airport operator's Turkish transport page says the **AntRay tram** runs on the **Havalimanı (Airport) – Meydan – Fatih** line, linking the airport, the city centre, the intercity bus station (Otogar) and Fatih. The Antalya Metropolitan Municipality describes this second-stage light-rail line, Meydan–Havaalanı–Expo, as 19.1 km long with 15 stations. We could not confirm from official pages where the tram stop sits in relation to each terminal, so ask at the information desk when you land.

**City buses** also run from the airport to the Otogar and the city centre. The airport says to check times in the AntalyaKart mobile app or on the transport information line, **0242 606 07 07**.

**How to pay.** The AntalyaKart FAQ gave these fares when we checked: 42 TL for a full-fare ride on an AntalyaKart, 46 TL for a single-use "Kullan At" ticket, and 50 TL with a contactless bank card, which gets no transfer discount. The FAQ adds that a transfer is free within an hour on buses, or within two hours between tram lines. The tram operator says children under seven ride free with a parent.

**Havaş.** The Havaş airport bus lists only one Antalya route: Antalya city centre to the airport, normally about 45 minutes, with departures timed to flights. It does not list routes to Belek, Side, Alanya or Kemer.

**The Otogar.** The bus station is 4 km north of the city centre, according to the airport operator, and the AntRay is the quickest way between the two. Minibuses from the Otogar serve routes including Aspendos, Side and Manavgat, and Alanya.

## Antalya Airport to Belek

Belek is the closest resort area. Ela Excellence Resort's factsheet puts the airport 35 km away, and GoTürkiye's golf page lists Sueno Golf Belek at "30 minutes to airport". A taxi or hotel transfer is the practical way to go: we found no official public bus route from the airport to Belek, and Havaş does not serve it. Browse [Belek hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek) or read our [Serik and Belek guide](/destinations/serik/). Golfers should check whether their package includes the transfer; our [Belek golf season guide](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/) covers the rest.

## Antalya Airport to Side

The ministry's Side page gives no distance, but the Barut group's 2025 brochure lists its Side hotels at 60 to 63 km from the airport. By public transport, take the AntRay or a city bus to the Otogar, then a minibus on the Side and Manavgat route. With luggage, a transfer is simpler. See [Side hotels](/hotels/?area=Side), our [Manavgat and Side district page](/destinations/manavgat/) and the [Side ancient city](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/) guide.

## Antalya Airport to Kemer

The ministry says Kemer is about **one hour from Antalya International Airport**. Official pages disagree on where Kemer minibuses leave from: the airport operator says they go from the Kemer bus stop west of the city centre, not the Otogar, while the ministry says they leave from the coach station about every ten minutes. Ask locally on the day. Browse [Kemer hotels](/hotels/?area=Kemer) and our [Kemer district guide](/destinations/kemer/).

## Antalya Airport to Kaş, and the Dalaman option

Kaş is the long one. The ministry gives **192 km** from Antalya Airport, while the Radisson Blu Kaş contact page gives 205 km and about three hours. Intercity buses between Kaş and Antalya take about four hours, the ministry says, and Kaş bus station is on the north side of town.

Dalaman Airport, over the border in Muğla, is often closer. Its own page puts Kaş **110 km** away, about 1 hour 45 minutes; the ministry says 160 km and the Radisson 148 km, so allow for the higher figures. From Dalaman, the airport lists shuttles only to Fethiye and Marmaris, not to Kaş, so plan a car or a transfer. See [Kaş hotels](/hotels/?area=Ka%C5%9F) and our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/).

## Heading to Alanya instead?

The ministry gives Alanya as 130 km from Antalya. Gazipaşa–Alanya Airport is a closer alternative for some flights: the state airports authority's aeronautical data put it 40 km east of Alanya, with buses, taxis and car hire available. Our [Alanya district guide](/destinations/alanya/) covers the town.

## During COP31 (November 2026)

GoTürkiye lists the UN climate conference COP31 in Antalya from 9 to 20 November 2026. The airport asked passengers to allow for traffic and to check the city's route notices for 9–13 November, Havaş warned that city journeys could take up to 120 minutes, and Antalya Ulaşım reassigned some trams during the conference. ## FAQ

### Which terminal do international flights use at Antalya Airport?

Terminal 2. The airport operator says all international flights have been operated from Terminal 2 since 27 October 2025. A free shuttle every 20 minutes links it with Terminal 1 and the Domestic terminal.

### Is there a tram from Antalya Airport to the city?

Yes. The airport operator says the AntRay tram serves the airport on the Havalimanı–Meydan–Fatih line, which also stops at the Otogar. Ask at the information desk where to board for your terminal.

### How far is Belek from Antalya Airport?

About 30–35 km. Ela Excellence Resort gives 35 km, and GoTürkiye lists Sueno Golf Belek as 30 minutes from the airport. Taxis and hotel transfers are the practical options.

### Is it better to fly into Dalaman for Kaş?

Often, yes. Dalaman Airport says Kaş is 110 km away, against 192 km from Antalya by the ministry's figure. There is no airport shuttle from Dalaman to Kaş, so book a transfer or hire a car.
