Tennis holidays in Antalya mostly mean Belek: the resort strip east of the city has the biggest court complexes, a year-round academy and resorts that keep courts open through the winter. Kemer and Side add two ROBINSON club resorts with a dozen courts each, but both close for winter. We read each resort's own sports pages on 25 November 2026 and list only what they publish: court counts, surfaces, floodlights, padel, what's free and what's charged, and the season.

A note before you compare: several resorts give different numbers on different pages. Where they do, we show both.

## Belek: the big tennis resorts

### Megasaray Club Belek and the Megasaray Tennis Academy

This is the largest published tennis set-up on the coast. The hotel's sports page counts **64 courts** "all year round": the hotel's own 10 artificial-grass and 11 red-clay courts, four padel courts and a pickleball court (26), plus the academy's 32 clay, four hard and two padel courts (38).

The Megasaray Tennis Academy (MTA) was founded in 2019 and says it works "throughout 12 months". It lists Davis Cup, WTA, ATP Challenger, ITF and Tennis Europe events among those it has hosted. Head coach Nika Kakulia leads junior and adult programmes, with weekend and weekly camps.

Prices on the academy's private-lessons page carry no date:

- **Private lesson:** €65 an hour on the hotel's carpet courts, €15 more on clay; ten lessons plus one free for €650.
- **Group lessons:** €90 for two players, €110 for three, €130 for four.
- **Padel:** a lesson with racket and balls €70; packages of 5+1 for €350 or 10+2 for €700.
- **Court hire without a coach:** clay or hard €15 an hour, academy padel €20, hotel padel €25; padel racket €5 per person per hour.

A second academy page prices packages differently (5+1 for €300, 10+2 for €600, cheaper for 45-minute lessons), and the academy's own pages give the total as 60 or 64 courts. Confirm the price list when you book. The hotel itself publishes no season dates.

### ROBINSON Nobilis: the best ROBINSON for winter

Nobilis is open **all year**, and it is the only one of the three ROBINSON clubs here that keeps tennis going through the winter. Its club page lists six quartz-sand courts (three floodlit), plus two grass courts at extra cost from April to November. From November to March an inflatable hall covers courts with an EPDM rubber surface. It is free to use and booked in the ROBINSON app.

Court use, HEAD racket hire and a weekly guest tournament are free. An outside tennis school gives adult and children's lessons, with prices "on request on site". Two padel courts cost extra. Golfers should read our [Belek golf holiday with a non-golfer](/blog/belek-golf-holiday-with-a-non-golfer/) guide too.

### Regnum Carya

Regnum's **winter 2026–27 fact sheet**, for 1 November to 31 March, lists two red-clay, four hard and one artificial-turf tennis court, four doubles and one singles padel court, and a pickleball court. Everything is marked as an extra charge, including court lighting and private lessons. The resort's own tennis page lists only "4 hard tennis courts", so the fact sheet is the fuller picture.

### TUI MAGIC LIFE Masmavi

Masmavi lists six quartz-sand courts, two floodlit (the same page also mentions "our seven tennis courts"). Its draw is coaching included in the price: group training five times a week at every level, with groups for children and teens. TUI's tennis page prices private lessons at €35 for one player, €40 for two and €45 for three or four, and says group lessons are included for ages 16 and up. There are two padel courts.

The season matters here. The resort ran from 1 May to 8 November in summer 2026, then 9–29 November, and reopens on 27 February 2027, so it is closed from about 30 November to 26 February.

### Kempinski The Dome and Calista

[Kempinski The Dome](/hotels/kempinski-hotel-the-dome-belek/) has four floodlit courts that it lists for padel or tennis, open to all guests from 07:00 to 19:00, with paddles and balls to rent and private coaching at extra cost. It doesn't say how the courts split between the two sports.

[Calista](/hotels/calista-luxury-resort/) lists three clay courts and one hard court with night lighting, plus panoramic padel courts. It publishes no prices or coaching details.

### Others in Belek

- **[Gloria](/hotels/gloria-golf-resort/):** the resorts' activity pages mention natural clay and hard courts, lessons, sparring partners and international group camps, but give no court numbers, and those pages carry a © 2024 footer. The Gloria Sports Arena catalogue lists one synthetic-turf and one hard tennis court.
- **[Maxx Royal Belek](/hotels/maxx-royal-belek-golf-resort/):** a "Royal Tennis Academy" with private and group lessons and ITF-standard courts; no numbers.
- **[Rixos Premium Belek](/hotels/rixos-premium-belek/):** its FAQ says tennis-court lighting and padel cost extra.

## Kemer and Side: club resorts with a dozen courts

### ROBINSON Çamyuva, Kemer (adults only)

[Çamyuva](/hotels/robinson-camyuva/) takes guests aged 18 and over. It has **12 tennis courts**, five of them floodlit. The facilities section calls them quartz sand and the FAQ calls them clay. Court use, HEAD racket hire and a weekly guest tournament are free; lessons from an outside school are priced on site, and new balls and stringing are charged. Two padel courts are charged and two pickleball courts are free, all booked in the ROBINSON app.

It is a summer resort: 17 April–8 November 2026 and **16 April–7 November 2027**. For other adults-only stays, see [adults-only hotels in Antalya](/blog/adults-only-hotels-antalya/).

### ROBINSON Pamfilya, Side

[Pamfilya](/hotels/robinson-pamfilya/) has **11 quartz-sand courts**, three floodlit, run by the ROBINSON tennis school with licensed coaches. Children start young: a ball school for ages 3–5 and tennis groups for 6–12 and 13–17. Courts, rackets and the weekly tournament are free; lessons and the two padel courts are charged. The 2027 season runs **23 April–6 November**.

### Corendon Tennis Club Kemer

Not a hotel but a dedicated club: 14 ITF-standard clay courts, with floodlights on the Centre Court and courts 7–10, a junior academy for ages 6–18, and play "365 days a year". It publishes no prices; packages are sold with Corendon Playa Kemer. Its official address is in Göynük, Kemer. Our [Corendon Hydros Club Kemer](/hotels/corendon-hydros-club-kemer/) page covers the group's family resort nearby.

### Club Med Palmiye

[Club Med Palmiye](/hotels/club-med-palmiye/) in Kemer lists tennis and padel activities and a tennis school, but publishes no court details on the pages we could open.

## Padel and pickleball

Padel is now on most resorts' lists: all three ROBINSON clubs, Masmavi, Megasaray, Carya, Kempinski and Calista publish courts. It is usually charged even where tennis is free. Pickleball is newer: Megasaray, Carya and Çamyuva each list one or two courts. The Turkish Tennis Federation said in June 2026 that QNB was sponsoring FIP padel tournaments in İzmir and Antalya that year, without naming an Antalya date.

## Tennis in Antalya in winter

If you are coming between December and March, your realistic choices are:

- **ROBINSON Nobilis**, with its covered hall from November to March;
- **Megasaray**, whose academy says it runs all year;
- **Regnum Carya**, whose winter fact sheet lists all its courts (all charged).

Çamyuva, Pamfilya and Masmavi are closed in the depths of winter. For other resorts that stay open, see [Antalya club resorts open in winter](/blog/antalya-club-resorts-open-in-winter/), and for the weather, [Antalya in winter](/blog/antalya-in-winter/).

### Tournaments to watch

The ITF's calendar lists professional events at Club Megasaray Hotel in November 2026: a women's W15 on clay from 9 to 15 November and a men's M15 from 23 to 29 November, each with $15,000 in prize money. We found no December 2026 or 2027 events yet. The academy's homepage also mentions a WTA 125 "Megasaray Hotels Open" in late February to mid-March, without a year.

## How to choose

- **Serious training:** Megasaray's academy, with its long price list and junior and adult programmes.
- **Free court time and a social tournament:** the ROBINSON clubs, where courts and rackets cost nothing.
- **Coaching in the price:** TUI MAGIC LIFE Masmavi's included group training.
- **Adults only:** ROBINSON Çamyuva, in season.
- **A winter week:** Nobilis or Megasaray.

Ask every resort the same three questions: which courts are floodlit and whether lights cost extra, whether rackets are free, and how far ahead you can book. Our [Serik district guide](/destinations/serik/) covers Belek, and the [Kemer district guide](/destinations/kemer/) the coast to the west.

## FAQ

### Which Antalya resort has the most tennis courts?

Megasaray Club Belek. Its sports page counts 64 courts with the Megasaray Tennis Academy: 26 at the hotel (including padel and pickleball) and 38 at the academy.

### Can I play tennis in Antalya in winter?

Yes. ROBINSON Nobilis is open all year and covers courts with an indoor hall from November to March, Megasaray's academy says it runs all year, and Regnum Carya's winter fact sheet lists all its courts at extra charge.

### Is tennis free at all-inclusive resorts in Belek?

It depends. At the ROBINSON clubs, court use and racket hire are free and lessons are charged. Masmavi includes group training. Regnum Carya charges for everything, including court lighting.

### How much is a tennis lesson in Belek?

From the prices published on 25 November 2026: €35 for one player at TUI MAGIC LIFE, and €65 an hour on Megasaray's carpet courts (€80 on clay). ROBINSON's tennis schools price on site.
