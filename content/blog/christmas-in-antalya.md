Christmas in Antalya is low-key. In Türkiye, 25 December is a normal working day, so the city carries on as usual while resorts put on festive programmes for their guests and the concert halls keep their winter season going. New Year's Eve is the bigger night, and 1 January is the only public holiday in the fortnight. This guide covers 20 December 2026 to 2 January 2027, plus one show the following week. It includes only what we could confirm on official, hotel or ticket pages on 17 November 2026: four dated shows, one hotel programme, St Nicholas Church in Demre and what the law says about the holidays.

## Christmas in Antalya at a glance

- **25 December 2026 (Friday):** an ordinary working day, not a public holiday.
- **24 December–1 January:** Xanadu Resort in Belek has published a festive programme, with a Christmas buffet on the 24th, a gala and fireworks on the 31st, and brunch on New Year's Day.
- **Shows with confirmed dates:** Ayna on 20 December, Özel Bir Rica on 22 December, Aşk Listesi on 23 December and Hastalık Hastası on 9 January 2027. All are in Turkish.
- **1 January 2027 (Friday):** a public holiday by law.
- **St Nicholas Church, Demre:** open 08:30–17:30 in winter, according to Turkish Museums. €17 for foreign adults.
- **Church services:** we couldn't confirm any Christmas service times, so check locally.

## Is Christmas a holiday in Türkiye?

No. Türkiye's general holidays are set by Law No. 2429 on National Holidays and General Holidays, adopted on 17 March 1981. Its second article makes the official and religious holidays it lists, plus New Year's Day, general holidays. Christmas is not on the list. So 25 December, a Friday this year, is a working day, and you can expect offices, banks and schools to open as normal.

You'll still see it marked where foreign guests stay. Xanadu's programme, for one, opens with a Christmas buffet on Christmas Eve. If you want to say it in Turkish, Santa Claus is Noel Baba.

New Year's Day is different. The law is brief: "1 Ocak günü yılbaşı tatilidir", meaning 1 January is the New Year holiday. In 2027 it falls on a Friday, so many people in Türkiye get a three-day weekend from 1 to 3 January. Public offices close, so check hours before you plan anything that depends on a state office. 31 December is not a holiday under the law.

We read the law's text on the website of the Grand National Assembly of Türkiye.

## St Nicholas Church in Demre

The coast's real link to Christmas is in Demre, a long drive west of Antalya. The church there was built over the tomb of Nicholas, the bishop of Myra whose legend grew into Santa Claus. Our [Myra and St Nicholas guide](/blog/myra-and-st-nicholas-church-demre/) tells his story. Turkish Museums describes the church's long history as a place of pilgrimage and says merchants from Bari carried off his relics in 1087.

### Visiting over the holidays

Turkish Museums lists the church, officially the Aziz (St.) Nikolaos Anıt Müzesi, with these details:

- **Winter hours (1 November–31 March):** 08:30–17:30, ticket office closes at 17:00. The portal prints these against Saturday and Sunday and gives no separate weekday rows.
- **Entry:** €17 for foreign visitors aged 8 and over. Under-8s are free; Turkish citizens pay 200 TL.
- **MuseumPass The Mediterranean:** €90 for seven days, valid at more than 40 sites. Our [MuseumPass guide](/blog/museumpass-the-mediterranean-antalya/) works out when it pays off.
- **Closures:** none listed, including 1 January.

On 10 November our Myra guide gave 08:30–19:00 and no fee, from the ministry's muze.gov.tr page. Turkish Museums now shows the shorter winter day, so treat 17:00 as your last entry.

### 6 December and this year's programme

St Nicholas's feast day is 6 December. The ministry's page, quoted in our Myra guide, gives it as the date he died, and the guide also describes the memorial service held in the church on that day in past years. As of 17 November 2026, no official programme for 2026 has been published. Demre Municipality's news page, whose latest item is dated 6 November, says nothing about it, and the Turkish Museums page doesn't mention the day. We'll update this guide if the ministry or the municipality announces one.

To make the trip, pair Demre with Kaş: see our [Antalya to Kaş day trip](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) and the [Demre district guide](/destinations/demre/). Winter days are short, so leave early.

## FAQ

### Is Christmas Day a holiday in Antalya?

No. Christmas isn't a public holiday in Türkiye, so 25 December is a normal working day. New Year's Day, 1 January, is a public holiday under Law No. 2429.

### What's on in Antalya over Christmas 2026?

As of 17 November 2026: Ayna on 20 December and Özel Bir Rica on 22 December at Türkan Şoray Kültür Merkezi, Aşk Listesi on 23 December at the Nazım Hikmet centre, Xanadu Resort's festive programme from 24 December to 1 January, and Hastalık Hastası on 9 January 2027. Check our [events page](/events/) for additions.

### Where can I spend New Year's Eve in Antalya?

Xanadu Resort in Belek has published a gala dinner, fireworks and a laser show for 31 December 2026, with brunch on 1 January. It gives no prices and doesn't say whether non-residents can book.

### Is there anything special at St Nicholas Church in Demre at Christmas?

No official Christmas or 6 December programme for 2026 had been published by 17 November. Turkish Museums lists the church as open 08:30–17:30 in winter, with the ticket office closing at 17:00, and €17 entry for foreign adults.
