Belek is one of Europe's winter golf spots, and December to February is when it is cheapest. It is not summer golf: days are short, mornings can be cold and wet days happen. This guide explains what to expect from a winter round in Antalya, what the published season and green-fee patterns say, and how to plan around the tournament weeks. It draws on our other [Belek golf guides](/golf/) rather than repeating them.

## The golf season

Belek's golf season runs **September to May**, according to Ercan Çek, a vice president of the hotel managers' association POYD, speaking to Anadolu Agency in a report published by Hürriyet Daily News on 30 September 2026. He said golf lets Belek hotels stay open longer than the beach season alone would. Clubs agree on the shape: Kempinski The Dome's golf page says the best months are generally March to May and September to November, when temperatures are comfortable and rain is less likely. Winter is the quiet, cheaper stretch in between.

Read our [Belek golf season guide](/blog/belek-golf-season-when-to-play/) for the whole year.

## What winter golf costs

National Golf Club publishes rack rates by date range. In its 2026–27 table (checked 9 November 2026), the period **1 December to 10 February** sits lower than the autumn and spring peaks, a little over half the top rate. Other clubs set their own rates, so ask for quotes for your dates. For figures, see [Belek green fees for winter 2026–27](/blog/belek-green-fees-winter-2026-27/).

## The weather

Winter in Antalya is mild but wet. Our guides give: December rain and sea conditions in [is December too cold in Antalya?](/blog/is-december-too-cold-in-antalya-weather-rain-sea-conditions/), January in [is January a bad time to visit?](/blog/is-january-a-bad-time-to-visit-antalya-weather-explained-honestly/) and February in [Antalya weather in February](/blog/what-is-antalya-weather-really-like-in-february/), which gives daytime highs of 13–16°C. Our [sunrise and sunset guide](/blog/sunrise-sunset-daylight-hours-antalya/) shows that mid-December has just under 9 hours 40 minutes of daylight, with sunset about 17:41, so plan to start early.

## Tournament weeks to know about

Winter is also tournament season, and events take up tee times. Highlights from our [Belek golf tournaments guide](/blog/belek-golf-tournaments-winter-2026-27/) (dates as published on 15 November 2026):

- **29 Nov – 6 Dec:** 21st Regnum Carya Pro-Am.
- **10–12 Dec:** 20th Turkish Amateur Open at Kaya Palazzo.
- **13–20 Dec:** 17th Golf Mad Turkish Junior Open at Gloria.
- **14–19 Jan 2027:** Legends Tour Qualifying School at Gloria.
- **30–31 Jan 2027:** U.S. Kids Golf Antalya Turkish Open at Carya (see [January events](/blog/antalya-in-january-2027-whats-on/)).

No club page we read says a course closes to visitors during a tournament, but ask when you book.

## What to pack

1. **Layers.** A fleece or windproof top over a polo; mornings are cool.
2. **Waterproofs.** Light rain gear, since showers are common.
3. **Gloves and a warm hat** for early tee times.
4. **Extra balls and a towel**; wet fairways cost balls.
5. **Spikes or waterproof shoes.**

Dress codes and handicap limits differ by club; see [Belek golf handicap limits and dress codes](/blog/belek-golf-handicap-limits-and-dress-codes/).

## FAQ

### Can you play golf in Antalya in winter?

Yes. Belek's season runs September to May, and winter is quieter and cheaper.

### Is winter golf in Belek cheaper?

National's published rack rates for 1 December to 10 February are lower than its peak rates.

### What is the best month for golf in Belek?

Clubs name March to May and September to November as the most comfortable months.
