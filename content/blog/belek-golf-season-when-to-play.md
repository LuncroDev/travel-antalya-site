Belek golf season is longer than most golfers expect. The main season runs from September to May, and there is still golf in the heat of summer and the quiet of midwinter. What changes through the year is the weather, the green fee and how busy the tee sheet is. Some weeks courses also close for maintenance. This guide sets out when to play in Belek, what the clubs' own pages say about seasons and rules, and what to check before you book flights.

## When is Belek golf season?

Belek's golf season runs **from September through May**. That is how Ercan Çek, vice president of the hotel managers' association POYD, described it to Anadolu Agency in a report published by Hürriyet Daily News on 30 September 2026. He said the golf calendar lets Belek hotels stay open longer than the beach season alone would. He also said the UK sends the most golfers, followed by other European countries, especially Germany.

The clubs agree on the shape of the year. The golf page of Kempinski The Dome, next to [Antalya Golf Club](/golf/antalya-golf-club/), says the best months are generally **March to May and September to November**, when temperatures are comfortable and rain is less likely. GoTürkiye's golf page markets Belek as a year-round destination, with an average temperature of 19°C and more than 300 sunny days a year.

## Belek golf month by month

Green fees are the clearest signal of demand. [National Golf Club](/golf/national-golf-club/) publishes its rack rates by date range, and the 2026–27 table (checked 9 November 2026) falls into a clear pattern:

| Period | National's rack rate level |
|---|---|
| 1 October – 30 November | Highest |
| 1 December – 10 February | Lower, a little over half the top rate |
| 11 February – 31 May | Highest |
| June | Lower |
| July – August | Lowest |
| 1–10 September | Lower |
| 11–30 September | Highest |

National's page doesn't label these as peak or low seasons, but the prices speak for themselves. Other clubs set their own rates, so use the pattern as a guide and get quotes for your dates.

### Spring (mid-February to May)

This is prime time. Days lengthen, the courses are in shape after winter, and the weather suits 36 holes. Expect the highest green fees and the busiest tee sheets, so book tee times early. Our [Antalya weather in March](/blog/antalya-weather-in-march-can-you-enjoy-the-city-yet/), [April](/blog/is-antalya-weather-good-in-april-for-a-holiday-or-too-risky/) and [May](/blog/is-may-one-of-the-best-months-for-antalya-weather/) guides describe the weather in more detail.

### Summer (June to August)

Green fees drop in the hottest months. Summer is also when courses do their heavy maintenance (see below). If you play in summer, book the earliest tee times, take a buggy and plenty of water, and check that your course isn't being cored that week.

### Autumn (September to November)

Autumn is the second peak. The sea is still warm, so non-golfing partners get beach days, and the courses come back from summer work. Watch the dates in September carefully, because some clubs close for overseeding then. For the weather, see [Antalya weather in October](/blog/antalya-weather-in-october-summer-feel-or-start-of-autumn/) and [November](/blog/is-antalya-weather-pleasant-in-november-or-already-winter/).

### Winter (December to mid-February)

Winter is the value season. Green fees are lower, the courses are quieter, and golf holidays keep a number of Belek hotels open when the beach resorts close. You will get some rain. Pack waterproofs, check which hotels are open, and read [Antalya in winter](/blog/antalya-in-winter/) and [Antalya weather in December](/blog/is-december-too-cold-in-antalya-weather-rain-sea-conditions/).

## Maintenance closures to avoid

This is the step most golf trips miss. Courses in Belek aerate their greens in summer and overseed in September, and some close completely while they do.

- **National Golf Club:** hollow-coring from 20 June to 31 August 2027, and closed for overseeding from 10 to 24 September 2027, according to its "About" page.
- **Carya Golf Club:** closed from 15 to 30 September 2027, with hollow-core aeration from 1 to 10 July and 1 to 10 August 2027, according to the Regnum Carya golf page.

Other clubs set their own dates. If your trip is between late June and the end of September, ask each club for its maintenance calendar before you commit. A course straight after coring can be playable but bumpy, and a closed course means changing your plans. See our [Carya Golf Club](/golf/carya-golf-club/) profile for the course itself.

## Tournaments that affect tee times

Big events bring atmosphere but also close courses to visitors.

- **Turkish Airlines Open.** The DP World Tour event was played at National Golf Club from 30 April to 3 May 2026. Regnum says the agreement keeps it at National in 2027 and 2028. GoTürkiye notes that earlier editions were held at the Montgomerie Maxx Royal and Regnum Carya. If you want to play National in spring 2027, check the tournament dates first.
- **Turkish Golf Federation (TGF) calendar.** The federation's 2026 domestic calendar lists several Belek events before the year ends. These include the Regnum Pro-Am at Carya (29 November–6 December), the Turkish Amateur Open at [Kaya Palazzo](/golf/kaya-palazzo-golf-club/) (10–12 December), the Turkish Junior Open at [Gloria](/golf/gloria-golf-club/) (13–20 December) and a winter challenge at Carya (17–20 December).

GoTürkiye's golf pages also note that Belek hosted the 2012 World Amateur Team Championship.

## Handicap and booking rules

Clubs set their own rules, and they are stricter than many first-time visitors expect.

- **National Golf Club** requires a handicap certificate for its championship course, with a maximum of 36 for men and women, and can refuse play without proof. Tee times are requested through its online form. Singles and twosomes may be paired with other players, a fourball has up to 4 hours 30 minutes, denim is not allowed and soft spikes are required. Under-16s get 50% off rack-rate green fees.
- **Antalya Golf Club** (PGA Sultan and Pasha courses): the Kempinski golf page lists the Sultan as par 71 and 6,477 m, with a handicap limit of 24. It lists the Pasha as par 72 and 5,731 m, with limits of 28 for men and 36 for women. The same page contains a contradictory line, so confirm with the club.
- **Cornelia Golf Club** describes its Faldo course as 27 holes, played as three loops called King, Queen and Prince. See our [Cornelia Golf Club](/golf/cornelia-golf-club/) profile.

Whatever the club, carry your handicap certificate and check buggy rules. National's own page gives two different minimum ages for driving a buggy, 16 and 18, so ask before a teenager takes the wheel.

Sources disagree. GoTürkiye says Belek has 15 golf courses, and the September 2026 Anadolu Agency report gives 15 courses and 10 golf hotels. SunExpress's golf page says 19. The numbers differ partly because some clubs run two or three courses or a 9-hole academy course. The Turkish Golf Federation lists 10 Antalya clubs. ## Getting there and where to stay

SunExpress's golf page puts Belek about 30 km from Antalya Airport, roughly half an hour by road. National Golf Club says it is about 25 minutes' drive from the airport. SunExpress, for one, sells golf bag carriage as an add-on that must be booked ahead, so check your airline's rules when you book the flight.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best month for golf in Belek?

For weather, April, May and October are hard to beat. The clubs point to March–May and September–November. For value, December to early February has lower green fees and quieter courses.

### Can you play golf in Belek in summer?

Yes. Courses stay open and green fees are at their lowest, but it is hot and summer is maintenance season. Book early tee times and check coring dates.

### Do I need a handicap certificate to play in Belek?

Usually yes. National Golf Club requires one, with a limit of 36. Antalya Golf Club publishes course-specific limits. Bring proof of your handicap.

### When do Belek courses close for maintenance?

Dates vary by club, usually in summer and September. For 2027, National lists overseeding from 10 to 24 September, and Carya is closed from 15 to 30 September.
