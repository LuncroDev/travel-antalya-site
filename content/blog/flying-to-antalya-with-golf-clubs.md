Flying to Antalya with golf clubs is simple once you know your airline's rule, and the rules differ more than you'd think. One carrier flies your bag free, another charges €50 a flight, a third wants it registered 72 hours ahead. On 20 November 2026 we opened the golf and sports-baggage pages of ten airlines and noted what each says: whether the golf bag sits inside your checked allowance or is a paid extra, how heavy it can be, what you may pack in it and when to book. **Every fee below is quoted as published on 20 November 2026.** Several depend on the route or fare, so check the price in your own booking before you pay.

## Türkiye's own airlines

### Turkish Airlines: one free set, for now

Turkish Airlines' golf page says the **first golf set flies free on international flights**; a second set pays a special baggage fee, listed on a separate page that didn't load for us. A later line limits free carriage to flights **until 31 December 2026 (inclusive)**, and the page links the offer to its World Golf Cup tournament, wording it as one set "per participant". It doesn't say what applies from 1 January 2027, so check again if you fly in the new year.

- **Weight:** 23 kg a set.
- **Reservation:** a reservation entry is mandatory.
- **Domestic flights:** 850 TRY a piece, and 1,000 TRY to Northern Cyprus. That matters if you book Istanbul–Antalya as a separate domestic ticket. Elite Plus members get one golf set free on domestic flights.

### Pegasus golf bag fee: €50 a piece

Pegasus's price table lists golf equipment at **€50 on international flights**, 750 TRY domestic and 900 TRY to Northern Cyprus. Its FAQ says sports equipment is special baggage charged per piece, and that golf fees are charged **for each leg of a connecting journey**. A return via Istanbul can mean four fees.

- **Weight:** 20 kg. Every extra kilo costs the route's excess-baggage rate.
- **Booking:** Pegasus suggests calling its call centre to add the bag before you fly.

### SunExpress sports equipment and the Golf Package

SunExpress charges golf equipment as special baggage **from €39.99 on international flights**, one way, or from 299.99 TL domestic. The flat rate covers 23 kg; excess on special baggage costs €10 per kg and route at check-in. Booking ahead through "My bookings" or the service centre saves up to 50% of the service fee, and SunExpress asks to hear about special baggage up to 48 hours before departure.

The **Golf Package** bundles a golf bag of up to 23 kg with a seat of your choice and can be added to the SunEco fare up to 36 hours before departure; its price wasn't on the page we opened. The SunExpress Golf Club lists free golf-bag carriage among member perks but publishes no fee or terms. Organising a group? The 2025–26 golf group offer (€30 per person and route) ended on 28 March 2026, and we found no 2026–27 version.

### Corendon Airlines: two prices

Corendon's sports-equipment table charges **€40 a golf bag**, up to 23 kg, and carries no more than 50 golf bags on one aircraft. Its baggage FAQ says something different: **€30 per item, per one-way flight**, with extra kilos at €10 each at the airport. Ask Corendon which applies and book early, given the 50-bag cap. Its FAQ also bars personal items from the golf bag.

## UK and European airlines

### Jet2 and easyJet

**Jet2** lets you add golf bags up to 22 kg "when booking online for an extra charge". It doesn't print a golf price. Its fees FAQ gives sports equipment "from £35" and says the exact figure appears during booking. To add clubs to an existing booking you must contact Jet2. Carriage depends on space, and Jet2's baggage page warns that adding sports equipment may raise the cost of your transfer.

**easyJet** classes golf clubs as small sports equipment, up to 20 kg, one item per person. Its fees page charges **£42 online or £47 at the airport, per item per flight**. You can't pool the clubs' weight with your hold bag, and there's no refund once booked.

### TUI Airways and British Airways

**TUI** asks you to pre-book sports equipment, golf clubs included, by phone; its luggage page warns that unbooked equipment may be refused at the airport. Its golf holidays page says you can add clubs "from just £27", without saying whether that's per person or per flight.

**British Airways** takes a golf bag, in a protective bag or hard case, as **one item of your checked allowance** if your ticket includes a hold bag. On Economy Basic you buy an extra bag, cheapest online. BA treats golf bags as out-of-gauge: drop it at the oversize desk at least two hours before a short-haul flight. It asks you to tell it about the bag when you book, and ideally 72 hours or more before you fly.

### Condor and Lufthansa

**Condor** prices golf by flight zone, per one-way flight: from €54.99 online in zone 1 up to €99.99 in zone 4, or €120 to €135 at the airport. Bags can weigh 30 kg and must be registered up to 8 hours before departure. The page doesn't say which zone Antalya is in, and the zone map didn't open for us.

**Lufthansa** counts golf equipment in your free allowance if it fits the piece and weight limits of your fare, except on Economy Light. Register it at least 72 hours ahead. Above the allowance, a flat rate is paid at the airport.

## What you can pack in a golf bag

Where airlines spell it out, shoes and balls may travel with the clubs:

- **Turkish Airlines:** clubs, balls, one pair of golf shoes, tees and the bag. No personal belongings.
- **Pegasus:** up to 14 clubs, 24 balls and one pair of shoes, 20 kg in all. No personal belongings.
- **British Airways:** a set of clubs, a golf umbrella, a dozen balls, tees and a pair of golf shoes.
- **Lufthansa:** clubs, balls, tees and one pair of golf shoes, all packed inside the bag.
- **Corendon:** no personal items.

Pack soft spikes: several Belek clubs require them, as our [handicap and dress code guide](/blog/belek-golf-handicap-limits-and-dress-codes/) explains. Taking an electric trolley? Lufthansa covers lithium-battery trolleys under separate battery rules, so check your airline's battery page first.

## Arriving at Antalya Airport with clubs

Antalya Airport's checked-luggage page lists golf bags among bulky items and tells you to check your airline's conditions before you travel. It doesn't mention an oversize counter. Its baggage-services page lists luggage trolleys in and outside the terminals and paid bag wrapping, 24 hours a day, in the Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 departure halls. ### Customs: bringing clubs into Türkiye

Türkiye's Ministry of Trade lists "golf equipment (excluding golf carts)" among the sports items a passenger can bring in duty-free. Listed items are limited by quantity rather than value, and must be for personal, non-commercial use.

## Check before you fly

1. **Open your airline's golf page on the day you book.** Fees change, and Turkish Airlines' free set is dated to 31 December 2026.
2. **Add the bag in advance.** Turkish Airlines, Condor and Lufthansa require registration, TUI asks you to phone, and online beats airport prices at easyJet and Condor.
3. **Count every leg.** Pegasus charges per leg on connections.
4. **Weigh the bag.** Limits run from 20 kg (Pegasus, easyJet) to 23 kg (Turkish Airlines, SunExpress, Corendon).
5. **Keep clothes out.** Turkish Airlines, Pegasus and Corendon ban personal belongings in the bag.
6. **Check your fare.** BA's Economy Basic and Lufthansa's Economy Light don't include the bag.
7. **Tell your transfer.** Golf bags need space in the vehicle.
8. **Leave time at the airport.** BA wants out-of-gauge bags dropped two hours before short-haul departures.

## FAQ

### Do golf clubs fly free to Antalya?

On Turkish Airlines, one golf set flies free on international flights, but its page dates that to 31 December 2026. British Airways and Lufthansa count the bag in your checked allowance if your fare includes one. Pegasus, SunExpress, Corendon, easyJet, Jet2, TUI and Condor charge for it.

### What is the Pegasus golf bag fee?

As published on 20 November 2026, €50 on international flights and 750 TRY on domestic flights, up to 20 kg. Pegasus charges per leg on connecting journeys.

### Can I put golf shoes in my golf bag?

Yes, at Turkish Airlines, Pegasus, British Airways and Lufthansa, which all list a pair of golf shoes as part of a golf set. Turkish Airlines, Pegasus and Corendon don't allow other personal belongings in the bag.

### Can I rent golf clubs in Belek?

Yes. Published prices checked on 14 November 2026 run from €35 a day at Sueno to €40 at Carya, National and Cornelia; weekly sets cost €110 at National. Our [golf buggy hire guide](/blog/golf-buggy-hire-in-belek/) has the full list.
