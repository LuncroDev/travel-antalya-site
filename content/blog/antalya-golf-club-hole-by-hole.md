This Antalya Golf Club hole-by-hole guide covers both of the club's 18-hole courses in Belek, the **PGA Sultan** and the **Pasha**. The club publishes a tab for every hole with its name, par, four tee lengths and a short playing tip, so this is one of the few Belek courses you can plan shot by shot before you arrive. We read the hole pages, the About page and the academy page on 28 November 2026; everything below comes from them, in our own words.

## Antalya Golf Club at a glance

Both courses were designed by **European Golf Design** with **David Jones**, the Senior Tour professional, as design consultant. The club's About page gives these headline figures:

| Course | Holes | Par | Length (About page) | Longest tees added up | Shortest tees added up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PGA Sultan | 18 | 72 | 6,477 m | 6,477 m | 5,003 m |
| Pasha | 18 | 72 | 5,769 m | 5,731 m | 4,326 m |

Each hole tab shows four tee lengths in metres. The pages don't name the tee colours, so the tables below give the **longest** and **shortest** tee for each hole. Two small notes before you print anything: the Pasha's own hole lengths add up to 5,731 m, not the 5,769 m on the About page, and each tab shows a stroke index only as an image, so we haven't copied it.

## The PGA Sultan: the championship course

The Sultan is the longer course and the one with more water. It opens and closes with solid par 4s and saves its par 5s for holes 3, 7, 12 and 16.

## The Pasha: shorter, but not soft

The Pasha is roughly 700 m shorter from the back than the Sultan, with friendlier par 3s and a run of short par 4s on the back nine. It still has lakes, streams and well-placed bunkers, and its greens slope.

## Which course should you play?

- **Low handicappers and first-timers in Belek:** the Sultan. It is the championship course, and holes 4, 6, 12 and 16 test every part of your game.
- **Mixed groups and higher handicaps:** the Pasha. At 4,326 m from the shortest tees it is one of the gentler 18-hole rounds in Belek; the courses-compared guide lists it among the shorter options.
- **A week's stay:** play both. The 11th and 13th on the Pasha and the 3rd and 16th on the Sultan are the holes where a gamble can pay off.

## FAQ

### How long is the PGA Sultan course?

The club gives 6,477 m, par 72, which matches the sum of its longest hole tees. From the shortest tees it plays 5,003 m.

### Is the Pasha course easier than the Sultan?

It is shorter, by roughly 700 m from the back tees, and its par 3s are short. The club's tips still warn of water on holes 5, 8, 12 and 15, so it isn't a pushover.

### Does Antalya Golf Club publish a stroke index?

Each hole tab shows one, but only as an image, so we couldn't copy it. Ask for the scorecard when you check in.

### Which is the signature hole?

The club itself calls the Pasha's 12th, The Pipe, its "greatest par 4", and the Sultan's 4th its toughest. The Sultan's 5th has the sea view.
