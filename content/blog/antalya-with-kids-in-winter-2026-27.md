Winter is a calmer time to bring children to Antalya. The beaches are quiet, the sea is cool, and the city's stages and indoor attractions take over. This guide pulls together what we have confirmed for families from late November 2026 to the end of January 2027, plus easy rainy-day and outdoor ideas. Most children's shows are in Turkish, so we note ages and formats.

## Children's shows, November to January

The Konyaaltı Nazım Hikmet centre has the largest run of children's theatre. Confirmed dates from our listings:

| Date | Show | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 22 Nov | [Ağustos Böceği ve Karınca](/events/agustos-bocegi-ve-karinca-2026-11-22-konyaalti/) | 14:15 | Musical play on the grasshopper and ant fable |
| 22 Nov | [Arı Maya](/events/ari-maya-2026-11-22-konyaalti/) | 15:30 | Maya's flower-growing plans lead to a lost pirate |
| 23 Nov | [Tembel Prens & Çalışkan Prenses](/events/tembel-prens-caliskan-prenses-2026-11-23-konyaalti/) | 11:00 | A show about effort and kindness |
| 23 Nov | [Sevimli Dostlar](/events/sevimli-dostlar-2026-11-23-konyaalti/) | 19:00 | Encourages care for the planet |
| 24 Jan | [Çizmeli Kedi Müzikali](/events/cizmeli-kedi-muzikali-2027-01-24-konyaalti/) | not yet published | Puss in Boots, 60 minutes, ages 3 and up |

The hall's January list also includes *Kırmızı Başlıklı Kız ve Yalancı Kurt* on 9 January (ages 2 and up) and *Yaramazlar Sirki* on 31 January. We have only a date for those; check Biletinial for the time. Tickets for *Çizmeli Kedi Müzikali* started at 500 TL on Biletinial when we checked on 18 November.

All of these are in Turkish. Young children who enjoy music, puppets and slapstick will manage without understanding the words; older children may not.

## The Nutcracker, 28 November

Antalya State Opera and Ballet's *Fındıkkıran* plays Haşim İşcan Cultural Center on 28 November 2026 from 15:00 to 17:10. It is a two-act, 130-minute production recommended for ages seven and over, per the Türkiye Culture Portal. Our [event page](/events/the-nutcracker-in-antalya/) has the details. For a family that does not speak Turkish, this is the easiest show on the list.

## Rainy-day ideas

- **Antalya Aquarium** in Konyaaltı has a 131 m tunnel, 40 themed tanks and an indoor Snow World, open daily from 10:00 according to our [guide](/things-to-do/antalya-aquarium/). It is a short walk from the beach.
- **Museums.** Read [Antalya museums for a rainy day](/blog/antalya-museums-rainy-day/) first: the main Antalya Museum building is closed, and the guide explains what to visit instead.
- **Heated pools.** Several resorts keep heated or indoor pools running in winter. See [hotels with heated pools in winter](/blog/antalya-hotels-with-heated-pools-winter/).

## Outdoor days when the weather allows

- **Walk the coast.** [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/) and [Karaalioğlu Park](/things-to-do/karaalioglu-park/) are good winter strolls.
- **Ruins.** Open-air sites such as [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/) and [Perge](/things-to-do/perge-ancient-city/) are comfortable in winter and uncrowded. Our [Aspendos and Side guide](/blog/aspendos-and-side-in-one-day/) has hours and fees.
- **Snow.** Saklıkent has a sledge run, and the guide site says only sleds rented at the resort are allowed on it. Snow is not guaranteed; read our [Saklıkent day-trip guide](/blog/saklikent-ski-day-trip-from-antalya/) before you set out.
- **Winter day trips.** See [winter day trips from Antalya](/blog/winter-day-trips-from-antalya/).

## Where to stay

Family resorts in Belek and Kemer vary in which facilities stay open in winter. Check the hotel's own winter notice, then browse [Belek hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek) and [Kemer hotels](/hotels/?area=Kemer). In town, [SeaLife Family Resort](/hotels/sealife-family-resort-konyaalti/) in Konyaaltı is one family-focused option. For general advice, read [is Antalya good for families?](/blog/is-antalya-good-for-families-things-to-do-in-antalya-with-kids/).

## What to pack

Layers, a waterproof jacket and closed shoes. December is wetter than many visitors expect. Our [what to wear guide](/blog/what-to-wear-in-antalya-in-different-months-weather-based-guide/) is month by month.

## FAQ

### Are there children's shows in English?

We have not found any in the winter listings we checked. The ballet is the easiest option.

### Is the beach usable in winter?

You can walk it; swimming depends on the day. See [can you swim in Antalya in winter?](/blog/can-you-swim-in-antalya-in-winter-weather-sea-temperature-guide/).

### Can we see snow near Antalya?

Sometimes. Saklıkent in the Bey Mountains is the nearest ski area, but its season depends on snowfall.
