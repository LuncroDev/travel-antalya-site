The AntalyaKart is Antalya's contactless travel card, and it is the cheapest way to ride the city's buses and AntRay trams. A full-fare ride costs 42 TL on the card, against 50 TL with a contactless bank card, and only the card gives you a free change from bus to tram. This AntalyaKart guide covers what the card costs, where to buy and top it up, how transfers work, which lines follow different rules, and who rides free. Every figure comes from the card's own [FAQ](https://www.antalyakart.com.tr/Page/Faqs) and [card-types page](https://www.antalyakart.com.tr/Page/KartCesitleri), and from Antalya Ulaşım, the municipal company that runs the trams, all checked on 14 November 2026. Where they say nothing, we say so.

For the wider picture, see our [plan your trip](/plan-your-trip/) page and our guide on [how to get to Antalya](/blog/how-to-get-to-antalya-flights-airport-transport-options/).

## AntalyaKart fares in November 2026

The FAQ prints one flat fare table for buses and trams alike. It has no zones and no separate airport price, and the table carries no date. The prices match our previous check on 13 November.

| How you pay | One ride |
|---|---|
| AntalyaKart, full fare (Tam) | 42 TL |
| Retired or teacher card | 40 TL |
| Student card | 20 TL |
| Kullan At single-use ticket, per ride | 46 TL |
| Contactless bank card | 50 TL |
| Transfer on an AntalyaKart | Free |

The cards themselves cost extra:

| Card | Price |
|---|---|
| Full card (Tam Kart) | 100 TL |
| Personalised full card, in your name | 200 TL |
| Kullan At card | 20 TL |
| Kullan At, 2 rides / 5 rides | 112 TL / 250 TL |
| Mobile AntalyaKart (phone card) | 19 TL, one-off |

The Kullan At packs work out at 46 TL a ride plus the 20 TL card. The FAQ does not say whether the 100 TL full card comes with any credit on it.

## Which way to pay

### A contactless bank card for a few rides

Any bank or credit card with the contactless symbol works at the readers. Hold it there until the screen shows "Kredi Kartı" and the fare, then take it away. Each ride is 50 TL, with no transfers and no discounts. The card-types page says one bank card can pay for more than one person. No page says whether cards issued abroad are accepted, so carry a backup.

### The full AntalyaKart for a week

The 100 TL full card isn't registered to anyone, so several people can share it. It saves 8 TL a ride against a bank card, which covers its price after 13 rides, sooner with transfers. A journey with one change from bus to tram costs 42 TL on the card, 92 TL on Kullan At and 100 TL on a bank card. Against a bank card, two such journeys pay for the card.

To pay for a companion, tap the card a second time on board. That second tap earns no transfer.

### Kullan At if you'd rather not top up

Kullan At ("use and throw away") tickets come in 2-ride and 5-ride packs from machines and dealers. You can't top them up, and they get no transfers or discounts. Like the full card, one ticket can be shared.

### Mobile AntalyaKart on an Android phone

The app can turn a phone with NFC into a card for a one-off 19 TL. The FAQ says it does not work on iPhones, because Apple restricts NFC access. Rides on the phone card do get transfers. To log in, the app asks for a 10-digit phone number without the leading zero, which is the Turkish format. The pages don't say whether a foreign number works.

## Where to buy an AntalyaKart

### Machines

The card's [list of sales and top-up machines](https://www.antalyakart.com.tr/Page/DolumCihazlari) runs to more than a hundred entries, most of them at tram stops. Three are listed at the airport's international (Dış Hatlar) tram stop, and one at the domestic terminal, opposite the bus stop. The list doesn't name the international terminal, and the airport operator does not say which terminal the airport tram stop is next to either, so ask at the information desk when you land. Other machines stand at the Otogar (the intercity bus station), Meydan, İsmetpaşa and the Müze stop on Konyaaltı Caddesi. The list does not say what payment the machines take.

### Dealers

Shops and kiosks sell and top up cards too. The municipality's transport department publishes a long [list of authorised dealers and top-up points](https://ulasim.antalya.bel.tr/Home/YuklemeNoktalari), from grocers to phone shops. Dealers take cash only, the minimum top-up is 0.05 TL, and there is no commission. If a dealer asks for a fee, report it to the call centre on 0242 606 07 07.

### Card centres

Two are open to the public:

- **Batıgar**, at the Batıgar tram stop beside the Otogar in Kepez (Dumlupınar Bulvarı No. 2/3). The FAQ calls it the interchange between the Fatih–Havalimanı–Expo and Varsak–Müze tram lines.
- **Doğu Garajı**, in the Doğu Garajı culture and trade centre, Cebesoy Caddesi No. 1, Çaybaşı, in [Muratpaşa](/destinations/muratpasa/).

Both open on weekdays 08:30–12:30 and 13:00–18:00, and on Saturdays 08:30–12:00. No Sunday hours are published, and both closed on 29 October for Republic Day. A third centre, on the Akdeniz University campus, opens only during student registration.

## How to top up and check your balance

Load cash at a machine, dealer or card centre, or pay by card in the AntalyaKart app (App Store, Google Play, Huawei AppGallery) or on the website:

1. Open "Kart İşlemleri" and enter your full card number.
2. Choose online top-up, pick an amount and pay by credit card.
3. Approve the 3D Secure code your bank sends by SMS.
4. Tap the card on a bus or tram reader. Online credit only reaches the card at that tap.

If a reader says your balance is too low right after an online top-up, that is why. Neither page says whether foreign-issued cards can pay online.

The same screen shows your balance, last top-up and last ride. The app's route planner finds the quickest bus or tram to places like [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/). Its "Yakın Noktalar" (nearby points) screen shows the closest stops, dealers and approaching buses.

## How AntalyaKart transfers (aktarma) work

The transfer rules sit in the FAQ:

- One free transfer, used between 5 minutes and 1 hour after your first tap.
- Between two different tram lines, the free window runs from 5 minutes to 2 hours.
- The first five minutes are a protection period. Board again in that time, or tap twice for a companion, and that ride earns no transfer.
- No transfers on Kullan At tickets or bank cards. Phone cards get them.
- No transfer between lines that share a route sign ("aynı levha"), or between a line and its "A" version, such as VL13 and VL13A.
- No transfers on vehicles with M plates, on terminal lines, or on lines DC27, 500, 501, 514, 515 and 515A.

The FAQ doesn't explain which vehicles carry M plates, what the terminal lines are, or where the numbered lines run. If your change involves one of them, budget for a second full fare.

## Lines with different rules

### AntRay trams

The trams use the same fare table as the buses, and tram-to-tram changes get the longer two-hour window. The airport operator says the AntRay tram serves the airport on the Havalimanı (Airport)–Meydan–Fatih line, which also stops in the city centre and at the Otogar. Our [airport transfer guide](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) covers the onward options. Antalya Ulaşım's tram rules allow hand luggage up to 25 kg.

During the [COP31 conference](/events/cop31-antalya/), Antalya Ulaşım changed tram timetables from 9 November. On 10 November it moved the T3 Varsak–Müze trams to the Fatih–COP31 section of T1, with buses covering T3 while the trams are away. Check its notices before you plan around T3.

### Airport buses

The airport operator says city buses run from the airport to the Otogar and the city centre. For times, it points to the AntalyaKart app or the transport line on 0242 606 07 07. No official page we found gives a separate airport fare, or says whether any of the excluded line numbers above are airport routes.

### The nostalgic tram

The red heritage tram runs between Zerdalilik and the Müze stop. Its rules say that if you stay on at either end, you tap again and pay again. But on 21 July 2026 the municipality's transport department said the tram was paused after technical faults and accidents, with buses on the same route. It added that the line will not be removed. We found no notice of a restart by 14 November. Our [Kaleiçi walking route](/blog/kaleici-walking-route/) works without it.

### Intercity coaches and Otogar minibuses

None of the AntalyaKart pages mention intercity coaches, boats, or the minibuses from the Otogar to Side, Manavgat and Alanya. Don't assume the card works on them. Our [Side day trip guide](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/) covers that route.

## Discounts, and who rides free

- **Children under seven** ride free with a parent on the AntRay and the nostalgic tram, according to Antalya Ulaşım's [tram rules](https://www.antalyaulasim.com.tr/Hizmetlerimiz/Antray). The AntalyaKart pages and the company's bus page give no child rule, so on buses, ask the driver.
- **Over-65s.** The card-types page says Turkish citizens aged 65 and over travel free by law. It publishes nothing for foreign seniors.
- **Students** pay 20 TL, and **teachers and retired people** 40 TL, but only with a personalised discount card. That needs Turkish paperwork, such as an enrolment certificate from a school approved by Turkey's education authorities. The pages list no discount for students visiting from abroad.
- **Free days.** The municipality sometimes makes its buses and the AntRay free for a day, as it did on 29 October 2026. You still tap, but nothing is taken.

## Lost cards, broken cards and refunds

To report a lost card, call 0242 606 07 07 to block it. Then take your ID, your last top-up receipt and a lost-card form to Batıgar or Doğu Garajı. The FAQ says the old balance is moved over if you go back to the branch seven days after getting your new card. The card-types page promises this only for the 200 TL personalised card, so treat a 100 TL full card like cash. A broken card goes to either centre with your ID.

The only refund rule published is that the Mobile AntalyaKart fee is not refunded. Nothing is published about refunding unused credit on a full card or a Kullan At ticket. Top up little and often, and plan to spend the last lira on your final ride.

## Beyond buses and trams

## FAQ

### Can I pay for Antalya's buses and trams with a bank card?

Yes, for 50 TL a ride, with no transfers or discounts. If you'll ride more than a dozen times, an AntalyaKart at 42 TL a ride is cheaper.

### How long is the AntalyaKart transfer window?

One hour for a single free transfer, starting five minutes after your first tap. Between two tram lines it is two hours. Kullan At tickets and bank cards get no transfers, and some lines are excluded.

### Do children ride free in Antalya?

On the trams, yes: under-sevens ride free with a parent. The AntalyaKart pages and the bus page publish no child rule, so ask the driver on buses.

### Can I buy an AntalyaKart at Antalya Airport?

The machine list includes card sales and top-up machines at the airport, three of them at the international (Dış Hatlar) tram stop. Neither the list nor the airport operator says which terminal that stop is next to, so ask at the information desk.
