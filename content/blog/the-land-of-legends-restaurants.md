The Land of Legends restaurants are the main reason many Belek and Lara hotel guests leave their all-inclusive for a night. The theme park's Shopping Avenue in Kadriye has seven sit-down places on its "Fine Dine" list, and four of them publish menus, hours and booking links. This guide compares them, explains the free evening shows, and flags the mix-ups in the restaurants' own contact details. We read every official page on 26 November 2026.

## The Land of Legends at a glance

- **Address:** Kadriye Mahallesi, Atatürk Avenue No: 104/1-2, 07525 Serik, Antalya (as printed on the park's site). Customer service: 444 9913 (from abroad +90 850 755 1 797).
- **The Fine Dine list:** Alâ Akşam, Asil, Ava, MykOrini, NEMO, Nickelodeon Cafe and Piazzetta Italiana, all tagged as part of the Shopping Avenue.
- **Opening hours:** the site shows "Theme Park hours: 10:00-21:00" on every page, but no separate hours for the Shopping Avenue. Each restaurant keeps its own hours (below).
- **Shows:** the Musical Boat Parade on the canal is billed as free, and the site's guide says the evening shows are free and usually start around 21:00–22:00, depending on the season.
- **Who runs the restaurants:** Asil, NEMO, MykOrini and Piazzetta Italiana all belong to SWOT Hospitality, and some share a phone number.

## Asil: meze, grills and set menus

Asil calls its style "Arabesque": Lebanese, Turkish and Moroccan dishes under one roof. The menu page names a grill platter, lobster thermidor, a fish tagine, a prawn casserole, Adana kebab, grilled octopus, truffle hummus and Gavurdağı salad.

- **Hours:** 14:00 to midnight, last food order 23:00, last drinks and desserts 23:15.
- **Set menus (in euros):** "Lunch the Asil Way", five courses, €35 a person, daily 14:00–17:00; "Rakı Night", four courses with Turkish music and a henna artist, €45 a person every night from 18:00. Both are for two or more people.
- **Happier Hours:** €40 a person, advertised as daily 22:00–01:00 — later than the restaurant's own closing time, so ask before you plan on it.

Book online through the link on Asil's contact page, or call +90 507 634 08 34. The site shares its menus and navigation with Asil's Dubai branch, so make sure you're on the Turkey pages. Full details are in our [Asil Restaurant profile](/restaurants/asil-restaurant-kadriye/).

## NEMO: the aquarium restaurant

The Land of Legends' news page calls NEMO Antalya's first aquarium restaurant, cooking Asian and European dishes; its blog adds sushi. NEMO's own menu page names a crispy duck salad, a sea bass dish with Granny Smith apple, tempura, dim sum, gyoza and sushi.

- **Hours:** lunch from 13:00, dinner 18:00 to midnight. The contact page prints two last food orders, 22:45 and 23:15, and last drinks and desserts at 23:45.
- **Booking:** through SevenRooms, or on +90 505 178 13 46.
- **Address:** Kadriye Mahallesi, Atatürk Caddesi No:515/1, The Land of Legends.

The theme park's news item says NEMO and Asil were both honoured by Gault&Millau. It gives no year or score, but both appear in our [Gault&Millau Antalya 2026 round-up](/blog/gault-millau-antalya-restaurants-2026/). See our [NEMO profile](/restaurants/nemo-restaurant-lounge/).

## MykOrini: Greek meze and sirtaki

MykOrini serves a Greek-inspired Mediterranean menu of meze and seafood, with grilled octopus among the named dishes. Its site promises a sirtaki show but gives no schedule.

- **Hours:** 16:00 to midnight, seven days, last food order 23:00, last drinks 23:15. The page summary says 01:00; go by the contact page.
- **Booking:** the contact page's "Book Now" button opens MykOrini's own booking page, but the "Reserve Your Table Today" buttons on its home and menu pages go to NEMO's booking page. Check the restaurant name before you confirm.
- **Phone:** +90 507 634 08 34, the same number as Asil and Piazzetta.

MykOrini's Antalya home page still carries an Istanbul title tag, a leftover from its sister branch. Our [MykOrini profile](/restaurants/mykorini-the-land-of-legends/) has more.

## Piazzetta Italiana: pizza and pasta

Piazzetta is the one to choose with children or a mixed group. The Land of Legends' guide calls it award-winning and mentions Neapolitan pizza and handmade pasta. The menu, marked "Prices valid as of 07.10.2026", lists fettuccine Alfredo, lobster tagliatelle, lasagne, carbonara, sea bass Mediterranean-style, ossobuco, beef fillet and pizzas from a Margherita up. Side dishes run from ₺575 for sautéed spinach to ₺695 for truffle-parmesan fries.

- **Hours:** daily 12:30–23:00, with the last food order printed as 23:15.
- **Booking:** online, or +90 507 634 08 34.
- **Address:** Kadriye Mahallesi, Atatürk 3 Caddesi No:1/11, Serik.

See our [Piazzetta Italiana profile](/restaurants/piazzetta-italiana-belek/).

## The other names on the list

**Alâ Akşam**, **Ava** and **Nickelodeon Cafe** appear on the Fine Dine list without menus, hours or descriptions on The Land of Legends' pages. Ask at your hotel or the park's information desk if you want to try them.

For a quick bite, the theme park lists casual outlets such as Burger Stop, Chikko Fast and Donut Worry Be Happy, and the Shopping Avenue's store pages show chains including Arabica, Burger King, Arby's and Cold Stone. Current campaigns mention a 10% discount for park guests at Garbs Italiano, and new Magnolia Bakery and Oakberry counters, without giving terms or dates.

## Time dinner around the shows

The park's event listings show what is on each day. For 26 and 27 November 2026 they included:

- **Chimera Fountain Show:** a short daytime show at 16:30 on 26 November, and a longer "Chimera Spectacular Fountain Show" from 20:00 to 21:30 on 27 November, with loud sound and light effects.
- **North Fountain Show:** 21:00–21:12 on 26 November.
- **Live music at MarinArt:** 20:00–21:30 on 27 November.

The site's guide says the shows are free. The **Canal Boat Tour** is sold at the ticket booths, so not everything on the canal is free. A practical plan: book a 19:00 table, then walk out for the fountain show.

## Winter visits

Several water rides and Aqua Land attractions are marked "Closed for the Season" on the theme park page, and some Adventure Land rides are temporarily closed. The listings cover only late November, so check the event page for your dates before you go. The restaurants publish year-round hours, but none mentions winter closures, so ring ahead on a quiet weekday.

## Getting there

The Land of Legends is in Kadriye, in the Belek resort area. Guests at the [Kingdom Hotel](/hotels/the-land-of-legends-kingdom-hotel/) are a short walk away, and many [Belek hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek) sit within a short taxi ride. Our [Serik district guide](/destinations/serik/) covers the area.

## FAQ

### Do I need a theme park ticket to eat at The Land of Legends restaurants?

The restaurants are on the Shopping Avenue, and the park's pages don't say a ticket is needed for it. They don't state an entry policy either, so confirm when you book.

### Which Land of Legends restaurant is best for families?

Piazzetta Italiana, with pizza and pasta and the longest opening hours (from 12:30). NEMO's aquarium setting is the other obvious pick.

### Are the evening shows free?

The park's guide says so, and the Musical Boat Parade is billed as free. The Canal Boat Tour is sold separately at the ticket booths.

### Is any restaurant at The Land of Legends Michelin-starred?

No. The MICHELIN Guide Türkiye does not cover Antalya. NEMO and Asil have been recognised by Gault&Millau instead.
