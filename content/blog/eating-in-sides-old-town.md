Side is a small peninsula town with a Roman harbour, a temple of Apollo and a street of restaurants running down to the sea. It is a pleasant day out from Antalya even in winter, but restaurant hours and menus change with the season, and several Side restaurants publish very little online. This guide covers three sea-view places in the old town, what their own websites say and what they leave out.

Side's sights are on our [Side page](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/), and the [Side day trip from Antalya](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/) gives a timed walk. Our [Aspendos and Side in one day](/blog/aspendos-and-side-in-one-day/) pairs it with the theatre.

## Zula Side: breakfast to dinner with a view

Zula sells itself on the view of the Mediterranean as much as the food. The website's menu, checked on 14 November 2026, runs from breakfast to dinner but shows **no prices and no opening hours**, so message or call 0533 191 07 77 to book and to check it is open.

What the menu lists:

- A **serpme kahvaltı**, the Turkish spread breakfast, with several local cheeses, olives, Afyon clotted cream, honeycomb, jams and village eggs.
- Starters such as sea bass tartare and wood-fired sardines; hot dishes such as octopus and spinach-and-chard fritters.
- Fish by the plate or for two, including a boneless sea bass and a mixed fish plate.
- Ottoman-style meat: *çökertme kebabı*, *beğendili köfte* and *testi kebabı*, baked in a clay jug, for two.
- Separate vegan and vegetarian sections.

[Zula profile](/restaurants/zula-side/).

## Side Orfoz: seafood by the harbour

Side Orfoz is on Nar Sokak near Liman Caddesi, in the historic harbour area. Its menu combines seafood with Turkish and international dishes: fish, seafood plates, pasta and meat. Note that this is the Side restaurant, not the Orfoz in Bodrum; any Michelin recognition belongs to the Bodrum venue, not to this one.

Reserve through the official contact, ask about terrace seating and confirm the current menu. Allow time to walk in, since cars cannot always reach the entrance. [Side Orfoz profile](/restaurants/side-orfoz-restaurant/).

## Karma Side: waterfront evenings

Karma is a waterfront restaurant in ancient Side. Gault & Millau's profile describes a seasonally prepared menu, vegetarian alternatives and an emphasis on locally sourced ingredients. Its lounge identity makes it more of an evening destination than a lunch stop, and Gault & Millau is not a Michelin distinction.

Confirm the restaurant reservation separately from any lounge or nightlife arrangement, and ask about seasonal hours. Our check could not fetch the official site reliably, so verify details with the venue. [Karma profile](/restaurants/karma-side/).

## Winter tips for Side

1. **Phone first.** Several Side restaurants close or shorten hours in winter, and none of the three sites above gives clear winter hours.
2. **Eat after the ruins.** See our [Side day trip](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/) for opening hours; the theatre has been closed to visitors.
3. **Dress for the sea breeze.** A terrace in January is cool.
4. **Combine with Manavgat.** The [Manavgat Waterfall](/things-to-do/manavgat-waterfall/) and [river boat trip](/blog/manavgat-river-boat-trip/) are nearby.
5. **Check the weather.** Our [winter guide](/blog/antalya-in-winter/) explains what stays open.

## FAQ

### Are Side restaurants open in winter?

Some are. The restaurants above publish no clear winter hours, so call before you go.

### Do the Side restaurants publish prices?

Zula's menu shows none. Ask before ordering.

### Is Side Orfoz connected to Bodrum's Orfoz?

We found no connection; treat any Bodrum awards as not applying to Side.
