Antalya's winter days are shorter than many visitors expect, and its summer days are long. If you plan ruins, hikes, boat trips or a Chimaera visit, knowing when it gets dark helps. This guide gives calculated sunrise, sunset and daylight length for Antalya city for each month from November 2026 to December 2027, and explains what the numbers mean for sightseeing.

## How we calculated these

We calculated the times with the open-source Astral library for Antalya's coordinates (about 36.90° N, 30.71° E) on the 1st and 15th of each month, using Türkiye's fixed UTC+3 time. These are astronomical sunrise and sunset at a flat horizon. Antalya has mountains to the west, so on Konyaaltı Beach the sun may drop behind the Bey Mountains earlier than the table shows, and the sky stays bright for a while after sunset. Treat the figures as accurate to a few minutes, and check a weather app for the day.

## What this means in winter

- **The shortest days are in mid-December**, with about 9 hours 40 minutes of daylight and sunset around 17:41. The latest sunrise, 08:10, falls on 1 January.
- **Sunset is earliest in early December**, around 17:40, before the latest sunrise in January. This is normal; the two do not coincide.
- **By mid-February** you gain more than an hour of daylight compared with December.
- **Plan ruins and mountain sites for the morning.** Mountain roads are best driven in daylight. See [winter day trips from Antalya](/blog/winter-day-trips-from-antalya/) and the [Saklıkent ski day trip](/blog/saklikent-ski-day-trip-from-antalya/).
- **The Chimaera** is best after dark, but the path is steep; our [Çıralı and Olympos guide](/blog/cirali-and-olympos-where-to-stay-eat-walk/) has tips.

## What this means in summer

From May to August the sun is up for 13 to 14.5 hours and sets after 19:45. Midsummer sunsets come at about 20:15, so evening events and beach dinners run late. The long days also mean strong sun through the afternoon; see [is Antalya too hot in summer?](/blog/is-antalya-too-hot-in-summer-compared-to-other-turkish-cities/).

## Best places to watch the sunset

Antalya faces south over a wide bay, with the Bey Mountains to the west. For an unobstructed view, try the cliff-top [Karaalioğlu Park](/things-to-do/karaalioglu-park/) or the harbour at [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/). [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/) has the mountains as a backdrop. We have not verified viewing conditions at specific cafés.

## Using these times

1. **Winter sightseeing:** start by 09:00 and plan to be back at your hotel by dusk.
2. **Photography:** the hour after sunrise and before sunset gives the softest light; check the day's weather first.
3. **Driving:** avoid unfamiliar mountain roads after dark.
4. **Turkey does not change its clocks**, so UTC+3 applies all year.

## FAQ

### What time is sunset in Antalya in December?

About 17:40 to 17:41 through December, according to our calculation.

### What time is sunrise in Antalya in January?

About 08:10 at the start of the month and 08:09 on 15 January.

### Does Turkey use daylight saving time?

No. Türkiye uses UTC+3 all year.
