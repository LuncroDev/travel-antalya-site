The Women's EHF EURO 2026 comes to Antalya in the first week of December, and it is the first time the tournament has been played in Türkiye. Antalya hosts one preliminary-round group, Group C, over three match days at the Antalya Sports Hall. This guide covers the dates, who is playing, what tickets cost according to the European Handball Federation (EHF), and how to fit a handball weekend into a winter break on the coast.

## The Women's EHF EURO 2026 in Antalya at a glance

The EHF says the tournament runs from **3 to 20 December 2026** and is the first European handball championship hosted by five countries: Poland, Romania, Czechia, Slovakia and Türkiye. Antalya's part is the preliminary round, **Group C**:

- **Türkiye** (the hosts, playing only their second EHF EURO)
- **Denmark** (2024 runners-up)
- **Spain**
- **Greece**

The EHF's host-city page says action in Antalya ends with Türkiye against Spain and Greece against Denmark. After that the tournament moves on to its main round in other host cities; the EHF's Antalya pages do not describe how Group C teams are routed there.

## Match days and times

The EHF lists three match days in Antalya, each with two games at 18:30 and 21:00 local time:

| Date | Games |
|---|---|
| Thursday 3 December | Türkiye – Greece; Denmark – Spain |
| Saturday 5 December | Spain – Greece; Denmark – Türkiye |
| Monday 7 December | Türkiye – Spain; Greece – Denmark |

Biletix, the ticket seller for Antalya, gives the running order for the first two days: Türkiye – Greece at 18:30 and Denmark – Spain at 21:00 on day one, then Spain – Greece at 18:30 and Denmark – Türkiye at 21:00 on day two. We could not confirm which game starts first on 7 December, so check the ticket page before you plan dinner. The EHF also reserves the right to change match order for television.

One time-zone note: the EHF points out that Antalya is two hours ahead of Central European Time during the tournament, so a 21:00 throw-off here is 19:00 in Copenhagen or Madrid.

## The venue: Antalya Sports Hall

The Antalya Sports Hall seats **10,000** and opened in 2016. It is in Muratpaşa, the central district. The EHF's venue page says it is six minutes from the city, 23 minutes from the airport and 11 minutes from the bus station. Those are the federation's figures; taxi times will vary with traffic, particularly on a weekday evening.

For a sense of where Muratpaşa sits, our [Muratpaşa guide](/destinations/muratpasa/) covers the old town and the city centre, and the hall is on the city side rather than out in the resort belt at Belek or Kemer.

## Tickets and prices

The EHF sells tickets through its official site, tickets.eurohandball.com/women2026. In its Antalya guide it lists:

| Ticket | Category 2 | Category 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Single day | 800 TRY | 1,500 TRY |
| Three-day pass | 2,000 TRY | 3,400 TRY |

Biletix also lists the Antalya sessions, one page per day and an all-days page covering 3–7 December. Prices can change and categories can sell out, so treat the table as a guide and read the current price on the ticket page. The EHF offers a ticket alert you can subscribe to. Bag checks and security screening are carried out at the entrance, according to the Biletix event rules.

## Planning a handball weekend in Antalya

Because the games start at 18:30, the days are free. Early December is mild by northern European standards, but it can be wet, so read [is December too cold in Antalya?](/blog/is-december-too-cold-in-antalya-weather-rain-sea-conditions/) before you pack, and our [Antalya in winter](/blog/antalya-in-winter/) guide for what stays open.

Easy daytime plans that fit around an evening match:

- A slow morning in [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/), the old town, with a walk down to the marina. Our [Kaleiçi walking route](/blog/kaleici-walking-route/) takes a couple of hours.
- [Karaalioğlu Park](/things-to-do/karaalioglu-park/) and the cliff-top view of the bay.
- A trip to the [Düden Waterfalls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/), which are a good winter outing when the weather is dry.
- A rainy-day backup: our [museums guide](/blog/antalya-museums-rainy-day/). Note that the Antalya Museum building is currently closed, so read that guide before you set out.

For where to sleep, city hotels in Muratpaşa put you closest. Browse [Antalya city hotels](/hotels/?area=Antalya%20city) or look at [Akra Antalya](/hotels/akra-hotel/) on the Lara coast road. The EHF does not name official team or fan hotels in the pages we read.

If you also want live music while you are here, the same week has plenty on; see our [December calendar](/blog/antalya-december-2026-week-by-week/).

## FAQ

### Which teams play in Antalya at the Women's EHF EURO 2026?

Group C: Türkiye, Denmark, Spain and Greece. Antalya hosts the preliminary round only.

### What are the dates?

Thursday 3, Saturday 5 and Monday 7 December 2026. Games are at 18:30 and 21:00 local time.

### How much do tickets cost?

The EHF lists single-day tickets at 800 TRY (category 2) and 1,500 TRY (category 1), and three-day passes at 2,000 TRY and 3,400 TRY. Check the ticket site for the current price.

### Where is the Antalya Sports Hall?

In Muratpaşa district. The EHF puts it six minutes from the city, 23 minutes from the airport and 11 minutes from the bus station.
