Antalya has a resident orchestra with a full winter season, and it is one of the easiest cultural evenings for visitors, because music needs no translation. The Antalya State Symphony Orchestra (ADSO) plays about 25 concerts this season, mostly on Friday evenings. This guide sets out what has been announced for 2026–27, based on a Hürriyet Daily News report from 13 October 2026, and what we could not confirm.

## The orchestra and the season

ADSO is affiliated with the Culture and Tourism Ministry's General Directorate of Fine Arts. According to Hürriyet Daily News, the 2026–27 season includes:

- **About 25 concerts** at the Atatürk Cultural Center (AKM), usually on **Fridays at 20:30**.
- A **season-ending concert on 28 May 2027 at the ancient Aspendos Theatre**, titled "Beethoven Now" and conducted by Aleksey Igudesman.
- An opening programme of Carl Orff's *Carmina Burana*.
- A **world premiere** of İlyas Mirzayev's Violin Concerto on 16 October. The report does not name the soloist.
- The orchestra's **first performance of the Nasreddin Hodja Suite**, and film music by Muammer Sun for the film *Kurtuluş*, performed after many years.

## Dates in the winter window

The report lists several special concerts. The ones that fall within the winter visiting season are:

| Date | Concert |
|---|---|
| 13 Nov | Atatürk Commemoration Concert |
| 18 Dec | İsmet İnönü Commemoration Concert |
| 25 Dec | New Year's Special Concert |
| 23 Apr 2027 | Antalya Children's Choir, third performance |
| 28 May 2027 | Season finale at Aspendos |

The orchestra also plays events for the Antalya Diplomacy Forum, the International Astronautical Congress and COP31, and several concerts in the Culture Road Festival, according to the same report. The report gives no dates for those.

## Getting the most from an orchestra night

1. **Book early.** Popular programmes sell out, per the orchestra's own director.
2. **Check the hall.** AKM has several halls, including the Aspendos Salonu; check which one your concert uses.
3. **Dress smart-casual.** Nothing formal is required for most concerts, but locals dress up a little.
4. **Combine it with dinner in [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/).** AKM is in the city, so a taxi is simple.
5. **Plan around the weather.** See [Antalya in winter](/blog/antalya-in-winter/).

## FAQ

### Where does the Antalya State Symphony Orchestra play?

Mostly at the Atatürk Cultural Center (AKM), usually on Fridays at 20:30. The season ends at the Aspendos Theatre on 28 May 2027.

### Is there a concert on 25 December?

Hürriyet Daily News lists a New Year's Special Concert on 25 December.
