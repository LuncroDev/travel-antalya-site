Antalya has a busy winter stage calendar, and almost all of the theatre and stand-up on it is in Turkish. If you visit without the language, you do not have to skip the evening out: ballet, jazz and orchestral concerts need no translation, and one theatre company plays in Russian with Turkish subtitles. This guide separates what you can follow from what you cannot, with real dates and venues from ticket sellers' pages.

## First, be realistic about the language

The big halls in Antalya, including the Türkan Şoray Cultural Center, the Atatürk Cultural Center (AKM) and the Konyaaltı Nazım Hikmet centre, sell Turkish-language plays and stand-up. None of the ticket pages we checked mentions English surtitles or translation. If a page does not say, assume the show is in Turkish.

Stand-up comedy is the hardest to enjoy without the language, because it depends on timing and local references. Plays are easier if you already know the story, and physical or musical shows are easiest.

## What works without Turkish

### Ballet: The Nutcracker, 28 November

The Türkiye Culture Portal lists *Fındıkkıran* (The Nutcracker), Tchaikovsky's ballet by Antalya State Opera and Ballet, on **28 November 2026 from 15:00 to 17:10** at Haşim İşcan Cultural Center. It is a two-act, 130-minute production suitable for audiences aged seven and over. Details are in our [event listing](/events/the-nutcracker-in-antalya/); check the official ticketing channel for availability.

### Jazz at The 251 Soul

Saxophonist Chris Potter plays [two nights on 27 and 28 November](/events/chris-potter-trio-2026-11-27-muratpasa/) at 21:00 at The 251 Soul in Akra Antalya. Guests must be 18 or older, seating is first come, first served, and you may share a table.

### Orchestral concerts

The Antalya State Symphony Orchestra plays about 25 concerts this season at AKM, usually on Fridays at 20:30, including a New Year's Special Concert on 25 December according to Hürriyet Daily News. See our guide to the [2026–27 season](/blog/antalya-state-symphony-orchestra-2026-27/).

### Russian-language theatre in Kemer, with Turkish subtitles

The company Otrazheniya ("Reflections") plays two Russian-language pieces at Kemer Belediyesi Kültür Salonu in December. Both are performed in Russian with Turkish subtitles, so they suit Russian speakers more than English speakers:

- [*Klara, Money and Love*](/events/klara-dengi-i-lyubov-2026-12-02-kemer/) on **2 December at 19:30**, a 90-minute memory play for ages 8 and up. iTicket listed one category at 600 TL including the service fee on 17 November.
- [*Rumba*](/events/rumba-2026-12-12-kemer/) on **12 December at 19:00**, an 80-minute drama, age limit 10 and over, 600 TL on 18 November.

Tickets for both are non-refundable. Our [Kemer guide](/destinations/kemer/) covers the town, and [Kemer hotels](/hotels/?area=Kemer) are close to the hall.

## Turkish-language shows worth knowing about

If you read Turkish, or are happy to watch a play whose story you know, the winter list includes:

- *Hamlet* at the Nazım Hikmet centre on [24 December at 20:00](/events/hamlet-2026-12-24-konyaalti/), with Erselcan Tokdemir.
- *Hastalık Hastası*, a Turkish take on Molière's *The Imaginary Invalid*, on [9 January at 20:00](/events/hastalik-hastasi-2027-01-09-konyaalti/), about 70 minutes per Biletinial.
- *Shirley Valentine* at Türkan Şoray on [14 December at 20:30](/events/shirley-valentine-2026-12-14-muratpasa/).

Browse the full list under [theatre in Antalya](/events/category/theatre/) and [comedy](/events/category/comedy/).

## How to book

1. **Use the ticket site named on the event page.** Biletix, Biletinial and iTicket all offer English versions of their pages.
2. **Check the start time again the week before.** Some pages show different times for the same show; where they do, we note it in the listing.
3. **Read the refund rules.** Several sellers say tickets not used on the day and time are void and cannot be refunded or exchanged.
4. **Arrive early.** Some pages ask you to arrive 30 minutes before the start, and may not admit latecomers.
5. **Bring ID** for discounted or age-limited tickets.

## FAQ

### Is there English-language theatre in Antalya?

We have not found any in the winter listings we checked. The shows that work best for visitors are ballet, jazz and orchestral concerts.

### Do the Russian-language plays have English subtitles?

The listings mention Turkish subtitles only.

### Where is the Nutcracker performed?

At Haşim İşcan Cultural Center in Muratpaşa, on 28 November 2026, 15:00 to 17:10, according to the Türkiye Culture Portal.
