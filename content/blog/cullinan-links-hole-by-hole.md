This Cullinan Links Golf Club hole-by-hole guide covers all 36 holes of the Aspendos and Olympos courses in Belek. European Golf Design laid out the club beside the Beşgöz River and the sea, and its fact sheet says it was built in 2021. **Aspendos is a par 72 and Olympos a par 71.** The club also lists a floodlit third course, Termessos, made from their two back nines. We read the club's hole pages, fact sheet, local rules and green-fee terms on 24 November 2026.

One warning: the hole pages give par and four length figures per hole, and nothing else. There is no stroke index, no unit and no hole notes. So the notes below are our own reading of the numbers, plus the course-wide advice the club publishes.

For photos and the club overview, see our [Cullinan Links Golf Club profile](/golf/cullinan-links-golf-club/). Earlier guides in this series cover [Carya](/blog/carya-golf-club-hole-by-hole/) and [Montgomerie Maxx Royal](/blog/montgomerie-maxx-royal-hole-by-hole/), and [Belek golf courses compared](/blog/belek-golf-courses-compared/) sets every club side by side.

## The courses at a glance

The general information page and the fact sheet give these figures:

- **110 hectares** for 36 holes. The home page calls Cullinan the only golf course in Turkey beside both a river and the sea; that is the club's own claim.
- **119 bunkers** (42 fairway, 77 greenside) and **10 lakes**, plus the river. The fact sheet calls a tee shot over the river its signature, without naming the hole.
- **Greens** of about 600 m² on average.
- **Wind:** from the north, off the Taurus Mountains, in the morning; from the south, off the sea, in the afternoon. Expect a hole to play differently after lunch.

### Which tee is which

Each hole page prints four unlabelled figures, and the second and third are identical on every hole. The local rules name three tees: **white** (championship), **yellow** (men) and **red** (ladies). The Turkish Golf Federation's chart for the club rates yellow twice, for men and for women. So we read the figures as white, yellow, yellow and red, and show three columns. The club doesn't spell this out, so check the card on the day.

The pages print **no unit**. The local rules give the 100, 150 and 200 marker stakes in metres, but we show the hole figures exactly as printed.

The federation's chart gives these figures, course rating first and slope second:

| Aspendos (par 72) | 70.5 / 130 | 68.1 / 118 | 73.5 / 129 | 70.2 / 124 |

Under the World Handicap System, 113 is the slope of a course of standard difficulty. Aspendos is rated the stiffer test from every tee. For women, the yellow tee is rated above par on all three courses. The chart carries no date, and the "Slope Rates" file on the Documents page would not open for us, so ask the starter which figures apply before you post a score.

## Termessos: the floodlit third course

The general information page lists three 18-hole courses: Olympos, Aspendos and Termessos. The fact sheet explains the third: Termessos is the floodlit 18, made from the back nine of Aspendos followed by the back nine of Olympos. The Termessos hole page matches that figure for figure. So there are 36 holes on the ground, as the home page says, and three routings on the card.

- **Under the lights you play both back nines:** first the Aspendos stretch with its longest hole and Cullinan's longest par 4, then the Olympos run of short par 4s and three par 3s.
- **Floodlit bookings need at least eight golfers**, who must check in an hour before. The free food-and-drink menu runs until 18:00.
- **Rate:** from 1 December 2026 to 11 February 2027, 18 floodlit holes cost €170 rack (€160 for Cullinan and Titanic guests), against €140 by day.

## Reading the card

- **Aspendos is a par-4 course.** Seven of its ten par 4s are 340 or longer from the white tee.
- **Olympos leans on short and long holes.** Eleven of its 18 are par 3s or par 5s, and five of its seven par 4s are under 330 from the white tee.
- **Red-tee players gain most on Olympos.** The 5th and 13th drop 78 and 77 from yellow to red; nowhere else does that step exceed 55.
- **White to yellow is a small step**, under 40 on every hole except Aspendos 10.

## Practical booking notes

- **Handicap:** a certificate is required; the maximum is 36 for men and 44 for ladies. See our [handicap limits and dress codes guide](/blog/belek-golf-handicap-limits-and-dress-codes/).
- **Shoes and dress:** soft spikes only. The local rules ask for proper dress but don't define it.
- **Buggies:** a shared buggy, one per two players, is compulsory and included in the green fee. The terms put a single buggy at €40 a round, but the rate table shows €30 from 1 December 2026 to 11 February 2027. The green-fee terms bar under-18s from driving, while the local rules say under-16s; plan on 18. See [golf buggy hire in Belek](/blog/golf-buggy-hire-in-belek/).
- **Green fees:** 18 holes cost €220 rack until 30 November 2026, then €140 to 11 February 2027 (€205 and €130 for Cullinan and Titanic guests), with one range token per player per day. Compare clubs in [Belek green fees for winter 2026–27](/blog/belek-green-fees-winter-2026-27/).
- **Food and drink:** all-inclusive with the green fee, except from 16 June to 31 August.
- **Hours:** open all year, 08:00–18:00 in winter and 07:00–19:00 in summer.
- **Maintenance:** in 2026, summer maintenance ran from 16 June to 31 August and overseeding to 9 October, with one 18-hole course open in rotation and no promise which. No 2027 dates yet on the pages we read; see [Belek golf course maintenance 2027](/blog/belek-golf-course-maintenance-2027/).
- **Booking:** the online form asks for 9 or 18 holes but not the course, so name Aspendos, Olympos or a floodlit Termessos round in the comments.
- **Getting there:** Kadriye, Üçkum Tepesi Caddesi 4/A, Serik; +90 242 505 05 00. The fact sheet gives 26 km from Antalya Airport; the home page says about 20 minutes. See [Antalya Airport to Belek golf courses](/blog/antalya-airport-to-belek-golf-courses/).

## FAQ

### How long are the Cullinan Links courses?

By our sums, Aspendos is 5,807 from white, 5,332 from yellow and 4,808 from red (par 72). Olympos is 5,407, 5,032 and 4,421 (par 71). No unit is given.

### Is Termessos a separate course?

Not on the ground. It is the floodlit 18 formed by the back nines of Aspendos and Olympos, par 71, with its own federation rating.

### Which course is harder, Aspendos or Olympos?

Aspendos, on the federation's chart: a slope of 130 from the men's white tee against 122 on Olympos, and higher ratings from every tee.

### Does Cullinan publish a stroke index?

Not on its hole pages, and the scorecard PDF on its Documents page would not open for us. Ask for a printed card at golf reception.
