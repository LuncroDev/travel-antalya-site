Golf buggy hire in Belek can add as much to a round as the gap between two green fees. On 14 November 2026 we opened the official pages of all eleven clubs in our directory and noted what each charges for a golf car, a trolley, a set of hire clubs and range balls. Five clubs publish some or all of these prices. Six publish none. All prices below are **published rates in euros, checked 14 November 2026; confirm when you book**, because every club keeps the right to change them.

This guide covers the extras. For the round itself, read [Belek green fees for winter 2026–27](/blog/belek-green-fees-winter-2026-27/). Travelling with teenagers? Buggy age limits and junior rates are in [junior golf in Belek](/blog/junior-golf-in-belek/).

Unlike green fees, these prices don't change with the season. ## Golf cars: €45 to €70 a round

### Carya and National: a €70 "reserve" car

Carya and National belong to the same Regnum group and print the same equipment table. A golf car costs €60 for 18 holes or €40 for nine. A third line, "reserve golf car 18 holes", costs €70; Carya's rental page calls it "Buggy Reserv". Neither club explains the difference. National says every buggy reservation must be made in advance through the reservation office, and both clubs refuse same-day cancellation of a reserved buggy. Ask what the extra €10 buys. Cars, trolleys, hire clubs and range tokens are hired only at the golf club reception.

### Cornelia and Sueno: the lowest published prices

Cornelia charges €50 for 18 holes and €25 for nine. Its "Prices" and "Greenfee" pages show identical figures, and everything is hired directly at the golf club.

Sueno's pro shop page lists a buggy at €45, the lowest figure we found. It doesn't say whether that covers 9 or 18 holes, or whether it is per car or per player. The club's 2025 factsheets count 110 buggies and say free shuttle buggies run between the Sueno hotels and the clubhouse. Pro-led groups can pre-book up to 10 buggies.

### Cullinan Links: buggy included, supplement disputed

Cullinan's green fee includes one buggy for every two players, and sharing is compulsory. A single buggy, or a non-golfer or caddie coming along, costs a supplement, and here the page contradicts itself. The table for 1 December 2026 to 11 February 2027 shows €30. The terms underneath say single use costs €40 a round (€50 from 10 September 2027), paid by credit card at reception. The autumn and spring tables show €40, which matches the terms. Get the winter figure in writing. The club runs 150 buggies and charges repair costs to the player the buggy is booked to.

### Per car or per player?

No club says. Two players sharing at National might pay €60 between them, or €60 each. Ask before you book.

## Buggy rules that change your day

- **Paths.** National wants buggies on the paths at all times, especially when wet, and never past the yellow ropes about 40 m short of each green. Sueno asks for carts on the paths near tees and greens.
- **Path-only weeks.** National limits buggies to the paths from 25 September to 4 October 2027, and Carya from 1 to 10 October 2027. Cullinan restricts buggies to paths during maintenance and after overseeding.
- **Driving age.** Cullinan sets 18 and Sueno 16. National's price notes and "About" page say 18, but the etiquette section on its course page still says 16. Plan on 18.
- **Cancellation.** Carya and National won't cancel a reserved buggy on the day of play.

## Trolleys and electric trolleys

Walking is the cheapest way round:

- **Cornelia:** €7 for a trolley and €25 for an electric one, for 9 or 18 holes.
- **Sueno:** €8 for a trolley. Its factsheets list 300 trolleys and 10 electric trolleys, but no electric-trolley price.
- **National:** €12, or €25 electric.
- **Carya:** €12 and €25 on the green-fee page, but €10 and €20 on both its equipment and rental-club pages.

Carya and National also sell an all-day deal of unlimited range balls plus a trolley: €60, or €40 for Regnum guests. Montgomerie Maxx Royal and Kaya Palazzo list trolleys for hire without prices. ROBINSON says its green-fee packages include no trolley or range token.

## Club hire: prices, brands and weekly sets

You don't need to fly with clubs. Carya, Cornelia, Cullinan, ROBINSON and Sueno all list left-handed sets:

- **Carya:** TaylorMade sets, which it says are fitted with the Stealth 2 range and renewed every two years, for men and ladies, with a choice of shafts. The green-fee page charges €40 a day and €110 a week; the equipment page says €30 and €90. Its equipment table has an "Under 16" column with the same prices, so juniors pay in full.
- **National:** €40 a day, €110 a week.
- **Cornelia:** €40 a full set. It lists 12 full sets of TaylorMade Qi10, for men, women and children. No weekly rate.
- **Sueno:** €35 a day, €140 a week, so the weekly rate is exactly four days' hire. Ladies', left-handed and junior sets are available on request.
- **Cullinan:** left- and right-handed sets at the pro shop; no price published.
- **ROBINSON Nobilis:** half sets, full sets and children's clubs, booked at the Golf Desk; no price published.
- **Montgomerie Maxx Royal, Gloria and Kaya Palazzo:** all say they rent clubs, without prices.

## Driving range costs

Range tokens cost €4 to €6, but only Cornelia says how many balls one holds:

- **Cornelia:** €4 for 30 balls.
- **Sueno:** €4 a token. Its 300 × 120 m range takes 50 players on grass and 40 on mats.
- **Cullinan:** €5 a token or €25 for unlimited balls, plus a €20 range fee. Cullinan Belek and Titanic hotel guests use the range free, and the green fee includes one token per player per day. The card doesn't say how the fee and the tokens combine.
- **Carya and National:** €6 a token, or €50 for unlimited balls (Regnum guests €30). National's range is 300 m long and 110 m wide, with 12 indoor and 18 outdoor bays plus a grass area for 50 players. Carya's is a two-way range of 340 by 80 m.
- **Gloria:** no ball prices for its 48 covered TrackMan bays, though lessons include range balls. It hires out its TrackMan Performance Studio for €40 and its PuttView room for €60, per 50 minutes (no season stated).
- **Montgomerie Maxx Royal** (25 hitting bays) and **Kaya Palazzo** (280 × 80 m): no prices.

## Discounts on buggies

- **Gloria:** the Glorian Member Club gives 25% off buggy rental at Diamond level and 50% at Platinum.
- **Montgomerie Maxx Royal:** the resort's 2025 Royalty Card leaflet lists 5% to 15% off buggy rental, depending on card tier, and only while you are staying at the resort.

## Shoes, lockers and caddies

- **Shoes:** no club publishes shoe hire, and National, Cornelia, Kaya Palazzo and Sueno all require soft spikes. Pack your own.
- **Lockers and bags:** Sueno's factsheet lists free locker rooms with showers and a free bag storage room. Cornelia lists bag storage without a price. The others don't say.
- **Caddies:** Sueno lists a caddie service without a price. Cullinan lets caddies walk with players for the supplement on its card.

## The six clubs with no published prices

Gloria, Kaya Palazzo, Montgomerie Maxx Royal and ROBINSON Nobilis all say they rent equipment, but print no prices. Antalya Golf Club shows its rates only as two images on its green-fee page, which we could not open, and its pro shop page carries brand logos and nothing on rentals. Lykia Links publishes nothing on equipment; read the closure warning on our profile before planning a winter round there. Ask each club for a written quote with the tee time.

## Booking checklist

1. **Ask whether the golf-car price is per car or per player.** No club says.
2. **Book the buggy with the tee time.** Carya and National won't cancel a reserved buggy on the day.
3. **Confirm the disputed prices.** Carya prints two sets of trolley and hire-club prices, and Cullinan two winter buggy supplements (€30 and €40).
4. **Pack golf shoes with soft spikes.** Nobody publishes shoe hire.
5. **Check late-September and early-October dates** for path-only rules.
6. **Arrive early.** Carya, National and Cornelia hire everything only at the club.

## FAQ

### How much does golf buggy hire cost in Belek?

Published rates checked on 14 November 2026: €45 at Sueno, and for 18 holes €50 at Cornelia and €60 at Carya and National (€70 for a "reserve" car). Cullinan includes a shared buggy in its green fee. Confirm when you book.

### Is a buggy included in the green fee in Belek?

Of the clubs that publish prices, only Cullinan Links includes one, shared between two players. Carya, National, Cornelia and Sueno charge for a golf car separately.

### Can you hire golf clubs in Belek?

Yes. Published prices run from €35 a day at Sueno to €40 at Carya, National and Cornelia, although one Carya page says €30. Weekly sets cost €110 at National and €140 at Sueno.

### How much are range balls in Belek?

Tokens cost €4 at Cornelia (30 balls) and Sueno, €5 at Cullinan and €6 at Carya and National. Unlimited balls cost €25 at Cullinan and €50 at Carya and National, or €30 for Regnum guests.
