Winter is the best time for day trips from Antalya. The ruins are quiet, the light is clear and the temperatures suit walking. The coast road may be wet on some days, and opening hours are shorter than in summer, so it pays to pick a trip that matches the weather. This guide sets out six easy trips and links to the full plan for each. Hours and fees change, so check the plan on the day.

## 1. Aspendos and Side

The classic pair. Our [Aspendos and Side guide](/blog/aspendos-and-side-in-one-day/) explains why Aspendos goes first in winter, gives opening hours and ticket prices (€15 and €5 at the time we wrote it), and ends with sunset at Side. Read [Aspendos beyond the theatre](/blog/aspendos-beyond-the-theatre/) if you want a longer look. You can also do Side on its own by bus; see [Side day trip from Antalya](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/), which notes the winter hours and the closed theatre.

Pages: [Aspendos](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/), [Side](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/), [Manavgat Waterfall](/things-to-do/manavgat-waterfall/).

## 2. Termessos and Karain Cave

A half-day trip to a mountain city and a cave. Our [Termessos and Karain Cave guide](/blog/termessos-and-karain-cave-half-day-trip/) gives winter hours, fees and the right order to visit. Termessos involves climbing, so wear proper shoes and expect it to be cooler up there. Pages: [Termessos](/things-to-do/termessos/), [Karain Cave](/things-to-do/karain-cave/).

## 3. Finike, Limyra and Arykanda

A full day west of Antalya that pairs Lycian rock tombs with hillside terraces and a harbour walk. In winter you can add Finike's oranges at their best; see [Finike oranges and Antalya's winter citrus](/blog/finike-oranges-antalya-winter-citrus/). Plan: [Finike, Limyra and Arykanda in one day](/blog/finike-limyra-arykanda-one-day/). Pages: [Limyra](/things-to-do/limyra-ancient-city/), [Arykanda](/things-to-do/arykanda-ancient-city/).

## 4. Kemer to Olympos

The coast road from Kemer to Olympos takes in the Tahtalı cable car, Phaselis, the Olympos ruins at Çıralı and the Chimaera flames at dusk. Our [Kemer to Olympos guide](/blog/kemer-to-olympos-coast-road/) has winter hours and fees. The cable car, which climbs to about 2,365 m, is weather-dependent, so check it on the day. Pages: [Tahtalı cable car](/things-to-do/tahtali-olympos-cable-car/), [Phaselis](/things-to-do/phaselis-ancient-city/), [Olympos and the Chimaera](/things-to-do/olympos-and-the-chimaera-flames/).

## 5. Alanya

Alanya is further east, so make it an early start. [Alanya in a day](/blog/alanya-in-a-day/) covers the castle by cable car, the museum, Cleopatra Beach and Damlataş Cave, then the Red Tower and harbour. Pages: [Alanya Castle](/things-to-do/alanya-castle/), [Cleopatra Beach](/things-to-do/cleopatra-beach/), [Alanya Red Tower](/things-to-do/alanya-red-tower-kizilkule/).

## 6. Saklıkent: a day on snow (if the season has opened)

The Ministry of Culture and Tourism says Saklıkent is 50 km west of the city centre, about an hour away, with ski areas at 2,000–2,400 m and a short season. Ski directories give the usual season as mid December to early April, but it depends on snowfall. See the [Saklıkent day-trip guide](/blog/saklikent-ski-day-trip-from-antalya/) before you go, and bring snow chains if you drive.

## A longer one: Antalya to Kaş

If you have a full day and an early start, [Antalya to Kaş](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) is a 187 km coast road with stops at Myra, St Nicholas Church, Kekova and Kaputaş. It is a long day; many people stay overnight.

## Tips for winter day trips

1. **Start early.** Winter days are shorter and ruins close earlier than in summer.
2. **Check the forecast and the road.** Mountain roads can be icy even when the city is sunny.
3. **Carry layers and a rain jacket.** Our [what to wear guide](/blog/what-to-wear-in-antalya-in-different-months-weather-based-guide/) covers the months.
4. **Check opening hours the day before.** Museum and site hours change by season.
5. **Consider a driver.** [Antalya airport transfers](/blog/antalya-airport-transfers-belek-side-kemer-kas/) explains transport options; many hotels also arrange day trips.

## FAQ

### What is the best day trip from Antalya in winter?

Aspendos and Side is the easiest, and Termessos is the most dramatic. For snow, try Saklıkent, but check conditions.

### Are the ancient sites open in winter?

Yes, though hours are shorter. Each guide above lists winter hours when we checked.

### Can I do a day trip without a car?

Some, such as Side, work by bus. For others a tour, a hired driver or a rental car is easier.
