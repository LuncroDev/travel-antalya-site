Antalya hotels with heated pools are what make a winter beach holiday work. From December to March the sea is cool and the evenings are cold, but a warm pool lets you swim every day. The catch is that "heated pool" means different things on different hotel websites: an indoor spa pool, an outdoor pool kept at a set temperature, or a children's aquapark that's warmed for some months only. On 19 November 2026 we opened the official pages of 13 hotels across Belek, Lara, Kemer and the city and noted exactly what each one says. Where a page gives no dates, temperature or hours, we say so.

## Why a heated pool matters in winter

The Turkish State Meteorological Service (MGM) gives Antalya's long-term average temperatures, measured from 1930 to 2025, as 11.7°C in December, 10.1°C in January, 10.7°C in February and 12.9°C in March. Average night-time lows sit between 6°C and 8°C. The sea holds its warmth longer: MGM's reading at Konyaaltı on 18 November 2026 was 21.7°C, with 22.7°C at Kemer and 22.3°C at Alanya, but that is a single snapshot and the water cools through the winter. Our [winter swimming guide](/blog/can-you-swim-in-antalya-in-winter-weather-sea-temperature-guide/) explains what to expect in the sea; this guide is about the pools.

## Belek: the biggest choice of heated pools

Belek's golf resorts stay busy in winter, and several of them now treat a warm pool as part of the winter offer. Browse all [Belek hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek) for more.

### Regnum Carya

Regnum Carya's "Winter Concept 2026–2027" factsheet runs from 1 November to 31 March and lists four heated pools: the outdoor main pool (1,890 m², 140 cm deep), an outdoor wellbeing pool (130 m²), an indoor wellbeing pool (305 m², with a lift chair for guests with limited mobility) and a 10 m² indoor children's pool. The factsheet says the heating runs "depending on weather conditions" and that the hotel may close some pools when occupancy is low. Villa private pools can be heated for an extra charge. A separate, undated Regnum news page names more heated pools, including an activity pool and an aquapark kids' pool; we'd rely on the dated factsheet.

### Ela Excellence Resort

Ela publishes the most precise list. Its heated pools are a semi-Olympic outdoor pool (865 m², 140 cm), an outdoor children's aquapark pool (177 m², 40 cm), an indoor pool (216 m², 140 cm) and a small indoor children's pool (17 m², 30 cm). A glass passage lets you swim from the indoor pool to the outdoor one. Ela says the heated pools are available through the winter season but that usage days may vary with the weather and occupancy. The site carries a © 2026 footer; no water temperature is given.

### Sueno Hotels Deluxe

Sueno Deluxe's beach and pool page describes a 323 m² heated indoor pool in the spa, linked to a heated outdoor pool just outside, and a 30 m² private heated children's pool. It gives the heating dates as 1 November to 1 April. No temperature or hours are printed.

### Gloria Serenity Resort

Gloria announced a new 500 m² heated outdoor pool at Serenity, kept at 28°C between October and April. The news item isn't dated, so it's unclear which April the pool opened. Serenity's spa also has an indoor pool that Gloria says is open all year, without saying it is heated.

### Xanadu and Kempinski The Dome

Xanadu lists a heated indoor pool and a heated outdoor pool among the facilities of its Shang-Du Spa, and says younger guests are admitted to the spa only at set hours. It gives no dates or temperatures. Kempinski describes a "temperature-controlled" indoor pool designed as a year-round retreat, open 08:00–20:00; it doesn't say "heated" or give a temperature.

## Lara and Kundu

These hotels are closest to the airport and the city. See [Lara & Kundu hotels](/hotels/?area=Lara%20%26%20Kundu) for the full list.

### Liberty Lara

Liberty Lara is the clearest on dates. Its indoor pool, for adults and children, is heated and open from 1 November to 1 April. The Apollon pool, 140 cm deep with a jacuzzi and for guests aged 16 and over, is heated in November and March. The hotel adds that operating hours and days may change with the weather. The page doesn't say the 2,000 m² main pool is heated.

### Titanic Deluxe Lara

Titanic's pool page lists an indoor pool, an indoor children's pool, a semi-Olympic pool and a children's slide pool as heated, and says the heated pools run through the winter season, November to April. The children's aquapark is heated from 1 November to 30 April. The adult aquapark, for children over six, closes from November to March. Note that a Titanic offer page says the aquapark runs year-round, which conflicts with the pool page; ask which slides are open on your dates.

## Antalya city and Konyaaltı

City hotels suit a winter break built around [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/), museums and day trips rather than a resort stay.

- **Akra:** six pools in total, with an indoor pool on a "special heated system" connected to the outdoor area. Akra says its pools are heated in winter to 28°C. The same page says the beach, outdoor pool and their food outlets operate from April to November, so in mid-winter expect to swim indoors.
- **Crowne Plaza Antalya:** IHG lists a heated indoor pool open 09:00 to midnight with a lifeguard on duty, at no charge. The outdoor pool runs 08:00–20:00, with no heating mentioned.
- **DoubleTree by Hilton Antalya City Centre:** Hilton mentions indoor and rooftop pools but doesn't say either is heated, so don't count on warm water.

## Kemer

- **DoubleTree by Hilton Antalya-Kemer:** Hilton describes a heated freshwater indoor pool, plus a sauna and steam room. Its page gives no hours and doesn't confirm winter opening dates, so check the hotel is open for your stay.
- **Fame Residence Kemer & Spa:** the "2026–2027 Winter" factsheet confirms the hotel is open from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027 and lists the Turkish bath, sauna and indoor pool among the free activities. It doesn't say the pool is heated.

## What to check before you book

Hotel pages describe pools in general terms. Before you pay, ask the hotel in writing:

1. **Which pools are heated on your dates?** Several hotels heat pools only "depending on weather conditions" or close some when occupancy is low.
2. **What temperature?** Only Akra and Gloria Serenity publish one (28°C).
3. **What hours?** Only Crowne Plaza and Kempinski print them.
4. **Is there an age rule?** Liberty's Apollon pool is 16+, and Xanadu admits children to the spa only at set hours.
5. **Is the indoor pool in the spa, and is it free?** Fame Kemer lists its indoor pool as free; most others don't say.
6. **Is the hotel open?** Many resorts close in winter. Of the hotels here, Regnum Carya and Fame Kemer publish 2026–27 winter dates.

## FAQ

### Are outdoor pools in Antalya heated in winter?

Some are. On the pages we read, Regnum Carya, Ela Excellence, Sueno Deluxe, Gloria Serenity, Xanadu and Titanic Deluxe Lara all mention heated outdoor pools, and Akra says its pools are heated in winter. Most add that heating depends on the weather.

### Which Antalya hotel publishes its pool temperature?

Of the 13 we checked, only two: Akra and Gloria Serenity, both 28°C.

### Can I swim in the sea instead?

Hardy swimmers do. MGM measured 21.7°C at Konyaaltı on 18 November 2026, but the sea cools through December and January. See our [winter swimming guide](/blog/can-you-swim-in-antalya-in-winter-weather-sea-temperature-guide/).

### Do city hotels have heated pools?

Yes: Crowne Plaza in Konyaaltı lists a heated indoor pool open until midnight, and Akra heats its indoor pool. DoubleTree City Centre has an indoor pool but doesn't say it is heated.
