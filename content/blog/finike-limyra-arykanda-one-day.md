Finike makes a practical base for seeing two very different Lycian sites in one day. At Limyra, look for the tombs cut into rock and the remains of a settlement spread across the foothills and plain. At Arykanda, the ruins climb in terraces above a valley. Start inland while you have daylight, then return toward the coast. This is a self-drive suggestion, not a bus itinerary or a guided tour.

## Morning: Arykanda's hillside terraces

Head from Finike towards Elmalı and look for signs to the site near Arif. [Arykanda](/things-to-do/arykanda-ancient-city/) sits below a steep cliff rather than on a flat town grid. Start with the bath and gymnasium remains on the lower terrace before deciding whether to continue up to the theatre and stadium. Take the slope seriously: allow more time if you're stopping for photographs or if stone surfaces are wet. If your group has mobility needs, check access directly rather than assuming a continuous step-free route.

## Lunch in Finike, then Limyra

Return towards the coast for lunch and head to [Limyra](/things-to-do/limyra-ancient-city/) afterwards. Search for the archaeological site by name: the provincial culture directorate puts it near Saklısu, while the English visitor portal's address field uses Arif, which is also the village listed for Arykanda. Follow local signs rather than selecting a village name alone. The road separates parts of the ancient site. Start with the signed visitor area, then decide whether you have enough daylight to look for the rock-cut tombs. The cover photograph was taken at Limyra, not at Arykanda.

## Winter hours and admission

The Turkish Museums listings show **08:00–17:00** for both sites between **1 November and 30 April**, with ticket desks closing at **16:30**. [Arykanda](https://www.turkishmuseums.com/museum/detail/1962-antalya-arykanda-archaeological-site/1962/4) lists free admission and [Limyra](https://turkishmuseums.com/museum/detail/1965-antalya-the-archaeological-site-of-limyra/1965/4) lists **€3 for foreign adults**. These are published terms, not live ticket availability. Check the site notices for your travel day and allow a buffer rather than timing the second visit to the last admission.

## If you only have time for one stop

Choose Limyra if you're already near Finike and mostly want to see Lycian tombs; choose Arykanda if you have time to drive inland and want a longer walk among the terraces. Either way, leave the [Finike waterfront](/destinations/finike/) for the end rather than letting a long lunch squeeze out the last ruins stop.
