Kalkan is a small town with a big dining reputation, and most of its evenings happen within a few minutes' walk of the harbour. This guide to where to eat in Kalkan focuses on three restaurants that publish their full 2026 menus with prices — a harbour meyhane and the two Korsan restaurants above the town beach — so you can see what dinner costs before you sit down. We read each restaurant's own website and menus on 28 November 2026. Prices are in Turkish lira and change often, so treat them as a guide.

## Pruva Meyhane: the harbour meyhane

**Pruva** calls itself a seafront meyhane at Kalkan Harbour, and its address is İskele Sokak No. 13. It opens **every day, 11:00–01:00**, according to its website, and brings in live **fasıl** music, the Turkish tavern style of singing and instruments that goes with rakı and meze. The site mentions sunset views from the harbour front.

The dinner menu prices a meyhane night clearly. A selection from the website on 28 November 2026:

- Fried calamari ₺750
- Grilled octopus ₺1,150

- Sea bass with prawns ₺1,350
- Grilled chicken ₺950
- Lamb chops ₺1,700

Drinks are on a separate menu, which we didn't open. Booking is by phone or WhatsApp on **+90 535 458 55 44**. There's more detail on our [Pruva Meyhane page](/restaurants/pruva-meyhane-kalkan/).

## Korsan Meze: Kalkan's veteran

**Korsan Meze** says it was established in **1979** and was one of Kalkan's first restaurants. It overlooks the beach, and its menu is built for sharing: "all meze and small plates are designed to be ordered for the table". The day's meze come from a daily list rather than the printed menu, so ask what is on.

## Korsan Fish Terrace: the rooftop

Upstairs from Korsan Meze, the **Fish Terrace** is a rooftop with white sofas and a view over the bay and the town beach; the restaurant's own line is that "there is no other terrace between us and the sea". Its dinner menu gives the address as Kalkan Mahallesi, Atatürk Caddesi No. 17/B. Music plays every evening, with live guitar and vocals by a musician named Ersoy on **Thursdays**.

The kitchen promises fish "always fresh and always served off the bone". From the 2026 dinner menu:

## Which one to choose

- **For a long, loud, sociable night:** Pruva. Meze, rakı and live fasıl on the harbour, and it stays open until 01:00.
- **For a relaxed family dinner:** Korsan Meze. Sharing plates suit groups and children who want to try a bit of everything, and the grills cover non-fish eaters.
- **For a special-occasion seafood dinner:** the Korsan Fish Terrace. Book a table at the rooftop's edge and go on a Thursday if you want live music.
- **For a light lunch:** Pruva opens from 11:00. Korsan Meze also links a lunch menu, but it is dated 2025, so check it is serving lunch on your dates.

## How a meze dinner works

Whichever you choose, the rhythm is similar. Start with a few cold meze for the table, then add one or two hot starters such as calamari or prawns. Order mains only once you see how hungry you still are; many people never get that far. Fish is usually grilled simply, with salad on the side, and dessert is often fruit or tahini halva. Take your time: a meze dinner in Kalkan is an evening, not a meal.

## Tips for eating out in Kalkan

- **Ask about whole fish before it's cooked.** Market-price fish is sold by weight; ask the weight and the price first.
- **Check the service line.** Korsan's 2026 menus say no service charge is added. Pruva's website doesn't mention one either way.
- **Book in season.** All three take reservations, and the harbour and rooftop tables go first.
- **Winter.** None of the three websites gives winter hours or closing dates. Kalkan is much quieter out of season, so phone before you walk down.

## FAQ

### Do Kalkan restaurants add a service charge?

Korsan Meze and Korsan Fish Terrace say on their 2026 menus that they no longer add one. Pruva's website doesn't say. Check the bill.

### Is Kalkan expensive to eat out?

At the restaurants above, main courses mostly run from about ₺950 to ₺1,900 on the 2026 menus, and meze from about ₺350. Wine and rakı are on separate lists.

### Which Kalkan restaurant has live music?

Pruva has live fasıl; the Korsan Fish Terrace has music every evening and live guitar on Thursdays.

### Are Kalkan restaurants open in winter?

None of these restaurants publishes winter hours. Call ahead between November and March.
