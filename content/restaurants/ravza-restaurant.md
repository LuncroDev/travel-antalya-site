Ravza began as a small three-table business in 1955 and grew into a multi-floor restaurant in central Alanya. Its official menu covers Turkish meat dishes, salads, starters and regional specialities.

## Food and setting

The published menu includes Alanya kebap, and the venue also discusses Alanya’s traditional külüklü soup. It is a better fit for an established town restaurant than for a rooftop sunset experience.

## Plan your visit

Check the current menu and contact the restaurant for a reservation, especially for large groups. Do not assume every regional dish discussed on its site is available daily.
