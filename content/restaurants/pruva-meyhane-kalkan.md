Pruva is a meyhane on Kalkan's harbour, the kind of place where the evening is built around a bottle of rakı, a spread of cold mezes and fish from the grill. The restaurant describes itself as a seafront dining spot at Kalkan Harbour, with sea and sunset views and live fasıl music. Its website carries a "© 2026 Pruva Restaurant" footer and journal posts from March 2026. The address is İskele Sokak No. 13, Kalkan, in the Kaş district.

## The food

The dinner menu follows the meyhane order: cold mezes, hot starters, then fish or meat. Prices are in Turkish lira; these are the figures we read on 19 November 2026:

- **Cold mezes:** broad bean dip (350 TL), carrot tarator (350 TL), rock samphire (400 TL), spicy ezme (400 TL) and hummus with beef (500 TL).
- **Hot starters:** courgette fritters (400 TL), fried calamari (750 TL), Albanian-style liver (750 TL), butter-garlic shrimp (750 TL) and grilled octopus (1,150 TL).
- **Fish:** grilled sea bass or sea bream (1,000 TL each) and sea bass with prawns (1,350 TL).
- **Meat:** lamb shish kebab (1,400 TL), lamb chops (1,700 TL) and beef kavurma (1,900 TL).
- **Dessert:** baked tahini halva (400 TL).

A separate lunch menu leans international, with burgers, wraps and pasta. Several lines on the online menus show a price with no dish name, so ask your waiter what they are. ## The setting

Pruva faces the boats in the harbour, and its glass front looks across the water. The home page promises live fasıl, the classic Turkish tavern music, and "long evening tables"; it gives no music schedule, so ask when you book. Elsewhere in Kalkan, [Korsan Meze](/restaurants/korsan-meze/) overlooks the beach, and for a long breakfast the next morning, [Avlu Kalkan](/restaurants/avlu-kalkan/) is up in the village of Bezirgan.

## When to go

The restaurant lists the same hours every day: 11:00 to 01:00. No season is given, and many Kalkan venues cut back in winter, so call before you make a special trip in the cold months. Sunset is the obvious time to arrive for the view.
