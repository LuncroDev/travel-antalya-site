Fatma Sultan serves Turkish and Ottoman dishes in a mansion-style building with indoor seating and a garden, just outside the old-town core. The current official Turkish page gives Arık Caddesi No:20.

## Food and setting

Highlighted dishes include hünkar beğendi, islim kebabı and hand-made mantı. A dedicated vegan menu is explicitly advertised. The venue states that pets are accepted in the garden and that an accessible entrance is available; confirm any individual access needs.

## Plan your visit

The official page lists Monday–Saturday 09:00–22:00 and Sunday noon–22:00. Book your preferred indoor or garden table and discuss allergies directly with staff.

## Published address

Haşimişcan, Arık Caddesi No:20, Muratpaşa, Antalya
