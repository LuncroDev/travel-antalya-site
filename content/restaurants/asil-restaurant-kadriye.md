Asil is a Lebanese, Turkish and Moroccan restaurant in Kadriye, the resort strip next to Belek. [The Land of Legends](/blog/is-antalya-good-for-families-things-to-do-in-antalya-with-kids/) lists it among the restaurants on its Shopping Avenue, and Asil gives its address as Kadriye Mahallesi, Atatürk 3 Caddesi No. 1/11, Serik. It belongs to SWOT Hospitality, the group behind [NEMO](/restaurants/nemo-restaurant-lounge/) and [Piazzetta Italiana](/restaurants/piazzetta-italiana-belek/) on the same avenue. One thing to know: the website is shared with a sister Asil in Dubai, so stay on the Turkey pages. They carry a "© 2026" footer, and the Antalya menus are dated October 2026.

## The food

The kitchen calls its style Arabesque. You start with cold and hot mezes from all three cuisines, then each diner orders a main. Dishes named on the Antalya menu page include:

- **Mezes and starters:** truffle hummus, hummus tantuni, muhammara, kibbeh nayeh (a raw tartare), chicken liver, Tunisian brik and Gavurdağı salad.
- **Mains:** Adana kebap, beef shashlik, lamb chops, an Asil grill platter, fish tajine, prawn casserole, sea bass in kadayıf pastry, grilled octopus and lobster thermidor.
- **Desserts:** rice-pudding pastilla and irmik helvası, a semolina halva.

The à la carte, dessert, wine and kids' menus are PDFs that we could not open, so we can't quote dish prices. The site does price three set offers in euros:

- **Lunch the Asil Way:** five courses (mezes, salad, starter, main, dessert) for €35 a person, daily 14:00–17:00.
- **Rakı Night:** a four-course set menu for €45 a person, every evening from 18:00. The page doesn't say how much rakı is included.
- **Happier Hours:** €40 a person for two hours with three dips, listed daily 22:00–01:00. Drinks are not mentioned.

The lunch and rakı sets are for two or more.

## The setting

The Land of Legends page describes a place built around food, décor and music. Rakı Night and Happier Hours both come with a henna artist. The music is unclear: the events list promises Turkish oriental music on Rakı Night, but the event's own page says background music. No seat count, terrace details or dress code are published. The award badges on the shared website are for the brand and the Dubai branch, not this restaurant. For the same reason, the photos on this page come from The Land of Legends' own Asil page, where the terrace looks out on the park's castle.

## When to go

Asil opens every day from 14:00 to midnight (The Land of Legends says 23:59). Last food orders are at 23:00, and last drinks and desserts at 23:15. Note that Happier Hours is listed until 01:00, an hour past closing, so check before you plan a late one. The set lunch suits a day around Kadriye, perhaps after a morning at the [Aspendos theatre](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/). For a dinner out from the buffet, the [Belek and Kadriye hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek) are close by.
