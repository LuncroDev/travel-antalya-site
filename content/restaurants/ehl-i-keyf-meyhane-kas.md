Ehl-i Keyf is a meyhane, the Turkish tavern where an evening is built from small plates and rakı. Its own story page frames it as a meeting place that mixes old meyhane culture with a relaxed Mediterranean mood: modern takes on traditional meze, produce from local suppliers, fresh seafood, and long tables made for conversation. The site carries a 2026 footer and a current menu.

## The food

The menu has three parts: meze, food and drinks. The cold meze list alone runs to about 40 dishes. Examples from the menu:

- **Paçanga böreği**, crisp filo filled with pastırma (cured beef), tomato, pepper and melted kaşar cheese.
- **Deniz börülcesi** (samphire) and **ot kavurma** (sautéed wild greens).
- **Kabak çiçeği dolması**, stuffed courgette flowers.
- **Vine-leaf sarma with sour-cherry sauce.**
- **Levrek marin**, marinated sea bass, and a mustard sea bass meze.
- **Girit ezme**, a Cretan-style cheese and herb dip.
- **Fish soup** made with Mediterranean fish.
- **Grilled octopus**, cooked over charcoal.
- **Kalamar dolma**, squid stuffed with cheese and seafood.
- **Levrek simit**, sea bass wrapped in puff pastry.
- **Karides güveç**, shrimp baked in a clay pot.
- **Kılıç şiş**, swordfish skewers grilled with bay leaves.
- **A seafood platter for two** with calamari, shrimp, sea bass, swordfish, octopus and sole.
- **Fırında helva** (baked halva) and **roasted fig with ice cream** for dessert.

Grills such as köfte and lamb chops, pasta and chicken dishes are also listed, and the daily fish is sold by the kilo. Prices are shown in Turkish lira. The drinks list is meyhane-strong: several rakı brands by the glass or bottle, mostly Turkish Kayra wines (some by the glass), beer, şalgam (fermented turnip juice), Turkish coffee and tea.

## The setting

The address is Andifli, Hükümet Caddesi No. 3, Kaş, and the site describes tables under olive trees in the heart of town, with warm lights, candles, a meze display and old meyhane songs. One older blog post on the same site places it on the Çukurbağ Peninsula instead; the contact and story pages put it in the centre of town, so check the map pin before you set off. Our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/) shows the town layout.

## When to go

The restaurant opens **every day from 11:00 to 02:00**, according to both its Turkish and English booking pages. A meyhane night starts late and runs long, so book a table for after sunset and order meze in rounds rather than all at once.

## Booking

Reservations are **same-day only**: call between 10:00 and 17:00 on the day you want to come, on +90 536 729 22 18 or +90 553 701 46 69 (WhatsApp is linked on the first number). The restaurant holds tables for 15 minutes, and groups of more than 30 must phone. Dress code, children's policy and parking are not published.
