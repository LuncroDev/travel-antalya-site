Alanya Kale Muhtarın Yeri is a restaurant on Kale Caddesi, the road that winds up to [Alanya Castle](/things-to-do/alanya-castle/) through the Hisariçi quarter. It says it opened in 1984 as a small business serving castle visitors, and it now runs from breakfast to midnight every day. Its photos show wooden terraces among the greenery and a sea view from the breakfast tables.

## The food

Mornings are for **serpme kahvaltı**, the Turkish spread breakfast, served 08:30–12:00. The site lists a cold-cuts plate, cheeses, jams, olives, börek and hot dishes. **Gözleme** (stuffed flatbread) comes with cheese, potato, kaşar, minced meat or a mix.

From 11:00 the main menu takes over. It is mostly grill and meat: beef tenderloin, "lokum" steaks, meat skewers, lamb shank, ribs (kaburga) and liver. Chicken comes in curry, mushroom and barbecue sauces, as a sauté, on skewers or grilled with mountain thyme. There are burgers, including a "Muhtar's" burger and a rib burger, and salads from çoban to Caesar and seafood. The menu page also shows sea bass and salmon fillets, pan-fried prawns, ravioli, a köfte on aubergine purée (beğendi) and desserts such as tiramisu.

No prices are published: every dish on the website reads "….₺". The site links to a separate digital menu that we couldn't open.

## The setting

The gallery shows a deck terrace under vines, a covered dining terrace with a bar, and a breakfast table with the sea far below. The home page promises a view of Alanya and the castle. The site doesn't say how many people it seats.

Its history is told two ways: one line says it began on Kale Caddesi in 1984, the footer says it was founded at the castle summit. The franchise page claims 63 years of restaurant experience, which doesn't fit a 1984 start. Treat the address below as the one to use.

## When to go

Breakfast before or after a morning at the castle is the obvious fit; see our [Alanya in a day](/blog/alanya-in-a-day/) plan for timings. The kitchen serves mains 11:00–24:00, and a night menu runs 17:00–24:00 (last orders). The footer gives the hours as 08:30–24:00, every day of the week. For more on the breakfast itself, read [Turkish breakfast in Antalya](/blog/turkish-breakfast-in-antalya/).

## Booking

The address is Hisariçi Mah., Kale Cad. No:40, 07400 Alanya. Phone **+90 242 513 51 88**, or use the reservation form on the website. For a harbour dinner later in the day, try [Güverte](/restaurants/guverte-restaurant-alanya/), and browse our [Alanya district guide](/destinations/alanya/) for more.
