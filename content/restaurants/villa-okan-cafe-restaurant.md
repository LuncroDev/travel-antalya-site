Villa Okan is on Bostancıpınarı Caddesi in central Alanya. The official site highlights its garden, historic atmosphere, Italian and Mexican dishes, cocktails and live music evenings.

## Food and setting

A children’s playground is specifically listed, making it relevant for families who want a sit-down meal. The broad international menu is a different proposition from a specialist kebab or seafood restaurant.

## Plan your visit

Contact the venue for the music programme, opening hours and a table in the desired area. Ask about playground access and suitability for your children’s ages rather than assuming supervised childcare.

Şekerhane, Bostancıpınarı Cd. No:28, Alanya, Antalya
