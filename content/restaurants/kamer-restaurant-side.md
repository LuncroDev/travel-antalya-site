Kamer is a restaurant on Barbaros Caddesi in the old town of Side, with tables on a stone terrace right above the rocks and the sea. Its About page says the story began in 1975 under the shade of fig trees. The menu runs from grilled fish to kebabs and pizza, so it works for a mixed group after a day at the ruins. The website carries a "Copyright 2026" footer. The address is Barbaros Cad. No. 47, Side.

## The food

The English menu, a PDF linked from the site, groups Mediterranean seafood, charcoal and wood-fire grills, pizza, pasta, burgers and vegetarian dishes. Every price line on it is blank, so we can't tell you what a meal costs. Dishes on the list include:

- **From the sea:** grilled sea bass, sea bream and salmon, grilled octopus and jumbo shrimp.
- **From the grill:** Adana kebab, the house Kamer kebab, a mixed grill, beef ribs and Chateaubriand.
- **Wood-fired and pasta:** seafood pizza, lahmacun with hazelnuts and spaghetti bolognese.
- **Desserts:** katmer and baklava.

There is also a breakfast plate, served until 1 pm, with fried eggs, sigara böreği (fried cheese rolls), olives, greens, tomatoes and cucumbers, local cheeses, honey and jam. The gallery shows meze, salads and drinks on the terrace.

## The setting

The site sells it as sea-view dining, and the photos show why: a terrace built out over the rocks, laid with white tablecloths and lit with strings of bulbs at night. The site doesn't say whether there is indoor seating for winter. Nearby in the old town are [Zula](/restaurants/zula-side/), [Side Orfoz](/restaurants/side-orfoz-restaurant/) and [Karma](/restaurants/karma-side/), if you want to compare menus.

## When to go

Sunset is the obvious time, after an afternoon at [Side Ancient City](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/) and the Temple of Apollo; our [Side day trip guide](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/) has a plan for the day. The website gives two sets of hours. The home page says every day 12:00–23:30, and to 00:30 at weekends. The footer says 11:30–23:00 Monday to Friday and 11:30–midnight at weekends. It doesn't mention a winter closure, so call before you go between November and March.

## Booking

Call +90 533 918 76 01, the reservations number on the contact page, or use the booking page on the website; the footer also lists +90 506 602 28 26 and info@kamerrestoran.com.
