Tirmis takes its name from the lupin-bean snack associated with Antalya. The official resort pages describe a contemporary restaurant built around regional dishes and produce, with freshly made yufka bread.

## Food and setting

Published examples include hibeş, şiş köfte, tahini-dressed piyaz and tahini pumpkin dessert. The venue is inside a resort and should not be presented as an unrestricted walk-in city restaurant.

## Plan your visit

Contact the resort to confirm whether non-residents may dine, the reservation procedure, any extra charge and seasonal opening. Current official pages use BAYOU Lara branding; older source pages use Lara Barut Collection.
