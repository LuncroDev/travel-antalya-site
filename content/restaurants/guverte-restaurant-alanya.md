Güverte, Turkish for "deck", is a restaurant on İskele Caddesi, the harbour street in central Alanya. Its site says it has served here since 2009 and sells it on the view: Alanya Castle and the Red Tower above the sea, and the harbour below. It belongs to the local Kaptan Hotels group, and the site carries a 2026 copyright and a full menu in Turkish.

## The food

The menu runs from Aegean-style cold starters and seafood hot starters to pasta, steaks, lamb and fish; the sister Kaptan Hotel's page calls it the place for fish and Mediterranean specialities. Examples:

- **Deniz börülcesi** (samphire) with olive oil and garlic, **bakla favası** (broad-bean purée) and marinated anchovies.
- **House-made pastırma** (cured beef) and house-smoked salmon and trout.
- **Ege usulü ahtapot**, Aegean-style octopus, and **ahtapot ızgara**, grilled octopus.
- Squid fried, grilled with garlic and basil, or cooked in a clay pot with white wine and tomato.
- Seafood wrapped in chard leaves, and a Güverte-style fish soup.
- **Kaptan böreği**, a pastry with walnuts and minced meat, and a börek of **gelincik** greens (poppy leaves).
- **Kağıtta levrek buğulama**, sea bass steamed in paper with thyme and sage, and **kılıç şiş**, swordfish skewers marinated in onion and lemon.
- **Kuzu incik**, baked lamb shank with a honey and plum sauce, and **kuzu külbastı**, grilled lamb with mountain thyme.
- **Sakızlı fırın sütlaç**, baked rice pudding with mastic and pistachio ice cream, and dried figs stuffed with walnuts.

There is a fish of the day, steaks with a choice of sauces, and a kids' list with chicken tenders, small burgers and köfte. Drinks cover wine, rakı, spirits and classic cocktails. No prices are shown. The home page also promotes "Fit ve Hafif" (fit and light) menus that the menu page does not list, so ask when you book.

## The setting

The address is Çarşı Mahallesi, İskele Caddesi No. 29, Alanya, on the same street as its sister Kaptan Hotel (No. 33). The site promises a refined atmosphere but does not describe the seating, any outdoor tables or which ones face the water. The Red Tower stands at the water's edge below the castle walls; our [Alanya Castle guide](/things-to-do/alanya-castle/) covers it and the Seljuk shipyard beside it, and the [Alanya district guide](/destinations/alanya/) has the rest of town.

## When to go

Opening hours are not published. The castle view counts most in daylight or at sunset, so ask for an early table facing the harbour. Out of season, phone first to check it is open.

## Booking

Call +90 242 782 40 21, the site's number for information and reservations; it also links an online booking page run through Kaptan Hotels. Dress code and parking are not published.
