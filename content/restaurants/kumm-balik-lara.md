Kumm Balık is a fish restaurant at Lara Caddesi No. 321 in Çağlayan, on the Lara side of Antalya. The website calls it a sea-view fish restaurant and makes a point of its play area, which makes it one of the easier seafood dinners in Lara for families. Its set-menu page was last updated on 6 October 2026.

## The food

The menu is classic Turkish fish-house fare: cold meze, seafood starters, fish from the grill and fixed menus. Most whole fish, such as sea bass, sea bream and bluefish, have no printed price. The menu asks you to check the day's price with your waiter. Prices in Turkish lira, as we read them on 21 November 2026:

- **Cold meze:** haydari, atom (yoghurt with dried chilli) and Cretan-style ezme at 360 TL each; avocado 420 TL; artichoke 460 TL.
- **Seafood starters:** marinated sea bass 460 TL, smoked salmon 480 TL, shrimp 720 TL, octopus carpaccio 1,280 TL and a mixed seafood plate 1,320 TL.
- **Not fish:** grilled köfte or chicken 820 TL each, and lamb chops 1,280 TL.

The **set menus** are the clearest deal, each priced without and with alcohol:

- **Menü 1 (2,400 / 2,900 TL):** five meze, including hibeş (Antalya's tahini dip), an avocado and walnut salad, fried calamari or shrimp, grilled sea bass, then semolina halva with ice cream or fruit.
- **Menü 2 (2,650 / 3,150 TL):** five meze, shrimp in saffron sauce and grilled sea bass, then a fig dessert with ice cream or fruit.
- **Menü 3 (3,400 / 3,900 TL):** meze including marinated sea bass, grilled octopus with Spanish-style shrimp, and grouper grilled or poached, then a dessert or fruit.

Drinks: Yeni Rakı is 340 TL for 4 cl or 3,520 TL a bottle, Efes beer 380 TL and a glass of red or white wine 550 TL. Some page summaries on the site still show lower, older prices, so treat these as a guide.

## The setting

The site describes a sea-view dining room. The play area has a play attendant, and the site says it is watched by camera with a screen in the main hall, so parents can see their children from the table. It gives no age range. The site doesn't mention a terrace, parking or capacity. For other fish on this stretch, compare [Göz Balık](/restaurants/goz-balik-lara/) and [Kalamar Balık](/restaurants/kalamar-balik-lara/).

## When to go

Kumm is on the main road between the city and Lara, so it fits an evening after the [Lower Düden falls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/) or a day on [Lara Beach](/things-to-do/lara-beach/). Our [Lara public beaches guide](/blog/lara-public-beaches/) covers the cliff platforms nearby. The website gives no opening hours, so check before you go.
