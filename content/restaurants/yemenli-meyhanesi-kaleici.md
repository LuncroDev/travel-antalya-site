Yemenli Meyhanesi is a meyhane in the heart of [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/), set in an old stone house with an indoor room and a garden. The restaurant says it first opened in December 2010 and took its current name and meyhane concept in 2017. We read its official website on 27 November 2026; the footer reads © 2026.

## The food

This is a classic meze-and-fish evening. The website promises **more than 20 cold meze** and **more than 10 hot starters**, and names a sample from each course:

- **Cold meze:** atom (strained yoghurt with hot dried chilli), artichokes in olive oil, and warm hummus with pastırma.
- **Hot starters:** fried shrimp, fried calamari and grilled octopus.
- **Mains:** grilled sea bass, grilled köfte and a chicken güveç (clay-pot stew) with kaşar cheese.
- **Desserts:** baklava, cheesecake and seasonal fruit.

A seafood display of jumbo shrimp, fresh squid, octopus and lobster is promised all year. There is a full bar and a long wine list. **The site prints no prices.** Its menu page links a PDF titled "18 09 2026 MENU", which we could not open, so ask for the current list before you order.

## The setting

The house is on Zeytin Sokak in the Kılınçarslan quarter of the old town. The restaurant describes it as a 19th-century stone building with seating indoors and in the garden, for about **120 guests** in all. Tables also spill onto the lane outside, as the restaurant's own photo shows. There is **live music every night from 20:00 to 23:59**, and on some nights a belly-dancing show.

## When to go

The restaurant says it is open **12 months a year**. Its website shows the day's hours as **11:45–01:00** but gives no weekly timetable, so check before a lunchtime visit. Come after 20:00 if you want the music. Kaleiçi has several other meyhanes with live music; compare [Kaleiçi Deli Divane](/restaurants/kaleici-deli-divane/) and [Ayar Meyhanesi](/restaurants/ayar-meyhanesi/), and read our guide to [ocakbaşı and meyhane nights in Antalya](/blog/ocakbasi-and-meyhane-nights-in-antalya/) for how the evening works.

## Booking

Call **+90 505 758 80 88** or message the WhatsApp number on the website (+90 555 365 83 76). The restaurant asks you to call for à la carte and set-menu options and to mention the car park when you reserve. After dinner, it's a short walk to the old harbour or [Hadrian's Gate](/things-to-do/gate-of-hadrian/). Hotels within walking distance are in our [Kaleiçi hotel list](/hotels/?area=Kalei%C3%A7i).
