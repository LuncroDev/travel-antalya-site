Avlu, "courtyard" in Turkish, does one thing: serpme kahvaltı, the Turkish spread breakfast where small plates cover the whole table. The name says Kalkan, but the contact page gives an address in Bezirgan, a village in Kaş district that a 2026 Kalkan travel guide puts 15–20 minutes from Kalkan centre. The site carries a 2026 copyright and a priced menu in Turkish and English.

## The food

The menu is short and all breakfast. The headline is the **Avlu serpme kahvaltı for two**, listed at 950 TL per person on the menu checked 12 November 2026. It brings:

- butter, honey, pekmez (molasses), tahini and several jams
- olives, avocado, walnuts and chocolate
- several cheeses, tomato and cucumber, a plate of greens, and olive oil with thyme and lemon
- eggs, **sahanda sucuk** (spiced sausage cooked in a small pan) and sausages
- **sigara böreği** (thin fried pastry rolls) and **paçanga** (a fried pastry parcel)
- house-made chips and fried peppers in a vinegar sauce
- **pişi** or **bazlama** (fried dough or griddle bread) and pancakes
- unlimited tea, served in a thermos.

Eating alone? The **single breakfast** is 600 TL: a smaller plate with fried peppers, cheese, tahini and pekmez, pişi, sigara böreği, sausage and an egg. Hot extras include **menemen** (eggs cooked with tomato and pepper) at 375 TL, a mixed omelette with sucuk, kaşar cheese, onion and pepper at 375 TL, and a plain omelette at 250 TL. Turkish coffee is 150 TL and orange juice 230 TL. Prices change, so check before you go.

## The setting

The address is Bezirgan, Çukurca Sokak No. 794, 07960 Kaş. The site speaks only of a warm, nature-filled atmosphere for a slow morning. It does not describe the courtyard the name suggests, the seating or the view. Use the directions link on the contact page rather than heading for Kalkan's harbour streets. Our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/) covers Kalkan and the wider district.

## When to go

The site lists the same hours every day of the week: 09:30 to 15:00. There is no evening menu, so treat it as a late breakfast or brunch. Season dates are not published; in winter, call ahead to check it is open. A long breakfast here pairs well with an afternoon at [Xanthos](/things-to-do/xanthos-ancient-city/) or on the sand at [Patara](/blog/patara-beach-and-ancient-city/).
