Etna Fish House is a small, family-run fish restaurant in the old part of Kaş, a couple of minutes uphill from the harbour. It's a casual place for a fish sandwich at lunch or a grilled fish and a glass of rakı in the evening, and every main dish comes with a free salad bar. The website carries a "© 2026 Etna Fish House" footer, and its own Kaş eating guide was updated on 1 September 2026. The address is Öztürk Sokak No. 17 D:F in Andifli, next to the Upper House hotel.

## The food

The family says it buys fish daily and cooks it grilled or pan-fried. The menu, in Turkish lira, as we read it on 19 November 2026:

- **Quick lunch:** fish wrap or mackerel sandwich (590 TL each), whiting sandwich (640 TL), calamari sandwich (740 TL) and fish and chips (890 TL).
- **Fish:** sea bream (1,150 TL) or sea bass (1,200 TL) by the portion, and fried red mullet (1,200 TL).
- **Seafood:** mussel skewers (840 TL), octopus casserole or octopus salad (1,290 TL each), and mixed seafood platters at 3,100 TL (small) or 5,900 TL.
- **Starters:** fish soup (440 TL), stuffed mussels, ten for 500 TL, Cretan herb paste (350 TL) and lakerda, salted bonito (840 TL).
- **Desserts and drinks:** baked rice pudding (390 TL), baklava (490 TL), a single Yeni Rakı (350 TL) and draught beer (240 TL).

The menu warns that prices vary with the season and the day's catch. The About page says tea and a few desserts are on the house after dinner, yet the menu prices the desserts, so check what's included.

## The setting

Tables sit out on the lane, and the restaurant's own guide says plainly that there is no sea view. Its gallery shows a lit street front and simple tables. For a sunset view with your fish, try [Elia Restaurant](/restaurants/elia-restaurant-kas/) out on the Çukurbağ Peninsula; for a long meze evening in the centre, the meyhane [Ehl-i Keyf](/restaurants/ehl-i-keyf-meyhane-kas/).

## When to go

The contact page lists every day from 11:00 to midnight and adds that hours may vary by season, so call ahead in winter. Lunch suits a day of walking around town or before a [Kekova boat trip](/blog/kekova-boat-trips/); dinner suits an evening in the old streets.

## Booking

Phone +90 531 402 04 04. The family asks for bookings in July and August; the rest of the year you can usually walk in.
