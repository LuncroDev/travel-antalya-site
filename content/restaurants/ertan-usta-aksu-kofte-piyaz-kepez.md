Ertan Usta is a köfte and piyaz restaurant in Kepez, the residential district north of Antalya's centre. It does one thing: the city's classic pairing of grilled meatballs with *piyaz*, a dried-bean salad dressed with tahini and lemon. The restaurant's story page is written in the first person by Ertan Usta (Ertan Kanal), who founded the place and still runs it. If you want to taste the dish locals eat at lunch, away from the old town, this is the kind of place to look for.

## The food

The menu, last updated on 18 October 2026, is short and built around set plates:

- **Köfte**: kneaded by hand and cooked over charcoal, served with bread and greens. You can order one portion, one and a half, or a small plate of four pieces.
- **Piyaz**: described as Antalya-style piyaz of dried beans (*kuru fasulye*) with a tahini-lemon sauce. The home page lists tahini, lemon, vinegar, garlic, tomato, parsley, egg and onion among the ingredients. It comes in small, single and one-and-a-half portions.
- **Köfte with a small piyaz**: the "concept menu", with ayran, salad, red cabbage and dessert.
- **Chicken wings** (*tavuk yaprak kanat*): a set plate with salad, red cabbage, dessert and ayran.
- **Lentil soup** (*mercimek çorbası*), full or small.
- **Dessert of the day**, which comes with the set menus.

Drinks are simple: water, bottled cola and ayran. Prices are listed in Turkish lira, with VAT included. The restaurant's name refers to an "Aksu" style of köfte and piyaz, but the site does not explain what makes it Aksu style, so ask if you are curious. ## The setting

The site says little about the room itself, only that the charcoal grill is lit every day. Expect a neighbourhood grill rather than a dining room with a view. The address is Yeni Mahalle, Aliya İzzet Begoviç Caddesi No. 11A, Kepez. Our [Kepez district guide](/destinations/kepez/) covers the area, which also holds the [Düden Waterfalls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/).

## When to go

The restaurant opens from **11:00 to 23:00** on Monday to Thursday, Saturday and Sunday, and is **closed on Fridays**. Köfte and piyaz is a lunch tradition in Antalya, so midday is the natural time to go.

## Booking

There is no online booking. The restaurant takes calls and WhatsApp messages on 0532 399 74 07, and asks groups and takeaway customers to call ahead so the food is ready.
