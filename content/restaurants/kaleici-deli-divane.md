Kaleiçi Deli Divane is a meyhane, the Turkish tavern where an evening runs on small plates, rakı and music. It sits on Uzun Çarşı Sokak inside [Kaleiçi](/things-to-do/kaleici-old-town/), Antalya's walled old town, and it is young: the website says it opened on New Year's Eve 2024. What sets it apart from quieter meze places is the programme. A fasıl band (Turkish classical and folk songs) plays every night, followed by a different solo singer for each day of the week. The site carries a "© 2026" footer.

## The food

The menu on the website is priced in Turkish lira, as seen on 17 November 2026:

- **Mezes:** 23 cold mezes. Most cost 275 TL, among them haydari, fava, humus, mutebbel, şakşuka and atom (yoghurt with dried chilli). Ezine cheese and girit ezme cost 350 TL. The dearest, at 400 TL, are Albanian-style liver, çiğ köfte, and tulum cheese with walnuts.
- **Hot starters:** fried potatoes at 350 TL; liver, calamari, shrimp and mushroom dishes at 650 TL; oyster mushrooms at 750 TL.
- **Mains:** sea bream, grilled köfte and chicken şiş at 750 TL, lamb şiş and chops at 950 TL, beef tenderloin at 1,050 TL and entrecôte at 1,250 TL. A mixed grill costs 4,500 TL.
- **Salads and fruit:** salads at 500 TL; fruit plates from 500 TL for two.

The only fish on the list is sea bream, so this is a meze-and-grill night rather than a seafood dinner. No rakı, wine or drinks prices are published. The site also advertises a set menu (five mezes, salad and a main, with tea and coffee) but says it ran in August and September only, and its prices are hard to read on the page.

## The setting

The photo on the site shows an indoor room with white and blue table linen and a stage at the back. No garden, terrace or seat count is mentioned. The nightly running order, as published, is approximate and may change:

- 16:00 doors open; service starts at 19:00.
- 20:00 fasıl band; 21:00 the solo singer of the night.
- 22:15 a belly-dance (oryantal) show, then the singer returns.
- Music ends at midnight; closing at 01:00.

## When to go

It opens every day, 16:00–01:00. Come for 20:00 if the music is the point, and pace the mezes; a meyhane night is meant to be long. For a quieter, earlier dinner in the old town, try [Hasanağa](/restaurants/hasanaga-restaurant/) or [Mermerli](/restaurants/mermerli-restaurant/), and read our [Kaleiçi walking route](/blog/kaleici-walking-route/) to plan the afternoon before.

## Booking

The address is Tuzcular Mahallesi, Uzun Çarşı Sokak No. 23, Muratpaşa. Reserve by phone or WhatsApp on +90 532 507 56 30 or +90 533 657 51 99. The site asks groups of ten or more to book at least a day ahead. More places to eat are in our [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/).
