Korsan Meze was established in 1979 and occupies a beach-view position in Kalkan. Its menu spans traditional Turkish meze, lamb, kebabs, chicken, fish and vegetarian dishes.

## Food and setting

The restaurant is part of the Korsan Kalkan group, but it is distinct from the Fish Terrace upstairs. Its broad menu and relaxed format make it a useful choice for a shared Turkish meal.

## Plan your visit

Book the Meze restaurant specifically, not the Fish Terrace. Check seasonal opening and the current menu; the official site also advertises takeaway and home delivery.
