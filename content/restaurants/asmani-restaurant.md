Asmani is the terrace restaurant at Akra Antalya. Its menu combines local flavours and international dishes, and the hotel describes its approach as casual fine dining with seasonal, farm-to-table influences.

## Food and setting

The rooftop panorama makes the setting especially relevant for a celebration or a scenic meal. This is a specific restaurant within Akra Antalya, not a separate hotel or a restaurant in central Kaleiçi.
