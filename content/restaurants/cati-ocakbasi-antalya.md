Çatı means "roof", and Çatı Ocakbaşı's slogan plays on it: let's meet under the same roof. It is an ocakbaşı, a grill house where kebabs cook over charcoal, in Şirinyalı, Muratpaşa. The site promises traditional dishes with modern touches, carries a 2026 copyright and has a very long priced menu in Turkish.

## The food

The menu runs to about 45 mains, some 90 cold meze, 15 hot starters, salads, desserts and fruit, then hundreds of drinks. From the menu page checked on 13 November 2026:

- **Adana** and **Urfa** kebabs at ₺950, **beyti** and **ali nazik** at ₺1,070, and **küşleme**, a small, tender lamb cut, at ₺1,300.
- The **Çatı roll** (₺1,070): Adana kebab with smoky aubergine, caramelised onion and kaşar cheese.
- Diyarbakır-style **ciğer şiş** (liver skewers, ₺1,070), **kuyruk yağı şiş** (lamb tail fat on a skewer, ₺600), **saç kavurma** of beef or lamb (₺1,250), and lamb chops or buttered beef tenderloin at ₺1,350.
- Cold meze such as **etli humus** (hummus with meat, ₺600), **Girit ezme** (Ezine, kaşar and village cheeses with walnut and basil, ₺380), **kuru dolma** (dried aubergine and pepper stuffed with beef, ₺500) and the house **Çatı meze** of avocado, aubergine, yoghurt, labneh and garlic (₺350).
- Hot starters including **içli köfte** with beef, bulgur, mozzarella and walnut (₺320 each), **yaprak ciğer** (lamb liver with garlic and spring onion, ₺800) and **sakatat kokteyl**, a plate of tongue, tripe and brain (₺600).
- A little fish: sea bream at ₺1,200, sea bass at ₺1,300, plus squid and jumbo prawns.
- Desserts such as **ananaslı tahin helvası** (tahini halva with pineapple and green apple, ₺470) and **incir tatlısı** (fig with apricot, walnut and honey, ₺490).

Rakı by the 4 cl glass starts at ₺450, and a ₺100 cover plate (kuver) is listed. The site contradicts itself on some prices: the home page shows Adana at ₺760 and beef tenderloin at ₺800, while the menu page lists ₺950 and ₺1,350. Ask before you order.

## The setting

The address is Şirinyalı Mahallesi, 1537. Sokak No. 2, Muratpaşa. The home and about pages say the restaurant has a view of the sea and talk of Mediterranean breezes. Neither confirms a roof terrace, despite the name, and seating, indoor space and capacity are not described. Our [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/) covers this stretch of the city.

## When to go

The home and about pages give the same hours every day: 14:00 to 01:00. The contact page lists none. That makes it a late-lunch or dinner stop. To see the promised sea view, come in daylight or at sunset.

## Booking

Call 0549 242 72 72, which the site lists as both phone and reservation number, or email info@catiocakbasiantalya.com. The "Rezervasyon" buttons open WhatsApp without a number, so phoning is simpler. Parking, card payment and children's facilities are not published.

[Asmani](/restaurants/asmani-restaurant/), at the Akra Antalya hotel, is another Şirinyalı address. For a city base, browse the [Antalya city hotels](/hotels/?area=Antalya%20city).
