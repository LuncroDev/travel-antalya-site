Naturel is on Uzun Çarşı, the old market street in central Kaş, and its tables are outside among the historic houses. Its website calls the cooking "Mediterranean and World cuisine". In practice that means a broad menu for groups who don't all want the same thing.

## The food

The menu is in sections. **Starters and meze** include falafel, courgette fritters, creamed mushrooms, fried liver, calamari and shrimp. **Fresh pastas** include lamb ravioli, spinach ravioli, seafood linguine and fettuccine Alfredo. **Mains** range from sea bass, as a fillet or grilled, and grilled salmon to lamb shank, osso buco, beef stew and several steaks. There are also bowls topped with salmon, chicken, meatballs or falafel. Crispy ice cream is the only dessert listed. If you want a traditional Turkish meze evening instead, [Bi Lokma](/restaurants/bi-lokma-restaurant/) is nearby in Andifli.

## The setting

The published address is Andifli Mahallesi, Uzun Çarşı, Gürsoy Sokak No. 6. That puts you in the old part of town, on the same street as the Lycian sarcophagus that Kaş calls the King's Tomb. Dining is open-air, so it is at its best on warm evenings. The website has an English version with the full menu, so you can choose before you sit down.

## When to go

The restaurant lists daily hours of 12:00–22:00. Come for lunch after a morning on the water, or for an early dinner before an evening walk around the harbour. If you are visiting from Antalya for the day, read our [Antalya to Kaş day trip guide](/blog/antalya-to-kas-day-trip/) and leave enough time for the drive back.
