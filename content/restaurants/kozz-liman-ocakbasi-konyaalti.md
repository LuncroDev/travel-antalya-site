Közz Liman is an ocakbaşı, a grill house where meat cooks over charcoal, run with a meyhane's love of meze and rakı. Its site places it in Liman Mahallesi, Konyaaltı (liman means harbour), carries a 2026 copyright and has a full priced menu in Turkish, English and Russian.

## The food

The site says meat is grilled over embers and the meze are made fresh each day. It also names where some ingredients come from: Karaman mayalısı bread, Divle obruk cheese aged underground in a sinkhole in Karaman, tulum cheese from Erzincan, Ezine cheese, pomegranate molasses from Burdur, halhalı olives from Burdur and Hatay, and spices from Gaziantep and Kilis. From the menu checked on 13 November 2026:

- Cold meze at ₺285 each, among them **atom** (strained yoghurt with Arnavut biberi, a dried hot pepper, garlic and butter), **hibeş** (a tahini, cumin and lemon dip), **köpoğlu** (roast aubergine with garlic yoghurt and tomato sauce) and **Girit ezmesi** (cheeses with rocket, basil and walnut). A platter of six to eight meze is ₺790.
- Hot starters such as **içli köfte** (bulgur shells filled with beef and walnut) at ₺185, beef tongue in butter at ₺600 and **yaprak ciğer** (thin-sliced lamb liver with onion and sumac) at ₺850.
- Kebabs over charcoal: **Adana** and the milder **Urfa** at ₺800, **Antep-style lamb shish** at ₺950, **ali nazik** (lamb mince on smoky aubergine and yoghurt) at ₺1,000 and **kuzu lokum**, marinated lamb tenderloin, at ₺1,200.
- **Köz karışık**, a mixed grill for one with Adana or Urfa, half portions of chicken, liver and lamb shish, and a lamb chop, at ₺2,750.
- **Kabak tatlısı**, pumpkin with tahini and walnuts, at ₺475.

A single rakı starts at ₺360 and wine is ₺530 a glass. Roasted white chickpeas come free. The menu says prices include VAT and there is no service charge. Prices change, so check on the night.

## The setting

The address is Liman Mahallesi, 27. Sokak No. 7A, 07130 Konyaaltı. The site says little about the room, beyond some tables that open onto the street, which it suggests for couples. It does not describe a terrace or a sea view. One section is headed "a few steps to the harbour", but no distance to the port, the beach or anything else is given. Our [Konyaaltı district guide](/destinations/konyaalti/) and [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/) page cover the area.

## When to go

The site lists the same hours every day: 14:00 to midnight, so a late lunch is the earliest option. It pitches the grill as an evening thing, lit as the sun goes down, so dinner is the natural slot. No seasonal closing is mentioned.

## Booking

Reservations go through WhatsApp on +90 532 485 67 07, the same number as the phone line. Parking, card payment, children's facilities and dress code are not published. Alcohol is served to over-18s only.
