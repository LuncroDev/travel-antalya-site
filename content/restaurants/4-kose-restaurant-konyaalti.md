4 Köşe is an evening restaurant in Konyaaltı built around long shared tables. Its own pages say it sits inside the Four Hills Hotel and describe its food as modern Mediterranean, served with signature cocktails. The motto on the site is "long tables, endless conversations", and the place is set up for groups as much as couples.

## The food

The evening menu is split the way a Turkish meyhane table works: starters, salads, cold meze, hot starters, mains and desserts, plus **rakı set menus** for a table that wants to settle in. The main-course page shows the kitchen mixing Turkish home cooking with modern café plates. Examples:

- **Sac köftesi** in the style of Ermenek, made with lamb and fine bulgur and served with basil yoghurt and house pickles.
- **The chef's börek**, filled with pulled meat, acuka and mushrooms, with purslane in yoghurt and an avocado dip.
- **Otlu mücver**, herb fritters with garlic yoghurt.
- Grain bowls with grilled salmon, köfte or avocado, and thin-crust *piazzetta* with three cheeses or with lamb kokoreç.

Prices are listed in Turkish lira on the menu pages.

## The setting

The restaurant's guide to Konyaaltı dinners talks up the sunset over the Bey Mountains, the range that rises behind [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/). It is a hotel restaurant on the Konyaaltı coastal strip rather than an old-town terrace, so it works well if you are staying on this side of the city. Our [Konyaaltı district guide](/destinations/konyaalti/) and the [Konyaaltı hotels](/hotels/?area=Konyaalt%C4%B1) list cover the area.

## When to go

The homepage and the reservation page give opening hours of **16:00 to 00:00, every day**. A guide on the same site gives 01:00 as the closing time, so ask if you plan to arrive late. A separate lunch menu is served from 12:00 to 16:00. Go just before sunset if you want the mountain view. Guests must be 12 or older, and pets are not allowed.

## Booking

Reservations are taken by WhatsApp or by phone on +90 552 149 06 44. The site gives no street address, only a map link, so use that or ask the hotel for directions.
