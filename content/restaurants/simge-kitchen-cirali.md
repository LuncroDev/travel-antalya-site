Simge Kitchen is a restaurant and cocktail bar in Çıralı, the beach village beside the ruins of Olympos. It sells itself on three things: pizzas baked over a wood fire, plates of its own design, and cocktails, with music treated as part of the evening. The address on the site is simply Çıralı No:25, Kemer; the ministry places the neighbouring Olympos site in Kumluca district, so you'll see both names on maps. The footer still carries a template copyright line, but the site's blog posts are dated June and August 2026, and the whole menu is priced in lira.

## The food

The menu reads like a modern bistro's rather than a meyhane's. Prices as listed on the site:

- **Breakfast (08:30–11:00):** a spread breakfast for two for ₺800, or a breakfast plate for ₺550.
- **Starters:** shrimp casserole with garlic and butter for ₺800, mushroom casserole with İzmir tulum cheese, smoked-salmon bruschetta on sourdough, beetroot with fig and walnut, hummus and Cretan-style ezme.
- **Pizza (₺630–1,200):** from a margherita at ₺630 to slow-cooked beef brisket with mushroom and rocket at ₺1,200. There is also a seafood pizza with shrimp and smoked salmon, a four-cheese with apricot purée, and a vegan one.
- **Mains and fish:** grilled sea bream or sea bass for ₺700, salmon fillet for ₺950, lamb chops with saffron rice for ₺1,400, beef tenderloin for ₺1,800, and keşkek (pounded wheat) with slow-cooked brisket for ₺950. Pastas, mantı, burgers and wraps fill out the list.

Vegetarian and vegan dishes are marked throughout, including a grilled oyster-mushroom "vegan steak" for ₺600. The cocktail list runs from classics, such as a dry martini for ₺490 or a negroni for ₺700, to house drinks like the bourbon-based Simge Sour for ₺600. The wines are mostly Turkish, with Paşaeli by the glass for ₺400.

## The setting

The site says little about the space itself. It talks about fitting in with Çıralı's natural surroundings and calls music part of the experience, but it doesn't describe the seating or say how far it is from the sand. The village sits on a long turtle-nesting bay (see [Çıralı Beach](/things-to-do/cirali-beach/)), next to the [Olympos ruins and the Chimaera flames](/things-to-do/olympos-and-the-chimaera-flames/). Our [Çıralı and Olympos guide](/blog/cirali-and-olympos-where-to-stay-eat-walk/) covers the rest of the village.

## When to go

Breakfast is the only time the site gives: 08:30 to 11:00. The cocktail list and the music point to evenings, but no closing time, weekly day off or season dates are published. Out of season, check before you make the trip. A day on the [coast road from Kemer](/blog/kemer-to-olympos-coast-road/) or up the [Tahtalı cable car](/things-to-do/tahtali-olympos-cable-car/) can end here.
