Barınak Balık Evi’s Lara branch is on 2047 Sokak in Çağlayan. The venue advertises daily seafood, regional meze and a garden suited to families and groups.

## Food and setting

The official page names grilled sea bass, buttered prawns and crisp calamari among its dishes. It is distinct from Antalya Balıkevi and Lara Balık, and also has a separate Döşemealtı branch.

## Plan your visit

Select the Lara branch when booking. The venue publishes daily noon–01:00 hours, but confirm kitchen service and current fish prices before your visit.

## Published address

Çağlayan, 2047. Sk. No:5, Muratpaşa, Antalya
