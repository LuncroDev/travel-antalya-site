Mermerli sits at the edge of the old harbour, with terraces overlooking the Gulf of Antalya and the mountains opposite. It is a classic place to watch the sun go down over the bay with a plate of fish and a view that sells itself.

Ask about the day's catch and book a terrace table in advance for sunset.
