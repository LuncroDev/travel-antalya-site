Redbox Meze & Cafe is a small meze place in Kaş's Andifli quarter. Its About page says the team started as Butik Catering in 2017 and opened the restaurant in 2022, mixing Istanbul meyhane dishes with Mediterranean ones. The site's footer still reads ©2022, but its menu photos were uploaded in 2026, and the prices below are what it showed on 26 November 2026.

## The food

Meze is the heart of the menu. Prices are in Turkish lira as printed; the menu carries no date.

- **Cold meze (₺250–350):** fava, beetroot borani, carrot tarator with walnuts, topik (the Armenian-Istanbul chickpea and onion parcel), hummus and Circassian chicken. Kadın budu köfte, the soft rice-and-meat patties, is ₺350.
- **Hot starters (₺300):** manca, prawn paçanga (a fried pastry roll), spring rolls and a Cretan-style spread.
- **Seafood:** shrimp saganaki and mussel saganaki at ₺700, soy-glazed mackerel at ₺500.
- **Specials:** the fish of the day (a choice of three, grilled or baked) is 900 TL; sea bass fillet baked in paper is ₺850, as is the "şarküteri köfte" with turkey sausage and İzmir tulum cheese. A crispy chicken wrap with vegetables and a mixed plate of olive-oil dishes are ₺800 each.

The home page also offers takeaway: call ahead and they'll prepare your order. Compare prices with [Bahçe](/restaurants/bahce-restaurant-kas/) and [Ehl-i Keyf](/restaurants/ehl-i-keyf-meyhane-kas/), the town's two big meze lists, in our [Kaş meze guide](/blog/kas-meze-and-meyhane-guide/).

## The setting

The address is Beyhan Cenkçi Caddesi No:7, Andifli, 07580 Kaş. The contact page also prints a fuller "No:7/2, İç Kapı No:2". Beyhan Cenkçi Caddesi runs from the town towards the Çukurbağ Peninsula, so you can walk from the harbour. The site describes "a cool, calm place in Kaş's summer heat", and its photos show a courtyard under a vine pergola, with red tables against a stone wall. It gives no seat count and mentions no sea view.

## When to go

The home page lists **16:00 to 22:00 every day**. The contact page gives no hours and no season, and many Kaş restaurants shorten their week in winter. It's an early-evening place: go before a walk round the harbour, or after a day at [Kaputaş Beach](/things-to-do/kaputas-beach/) or on a [Kekova boat trip](/blog/kekova-boat-trips/).
