Ayar Meyhanesi has served in Kaleiçi since 2017. The restaurant’s official introduction centres on fresh seafood and meze. It is located on Hesapçı Sokak in the old town.

## Food and setting

The venue refers to recognition in the İncili Gastronomy Guide. That is a different guide and is not a Michelin star. Its meyhane format is suited to a shared meze meal rather than a quiet tasting-menu experience.

## Plan your visit

Reserve through the official contact and ask about the evening’s music programme, table area and current menu if these matter to your visit.

## Published address

Kılınçarslan Mah., Hesapçı Sok. No:59, Muratpaşa, Antalya
