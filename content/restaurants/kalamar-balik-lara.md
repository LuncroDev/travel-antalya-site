Kalamar Balık is a seafood restaurant in Eski Lara, the older part of Antalya's Lara district. Its own pages promise fish and seafood bought fresh every day, cooked by its chefs to traditional recipes with modern touches, and served at sea-view tables on the cliffs. The site carries a 2026 copyright and a full online menu, in Turkish.

## The food

The menu is long and built for sharing: soups, salads, 34 cold meze, 36 hot starters, 27 mains and 12 desserts. The restaurant says dishes and availability change with the season. Examples:

- **Deniz mahsulleri çorbası**, a seafood soup with crab, octopus, shrimp and squid.
- **Lakerda**, salted bonito served with onion.
- **Levrek marin**, marinated sea bass with mustard.
- **Girit kabak**, roast courgette with yoghurt and walnuts, and **pembe pimpirik**, beetroot with yoghurt and pistachio.
- **Gillardeau oysters.**
- **Grilled octopus**, boiled first and then grilled, one of the dishes the menu highlights.
- **Kalamar tava**, fried squid with tarator (a garlicky nut sauce).
- **Balık kokoreç**, mixed seafood with tomato and chilli in a hot casserole.
- **Balık mantı**, seafood dumplings with garlic yoghurt.
- **Levrek simit**, sea bass baked in puff pastry.
- **Kalkan (turbot)**, pan-fried, grilled or baked in the tandır oven.
- **Black Sea-style levrek buğlama**, steamed sea bass.
- **Lahos terleme**, grouper cooked with butter, potato and garlic.
- **Lobster thermidor** and **king crab** for a special night.
- **Sıcak incir**, warm fig with honey and ice cream, and **katmer**, a thin pastry with pistachio and kaymak.

The site shows no prices and has no drinks page, so ask about rakı and wine when you book.

## The setting

The address is Çağlayan Mahallesi, Lara Caddesi No. 323/1, Muratpaşa. The restaurant places itself on the Antalya cliffs (Falezler) and leans on its sea view throughout the site. It pitches itself for family meals, business dinners, birthdays and anniversaries. Seating, terrace size and capacity are not described. Lara's cliff-top coast is also where the [Lower Düden waterfall](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/) drops into the sea; see our [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/) for the area.

## When to go

Opening hours are not published on the site. A sea view is worth most in daylight or at sunset, so ask for an early table and a seat facing the water.

## Booking

Reservations are by phone on +90 535 458 49 39; the site's booking buttons simply dial that number. There is no online form. Dress code, children's facilities and parking are not published.
