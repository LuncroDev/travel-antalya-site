Pablito offers a broad international menu within Akra Antalya. Cuban-inspired decor, coffee and cocktails are part of its identity, while the food ranges across grills, pizzas and locally influenced fusion dishes.

## Food and setting

The menu breadth makes this a practical alternative to a more formal dining room when a group wants different dishes. It is a separate venue from Asmani even though both are in the same hotel.

## Plan your visit

The official page lists service from 08:00 to 23:20. Confirm kitchen hours, reservations and outside-guest access with Akra before travelling.
