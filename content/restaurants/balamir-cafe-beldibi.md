Balamir Cafe is an all-day café-restaurant in Beldibi, the resort village outside Kemer town, at Molla Musa Caddesi 26/1 in Bahçecik Mahallesi. The site dates the business to 2004 and claims more than 20 years in the trade. It is clearly current: the footer reads © 2026, the menu says prices were last updated on 18 August 2026, and the blog posts are dated August 2026. Every price is in lira with VAT included, and the menu warns that a service charge may be added.

## The food

Think beach-café menu with a grill section, not a fish house. Prices as listed on the site:

- **Breakfast:** only egg dishes appear online. Menemen is ₺220 (₺230 with extra cheese), omelettes start at ₺220 and fried eggs with sucuk are ₺180.
- **Grills:** meat döner runs from ₺350 for 100 g to ₺450 for 200 g, chicken döner from ₺330. Adana kebab is ₺550 and grilled köfte ₺420.
- **Pizza, burgers and pasta:** pizzas go from a margherita at ₺390 to a mixed pizza at ₺440. Burgers start at ₺420 for chicken, topped by the house Balamir burger at ₺755. Spaghetti bolognese is ₺430, mantı ₺430, a Caesar salad ₺480 and toasties from ₺195.
- **Desserts:** lemon, raspberry and San Sebastián cheesecakes (₺240–280), plus tiramisu for ₺250.

One clash to note: the blog prices the house burger at ₺540, the August menu at ₺755. Go by the menu.

Drinks fill most of the list. Turkish coffee is ₺100 and a cappuccino ₺155, with smoothies, milkshakes, frozen drinks and mocktails besides. Cocktails such as a mojito or piña colada cost ₺500, a pineapple margarita ₺600, and local beers start at ₺200. The menu also has vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free filters.

## The setting

The site lists an air-conditioned indoor room, an open-air area, a pool, sunbeds and Wi-Fi. Its blog adds a large terrace with green seating and says the sea is close, without giving a distance. The gallery page is still marked as coming soon. Beldibi's big resorts, such as [Rixos Sungate](/hotels/rixos-sungate/) and [AKKA Antedon](/hotels/akka-antedon/), are in our [Kemer hotels](/hotels/?area=Kemer) list.

## When to go

The footer and contact page give 10:00 to midnight, with a note that hours change with the season. The blog posts say 08:00 to 23:00 every day instead, so check before you come for breakfast. The home page advertises a happy hour with 25% off all drinks and a weekend brunch, but gives no times for either. With a pool and sunbeds, this is a summer daytime stop first. In winter, ring ahead.
