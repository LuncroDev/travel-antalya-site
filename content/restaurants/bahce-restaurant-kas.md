Bahçe Restaurant is a meze restaurant on Uzunçarşı in the centre of Kaş. Its menu is the reason to look it up: dozens of cold starters, each with a price in lira, plus hot starters, kebabs and grills. The site was updated through 2026: its menu pages carry 2026 modification dates, the latest in October. It gives no opening hours or history, so treat it as a place to check by phone first.

## The food

The menu is organised like a meyhane's, around meze. Prices as listed on the site:

- **Olive-oil mezes (330–420 TL):** stuffed artichoke and artichoke hearts, vine leaves with sour cherries, stuffed courgette flowers, samphire, fava, hummus, mücver (courgette fritters), şakşuka and a pomegranate salad with walnuts.
- **Yoghurt mezes (330–340 TL):** haydari with dill, mütebbel, roasted aubergine with garlic yoghurt, beetroot with green apple, and "atom", dried hot pepper in garlicky yoghurt.
- **Seafood mezes (500–530 TL):** marinated sea bass, smoked anchovy, mustard shrimp, uskumru çiroz (dried mackerel) and lakerda (salted bonito).
- **Hot starters (400–1,200 TL):** shrimp or calamari in the pan for 880 TL, octopus with butter and garlic for 1,200 TL, grilled halloumi, içli köfte, Albanian-style liver and hummus with pastırma.

The main-course list has meat dishes, kebabs, steaks, seafood, chicken, vegetable dishes and pasta. We didn't open every sub-page, so check the site for those prices.

## The setting

The name means "garden". The site doesn't describe the seating, but its one photo shows a whitewashed arched gateway under bougainvillea, opening onto a paved path and tables in a garden. For the rest of the town, see our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/). Other Kaş restaurants with pages on this site include [Etna Fish House](/restaurants/etna-fish-house-kas/) and [Elia](/restaurants/elia-restaurant-kas/), and [Ehl-i Keyf Meyhane](/restaurants/ehl-i-keyf-meyhane-kas/) is another meze evening in town.

## When to go

Evening is meze time. Order a spread of cold plates to share, then one or two hot starters. In winter many Kaş restaurants shorten their hours, and Bahçe publishes none, so phone before you walk up. A day of [diving](/blog/diving-in-kas/) or a [Kekova boat trip](/blog/kekova-boat-trips/) ends well here.

## Booking

Call +90 242 836 23 70. The Turkish home page gives the address as Andifli Mahallesi, Uzunçarşı Caddesi No. 33; the English and Russian pages say No. 29. To stay in town, browse [Kaş hotels](/hotels/?area=Ka%C5%9F).
