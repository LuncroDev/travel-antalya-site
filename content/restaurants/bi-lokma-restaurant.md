Bi Lokma’s official site describes breakfast, garden dining, meze and freshly prepared Turkish dishes. It is on Uğur Mumcu Caddesi in Andifli and offers a broad menu rather than a fixed tasting format.

## Food and setting

Published dishes include home-style mantı, meat-stuffed vine leaves, grilled fish, liver and oven-baked rice pudding. It is a useful option when a group wants recognizable Turkish dishes and a slower garden meal.

## Plan your visit

Use the official reservation or contact page, especially for a group or a peak-season evening. Confirm opening dates and which breakfast or main menu is available for your planned time.

## Published address

Andifli, Uğur Mumcu Cd. No:21, Kaş, Antalya
