Sıra Meze Arasıcak is built around two parts of a Turkish meal: meze, the small cold plates, and ara sıcak, the hot starters that come before the main course. Its website says the aim is to carry classic recipes to a new generation of diners, with modern presentation, fresh ingredients and plates made for sharing. There are two branches in Antalya, one in Gürsu in Konyaaltı and one in Çağlayan in Lara. The site carries a © 2026 footer and also advertises franchising.

## The food

The website names five highlights:

- **Sıra Bomba**, the house signature. The site doesn't say what goes into it, so ask.
- **Courgette flowers stuffed with smoked cheese.**
- **A hot-starter plate with honey yoghurt.**
- **Sıra-style karnıyarık bites**, a small version of karnıyarık, aubergine filled with minced meat.
- **Spiced roasted aubergine purée.**

The Konyaaltı branch's digital menu goes further than meze. It has sections for meze, hot starters, red meat, chicken, fish, salads, and desserts and fruit, plus soft drinks, alcoholic drinks and cocktails. It lists no dishes or prices under those headings, and neither does the website. The idea is to share: order several cold plates and a couple of hot ones for the table, then decide on a main course. For more on the local food, read our guide to [what Antalya is known for](/blog/what-food-is-antalya-known-for-local-dishes-to-try/).

## The setting

The site doesn't describe the rooms. Its three photos show a laid table at Konyaaltı, and the dining room and table service at Lara. The Konyaaltı branch is on Atatürk Bulvarı (No. 231) in Gürsu. [Iva Sushi Bar](/restaurants/iva-sushi-bar-konyaalti/) is in the same quarter. The Lara branch is at Lara Caddesi No. 329/C in Çağlayan, Muratpaşa. That puts it in the same quarter as [Sıralı Kebap](/restaurants/sirali-kebap-antalya/) and [Kalamar Balık](/restaurants/kalamar-balik-lara/).

## When to go

Neither branch publishes opening hours, so phone before you set off. Meze is made for a slow evening with a group, and our guide to [ocakbaşı and meyhane nights](/blog/ocakbasi-and-meyhane-nights-in-antalya/) explains how that kind of meal works. Pick the branch that suits your day: Gürsu after an afternoon on [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/), or Çağlayan after sunset at [Lower Düden](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/) or a day on [Lara Beach](/things-to-do/lara-beach/).
