Elia is a seafood and meze restaurant on the Çukurbağ Peninsula, west of Kaş town. It calls its style a "modern meyhane": the long, slow evening of a Turkish tavern, updated for today. The name comes from the olive tree, and the restaurant's own story puts local fish and an open fire at the centre of the kitchen.

## The food

The restaurant says its fish comes from local fishermen, naming sea bass, sea bream, calamari and octopus, and that meat and seasonal vegetables are cooked over a wood or charcoal fire. The food menu bears that out:

- **Seafood meze selection**, dressed with herbs and citrus, alongside yoghurt-based and olive-oil meze.
- **Octopus**, either marinated in the house sauce or grilled over wood.
- **Fried calamari** with tarator, the garlicky nut sauce.
- **Sweet chilli shrimp.**
- **Grilled sea bream or sea bass**, plus sea bass wrapped in halloumi and sea bass with basil sauce.
- **Paçanga börek**, pastry filled with pastırma (cured beef) and kaşar cheese.
- **Edirne-style liver**, cut thin and pan-fried.
- **Lamb chops, braised beef and grilled köfte** for those who want meat.
- **Fig dessert with ice cream.**

Salads include rocket, Greek, shepherd's and avocado. A separate snack menu lists a hamburger, which the site says is not served at dinner. Prices are shown in Turkish lira.

## The setting

The site sells Elia on its view: the Çukurbağ Peninsula, the sea and the Kaş sunset, with the sound of the waves and music in the background. It does not describe the seating, so ask for a table with a sea view when you book if that matters. The address is Çukurbağ Yarımadası, Beyhan Cenkçi Caddesi No. 7, Kaş. Our [Kaş district guide](/destinations/kas/) shows where the peninsula sits in relation to the harbour.

## When to go

Elia opens **every day from 14:00 to midnight**, according to both its English and Turkish pages. Arrive before sunset for the view, then settle in for meze. The restaurant asks you to get in touch first for special events.
