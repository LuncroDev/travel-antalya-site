MykOrini is a Greek island-themed restaurant at The Land of Legends, the theme-park complex in Kadriye, Belek. The address is Kadriye Mahallesi, Atatürk Caddesi No. 515/1. SWOT Hospitality runs it, and its menus are dated October 2026. Some page titles on the site still mention Istanbul, so make sure you are reading the Antalya pages.

## The food

The site promises authentic Greek flavours with a modern touch: fresh seafood, Greek island dishes and reworked classics. The only dishes it names in the page text are the chef's meze, an octopus salad and a range of salads. The full kids', main, drinks and dessert menus are PDFs, and the site shows no prices in its page text, so check the menus before you go. The site adds that all prices are subject to tax.

## The setting

The site promises an "island wonderland" with an aquatic feel, and evenings with live entertainment: sirtaki, plate smashing and the Zorba dance. Expect a lively, theatrical dinner rather than a quiet one. It shares the complex with [NEMO](/restaurants/nemo-restaurant-lounge/), [Asil](/restaurants/asil-restaurant-kadriye/) and [Piazzetta Italiana](/restaurants/piazzetta-italiana-belek/), and [The Land of Legends Kingdom Hotel](/hotels/the-land-of-legends-kingdom-hotel/) is part of the same complex.

## When to go

The contact page gives 16:00 to midnight, seven days a week, with the last food order at 23:00 and last drinks at 23:15. The site's page summary says 01:00 instead; go by the contact page. No season or closing dates are published.

## Booking

The contact page links an online booking form and WhatsApp (+90 507 634 08 34); email dine@swothospitality.com. Some pages also link NEMO's booking page instead, so check you are booking the right restaurant. The house rules:

- **Dress code:** "elegant". The restaurant says it may refuse service for unsuitable clothing.
- **Cancellations:** at least two hours ahead, or you may be charged.
- **Special dates:** New Year's Eve, Valentine's Day and public holidays need pre-payment.
