Lara Balık operates separate Işıklar and Konyaaltı restaurants. This profile refers to Işıklar at Tevfik Işık Caddesi No:8, a useful distinction when arranging transport or a reservation.

## Food and setting

The restaurant’s focus is seasonal fish, seafood and a broad meze selection. Choose fish after asking what is available that day, rather than assuming a fixed menu from an older review.

## Plan your visit

The official contact page asks guests to reserve by telephone. Confirm the Işıklar branch and ask whether fish is priced per portion or by weight before ordering.

## Published address

Gençlik, Tevfik Işık Cd. No:8, Muratpaşa, Antalya
