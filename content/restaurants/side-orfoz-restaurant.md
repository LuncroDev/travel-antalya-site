Side Orfoz is on Nar Sokak near Liman Caddesi in ancient Side. Its official menu and description combine seafood with Turkish and international dishes in a sea-view setting.

## Food and setting

This is the Side restaurant, not Orfoz in Bodrum. Michelin recognition associated with the Bodrum venue must not be attached to this business. The published menu includes fish, seafood plates, pasta and meat dishes.

## Plan your visit

Use the official contact to reserve, ask about terrace seating and confirm the current menu. Allow for walking within the ancient town rather than assuming direct car access to the entrance.

## Published address

Side Selimiye Mah., Liman Cad., Nar Sok. No:5, Manavgat, Antalya
