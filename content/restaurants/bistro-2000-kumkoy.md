Bistro 2000 is a steakhouse, sports bar and shisha lounge in Kumköy, the resort strip along the Ilıca coast west of ancient Side. It's a casual, holiday-style place for a fry-up in the morning, a steak or kebab at dinner and a match on the big screens afterwards. The About page says it has been open since 1999, and the home page says it has spent 26 years in the same spot. Every page carries a "© 2026" footer. The contact page gives the address as Ilıca Mahallesi, Sahil Caddesi 35. Sokak, Kumköy Caddesi No. 2, Manavgat.

## The food

The menu lists every price in euros, pounds and lira. Here are the euro prices, with the lira price in brackets, as we read them on 20 November 2026:

- **Breakfast:** Full English €15.00 (₺862.50) or Turkish breakfast €8.00 (₺460.00).
- **Steaks:** lokum steak, 200 g, €25.00 (₺1,437.50); rib-eye €35.00 (₺2,012.50); T-bone, 400 g, €45.00 (₺2,587.50); Chateaubriand, 550 g, €80.00 (₺4,600.00).
- **Turkish grills:** Adana, Urfa or shish kebab at €18.00 each (₺1,035.00); testi kebab for two €50.00 (₺2,875.00); mixed grill for two €56.00 (₺3,220.00).
- **Fish and others:** grilled sea bass or sea bream €18.00 (₺1,035.00), fajitas €28.00 (₺1,610.00) and vegetable casserole €13.00 (₺747.50).
- **Children:** a kids' menu runs from chicken nuggets at €6.00 (₺345.00) to a 100 g fillet steak with chips at €13.00 (₺747.50).
- **Drinks:** draught Efes, 50 cl, €5.00 (₺287.50); rakı or a glass of wine, €6.00 each (₺345.00); shisha €10.00 (₺575.00).

The restaurant says its beef cuts are carefully chosen and cooked to the doneness you ask for. It doesn't say where the beef comes from or whether it's aged.

## The setting

The sports-bar page describes an indoor room and a large outdoor terrace, with big HD screens visible from every table. They show football (the Premier League and the Champions League among others), Formula 1, the NBA, rugby, boxing and more, depending on what's being broadcast. Families are welcome, and the drinks list has mocktails for children. For a quieter dinner by the sea in the old town, try [Side Orfoz](/restaurants/side-orfoz-restaurant/) at the harbour or [Zula Side](/restaurants/zula-side/).

## When to go

The published hours are breakfast 08:00–12:00, lunch 12:00–15:00, happy hour 15:00–17:30, dinner 17:30–22:00 and the bar 15:00–02:00. The site recommends booking, especially on summer evenings. Come for breakfast before a walk round [Side Ancient City](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/), or for dinner after a [Manavgat river boat trip](/blog/manavgat-river-boat-trip/).

## Booking

Book by WhatsApp on +90 532 265 99 67, by email to info@bistro2000.net or through the reservation page. The home page offers a free evening pick-up within about 6–7 km for two or more guests. The steak page puts a dolmuş (shared minibus) ride at about €2 a person. Our [Side day trip guide](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/), the [Manavgat and Side district page](/destinations/manavgat/) and the list of [Side hotels](/hotels/?area=Side) cover the rest of the area.
