Piazzetta Italiana is an Italian restaurant in Kadriye, in the Belek resort area of Serik district. It calls itself "casual chic" Italian family dining, with some ingredients imported from Italy and local produce on a seasonal menu. The menus online carry prices "valid as of 07.10.2026". It belongs to SWOT Hospitality, which also runs the Asil restaurant at the same address.

## The food

The à la carte menu is in Italian with Turkish translations. It runs through:

- **Starters and salads**: parmigiana di melanzane, fritto misto, beef carpaccio, burrata, caprese, a Niçoise-style "Nizzarda" and Caesar salad, plus seafood soup and minestrone.
- **Pizza**: nine, from Margherita and Quattro Formaggi to Diavola, an "Alla Turca" and the house Piazzetta.
- **Pasta and risotto**: carbonara, arrabbiata, pappardelle Bolognese, ravioli and linguine with king prawns, linguine alle vongole, lobster tagliatelle and a signature lasagna. The risottos are mushroom, and king prawn "alla Siciliana".
- **Mains**: Mediterranean-style sea bass, salmon meunière, king prawns, a seafood platter for two, ossobuco, beef fillet and chicken Milanese.
- **Desserts**: tiramisu, chocolate fondant, panna cotta, cheesecake, affogato and homemade ice cream.

All prices are in Turkish lira with VAT included. On the PDF the prices sit apart from the dish names, so ask the price of a dish when you order rather than relying on what we could read. The drinks list is clearer: every cocktail, from a Negroni to the house Sicilian Sunset, is 850 TL, and the three mocktails are 650 TL each. There is a separate kids' menu, and the site says group menus exist too. No service charge is mentioned.

## The setting

The address is Kadriye Mahallesi, Atatürk 3 Caddesi No. 1/11, Serik. The site describes a spacious dining room with a pizza counter and a bar, plus what it calls a "panoramic alfresco" area for eating outside. It does not say what the outdoor area looks over, and it mentions no sea view. Our [Serik district guide](/destinations/serik/) covers Kadriye and Belek.

## When to go

The site gives daily hours of 12:30 to 23:00, with last food orders at 23:15. The two times conflict, so arrive by 22:00 for a full dinner. It's an easy evening out for guests at [Belek and Kadriye hotels](/hotels/?area=Belek) who want a break from the buffet. By day, the [Aspendos theatre](/things-to-do/aspendos-theatre/) is the classic outing from this coast.
