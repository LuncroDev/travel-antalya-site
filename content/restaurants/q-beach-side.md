Q Beach Side is a restaurant and lounge on the beach at Evrenseki, the resort strip east of Side. Its tables stand on a lawn facing the sea, and the site sells it as a place to stay all day, from Turkish breakfast to a cocktail at midnight. It is a seasonal venue: the site says it is **closed during the winter season**, so plan it for spring to autumn. The address is Evrenseki Mah., Beach Park No. 43, Side – Manavgat, and the website carries a "© 2026" footer.

## The food

The food menu, a PDF in Turkish, English, German and Russian, is long and priced in Turkish lira. Some examples as listed:

- **Breakfast:** a breakfast platter for 900 TL, or a mixed breakfast for two for 2,000 TL.
- **From the sea:** grilled sea bass or sea bream (400–600 g) for 1,200 TL, grilled salmon fillet for 1,400 TL, fried calamari or shrimp for 1,300 TL, and a mixed fish platter for two for 8,000 TL.
- **Grills and steaks:** grilled meatballs for 1,100 TL, a mixed grill platter for 2,350 TL, and steaks from 2,200 TL.
- **Local dishes:** clay-pot kebabs for two, from 3,750 TL for chicken to 5,000 TL for beef.
- **Lighter plates:** pizzas from 750 TL, salads from 650 TL and pasta from 750 TL. There are vegetarian and gluten-free sections too.

The drinks menu adds beer, local and imported spirits, classic and frozen cocktails, and illy coffee.

## The setting

The photos show a lawn running down to the sand, sofas in a lounge area and loungers on the beach. It is an open-air place built for the summer. If you want old-town atmosphere instead, [Kamer](/restaurants/kamer-restaurant-side/) and [Zula](/restaurants/zula-side/) are in Side's old town; [Bistro 2000](/restaurants/bistro-2000-kumkoy/) is in Kumköy.

## When to go

The site gives the hours as every day 09:30–23:55 and says it closes for winter, without dates. Go for a long lunch on a beach day, or for dinner at sunset, when the gallery shows the tables lit with lanterns. Pair it with the [Temple of Apollo and the ruins](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/) or a [Manavgat river boat trip](/blog/manavgat-river-boat-trip/).
