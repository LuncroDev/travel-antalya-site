Kale Panorama is on Kale Caddesi in Tophane, on the route toward Alanya Castle. Its official site emphasizes the historic setting and terraces with city views.

## Food and setting

The draw is the elevated setting as well as the meal, so it belongs in a different part of an itinerary from central harbour restaurants. Consult the current menu rather than relying on old tour-operator package descriptions.

## Plan your visit

Reserve and confirm the terrace, access arrangements and how to get there. Transfers promoted by third-party travel sites are not assumed to be included in every direct booking.

## Published address

Tophane Mah., Kale Caddesi No:15, Alanya, Antalya
