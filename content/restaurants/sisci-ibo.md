Şiş köfte with tahini piyaz is one of Antalya's best-known local meals, and Şişçi İbo is a family business built around it. Its website says the trade began with the founders' grandfather in Korkuteli in the 1950s, that the name Şişçi İbo dates from 1974, and that the third generation now runs it.

## The food

Start with the two dishes the restaurant is named for. The şiş köfte is minced meat grilled on a skewer. The site says it is made from male goat (erkeç) raised in the Beydağları mountains and served in 180 g portions. Order it with **piyaz**, Antalya's white-bean salad dressed with tahini. The Lara menu also lists a spicy version with onion and a dish called Şişkender.

The rest of the menu is a full grill house: chicken and lamb skewers, lamb chops and ribs, beef cuts and a dry-aged steak section. Among the meze are hibeş, the spicy tahini dip typical of Antalya, plus muhammara and haydari. For dessert there is kabak tatlısı (candied pumpkin), Arap kadayıfı and oven-baked rice pudding.

## The setting

There are three branches. The Lara branch is at Fener Mah., Fener Cad., Beyaz Dünya İş Merkezi No. 15 in Muratpaşa. The Konyaaltı branch is at Arapsuyu Mah., 600 Sok. No. 47, opposite the PTT Kargo entrance. The third is in Korkuteli, inland. Both city branches are in residential districts rather than the old town, so expect a local grill house, not a sea-view terrace.

## When to go

Lunch is a good time for a şiş köfte and piyaz stop. The Konyaaltı branch suits a meal before or after [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/). The Lara branch is convenient if you are staying in one of the [Lara hotels](/hotels/?area=Lara%20%26%20Kundu). For another take on Antalya's local dishes, compare [Tirmis](/restaurants/tirmis/) or [Sıralı Kebap](/restaurants/sirali-kebap-antalya/).

## Booking

The site gives takeaway phone numbers for each branch, for example (242) 337 07 07 for Lara. It does not publish opening hours or an online booking system. Call ahead for a large group. For more on the city's neighbourhoods, see our [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/).
