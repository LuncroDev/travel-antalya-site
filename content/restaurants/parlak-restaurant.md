Founded in 1954, Parlak is set inside the historic Zincirli Han near Antalya’s clock tower. Its official site lists Turkish pot dishes, meze, charcoal-grilled chicken, meat and fish.

## Food and setting

The han location gives a different atmosphere from the seafront dining rooms. Charcoal chicken is the venue’s stated speciality; its wider menu makes it suitable for a traditional sit-down meal in the city centre.

## Plan your visit

The venue publishes daily 12:00–00:00 hours. Use the official directions into Zincirli Han and reserve for a group or a particular seating area.
