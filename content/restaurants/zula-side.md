Zula is a restaurant in Side, in Manavgat district, that sells itself on the view as much as the food. Its pages talk of the Mediterranean at sunset, sea breezes and moonlight, and describe a kitchen built on Mediterranean cooking with Ottoman dishes and a few world-food detours. The site carries a 2026 copyright and a long menu in Turkish.

## The food

The menu runs from breakfast to dinner. It names every dish but shows no prices (checked 14 November 2026), so ask before you order. Highlights:

- **Serpme kahvaltı**, the Turkish spread breakfast: Ezine, village, string and İzmir Bergama tulum cheeses, Aegean olives, Afyon kaymak (clotted cream), Erzincan honeycomb, churned butter, green-fig and kumquat jams, butcher's sucuk, village eggs, fruit, nuts and mini bazlama. Menemen and omelettes are listed too.
- Cold starters such as **sea bass tartare**, tuna and beef tartare, buffalo burrata, and **sardines cooked over a wood fire** with field tomatoes.
- Hot starters including **atom ahtapot**, octopus cooked down with garlic, shallots and chilli-spiked wine, and **Osmanlı mücveri**, fritters of spinach, chard and leek with strained yoghurt.
- Fish: grilled sea bass fillet, **levrek lokum** (boneless sea bass for two) and a mixed fish plate of sea bass, Norwegian salmon, octopus, tiger prawns and squid, for two or more.
- Ottoman-style meat: **çökertme kebabı** on buttered village bazlama with garlic yoghurt and matchstick potatoes, **beğendili köfte** with smoky aubergine and béchamel, and **testi kebabı**, meat or chicken baked in a clay jug, for two.
- Separate vegan and vegetarian sections: a bowl of karakılçık bulgur, mung beans, avocado, walnuts and dried apricot, grilled vegetables, and poplar mushrooms sautéed in olive oil and garlic.

Pizza, pasta, burgers and desserts such as San Sebastian cheesecake round it off.

## The setting

The address is Side Lale Sokak, Barbaros Caddesi No. 6, 07600 Manavgat. The FAQ places Zula in the centre of Side with a sea view. The site does not describe the seating, a terrace or an indoor room, and parking is not covered. Families get a children's menu and, says the FAQ, safe areas for children. Zula also hosts birthdays and company dinners.

Zula is the third Side restaurant on this site, after [Side Orfoz](/restaurants/side-orfoz-restaurant/) on the harbour and [Karma Side](/restaurants/karma-side/). Our [Manavgat district guide](/destinations/manavgat/) covers the wider area.

## When to go

Opening hours and season dates are not published on any page we checked. Breakfast is on the menu, but serving times are not given. The site itself pitches sunset. In winter, message before you go.

Dinner here fits neatly after a walk around [Side Ancient City](/things-to-do/side-ancient-city/); our [Side day trip guide](/blog/side-day-trip-from-antalya/) has the route.

The "Rezervasyon Yap" button opens WhatsApp on 0533 191 07 77, which is also the phone number. You can email info@zulaside.com. The FAQ recommends booking ahead in peak season and for evenings.
