Panorama Lake Restaurant is a fish and grill restaurant on a lake shore at Selimli Mevkii 5/2, in Değirmenli village in Manavgat district. The site says it has served guests looking for quiet and a lake view for more than 25 years, and smoked fish is the dish it pushes hardest. The footer is dated 2026, though the photos on the site were uploaded in 2024 and the social posts it embeds carry no dates.

## The food

The menu appears in Turkish, English, German and Russian, and none of the versions shows a price. The site says its prices suit every budget; ask for prices when you book.

- **Fish:** trout, sea bream (çupra) and sea bass, each baked or grilled, plus smoked mackerel, smoked eel and smoked trout.
- **Pans and pots:** sac kavurma (meat seared on a domed iron pan) and güveç (a clay-pot stew), each in lamb, beef or chicken.
- **Grills:** lamb and chicken şiş, köfte, and stoofvlees, the Flemish beef stew, an unusual find on a Turkish lake.
- **Salads:** white cheese and walnut, smoked trout, chicken, tuna or a plain salad, with chips on the side.
- **Desserts:** crêpes, a crêpe with ice cream, or vanilla ice cream with chocolate sauce and cream.

To drink there's red, white and rosé wine, Efes and Tuborg beer, rakı, vodka and sangria. Hot drinks run from Turkish coffee and apple tea to a latte.

## The setting

The site never names the lake. Its text talks of the lake view, blue water and boat tours, and it invites bookings for birthdays, proposals and other private events. The restaurant's Instagram posts on the site tag Oymapınar Dam, which is the closest it comes to saying where you'll be. It doesn't describe the seating or a terrace, and swimming appears only as a hashtag, so don't count on a swim unless the staff confirm it.

It works as a lunch stop on an inland day out. The [Manavgat Waterfall](/things-to-do/manavgat-waterfall/) and the [Manavgat river boat trip](/blog/manavgat-river-boat-trip/) are in the same district, and our [Manavgat guide](/destinations/manavgat/) covers the rest.

## When to go

The published hours are 09:00 to 21:00, every day of the week. A post on the site says breakfast started because so many guests asked for it, and another pitches an open-air weekend breakfast. No breakfast menu or serving times are given. A third post announces the opening of the summer season, but there are no season dates and nothing about winter. Outside summer, check the restaurant is open before you set off.
