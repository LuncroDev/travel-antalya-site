The Rabbit Hole is the restaurant of Olympos Mountain Lodge, a small country hotel in Beycik village, 900 m up at the foot of Mount Tahtalı above Kemer. Dinner is a single tasting menu that changes every evening. Chef Mehmet Ali Börtücene and his team cook it, and the lodge describes a sustainable, low-waste kitchen built on local, seasonal produce from the land and the sea. Gault&Millau Türkiye gave it two toques (13/20) in its 2026 guide, in its "Chefs' Tables" band.

## The food

There is no à la carte menu at dinner. You eat what the kitchen cooks that night, and the lodge says to contact it for the day's menu. The price on the restaurant and menus pages is **4,500 TL per person**; neither page says what that includes, so ask about drinks. An older packages page, last updated in 2024, described a six-course tasting dinner with wine pairing by the lodge's sommelier. The current pages give no course count.

By day, a snack menu runs from 12:00 to 17:00. Posts on the home page also advertise a weekend brunch on Saturdays and Sundays from 08:30.

The restaurant began in Ankara, where it ran for six years before the owners, Su and Mehmet Ali, took over the lodge in December 2020.

## The setting

The lodge sits in about 10 acres of garden and olive grove, and the site promises a panoramic view down to the Mediterranean. The home page's posts mention both a chef's table and the main dining room. Its welcome page puts Antalya about 70 km (an hour) away and the airport about 90 km (an hour and a half), with the last 5 km up from the coast road at the Beycik turn after Kemer and Tekirova. That page dates from 2022, so check the drive.

Nearby, the [Tahtalı cable car](/things-to-do/tahtali-olympos-cable-car/) and [Phaselis](/things-to-do/phaselis-ancient-city/) make a day of it. Our [Kemer district guide](/destinations/kemer/) covers the coast below.

## When to go

Dinner is served from 19:00 to 21:00, with the last sitting at 20:30. No closing days or season are published. The lodge is 5 km up from the coast road, so plan your drive back after dinner, or book a room.

You may see MICHELIN mentioned in the lodge's social posts. MICHELIN's website lists Olympos Mountain Lodge among its hotels, but the restaurant itself holds no MICHELIN distinction.

## Booking

Booking is required. Non-residents must reserve at least one day ahead, for dinner and for breakfast. Use WhatsApp or call +90 533 042 20 62, or email info@olymposmountainlodge.com; the home page also links an online booking form. Gault&Millau's 2026 profile lists a different phone number, so use the lodge's own.

The lodge publishes no dress code or children's policy. For more of the Gault&Millau list, read our [Gault&Millau Antalya guide](/blog/gault-millau-antalya-restaurants-2026/), and for the coast road, the [Kemer to Olympos drive](/blog/kemer-to-olympos-coast-road/).
