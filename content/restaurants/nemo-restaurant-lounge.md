NEMO is located at The Land of Legends in Kadriye, near Belek. The dining room faces a large aquarium, while the menu combines modern European seafood with Japanese and wider Asian dishes.

## Food and setting

Published menu examples include tempura, dim sum, gyoza, sushi, crispy-duck salad and sea bass. The restaurant advertises Gault & Millau recognition, which must not be described as a Michelin award.

## Plan your visit

Reserve directly and confirm the route into the restaurant and any venue-entry requirements. The current contact page lists lunch from 13:00, dinner from 18:00 to midnight and the last food order at 23:15.

## Published address

Kadriye Mah., Atatürk Caddesi No:515/1, The Land of Legends, Serik, Antalya
