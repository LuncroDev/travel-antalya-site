Hasanağa serves traditional Turkish food in a historic house with a garden courtyard in Kaleiçi. It is a comfortable place for a first-night dinner in the old town, with meze, grills and fish on the menu.

As in most old-town restaurants, the garden tables are the ones to ask for in summer.
