Göz Balık calls itself a seafood restaurant in Lara, and its address, Lara Caddesi 113/A, sits in Şirinyalı, Muratpaşa. The story on its home page is about looking after the fish rather than simply cooking it, with produce chosen fresh each day and music in the background. The site carries a 2026 copyright and a long priced menu in Turkish.

## The food

Prices below are from the menu as we saw it on 14 November 2026. They change, so check on the day.

- **Cold meze**: close to 40, from sliced Ezine cheese at ₺210 to octopus salad at ₺950. Seafood ones include **lakerda** (cured bonito, ₺480), **uskumru çiroz** (lemon-cured mackerel, ₺480) and sea bass marinated with peppercorn mustard (₺550). On the vegetable side: muhammara (₺350), purslane with beetroot, yoghurt and almonds (₺390), and **kale biberi**, stuffed peppers with a walnut-butter sauce (₺500).
- **Hot starters**: around 30. Try **Göz tava**, shrimp with spinach and sun-dried tomato in a white wine cream (₺900); **karides mantı**, crispy shrimp in yufka pastry over yoghurt with sumac butter (₺820); **levrek simit**, sesame-coated sea bass (₺715 a piece); or a grilled octopus leg with garlic and chilli (₺1,320).
- **Fish and mains**: sea bass, rock bass, whiting, red mullet, grida, mercan and jumbo prawns are sold by the kilo. Fixed prices include fish köfte (₺850), steamed sea bass (₺1,100) and a sea bass skewer with tomato, pepper and bay leaf (₺2,100). For non-fish eaters: köfte (₺850), lamb chops (₺1,600) and a 180 g beef tenderloin (₺1,550).
- **Desserts**: candied pumpkin or kadayıf with kaymak, and a fig dessert, each ₺480.

Drinks lean towards rakı. Yeni Rakı starts at ₺370 for 4 cl and runs to ₺3,300 for 70 cl. House wine is ₺550 a glass, and Turkish coffee ₺110. The menu lists no cover or service charge.

## The setting

The full address is Şirinyalı Mahallesi, Lara Caddesi 113/A, 07160 Muratpaşa. The site promises a warm, friendly atmosphere with background music. It does not mention a sea view, a terrace, the number of tables or parking, so do not count on any of them. Our [Muratpaşa district guide](/destinations/muratpasa/) covers this stretch of the city.

## When to go

Neither the home page nor the menu gives opening hours, so phone first, above all for lunch. A long meze-and-rakı dinner suits this kind of menu best. By day, the lower [Düden Waterfalls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/), on the coast in the Lara area, make an easy outing.

## Booking

Call or WhatsApp +90 543 529 99 86, the only number on the site. It does not say whether a reservation is required. Card payment is not published.
