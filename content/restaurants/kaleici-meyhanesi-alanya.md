Kaleiçi Meyhanesi is a meyhane on İskele Caddesi, the harbour street below [Alanya Castle](/things-to-do/alanya-castle/), not to be confused with Antalya city's old town of the same name. A meyhane evening is built on cold mezes, a few hot starters and rakı, with a grill or a fish to finish, and this one publishes its full menu with prices. The digital menu we used carries a 2026 footer.

## The food

The English menu lists 60 dishes and 43 drinks. Prices below are in Turkish lira as seen on 17 November 2026:

- **Mezes:** 33 of them, the backbone of the menu. Humus is 310 TL, lakerda (cured bonito) 440 TL and a plain white cheese 125 TL.
- **Hot starters:** five, including fried calamari at 725 TL and Spanish-style shrimp at 800 TL.
- **Mains:** 15. Sea bass and sea bream (400 g) are 800 TL each, Tekirdağ köfte 750 TL, chicken skewers 700 TL, lamb chops (200 g) 1,300 TL and sliced beef tenderloin (300 g) 2,000 TL.
- **Salads and desserts:** an arugula salad at 370 TL and katmer, the flaky pastry, at 525 TL.

Drinks are where it stands out: 30 rakı entries, from a 20 cl Efe Gold at 1,200 TL to a 35 cl Yeni Rakı at 1,330 TL and a full litre at 3,100 TL. Seven wines start with Buzbağ red at 1,200 TL a bottle. Beer runs from 295 TL for a Tuborg to 450 TL for a Guinness. No service charge is mentioned.

## The setting

The address is Çarşı Mahallesi, İskele Caddesi No. 80, in the harbour area. The menu shows no photos of the room, and we found no official page describing the seating, music or views, so ask when you book.

## When to go

Make it the end of a day on the castle hill and [Cleopatra Beach](/things-to-do/cleopatra-beach/); our [Alanya in a day](/blog/alanya-in-a-day/) plan finishes on this street. [Güverte](/restaurants/guverte-restaurant-alanya/), on the same street, is the alternative if you want a broader menu.
