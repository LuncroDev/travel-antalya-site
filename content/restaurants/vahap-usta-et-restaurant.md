Vahap’s published menu is organized around beef and lamb cuts, dry-aged meat, kebabs, köfte and burgers, with starters and meze alongside. Its location is in Gürsu on Konyaaltı’s western side.

## Food and setting

Published examples include ali nazik and beyti sarma, with starters such as içli köfte and thin-sliced liver. This is a dedicated restaurant rather than a beach club, and the range suits diners choosing between Turkish grills and steak.

## Plan your visit

Use the venue’s reservation contact. Confirm the current cut, portion size and price before ordering premium meat, and request specific dietary accommodation directly.

## Published address

Gürsu, Atatürk Blv. No:160, Konyaaltı, Antalya
