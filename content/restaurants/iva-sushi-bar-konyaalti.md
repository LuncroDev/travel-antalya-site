Iva Sushi Bar & World Cuisine is a sushi restaurant in the Gürsu quarter of Konyaaltı, and the website says it's a short distance from the beach, with plenty of parking. It opened in 2014 and once had a branch in Kaleiçi. That branch has moved, and the site now says all its service is in Konyaaltı. Next to the sushi there are more than 80 cooked dishes, from noodles to steak, so it works for a mixed group. The website carries a "© 2026" footer. The contact page gives the address as Gürsu Mahallesi 355. Sokak, N Blok No. 15/NG.

## The food

The menu runs to 35 sections with full ingredient lists. Prices in Turkish lira, as we read them on 20 November 2026:

- **Nigiri:** from kani (crab stick) at 110 TL to unagi (eel) at 270 TL; salmon is 160 TL. The menu doesn't say how many pieces you get.
- **Special rolls:** 23 of them, from the Vegetarian Roll (420 TL) to the Philadelphia Deluxe (950 TL). The Philadelphia and California rolls cost 690 TL each, and the Anatolia Roll, with tuna and sea bass, costs 890 TL.
- **Sets:** the 26-piece vegetarian set costs 990 TL, the 24-piece Iva Roll Set 1,470 TL and the 70-piece Iva Big Set 5,150 TL.
- **Starters:** edamame (350 TL), shrimp tempura (690 TL) and salmon tartare with avocado and tobiko (790 TL).
- **Not sushi:** chicken noodles (470 TL), beef noodles (750 TL), a 280 g entrecôte in Spanish sauce (1,070 TL) and a beef fajita (1,190 TL).
- **Desserts and drinks:** fried ice cream (350 TL), tiramisu (360 TL), Asahi beer (300 TL) and Tsingtao (360 TL).

The site writes thousands with a dot, so "5.150 ₺" means 5,150 TL.

## The setting

The website calls it a restaurant near the sea in Konyaaltı. It doesn't mention a terrace or a sea view, so don't count on either. The gallery is captioned "moments from our restaurant and our sushi". The site also offers delivery within 15 km through WhatsApp, which is handy if you're staying in an apartment. Two neighbours offer something different. For grilled meat in the same district, try [Vahap Usta](/restaurants/vahap-usta-et-restaurant/) in Gürsu. For charcoal kebabs and meze, try [Közz Liman](/restaurants/kozz-liman-ocakbasi-konyaalti/).

## When to go

The site lists every day from 13:00 to midnight. That makes it an easy late lunch or dinner after an afternoon on [Konyaaltı Beach](/things-to-do/konyaalti-beach/), or after a rainy-day visit to [Antalya Aquarium](/things-to-do/antalya-aquarium/), also in Konyaaltı.

## Booking

Call or message +90 531 782 60 70, or use the form on the contact page. The restaurant also takes group bookings for birthdays and business dinners. The contact page says 355. Sokak, but the About page says Boğaçay Caddesi No. 65/A, so confirm the location when you book.
