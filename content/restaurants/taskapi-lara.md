Taşkapı Lara is a restaurant built around the evening's music: you come for dinner, a table of meze and Turkish singers on stage until past midnight. It is on Lara Caddesi in Çağlayan, on the city side of Lara, and says it is in its tenth year. We read its official website on 27 November 2026; the footer reads © 2026.

## The food

The menu follows the meyhane pattern, but the website names only categories, not dishes or prices: **cold and hot meze**, **grilled meats**, meats served sizzling on a cast-iron **sac**, and fresh seasonal salads. The restaurant's own photos show meze in small bowls and a pan flaring over the flame. Expect a set-menu-style night rather than a quick à la carte supper, and ask what is included when you book.

## The setting

The restaurant's photos show an indoor hall with a stage at one end, long tables and low lighting. Each weeknight has a resident singer, according to the website:

- **Tuesday and Friday:** Tuna Akkuş
- **Wednesday:** Meltem Leylekoğlu
- **Thursday and Saturday:** Erdinç Karadeniz

On **Sundays** the hall hosts special concerts with guest artists. The website lists Sunday dates in late November and December, but without a year, and one earlier date has already passed, so check the current line-up by phone. Posters on the site show Turkish singers such as Muazzez Ersoy and Kutsi as past or coming guests.

## When to go

The published hours are **Tuesday to Saturday, 21:00–00:15**, with Sunday concerts from **22:00 to midnight**. Monday is not listed, so treat it as a closing day. This is a late dinner: arrive at nine and plan to stay. For a quieter fish supper in the same part of town, compare [Barınak Balık Evi](/restaurants/barinak-balik-evi-lara/) or [Kalamar Balık](/restaurants/kalamar-balik-lara/), and see our guide to [live music in Antalya this winter](/blog/live-music-antalya-winter-venues/).

## Booking

Book ahead on **0549 355 07 07** by phone or WhatsApp; the website asks guests to reserve in advance. The address is Çağlayan Mahallesi, 2020 Sokak, Lara Caddesi No. 8, Muratpaşa.
