Oburus Momus is a vegan and vegetarian restaurant on the Kaş peninsula. Its menu draws on several cuisines, with vegetable bowls, noodles and dishes using ingredients such as tempeh.

## Food and setting

The official address is Adil Akba Sokak No:13. This is not a restaurant in the tight harbour streets, so plan transport rather than assuming it is a quick stroll from central Kaş.
