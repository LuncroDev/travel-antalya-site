The Fish Terrace sits on the roof above Korsan Meze, facing the bay and town beach. The official description emphasizes Mediterranean fish dishes, generally served off the bone, alongside non-fish choices.

## Food and setting

The venue has its own menu, reservations and telephone number. Turkish wines and cocktails are part of its offering. The rooftop setting differentiates it from the downstairs restaurant.

## Plan your visit

Reserve the Fish Terrace explicitly, and confirm seasonal service, stair/access requirements and your preferred table. Music schedules can change, so check directly if you are seeking a quiet evening.

## Published address
