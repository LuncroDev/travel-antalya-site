Usre Restoran is a large Turkish restaurant in Kepez, Antalya's northern district, on the road beside Kepez City Forest (Kepez Kent Ormanı). It serves from breakfast to late dinner: kebabs and köfte from the grill, pide and lahmacun from the oven, and a weekend open-buffet breakfast. The restaurant says it has been running for 16 years and is part of the Seçen group. Its site posted news as recently as August 2026, and the menu below is from its own QR menu page.

## The food

Prices are as we saw them on 15 November 2026. They change, so check on the day.

- **Grills**: Adana, Urfa and "Antalya" kebabs at ₺750 each, Paşa kebab at ₺900, butcher's köfte (kasap köfte) and şiş köfte at ₺650, and yoghurt-topped Tire köfte at ₺850.
- **Local dishes**: **Antalya-style piyaz**, the city's white-bean salad with tahini, is ₺300. The menu files it under dishes with a geographical indication.
- **Soups**: lentil ₺200, chicken ₺250, kelle paça ₺300.
- **Oven**: lahmacun ₺300 (a mini one ₺150), etli ekmek ₺450 and several filled pides.
- **Breakfast**: a single breakfast plate ₺600; a weekday breakfast tray for two ₺1,200; menemen from ₺165; kuymak (cornmeal and cheese) ₺320.
- **Desserts**: baklava with pistachio, oven rice pudding and **Manavgat-style pumpkin with tahini**, each ₺350.
- **Drinks**: tea ₺50, Turkish coffee ₺110.

The menu page also names İskender, lamb chops, beef fillet, slow-cooked lamb neck and künefe, but those had no prices on the QR menu when we looked.

## The weekend breakfast buffet

On Saturdays and Sundays from 08:00 to 14:00, Usre lays out an open breakfast buffet. Its July 2026 post lists white, kaşar, tulum and Ezine cheeses, menemen, omelettes, sigara böreği, pişi, sucuk, kaymak with honey, tahini and molasses, tandır bread and simit, with unlimited tea, juices and filter coffee. The QR menu prices it at ₺990 for adults and ₺390 for children aged 6 to 12. For more on the ritual, see our [Turkish breakfast guide](/blog/turkish-breakfast-in-antalya/).

## The setting

The address is Duacı, Kepez Kentormanı yanı yolu, 07220 Kepez. The restaurant describes itself as surrounded by nature, and its photos show a long building with a covered terrace beside pine trees. It publishes nothing on parking or a view. Our [Kepez district guide](/destinations/kepez/) covers the area.

## When to go

The site gives daily hours of 08:30 to 23:00, but the weekend buffet starts at 08:00. Weekend mornings are the time for breakfast; weekday evenings suit a grill dinner. It's a drive or taxi from the centre, so it fits a day that also takes in the [Düden Waterfalls](/things-to-do/duden-waterfalls/).

## Booking

Call (0242) 251 04 44 or email info@usrerestoran.com. The site's booking page has no form, and the restaurant recommends reserving ahead for the weekend buffet. For a smaller, older-style köfte and piyaz stop, compare [Ertan Usta](/restaurants/ertan-usta-aksu-kofte-piyaz-kepez/), and read [what food is Antalya known for](/blog/what-food-is-antalya-known-for-local-dishes-to-try/) before you order.
