Cullinan Links occupies a site beside the Beşgöz River and the Mediterranean. Aspendos and Olympos each have 18 holes. The Termessos floodlit routing combines their back nines; it is not a third separate 18-hole physical course.

## Course and facilities

The official site lists rental clubs, golf buggies, putting greens, short-game areas and lessons. It also publishes course-maintenance and overseeding arrangements, which can affect which course is available.

## Access and booking

Confirm the course, daylight or floodlit round and rate inclusions. Published maximum handicaps are 36 for men and 44 for women. The official page says food-and-drink all-inclusive green-fee arrangements apply only in winter; verify your exact booking.

## Course layouts

- Aspendos · 18 holes
- Olympos · 18 holes
- Termessos night routing · 18 holes
