Nobilis offers an 18-hole golf course on the ROBINSON resort grounds in the Belek area. Its parkland setting is associated with mature pine trees and the Acısu River.

## Course and facilities

The resort’s official page lists its golf club and access to golf services in the wider Belek region. A golf shuttle to other courses is advertised for a fee, not as an automatically free service.

## Access and booking

The official resort page lists handicap limits of 28 for men and 36 for women. Confirm current visitor access, green fees, any resort-package inclusion, lessons and seasonal maintenance before booking.

## Course layouts

- Nobilis · 18 holes · par 72
