Cornelia’s Faldo course has 27 physical holes, not three separate 18-hole courses. The King, Queen and Prince names describe different 18-hole combinations of the three nines.

## Course and facilities

The club lists a Sir Nick Faldo-designed championship layout and academy. Its rotating-nines format gives operational flexibility during maintenance; it does not guarantee your preferred routing on a specific day.

## Access and booking

The official course page lists handicap limits of 28 for men and 36 for women. Confirm which 18-hole combination is assigned, current seeding dates and outside-guest rates. Do not treat the old 2024 maintenance notice as current.

## Course layouts

- King routing · 18 holes · par 72
- Queen routing · 18 holes · par 72
- Prince routing · 18 holes · par 72
