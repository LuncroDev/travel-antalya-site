Kaya Palazzo Golf Club’s official course page describes an 18-hole layout on a pine-forest site of roughly 650,000 square metres. The course combines broad fairways with strategically positioned bunkers.

## Course and facilities

The golf club has its own course information, scorecard, clubhouse restaurant and tee-time booking pages within the hotel group’s site. It should be treated as a golf venue rather than merely a hotel amenity.

## Access and booking

Use the tee-time contact and confirm visitor access, current handicap limits, maintenance, equipment hire and green-fee inclusions. A general room booking is not a tee-time confirmation.

- Kaya Palazzo course · 18 holes
