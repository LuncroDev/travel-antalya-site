National’s championship course was designed by David Feherty and David Jones and opened on 18 November 1994. It runs through pine and eucalyptus woodland. The current official course page lists 18 holes, with the final nine floodlit.

## Course and facilities

The club lists a driving range, putting and chipping areas, rental clubs, trolleys and buggies. Its course page records hosting the Turkish Airlines Open from 30 April to 3 May 2026.

## Access and booking

A handicap certificate is required; the current official limit is 36 for men and women. Book through the club and check seeding dates, twilight routing and equipment. Do not copy older 27-hole descriptions as the current layout.
