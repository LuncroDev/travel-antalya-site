Montgomerie Maxx Royal is a par-72 course in Belek’s pine-forest and coastal resort landscape. The official championship page lists a length of 6,522 metres and confirms floodlights on holes 10–18.

## Course and facilities

The club offers a driving range, putting and chipping areas, golf lessons, rental clubs and golf cars. The routing begins and finishes with par fives.

## Access and booking

Contact the golf club for a tee time rather than assuming hotel accommodation includes golf. Published handicap limits are 28 for men and 36 for women; soft spikes only. Confirm the specific day/night round and rate inclusions.

## Course layouts

- Championship Course · 18 holes · par 72
