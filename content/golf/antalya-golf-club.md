Antalya Golf Club is in the Belek/Kadriye resort area, not central Antalya. Its two courses were created by European Golf Design with David Jones as design consultant.

## Course and facilities

The Sultan is the longer championship test. Pasha is shorter and has more generous fairways, although doglegs, bunkers and undulating greens still require planning. The academy offers individual, group and junior instruction.

## Access and booking

Request the specific course, tee time and handicap policy. Confirm visitor green fees and what your booking includes; a booking at a nearby hotel does not by itself prove a golf reservation.

## Course layouts

- PGA Sultan · 18 holes · par 72
- The Pasha · 18 holes · par 72
