Sueno has 36 physical holes across two courses designed by International Design Group. Pines is the longer par-72 layout, while Dunes is a shorter par-69 option.

## Course and facilities

The official course page describes parkland-style fairways, a driving range and extensive putting and short-game facilities. The hotel and golf facilities are closely integrated.

## Access and booking

Specify Pines or Dunes in the reservation. The current course page lists handicap 36 for men and women. Request current maintenance dates and green-fee inclusions; older seeding notices remain visible on some pages.

## Course layouts

- Pines · 18 holes · par 72
- Dunes · 18 holes · par 69
