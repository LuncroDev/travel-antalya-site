Carya is a Thomson Perrett & Lobb design set among pine forest in the Belek region. The club opened in 2008 and offers 18-hole golf under floodlights as well as daytime rounds.

## Course and facilities

The official course page lists a double-sided academy, teaching studios, putting and short-game areas, rental clubs and golf buggies. The heathland-inspired design is a distinctive alternative to a conventional resort parkland course.

## Access and booking

The official site accepts tee-time requests, including a floodlight option. Published handicap limits are 36 for men and women, and non-metal spikes are required. Confirm the booking, night-golf slot, hire equipment and rate inclusions.

- Carya · 18 holes · par 72
