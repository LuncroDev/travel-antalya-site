Lykia Links is at Lykia World in Denizyaka, in Manavgat district east of Belek. The course is known for sandy coastal terrain, open horizons and wind exposure rather than the sheltered pine-forest setting of many Belek courses.

## Course and facilities

The official hotel site continues to describe its links golf operation. A current golf-tour operator notice states that the hotel, golf course and club will close for renovation from 1 December 2026.

## Access and booking

Before any booking for 1 December 2026 onward, obtain direct confirmation of whether the course is operating and its reopening date. The reported closure has not been independently confirmed by a published official notice in this research.

## Course layouts

- Lykia Links · 18 holes · par 72
