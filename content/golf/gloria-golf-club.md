Gloria Golf Club combines two 18-hole championship courses, Old and New, with the nine-hole Verde course. The complex was designed by Michel Gayon and is linked to the Gloria resort group.

## Course and facilities

Verde Select combines Verde’s nine holes with the front nine of either Old or New. It is an alternative routing, not an additional physical course. The golf complex offers practice and academy facilities.

## Access and booking

Choose the exact course or routing and ask about visitor access, handicap requirements, hire equipment and current maintenance. Clubhouse food-and-drink inclusions depend on the booked package; do not assume every green fee includes them.

## Course layouts

- Old Course · 18 holes
- New Course · 18 holes
- Verde Course · 9 holes
